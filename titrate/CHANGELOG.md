# Step up versions

/titrate/ always opens the newest version. Older versions stay live at their own path.

## v1 (7 Nov 2026) at /titrate/v1/
- Weekly step-up planner for GLPs: vial real mg, water, first shot date, step ladder in units, weeks per step.
- Each week shows date, units and real dose. Tap a week: pick a step, Double, Skip, or type units; "From here on" redraws every later week, "Just this week" is a one-off (trip doubles, skips).
- New vial flag, total used and mg left, Copy for WhatsApp, Copy link (the link carries every edit). Not in nav.
