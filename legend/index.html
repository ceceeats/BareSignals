Today · Acne v1 (Case)

What it is. A spot is a pore going wrong in four ways at once: the oil gland pumps too much oil, dead skin cells plug the opening, a skin bacterium everyone carries thrives inside, and the immune system overreacts. Every treatment on this page works by switching off one or more of those four.

What studies found. Dummy creams alone cut spots 35 to 60% in 12 weeks, so only the gap counts. Isotretinoin, the one drug that hits all four, gave the biggest gap of anything: about 58% more spots gone than placebo across 179 trials. No peptide has a proper acne trial…

What people report. Of about 42 people who put copper peptide (GHK-Cu) on their skin, half said red or brown marks and scars looked better, mostly while also using peels or acids…

Watch out. Itchy, same-size bumps in heat and humidity are often a yeast, not acne…
