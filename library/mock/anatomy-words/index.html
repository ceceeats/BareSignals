BPC-157

BPC-157

Tendon and gut repair, injury recovery

Tendons and ligaments heal slowly because very little blood reaches them. BPC-157 works like a foreman for repair: it calls new blood vessels into the injury, so repair cells and building materials can actually get there. In rats, cut Achilles tendons healed stronger and a fully cut muscle reconnected within 72 days, and in a small knee series 14 of 16 people said their pain eased. Most of what we know comes from animal work; the first proper human trial starts in 2027.

What it does

Tissue repair Cut rat Achilles tendons healed stronger with more collagen; a fully cut thigh muscle reconnected and regained strength over 72 days. No human tendon trial yet; the first (Arkansas, rotator cuff) starts January 2027.

Digestive & gut Shields the rat stomach from NSAID ulcers and calms colitis. In people, 53 with ulcerative colitis on 2 weeks of enemas did not do clearly better than placebo.

Inflammatory signaling Calms injury inflammation

Pain & sensory 14 of 16 knee-injection patients said pain was better; on BPC-157 alone, 7 of 12 felt it for 6 to 12 months. A clinic chart review with no control group.

Thymosin Beta-4

Thymosin Beta-4

Wound and heart repair, recovery

Before damaged tissue can be fixed, repair cells have to crawl to it. Thymosin beta-4 is the body’s own scaffolding crew: almost every cell makes it, and it holds actin, the building block cells crawl with, ready for wherever repair is needed. In rats it sped wound cover by 42% within 4 days and helped grow new blood vessels. It is the full-length repair protein many “TB-500” vials actually contain; in people, eye-drop trials have not yet hit their goals.

What it does

Tissue repair In rats with skin wounds, wound cover rose 42% by day 4 and up to 61% by day 7, with more collagen and new vessels. On leg ulcers in people, about 1 in 4 fully healed within 3 months on the gel, a hint, not a win.

KPV

A · now: box tags

KPV

Gut inflammation, skin calming

Inflammation is the body’s alarm, and sometimes it keeps ringing after the danger has passed. KPV is a three-amino-acid piece of a natural hormone (alpha-MSH) that helps switch the alarm off, keeping the hormone’s calming effect without its tanning one. Swallowed, it rides a gut transporter straight into the cells lining the colon, and in mice with colitis it calmed the colon and helped the lining heal. People add it to blends for an irritated gut and skin; human studies have not started yet.

Inflammation signalingTissue repairColon liningImmune signaling

What it does

Inflammatory signaling Users report calmer eczema, psoriasis and rosacea, but outside sellers’ sites we found no first-hand skin reports and no KPV acne study.

Tissue repair KPV gels cut inflammation and fought MRSA in wound models (animals).

Digestive & gut In mice with colitis, oral KPV calmed the colon and helped the lining heal; a colon-targeted form reached the colon 3.8 times better and worked at a 20 times lower dose. No human gut trial.

Immunity Blocks NF-κB, the main switch for inflammation signals, inside gut and immune cells (lab work).

5-Amino-1MQ

Fat loss, metabolism, NAD

Losing fat means getting fat cells to let go of what they store. 5-Amino-1MQ blocks NNMT, an enzyme that spends the cell’s NAD and methyl groups, a bit like closing a leak in the cell’s fuel line. In obese mice the fat cells shrank and cell metabolism kept more NAD; in older mice the muscle stem cells were supported and the muscle recovered. People who take it mostly report more energy and drive. All of this is mouse work; there are no human studies yet.
