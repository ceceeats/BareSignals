# Noopept
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/noopept
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: omberacetam, GVS-111, DVD-111, N-phenylacetyl-L-prolylglycine ethyl ester, Ноопепт
2. Origin: Lab-designed: dipeptide prodrug of cyclo-prolylglycine
3. Sequence: PG (2 amino acids). Pro-Gly dipeptide capped with an N-phenylacetyl group and an ethyl ester; converts to cyclo(Pro-Gly)
4. Shown with an FDA approved badge
5. Legal and approval status: Registered medicine in Russia, sold over the counter (since about 2006). In the US an unapproved new drug: FDA has sent warning letters (2019) and treats it as needing a drug application, yet it is widely sold online. Prescription only in Australia, controlled in Hungary since 2020, sale for human use banned in the UK. Not named on the WADA list.

## Summary
6. Noopept is a lab-designed dipeptide related to piracetam, not a natural peptide. It is used for memory and focus, and acts as a prodrug of cyclo-prolylglycine (cGP), a compound the body also makes, which boosts excitatory signalling and growth factors in rodents. Russian open studies report memory gains but had no placebo arm. About half of users notice nothing.
7. Punch line: Piracetam's tiny Russian cousin, a prodrug of cGP. A nootropic staple for recall and fluency, stacked with choline.
8. Used for: Memory, focus, verbal fluency

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
9. Cognition: Better memory scores in Russian studies
10. Neural plasticity: Raises BDNF and NGF in rodents
11. Neural protection: Shields neurons from glutamate damage in lab work

## How it works
12. Turns into cGP, the brain's own small cyclic peptide · Prodrug of cyclo(Pro-Gly) · role Cognition
   After absorption Noopept is cleaved and closed into a ring to form cyclo-prolylglycine, or cGP, a small cyclic peptide the brain already makes from IGF-1. cGP is thought to carry most of its effects. Human pharmacokinetics are poorly described, so how much cGP forms in people is uncertain.
13. Boosts fast excitatory signalling · AMPA positive modulation (cGP) · role Cognition
   cGP positively modulates AMPA receptors, the glutamate receptors behind fast excitatory signalling between neurons. Stronger AMPA signalling supports learning and memory formation. Its protective effects depend on AMPA and TrkB activation in lab work.
14. Raises growth factors for neurons · BDNF, NGF up (rodent) · role Neural plasticity
   In rodents it raised BDNF and NGF, growth factors that keep neurons healthy and support new connections. In cell culture cGP also raised BDNF. These effects have not been measured in people.
15. Blunts calcium and glutamate toxicity · Antioxidant, anti-excitotoxic · role Neural protection
   Rodent work shows it reduces damage from excess glutamate and calcium, which kill neurons when they flood in after injury. It also showed antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. This protection is shown in animals and cells, not in human trials.
16. Mechanism overview: Noopept is N-phenylacetyl-L-prolylglycine ethyl ester. After absorption it is cleaved and cyclised to cyclo-L-prolylglycine (cGP), an endogenous diketopiperazine also produced from IGF-1. cGP positively modulates AMPA receptors and its neuroprotective effects depend on AMPA and TrkB activation; in cell culture it raises BDNF. Rodent work also reports antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, reduced glutamate and calcium toxicity, and HIF-1 activation. Human pharmacokinetics are poorly described.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
17. Typical: 10–30 mg/day · 10 mg 2–3× daily · cycle 1.5–3 months, then 1 month off (Russian label) · route Oral, sublingual (label)
18. Half-life: Short; metabolite cGP lasts longer (rat)
19. Most people take: 10 mg twice daily (20 mg/day) is the Russian labelled dose, with a 30 mg/day ceiling. Users take 10 to 30 mg a day split into two or three doses; occasional reports of 40 to 60 mg.
20. Russian label: 1.5 to 3 months on, then about a month off. Longest human study lasted 56 days.

## What people report
21. A long running r/Nootropics staple taken 10 to 30 mg a day, often with a choline source and sometimes alongside Semax or Selank. Positive reports describe quicker recall, verbal fluency and clarity within an hour; many feel nothing. Headache is the most common complaint, then irritability, poor sleep with late doses, and in some a flat mood, low motivation or low libido. No aggregator counts located.
22. Liked / worked: Better memory scores in Russian open studies
23. Liked / worked: Low dose, well tolerated
24. What worked: Russian open studies report better memory scores in mild cognitive impairment and after stroke, with good tolerance.
25. Went wrong / did not work: No placebo controlled trials
26. Went wrong / did not work: Many users feel nothing
27. Went wrong / did not work: Blood pressure rise in a quarter of one record
28. What went wrong: No placebo controlled, Western or healthy adult trials. Roughly half of users report noticing nothing. Blood pressure rose in about a quarter of people in one tolerability record. Products tested often hold the wrong amount (9 of 12 off label).
29. Most reported: 10 to 30 mg a day, often with choline
30. Most reported: Quick clarity for some
31. Most reported: Headache most common
32. Most reported: Flat mood or low libido in some

## What has been tested
33. Cognition: Mild cognitive disorders vs piracetam (human)
34. Cognition: Post stroke cognition, 60 patients (human)
35. Neural plasticity: BDNF and NGF expression (rat)
36. Few human studies, none placebo controlled. Neznamov and Teleshova 2009 compared it with piracetam over 56 days in vascular and traumatic mild cognitive disorders. Amelin 2011 gave 20 mg daily for two months to 60 stroke patients in an open study. Rodent work shows BDNF and NGF increases.
37. What it helps: Russian open studies in mild cognitive impairment and after stroke report memory gains at 20 mg a day with good tolerance, but none had a placebo arm. Users take it for recall and verbal fluency; responses split, and headache is the usual complaint.
38. Trial: none. No registered human trial found. (checked 2026-10-23) [S1]

## Synergies (paired with)
39. Semax: Semax is reported to raise BDNF and monoamine turnover, while Noopept works through AMPA signalling and growth factors. Users pair them for memory and focus from overlapping angles. The pair has not been tested together.
40. Selank: Selank is used for calm and anxiety, which can balance the stimulating side of a focus stack with Noopept. People pair them for clear thinking without edge. It is user logic, not trial data.
41. Semax + Selank: The Semax and Selank blend adds BDNF support, alertness and calm on top of Noopept's AMPA and growth factor effects. Users stack all three for focus with calm. The combination has not been tested.
42. Dihexa: Dihexa boosts HGF and c-Met signalling to build synapses, while Noopept works through AMPA and BDNF. Both aim at memory through different growth pathways. The pair has not been tested together, and c-Met's link to cancer is a reason for caution.
43. Cerebrolysin: Cerebrolysin is a pig brain peptide mix with nerve growth activity, used for stroke and brain injury, and Noopept has been studied open label after stroke. People pair them for recovery and cognition. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
44. Designed in Moscow in the 1990s by Gudasheva, Seredenin and colleagues as a dipeptide relative of piracetam. Its main metabolite is cyclo-prolylglycine (cGP), a compound the body also makes.
45. First studied for: Mild cognitive problems after stroke, head injury and vascular disease in Russia.
46. Name story: 'Noo' from the Greek nous, mind, plus 'pept' for peptide. The INN name omberacetam files it with the racetams. [S2] (reported)
47. 1996: Synthesis reported by the Zakusov Institute group in Moscow
48. Early 2000s: Registered in Russia as Noopept
49. 2009: Open comparison with piracetam in mild cognitive disorders
50. 2011: Open study in 60 stroke patients
51. 2019: FDA warning letter to a US seller
52. Today: OTC in Russia, grey market online elsewhere
