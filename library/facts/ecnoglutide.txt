# Ecnoglutide
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/ecnoglutide
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: XW003, ecno, Sciwind GLP-1, biased GLP-1
2. Origin: Lab-modified natural chain: cAMP-biased GLP-1 analogue
3. Legal and approval status: Approved in China: type 2 diabetes in January 2026 and chronic weight management in March 2026 (NMPA). Not approved by the FDA or EMA. Not on the WADA list (inferred).

## Summary
4. Ecnoglutide is a GLP-1 receptor drug from Sciwind Biosciences in China, tuned to favour the cAMP signal over beta-arrestin, which the company says slows receptor wearing out. It is used for type 2 diabetes and weight loss, cutting appetite like other GLP-1 drugs. In Chinese adults it gave semaglutide-range weight loss, up to about 15% at 48 weeks. It is approved in China only.
5. Punch line: China's homegrown biased GLP-1, sold by Pfizer China. Weekly shot; up to 15% weight loss in company data.
6. Used for: Weight loss, type 2 diabetes

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Appetite & satiety: Cuts appetite like other GLP-1 drugs
8. Glucose & insulin: Approved in China for type 2 diabetes
9. Fat metabolism: Up to 15% weight loss in 48 weeks
10. Organ function: Halved liver fat in Phase 3

## How it works
11. Activates the GLP-1 receptor · GLP-1R agonist · role Appetite & satiety
   Ecnoglutide activates the GLP-1 receptor in the brain's appetite centres and gut. Hunger falls and the stomach slows. In the SLIMMER Phase 3 human trial in Chinese adults, weight loss was in the range of semaglutide.
12. Favours cAMP over beta-arrestin · Biased agonism · role Glucose & insulin
   When the GLP-1 receptor is switched on it sends signals through cAMP and also recruits beta-arrestin, which pulls the receptor inside the cell. Ecnoglutide is a biased agonist that favours the cAMP route. The aim is stronger insulin and appetite signalling per dose.
13. Less receptor shutdown over time (company claim) · Reduced desensitisation · role Appetite & satiety
   Less beta-arrestin recruitment should mean fewer receptors pulled off the cell surface, so the response fades less over time. The company claims this reduced desensitisation. It has not been shown to give a clinical edge over standard GLP-1 drugs.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
14. Typical: 1.2, 1.8 or 2.4 mg · Once weekly · cycle Ongoing · route Sub-Q (trial)
15. Most people take: Phase 3 doses were 1.2, 1.8 and 2.4 mg once a week; 2.4 mg gave the most weight loss. Chinese label dosing not found in the sources checked.

## What people report
16. Discussed as China's first homegrown 'biased' GLP-1 and as a cheaper rival to semaglutide; Pfizer China sells it. Little first-hand use reported outside China.
17. Liked / worked: Up to 13.2% weight loss at 40 weeks
18. Liked / worked: Liver fat down 53%
19. What worked: SLIMMER: 9.1%, 10.9% and 13.2% weight loss at 40 weeks by dose versus 0.1% gain on placebo; 15.4% at 48 weeks on 2.4 mg in company data, still falling. Liver fat down 53% at 40 weeks on 2.4 mg. Adolescents: BMI down 11 to 13% at 20 weeks.
20. Went wrong / did not work: Common mild GI effects
21. Went wrong / did not work: China-only data
22. What went wrong: Stomach side effects are common (adverse events in 93% on drug versus 84% on placebo, mostly mild GI). Data are from Chinese patients only so far.
23. Most reported: China's first biased GLP-1
24. Most reported: Cheaper semaglutide rival

## What has been tested
25. Fat metabolism: Obesity, SLIMMER Phase 3, China (trial)
26. Glucose & insulin: Type 2 diabetes, Phase 3 (trial)
27. Fat metabolism: Adolescents with obesity, Phase 1b (trial)
28. Phase 3 SLIMMER in 664 Chinese adults at 1.2, 1.8 and 2.4 mg weekly; type 2 diabetes Phase 3; a 20-week Phase 1b in 48 adolescents, with a larger adolescent Phase 3 underway.
29. What it helps: In Chinese adults it gives weight loss in the range of semaglutide: 13.2% at 40 weeks on 2.4 mg, and 15.4% at 48 weeks in company data, with a large drop in liver fat. It is approved in China only.
30. Trial: active · Phase 3 (adolescents). After approval in Chinese adults, a Phase 3 in adolescents with obesity is underway. (checked 2026-10-23) [S1]

## Synergies (paired with)
31. Semaglutide: Semaglutide and ecnoglutide both activate the GLP-1 receptor, with ecnoglutide biased toward cAMP signalling. They are alternatives giving similar weight loss. Stacking them has not been tested.
32. Liraglutide: Liraglutide is an older daily GLP-1 drug on the same receptor. The two are alternatives, not complementary. The pair has not been tested together.
33. GLP-1: Native GLP-1 is the hormone ecnoglutide is modelled on, but it lasts about two minutes. It is a reference point for the mechanism, not a working partner. There is no trial data on the pair.
34. Mazdutide: Mazdutide adds a glucagon arm for liver fat and energy use, while ecnoglutide is GLP-1 only. Both act on GLP-1, so combining them doubles that side. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
35. Made by Sciwind Biosciences in Hangzhou, China. It is a GLP-1 drug tuned to favour one signal inside the cell (cAMP) and avoid another (beta-arrestin), which the company says slows the receptor wearing out.
36. First studied for: Weight loss and type 2 diabetes in China.
37. Jun 2025: SLIMMER Phase 3 in The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology: up to 13.2% at 40 weeks
38. Jan 2026: Approved in China for type 2 diabetes

40. Mar 2026: Approved in China for weight management
