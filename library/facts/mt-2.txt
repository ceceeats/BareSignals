# MT-2
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/mt-2
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Melanotan II, MT-II, Melanotan-2, Ac-Nle-c[Asp-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Lys]-NH2
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: LDHFRWK (7 amino acids). Ring-closed (cyclic); Nle shown as L, D-Phe shown as F; acetylated start, amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Never approved; flagged by regulators as unsafe/illegal to sell. On the FDA PCAC agenda (Melanotan II). Grey-market only.

## Summary
5. MT-2 is Melanotan II, a synthetic cyclic analogue of alpha-MSH that switches on melanocortin receptors broadly. People inject it mainly for sunless tanning, and it also raises libido and cuts appetite. It is unapproved and sold on the grey market, with common nausea and reports of darkened moles and a melanoma concern.
6. Punch line: The Barbie drug. Injected for a sunless tan; also lifts libido, cuts appetite, darkens moles. Nausea is common.
7. Used for: Tanning without much sun; libido

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Tanning: Darkens skin without sun
9. Skin & aesthetics: Darker moles and freckles
10. Connection & desire: Raises arousal
11. Appetite & satiety: Lowers appetite

## How it works
12. Darkens skin by switching on pigment cells · MC1R, eumelanin · role Tanning
   Melanotan II activates MC1R on melanocytes, the pigment cells in skin. This raises cAMP inside the cell and shifts output toward eumelanin, the dark brown pigment. Human studies show reliable tanning with little or no sun.
13. Darkens moles and freckles too · Melanocyte MC1R · role Skin & aesthetics
   The same MC1R activation reaches every melanocyte, including those in moles and freckles, which darken too. Users also report new pigmented spots, and case reports have raised a melanoma concern that remains unresolved.
14. Triggers arousal and erections · Central MC3R, MC4R · role Connection & desire
   Melanotan II crosses into the brain, where it activates MC3R and MC4R in the hypothalamus. These receptors drive sexual arousal, and early human studies confirmed spontaneous erections in men. The broad receptor activity also brings nausea and flushing.
15. Cuts appetite · Hypothalamic MC4R · role Appetite & satiety
   MC4R in the hypothalamus is a central appetite brake. Activating it cuts hunger, and human studies show a drop in appetite on melanotan II. Nausea likely adds to the effect.
16. Mechanism overview: Melanotan II is a cyclic alpha-MSH analogue that hits several melanocortin receptors at once. At MC1R on melanocytes it raises cAMP and switches pigment output toward dark eumelanin, which tans skin and also darkens moles and freckles. It crosses into the brain, where MC4R and MC3R activation in the hypothalamus drives sexual arousal and spontaneous erections and cuts appetite. That lack of selectivity explains both its appeal and its nausea, flushing and pigment changes.

## What has been tested
37. Tanning: Sunless tanning (human)
38. Connection & desire: Erectile response (human)
39. Appetite & satiety: Appetite drop (human)
40. Early human tanning and erectile studies confirmed the effects; development as a drug was dropped, partly over its broad, messy receptor activity.
41. What it helps: Human studies show reliable sunless tanning, erectile response in men and a drop in appetite. Users inject it mainly for tanning and confirm it works, though nausea is common. Reported downsides are darkened moles, new pigmented spots and a melanoma concern raised by case reports, alongside widespread unregulated sale.
42. Trial: none. Only small university trials for erectile dysfunction and tanning in the late 1990s and early 2000s; its descendants bremelanotide and afamelanotide became the approved drugs instead. (checked 2026-10-23) [S5] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
43. People with many moles or melanoma history: Case reports of eruptive moles and melanoma after melanotan use. (reported)
44. People with high blood pressure or heart disease: Same receptor class as bremelanotide, which raises blood pressure. (inferred)
45. People on oral naltrexone: Bremelanotide lowers naltrexone levels; melanotan II shares the mechanism. (inferred)
46. Men prone to priapism (sickle cell): Melanotan II causes spontaneous erections; priapism is reported. (reported)

## Synergies (paired with)
47. PT-141: PT-141 is a metabolite of MT-2 tuned toward MC3R and MC4R for desire, while MT-2 adds strong MC1R tanning. They hit the same receptor family, so stacking adds overlap and more nausea risk. The pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
48. Melanotan II, a synthetic cyclic analogue of alpha-MSH that activates melanocortin receptors broadly, driving tanning, appetite suppression and erections.
49. First studied for: Developed at the University of Arizona as a sunless-tanning agent; the erection effect spun off into PT-141.
50. Name story: Melanotan II, the Arizona tanning project's second analog. It reportedly got a new career when one of the scientists accidentally injected himself with double the dose and had an eight-hour erection, which led to PT-141. [S6] (reported)
51. 1980s to 1990s: Built at University of Arizona as a sunless tanning agent
52. 1990s: Erection effect spun off into PT-141
53. 2000s to 2010s: Drug development dropped; regulators warn against sales
54. Today: Grey-market tanning peptide; on the FDA PCAC agenda
