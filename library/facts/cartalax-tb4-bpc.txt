# Cartalax + Tβ4 + BPC-157
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/cartalax-tb4-bpc
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Cartalax/TB4/BPC 20 mg, AED + thymosin beta-4 + BPC-157, joint blend, cartilage blend
2. Origin: Blend of 3 peptides
3. Legal and approval status: US: not approved. BPC-157 and the TB-500 fragment were removed from FDA Category 2 on 15 Apr 2026 and won an 8-6-1 PCAC vote in July 2026; full Tβ4 and Cartalax were not on that list. WADA: Tβ4 and BPC-157 banned. Russia: AED sold as an oral bioregulator supplement, not a medicine.

## Summary
4. This three-peptide joint blend combines the cartilage bioregulator Cartalax with full thymosin beta-4 and BPC-157. People combine them so Cartalax targets cartilage cells while the other two bring blood supply and general tissue repair. Evidence is per component, Cartalax data is cell culture and Russian sources, and no study has tested the three together.
5. Punch line: Cartalax targets cartilage; Tβ4 and BPC-157 add blood flow and repair. Used for knee and hip wear.
6. Used for: Joint and cartilage support

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Bone & cartilage: Cartalax aimed at cartilage cells
8. Tissue repair: Tβ4 and BPC-157 tissue repair
9. Inflammatory signaling: All three dampen inflammation
10. Aging & senescence: Bioregulator framed as anti-aging for joints
11. Muscle & performance (backed only by animal or user reports): Tβ4 and BPC-157 used for muscle injury

## How it works
12. Prompts cartilage cells to multiply, in culture · AED on chondrocyte proliferation · role Bone & cartilage
   Cartalax, the tripeptide AED, acts on chondrocytes, the cells that make and maintain cartilage. In rat chondrocyte cultures it raised cell numbers, more so in cells from old rats. This is cell data only, from the Khavinson bioregulator line.
13. Said to switch on cartilage matrix genes · Collagen II, aggrecan (claimed) · role Bone & cartilage
   AED is claimed to switch on cartilage matrix genes, mainly collagen type II and aggrecan, the proteins that give cartilage its strength and cushion. That would rebuild matrix rather than only add cells. This rests on Russian bioregulator claims, not independent data.
14. Moves repair cells and builds new vessels · G-actin sequestering, cell migration · role Tissue repair
   Thymosin beta-4 binds G-actin so repair cells can move, and it promotes new blood vessels. Joint tissue has a poor blood supply, so bringing in cells and vessels is the logic for its role here. It has Phase 2 human trials in wounds and dry eye, but not in joints.
15. Speeds tendon and ligament healing in animals · Angiogenesis, FAK-paxillin · role Tissue repair
   BPC-157 drives vessel growth and moves tendon fibroblasts through the FAK-paxillin pathway. That speeds tendon and ligament healing around the joint in rodent models. Human data are thin, and no study tests the three together.
16. Mechanism overview: Cartalax is the tripeptide Ala-Glu-Asp (AED), from the Khavinson bioregulator line, claimed to act on chondrocyte gene expression; published data are rat chondrocyte cultures showing more cell division. Thymosin beta-4 is a 43-residue actin-sequestering peptide that promotes cell migration and angiogenesis, with phase 2 human wound and eye trials. BPC-157 is a 15-residue gastric fragment that speeds tendon and ligament healing in rodents. The stack pairs a cartilage-specific signal with two general repair signals.

## What is inside the blend
17. Cartalax (AED): 10 mg · Cartilage cell signal
18. Tβ4: 5 mg · Cell migration and new blood vessels
19. BPC-157: 5 mg · Tendon and ligament healing
20. Most common ratio: Cartalax 10 · TB-500 5 · BPC-157 5 = 20 mg (6 of 13 listings (tied))
21. Other ratio seen: Cartalax 10 · TB-500 10 · BPC-157 10 = 30 mg (6 listings (tied))
22. Other ratio seen: Cartalax 20 · TB-500 5 · BPC-157 5 (page contradicts itself) = 30 mg (1 listing)
23. Version Common retail: Cartalax 10 · Tβ4 5 · BPC-157 5 = 20 mg
24. Why Cartalax is in it: The only part aimed at cartilage cells themselves
25. Why Tβ4 is in it: Moves repair cells and builds vessels into poorly supplied joint tissue
26. Why BPC-157 is in it: Tendon and ligament repair around the joint
27. Mixing chemistry: Tβ4 is most stable around pH 5.5 to 6, BPC-157 around 6.5 to 8.5 by their patents; the vial is a compromise.
28. Mixing chemistry: Tβ4 carries a methionine that oxidises (its main breakdown product); keep the vial cold and away from light.
29. Mixing chemistry: Cartalax is half the vial's mass and is an acidic tripeptide (two acid residues), so it likely sets the vial's pH.
30. Mixing chemistry: At 43 residues Tβ4 is the one most likely to clump; gentle swirling, no shaking.
31. Self-mixing: Separate vials let you dose Tβ4 and BPC-157 at their usual amounts instead of the low share they get here, at the cost of more injections or an unknown combined pH.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
32. Typical: No standard dose · 1× daily · cycle 30 days to 8 weeks · route Sub-Q (reported)
33. Half-life: per component
34. Most people take: No published blend dose. People size it on Cartalax (about 1 mg a day in stack posts). 20 mg in 2 mL: 10 units gives 500 mcg Cartalax, 250 mcg Tβ4, 250 mcg BPC-157; 20 units doubles it.

## What people report
35. Sub-Q once daily, usually 30 days to 8 weeks. One promoter's joint stack runs Cartalax 1 mg, BPC-157 500 mcg and TB-500 500 mcg daily (plus GHK-Cu); this blend can't reach that BPC dose without overshooting Cartalax.
36. Liked / worked: Each part has a distinct joint role
37. Liked / worked: Tβ4 has human phase 2 wound data
38. Liked / worked: AED raised chondrocyte numbers in culture
39. What worked: No blend-specific report pool. Writers promoting Cartalax stacks say people whose joints improved on BPC-157 and TB-500 but never felt restored describe joints that finally felt different with Cartalax added. Unquantified.
40. Went wrong / did not work: Cartalax evidence is preclinical
41. Went wrong / did not work: Tβ4 and BPC-157 underdosed at this ratio
42. Went wrong / did not work: No trial of the stack
43. What went wrong: All Cartalax joint claims are preclinical or from Russian bioregulator sources. At this ratio Tβ4 and BPC-157 are dosed well below their usual standalone amounts. Cartilage change takes months, so short runs are easy to misread. No adverse-event data for the combination.
44. Most reported: Run 30 days to 8 weeks
45. Most reported: Picked for knees and hips, early wear
46. Most reported: Promoters say it helps after BPC/TB plateaus

## What has been tested
47. Never tested as a combination. Cartalax (AED) has rat chondrocyte cultures (cell numbers up 1.4 to 2.1 times, more in cells from old rats) and Russian bioregulator literature; Tβ4 has phase 2 human wound and eye trials; BPC-157 has animal injury models. A June 2026 stack review calls the joint stack community practice, not validated practice.
48. What it helps: Evidence is per component. AED: cell culture only. Tβ4: phase 2 human trials in pressure ulcers, epidermolysis bullosa and dry eye. BPC-157: rodent injury models, thin human data. No study tests them together; joint benefit is promoter-reported and unquantified.

## Who shouldn't take it
49. People with active or recent cancer: Raises VEGFR2 signalling and new vessel growth in animal work; tumours use the same pathway. (inferred)
50. Drug-tested athletes: Banned since 2022 under S0 (non-approved substances); positives have led to bans. (verified)
51. People treating a diagnosed joint disease with it: No controlled trials show benefit; delays proven care. (inferred)
52. People with proliferative retinopathy or vascular malformations: Pro-angiogenic peptides could feed abnormal vessel growth. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
53. GHK-Cu: GHK-Cu adds copper delivery and collagen remodelling, while the stack aims at cartilage cells and joint repair. People add it for broader matrix support. Nothing here has been tested together, so the pairing is user logic, not trial data.
54. KPV: KPV blocks NF-κB in immune cells, while the stack works on cartilage and soft tissue repair. People add it to calm inflamed joints during repair. The combination has not been tested.
55. Chonluten: Chonluten is another Khavinson bioregulator, claimed to tune airway tissue genes, while Cartalax is the cartilage member of the same line. People run several bioregulators in one course. Both rest on claimed data, and the pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
56. A three-peptide joint vial: the cartilage bioregulator Cartalax (Ala-Glu-Asp, 10 mg) with full thymosin beta-4 (5 mg) and BPC-157 (5 mg).
57. First studied for: Built for joints: Cartalax aimed at cartilage cells, Tβ4 and BPC-157 for general tissue repair and blood supply.
58. Name story: Cart from cartilage plus the -lax ending used across the Khavinson bioregulator range. The active is the tripeptide Ala-Glu-Asp, AED. [S1] (inferred)
59. 1981: Thymosin beta-4 sequenced
60. 1993: BPC-157 first published
61. 1990s to 2000s: Khavinson group develops tissue bioregulators; AED linked to cartilage
62. 2023: AED shown to raise rat chondrocyte numbers in culture
63. 2026: Cartalax joint stacks promoted; three-peptide vial appears
64. Today: Sold as a joint blend, 10 + 5 + 5 mg
