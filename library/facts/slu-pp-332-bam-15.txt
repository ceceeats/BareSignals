# SLU-PP-332 + BAM-15 oral
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/slu-pp-332-bam-15
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: SLU-PP-332 / BAM15 capsules, SLUBAM, BAM15 + SLU-PP-332 stack, ERR agonist + mitochondrial uncoupler, oral fat-loss stack
2. Origin: Blend of 2 small molecules, oral capsule
3. Legal and approval status: Preclinical everywhere: no approved product, no registered human trial for either compound. Sold as a research chemical. WADA: SLU-PP-332 is an ERR agonist, and metabolic modulators are banned under S4 (inferred); BAM-15 falls under S0.

## Summary
4. This oral capsule pairs two lab compounds sold for fat loss: BAM-15, a mitochondrial uncoupler that makes cells burn fuel as heat, and SLU-PP-332, an exercise mimic that switches on ERR receptors. All evidence is in mice and per compound, and neither has a human trial. The capsule's SLU-PP-332 share is tiny and poorly absorbed, so it works, if at all, as a BAM-15 tablet.
5. Punch line: Oral fat-loss capsule: an exercise mimic plus a mitochondrial uncoupler that burns fuel as heat. Mouse data only.
6. Used for: Fat loss, often on top of a GLP-1

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Cellular energy: BAM-15 burns fuel as heat; SLU-PP-332 mimics exercise
8. Fat metabolism: Both cut fat mass in mice
9. Muscle & performance: SLU-PP-332 raised endurance in mice
10. Glucose & insulin: BAM-15 improved insulin response in mice
11. Organ function (backed only by animal or user reports): BAM-15 cleared liver fat in mice

## How it works
12. Lets mitochondria leak protons so fuel burns as heat · Mitochondrial uncoupling, inner membrane · role Cellular energy
   BAM-15 carries protons back across the inner mitochondrial membrane, uncoupling fuel burning from ATP production. The cell has to burn more fat and sugar to keep its energy up, and the extra is lost as heat. Unlike DNP, it spares the outer cell membrane. Shown in mice.
13. Cut fat and liver fat without eating less · BAM15 in diet-induced obese mice · role Fat metabolism
   In diet-induced obese mice, BAM-15 taken in food cut body fat and liver fat and improved insulin response on clamp testing. Food intake, lean mass and body temperature did not change. These are mouse results; there are no human studies.
14. Switches on the 'exercise' genes in muscle · ERRα/β/γ agonist · role Muscle & performance
   SLU-PP-332 switches on ERR alpha, beta and gamma, the estrogen-related receptors that run endurance gene programmes in muscle. That builds more mitochondria and shifts muscle toward fat burning. Shown in mice given injections; it is described as poorly absorbed by mouth.
15. More fat burning and endurance in mice · Oxidative fibre shift, PGC-1α · role Cellular energy
   Through PGC-1α and an oxidative fibre shift, injected SLU-PP-332 raised treadmill endurance and cut fat mass in mice. The oral dose in this capsule is far below any studied amount. Nobody has tested the two compounds together.
16. Mechanism overview: BAM-15 is a lipophilic weak acid that carries protons across the mitochondrial inner membrane, uncoupling oxidation from ATP synthesis without depolarising the plasma membrane (unlike DNP). In diet-induced obese mice it was orally bioavailable and reduced fat mass and hepatic lipids, improving insulin sensitivity on clamp, with no change in food intake, lean mass or body temperature. SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic pan-agonist of estrogen-related receptors α, β and γ that drives oxidative muscle gene programmes; mice received 25 to 50 mg/kg intraperitoneally once or twice daily. Its oral bioavailability is described as poor, which led to the orally active analogue SLU-PP-915. A 250 mcg oral dose is roughly 0.1% of a mouse-scaled 50 mg/kg dose.

## What is inside the blend
17. BAM-15 50 mg: 50 mg per capsule · Uncoupler: raises resting fuel burn, works by mouth
18. SLU-PP-332 250 mcg: 0.25 mg per capsule · ERR agonist: exercise-like muscle signal (tiny oral share)
19. Most common ratio: BAM-15 50 mg · SLU-PP-332 250 mcg per capsule, 60 capsules = 50.25 mg per capsule (9 of 10 listings)
20. Other ratio seen: BAM-15 50 mg · SLU-PP-332 50 mg per capsule = 100 mg per capsule (1 listing (option))
21. Other ratio seen: BAM-15 50 mg · SLU-PP-332 100 mcg per vial = liquid vial (1 listing, discontinued)
22. Version Most listings: BAM-15 50 mg · SLU-PP-332 250 mcg per capsule, 60 capsules = 50.25 mg per capsule
23. Version Older tablet (not found this pass): SLU-PP-332 100 mcg · BAM-15 25 mg per tablet, 60 tablets = 25.1 mg per tablet
24. Why BAM-15 is in it: The only part with oral fat-loss data; raises calorie burn at rest
25. Why SLU-PP-332 is in it: Adds an exercise-like muscle signal, in theory
26. Mixing chemistry: Dry capsule, not a liquid vial: no pH or reconstitution issues.
27. Mixing chemistry: Main risk is fill accuracy: 250 mcg is hard to spread evenly through a 50 mg capsule.
28. Mixing chemistry: Keep dry and sealed; moisture and heat speed breakdown of both.
29. Mixing chemistry: Ask for per-component mg by LC-MS or HPLC, not one purity number.
30. Self-mixing: Buying each as separate capsules lets people dose BAM-15 and SLU-PP-332 independently; the combined capsule locks a ratio that is almost all BAM-15.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
31. Typical: 1 capsule (50 mg BAM-15, 250 mcg SLU-PP-332) · 1× daily · cycle 4–8 weeks (BAM-15 reports) · route Oral (reported)
32. Half-life: per component
33. Most people take: No standard dose. People size it by the BAM-15 share: one 50 mg capsule a day; guides cap BAM-15 near 70 mg a day, so a second capsule goes past it. The SLU-PP-332 that comes along is far below any studied amount.

## What people report
34. One capsule a day; seller guides cap BAM-15 near 70 mg a day. People watch for warmth, sweating and a fast heartbeat. An older tablet used 25 mg BAM-15 with 100 mcg SLU-PP-332. Often stacked with a GLP-1 such as retatrutide.
35. Liked / worked: BAM-15 cut fat without eating less, in mice
36. Liked / worked: BAM-15 works by mouth in mice
37. Liked / worked: SLU-PP-332 raised endurance in mice
38. What worked: In mice, each one lowered fat and improved insulin response. Seller content claims the pair burns fat 'at rest and in training' while keeping muscle; no human outcome data back it.
39. Went wrong / did not work: Never given to people in a trial
40. Went wrong / did not work: SLU-PP-332 share ~1/800 of the mouse-scaled dose
41. Went wrong / did not work: Uncoupler class carries overheating risk
42. What went wrong: The SLU-PP-332 share is tiny. 250 mcg is roughly 1/800 of the dose used in mice after scaling to a human, and the compound is poorly absorbed orally, so the capsule works, if at all, as a BAM-15 tablet. One reviewer reported fatigue and burnout within two weeks on SLU-PP-332 alone.
43. Most reported: One capsule a day
44. Most reported: Warmth, sweating, fast heartbeat
45. Most reported: Cap near 70 mg BAM-15 a day
46. Most reported: Stacked with GLP-1s

## What has been tested
47. Fat metabolism: BAM15 in diet-induced obese mice, oral (mouse)
48. Glucose & insulin: BAM15 clamp studies (mouse)
49. Muscle & performance: SLU-PP-332 treadmill endurance, injected (mouse)
50. Fat metabolism: The two combined (none)
51. Never tested together. BAM-15: orally bioavailable in mice, cut body fat and liver fat without changing food intake, lean mass or body temperature (Nature Communications 2020). SLU-PP-332: given by injection to mice at 25 to 50 mg/kg; raised endurance and cut fat mass; described as poorly absorbed by mouth.
52. What it helps: All evidence is in mice and per compound. BAM-15 cut body fat and liver fat and improved insulin response when eaten in food. SLU-PP-332 improved endurance and fat mass when injected. Nobody has tested the pair, and the capsule's SLU-PP-332 share is far below any studied amount.

## Who shouldn't take it
53. People exercising or living in heat: Uncouplers like DNP cause fatal overheating. (inferred)
54. Drug-tested athletes: Sold as an "exercise mimetic"; metabolic modulators fall under WADA S4.4. (inferred)
55. People on thyroid hormone or stimulants: Metabolic rate already raised; heat load stacks. (inferred)
56. People with heart disease: ERR agonism reshapes heart muscle metabolism; no human data at all. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
57. Retatrutide: Retatrutide cuts appetite and raises energy use through three receptors, with large human trials. This capsule adds a calorie burn signal without eating less, from mice only. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
58. Tirzepatide: Tirzepatide cuts appetite through GIP and GLP-1 receptors and has large human trials. People add this capsule to raise calorie burn on top of eating less. The pair has not been tested together.
59. Semaglutide: Semaglutide reduces appetite through the GLP-1 receptor, while BAM-15 raised fat burning in mice without changing intake. People combine eating less with burning more. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
60. MOTS-c: MOTS-c activates AMPK, the energy sensor, another exercise mimic route alongside SLU-PP-332's ERR signal. People stack them for endurance and fat loss. Each has mouse data only, and the pair has not been tested.
61. 5-Amino-1MQ: 5-Amino-1MQ blocks NNMT in fat cells and shrank fat cells in obese mice, a different target from uncoupling and ERR. People add it for fat loss. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
62. L-Carnitine: L-carnitine shuttles fatty acids into mitochondria, feeding the fat burning that BAM-15 speeds up. The pairing makes sense on paper. Carnitine has small oral weight loss effects; the pair has not been tested.
63. AOD-9604: AOD-9604 is a growth hormone fragment aimed at fat breakdown, with rodent data only. Users add it to this capsule for fat loss. The pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
64. An oral capsule pairing two lab compounds sold for fat loss: BAM-15, a mitochondrial uncoupler that makes cells burn fuel as heat, and SLU-PP-332, an 'exercise in a pill' that switches on ERR receptors. The common capsule holds 50 mg BAM-15 and 250 mcg SLU-PP-332, 60 capsules a bottle.
65. First studied for: Grey-market stack that took off in 2025, pitched as fat burning that spares muscle. Neither compound has been given to people in a registered trial.
66. Name story: SLU stands for Saint Louis University, where the ERR agonist series was made; PP-332 is its compound number. BAM15 is a code from its discovery screen. The name just lists both. [S1] (reported)
67. 2013: BAM15 described as a mitochondrial uncoupler that spares the cell membrane
68. 2020: BAM15 cuts fat and liver fat in obese mice, taken by mouth
69. 2023: SLU-PP-332 raises running endurance in mice
70. 2024: SLU-PP-332 eases metabolic syndrome in obese mice
71. 2025: Sold together as oral tablets and capsules
72. 2026: Oral successor SLU-PP-915 reported in mice
