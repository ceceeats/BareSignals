# Cortagen
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/cortagen
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: bioregulator, AEDP, Ala-Glu-Asp-Pro
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: AEDP (4 amino acids)
4. Legal and approval status: Not an approved drug anywhere; sold as a Khavinson bioregulator.

## Summary
5. Cortagen is a short synthetic peptide from the Khavinson family of Russian bioregulators, aimed at the brain cortex. It was developed for nerve repair and cognition, and it is claimed to shift cortex gene activity and aid nerve regrowth. Russian rat studies report regeneration after injury, but the claims rest largely on one research group and lack Western validation.
6. Punch line: Khavinson's brain cortex bioregulator. Built in Russia for nerve repair; longevity forums take it for cognition.
7. Used for: Brain recovery and cognition

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Neural protection: Brain cortex support
9. Cognition: Cognition claims
10. DNA & telomeres: Gene-regulating claims
11. Tissue repair: Nerve repair claims

## How it works
12. Claimed to tune tissue genes · Khavinson group · role DNA & telomeres
   The Khavinson group proposes that Cortagen acts directly on DNA to adjust gene activity in the tissue it targets, not through a surface receptor. The intent is to restore a younger expression pattern. The claim comes from one group, without Western replication.
13. Claimed to shift gene activity in brain cortex · Cortex gene expression · role Neural protection
   The group reports that Cortagen shifts gene activity in cells of the brain cortex, the outer layer that handles thinking and memory. Russian human reports describe effects on brain function. None of this has been independently confirmed.
14. Claimed to aid nerve regrowth after injury · Nerve regeneration (rat) · role Tissue repair
   In rat injury models, the group reports that Cortagen helped damaged nerves regrow and recover. That is the basis for its use in brain and nerve recovery. These are Russian animal findings, not independently repeated.
15. Mechanism overview: Cortagen is a short Khavinson bioregulator aimed at the brain cortex. The group claims it shifts gene activity in cortical tissue and aids nerve regrowth after injury, shown in rat work. These claims come from one group without independent Western replication.

## Who shouldn't take it
36. People with active or recent cancer: Marketed as gene-regulating; no human tumour safety data. (inferred)
37. People treating a diagnosed brain disease with it: No controlled trials show benefit; delays proven care. (inferred)

39. Thymalin: Thymalin rebalances T and B cells in Russian human data, while Cortagen is aimed at brain recovery, so the pair covers immune and brain aging in one course. The combination is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
40. A short synthetic peptide from the Khavinson family of Russian bioregulators, aimed at the brain cortex.
41. First studied for: Developed in Russia as a brain-specific bioregulator for nerve repair and cognition.
42. Name story: Cortex plus '-gen': Khavinson's group designed it from the amino acid analysis of Cortexin, a brain-cortex peptide extract. AEDP is the one-letter code for Ala-Glu-Asp-Pro. [S5] (verified)
43. 1990s to 2000s: Khavinson bioregulator peptide aimed at the brain cortex
44. Today: Russian bioregulator for nerve repair and cognition; thin data
