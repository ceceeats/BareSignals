# 5-Amino-1MQ
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/5-amino-1mq
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: methylquinolinium, 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium iodide, 5-Amino-1-MQ iodide, NNMT inhibitor 5-amino-1MQ
2. Origin: Not a peptide
3. Legal and approval status: Unapproved research chemical everywhere.

## Summary
4. 5-Amino-1MQ is a small molecule, not a peptide, that blocks the enzyme NNMT, which links NAD and methylation metabolism in fat. It is studied to shrink fat cells and raise cellular NAD, and users take it as an oral capsule for fat loss and energy, often stacked with MOTS-c and NAD+. Evidence is mouse-only, and no human trials exist.
5. Punch line: Oral NNMT blocker, not a peptide. Shrinks fat cells and lifts NAD in mice; often stacked with MOTS-c.
6. Used for: Fat loss, metabolic support

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Fat metabolism: Shrinks fat cells in mice
8. Cellular energy: Raises cell NAD in mice
9. Tissue repair: Muscle stem cell support in mice
10. Drive & mental stamina: Users report more energy
11. Muscle & performance (backed only by animal or user reports): Older mouse muscle recovered
12. Aging & senescence (backed only by animal or user reports): Studied for aging metabolism
13. Pain & sensory (backed only by animal or user reports): Some users report less pain

## How it works
14. Blocks a fat-cell enzyme · NNMT inhibition · role Fat metabolism
   5-Amino-1MQ is a small molecule that inhibits nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT). NNMT is highly expressed in the fat tissue of obese animals. Blocking it shifts fat cells toward burning more energy and storing less fat, shown in mouse studies only.
15. Spares NAD and methyl groups · NAD, SAM pools · role Cellular energy
   NNMT normally methylates nicotinamide using SAM, which drains both the NAD salvage pathway and the methyl donor pool. Blocking NNMT keeps nicotinamide available to make NAD+ and preserves SAM. This sparing effect is shown in cell and mouse work.
16. Shrank fat cells in obese mice · Adipocyte NNMT · role Fat metabolism
   In diet-induced obese mice, the inhibitor reduced fat build-up, shrank fat cells and improved metabolic markers. These are the core findings behind its fat loss use. No human trials exist.
17. Claimed to aid aged muscle stem cells · NNMT, satellite cells · role Tissue repair
   One research group claims NNMT inhibition reactivates muscle stem cells, known as satellite cells, in aged animals. That would support muscle repair with age. The claim comes from a single group and has not been tested in people.
18. Mechanism overview: 5-Amino-1MQ is a small molecule, not a peptide, that inhibits nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT). NNMT methylates nicotinamide using SAM, which drains both the NAD salvage pathway and the methyl donor pool. NNMT is highly expressed in the fat tissue of obese animals. Blocking it keeps nicotinamide available for NAD+ synthesis and preserves SAM, shifting fat cells toward higher energy expenditure and less lipid storage. One group also claims it reactivates aged muscle stem cells.

## What has been tested
34. Fat metabolism: Diet-induced fat gain (mouse)
35. Cellular energy: Energy metabolism (mouse)
36. Mouse studies: NNMT inhibition reduced fat mass and improved metabolic markers. Human data are absent.
37. What it helps: Evidence is mouse-only: in diet-induced obese mice it reduced fat accumulation, shrank fat cells and improved metabolic markers. No human trials exist. Users take it as an oral capsule and report fat loss, drive and energy, often stacked with MOTS-c and NAD+.
38. Trial: none. No registered human trial found; evidence is a handful of mouse obesity studies. (checked 2026-10-23) [S5] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
39. People with active or recent cancer: Blocking NNMT raises NAD+ and shifts methylation; tumour effects untested. (inferred)
40. People with liver disease: NNMT is a major liver enzyme; blocking it in damaged livers is untested. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
41. MOTS-c: 5-Amino-1MQ spares NAD and shifts fat cells toward burning energy, while MOTS-c activates AMPK and mimics the metabolic effect of exercise in mice. People stack them for fat loss, and they are sold pre-mixed as NAD+ + MOTS-c + 5-Amino-1MQ. The pair has not been tested together.
42. NAD+: 5-Amino-1MQ slows the NNMT drain on the NAD salvage pathway, while NAD+ adds supply directly. People pair them to fill the pool and close the leak at once, and they come pre-mixed as NAD+ + MOTS-c + 5-Amino-1MQ. The combination has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
43. A small molecule, not a peptide: 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, an inhibitor of the enzyme NNMT, which sits at a junction of NAD and methylation metabolism in fat.
44. First studied for: Studied to shrink fat cells and raise cellular NAD, mostly for metabolic and body-composition goals.
45. Name story: The name is chemistry shorthand: 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a quinoline ring with a methyl on position 1 and an amino group on position 5. It carries a permanent positive charge, so it is sold as a salt, usually the iodide. [S6] (verified)
46. 2010s: Small-molecule NNMT inhibitor studied in mice for fat loss
47. Today: Oral grey-market fat-loss and energy capsule; no human data
