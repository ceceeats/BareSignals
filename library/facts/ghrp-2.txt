# GHRP-2
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/ghrp-2
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: pralmorelin, pralmorelin hydrochloride, pralmorelin dihydrochloride, KP-102, GPA-748, WAY-GPA-748, GHRP Kaken 100
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: AAAWFK (6 amino acids). D-Ala and D-2-naphthylalanine shown as A, D-Phe shown as F; amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Approved in Japan as a diagnostic test for growth hormone deficiency (pralmorelin). Unapproved elsewhere. WADA-banned.

## Summary
5. GHRP-2 is a synthetic hexapeptide that mimics ghrelin and triggers a strong pulse of growth hormone. People use it for the GH effect, lean mass and sleep, and it also raises hunger. It is approved in Japan only as a diagnostic test, it also raises prolactin and cortisol, and many users prefer ipamorelin for that reason.
6. Punch line: Ghrelin mimic with a strong growth hormone pulse. Taken for lean mass and sleep; less hunger than GHRP-6.
7. Used for: GH release, appetite, recovery

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Endocrine & growth: Strong GH release
9. Muscle & performance: Lean mass reported
10. Appetite & satiety: Raises hunger
11. Sleep & rhythms: Better sleep reported

## How it works
12. Mimics ghrelin to drive a strong GH pulse · Ghrelin receptor, GHSR-1a · role Endocrine & growth
   GHRP-2 binds the ghrelin receptor, GHSR-1a, on pituitary and hypothalamic cells, the same switch the hunger hormone ghrelin uses. That sets off a strong pulse of growth hormone from the pituitary. Human studies confirm the release, and it has been used to test for GH deficiency.
13. Also lifts hunger, prolactin and cortisol · Less selective GHS · role Appetite & satiety
   It is a less selective secretagogue than ipamorelin, so the same receptor activity also spills over into other hormone systems. Hunger rises through ghrelin signalling in the brain, and prolactin and cortisol climb as well. These side effects are shown in human studies and are why many users switch to ipamorelin.
14. Amplifies the brain's own GHRH pulses · Synergy with GHRH · role Endocrine & growth
   GHRH from the hypothalamus and GHRP-2 act on different receptors in the pituitary, and together they release more GH than either alone. GHRP-2 amplifies each natural GHRH pulse rather than replacing it. This synergy is well established in human hormone studies.
15. GH pulse lifts IGF-1, aiding muscle and recovery · GH/IGF-1 axis · role Muscle & performance
   Each GH pulse travels to the liver and other tissues, which respond by making IGF-1, the main messenger of growth hormone. IGF-1 drives protein building and tissue repair in muscle. The GH rise is shown in people, while lasting muscle or recovery outcomes have not been trialled.
16. May deepen slow-wave sleep (ghrelin data) · Ghrelin, sleep EEG · role Sleep & rhythms
   Ghrelin receptor signalling has been linked to changes in the sleep EEG, including more slow-wave or deep sleep. This idea comes from ghrelin research, not from sleep trials of GHRP-2 itself. Treat it as a plausible extra, not a shown effect.
17. Mechanism overview: GHRP-2 is a synthetic hexapeptide that activates the ghrelin receptor, GHSR-1a, on pituitary and hypothalamic cells. That triggers a strong growth hormone pulse and amplifies the brain's own GHRH pulses, so the two work synergistically. It is less selective than ipamorelin, so it also raises prolactin, cortisol and hunger. The GH pulse lifts IGF-1, aiding muscle and recovery, and ghrelin data suggest effects on slow-wave sleep.

## Who shouldn't take it
39. People with Cushing’s or high cortisol: Raises cortisol and ACTH alongside GH. (reported)
40. People with prolactinoma or high prolactin: GHRPs push prolactin up alongside GH, worsening prolactin problems. (reported)
41. People with active or recent cancer: It raises GH and IGF-1, growth signals that tumour cells also respond to. (inferred)
42. Drug-tested athletes: GH-releasing peptides are named on the WADA banned list. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
43. CJC-1295 no DAC: CJC-1295 no DAC works on the GHRH receptor while GHRP-2 works on the ghrelin receptor, so together they release more GH than either alone. It is a classic pairing built on established GHRH and GHRP synergy, but this exact pair has not been trialled.
44. CJC-1295 with DAC: The DAC version keeps GHRH signalling switched on for about a week, and GHRP-2 adds sharp pulses on top. People pair them for higher GH and IGF-1 over time, but the pairing is user logic, not trial data.
45. Sermorelin: Sermorelin supplies a short GHRH signal that keeps the pituitary's natural feedback intact, and GHRP-2 amplifies the pulse it starts. The two receptors work together in principle, but the pair itself has not been tested together.
46. Tesamorelin: Tesamorelin is a stabilised GHRH with trial data for cutting visceral and liver fat, while GHRP-2 boosts GH through the ghrelin receptor. Users combine them to push GH higher, but no study has tested the pair, and GHRP-2 adds hunger and cortisol.

## Where it comes from
47. A synthetic GH-releasing hexapeptide, a ghrelin-receptor agonist that drives a strong GH pulse.
48. First studied for: A GH-releasing agent and a probe of the ghrelin system; studied as a diagnostic.
49. Name story: Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide 2, from a series descended from opioid enkephalin analogues. Officially it's pralmorelin, and Japan sells it as a one-shot diagnostic for growth hormone deficiency, the first secretagogue used clinically. [S6] (verified)
50. 1990s: Built as a potent GH-releasing hexapeptide; human studies follow
51. 2000s: Approved in Japan as pralmorelin, a GH deficiency test
52. Today: Strong but less selective GH releaser; unapproved outside Japan, WADA-banned
