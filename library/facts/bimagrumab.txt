# Bimagrumab
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/bimagrumab
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: BYM338, LY3985863
2. Origin: Lab-designed antibody
3. Legal and approval status: Phase 2 as of Oct 2026: BELIEVE published in Nature Medicine 2026, a tirzepatide combo Phase 2 (NCT06643728) ongoing, a diabetes combo trial stopped Sep 2025; no Phase 3 announced. WADA bans anti-activin receptor IIB antibodies under S4 (reported).

## Summary
4. Bimagrumab is a Novartis antibody that blocks activin type II receptors, cutting off the myostatin and activin signals that cap muscle growth. Built for muscle-wasting disease, it is now studied for fat loss that spares muscle. A Phase 2 in type 2 diabetes removed about a fifth of fat mass while adding lean mass. It failed in inclusion body myositis, and cramps, acne and dropouts are concerns.
5. Punch line: Antibody that lifts the myostatin brake on muscle. Weight drops as fat while lean mass holds or grows.
6. Used for: Fat loss while keeping muscle

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Muscle & performance: Builds and keeps lean mass
8. Fat metabolism: Burns fat, spares muscle
9. Glucose & insulin: Better HbA1c and insulin sensitivity
10. Inflammatory signaling: hsCRP down up to 84%
11. Aging & senescence (backed only by animal or user reports): Tested for age-related muscle loss

## How it works
12. Blocks the brake on muscle growth · ActRIIA/ActRIIB antagonist · role Muscle & performance
   Bimagrumab is an antibody that blocks activin type II receptors A and B (ActRIIA and ActRIIB). These receptors carry the signals that hold muscle size in check. Blocking them lets muscle fibres grow, shown in human phase 2 trials.
13. Shuts out myostatin and activin signals · Myostatin, activin A, GDF-11 · role Muscle & performance
   Myostatin, activin A and GDF-11 all signal through these receptors to limit muscle growth. With the receptors blocked, none of them can act, which removes several brakes at once. Lean mass rose in human trials, though it failed to help inclusion body myositis.
14. Pushes fat toward burning · Adipose ActRII blockade, browning · role Fat metabolism
   The same receptors sit on fat cells. Blocking them there shifts fat toward breakdown and burning, with signs of browning. In a 2021 phase 2 trial in type 2 diabetes, monthly infusions removed about a fifth of fat mass.
15. Improves insulin sensitivity · Muscle and fat glucose uptake · role Glucose & insulin
   With more muscle and less fat, glucose uptake into muscle and fat improves and the body responds better to insulin. The 2021 phase 2 in type 2 diabetes showed HbA1c falling alongside fat loss.
16. Mechanism overview: Bimagrumab is a fully human monoclonal antibody that binds activin type II receptors A and B (ActRIIA/ActRIIB). These receptors carry the signals of myostatin, activin A and GDF-11, which normally hold muscle size in check, so blocking them lifts the brake and muscle fibres grow. The same receptors sit on fat cells, and blocking them there shifts fat toward breakdown and oxidation and improves insulin sensitivity. The result is a drug that removes fat and adds lean mass at the same time, unlike GLP-1 drugs, which cut appetite and lose some muscle along the way.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
17. Typical: 10 or 30 mg/kg · every 4 or 12 weeks · cycle 48–72 weeks in trials · route IV (Sub-Q version in trials) (verified)
18. Half-life: Long (weeks); allows monthly or quarterly IV
19. Dose note: Weight-based IV dosing from trials only; Sub-Q dose not published.
20. Most people take: Trial doses only, by IV infusion: 10 mg/kg every 4 weeks (2021 diabetes trial) or 10 or 30 mg/kg every 12 weeks with loading doses (BELIEVE). A weekly Sub-Q form is in Phase 2. No community dose.
21. Monthly IV: 10 mg/kg (max 1,200 mg) · every 4 weeks · 48 weeks · IV · for 2021 Phase 2, type 2 diabetes with obesity [S1] (verified)
22. Quarterly IV: 10 or 30 mg/kg · every 12 weeks, extra doses at wk 1 and 4 · 48–72 weeks · IV · for BELIEVE, with or without semaglutide [S2] (verified)

## What has been tested
39. Fat metabolism: Obesity with type 2 diabetes, Phase 2 (human)
40. Fat metabolism: Obesity with semaglutide, BELIEVE (human)
41. Muscle & performance: Inclusion body myositis, Phase 2b/3 (human)
42. Aging & senescence: Sarcopenia in older adults, Phase 2 (human)
43. Glucose & insulin: HbA1c in type 2 diabetes (human)
44. Phase 2/3 in inclusion body myositis (failed 2016), Phase 2 in sarcopenia, a 2021 Phase 2 in type 2 diabetes with obesity, and Phase 2b BELIEVE (507 people) with semaglutide.
45. What it helps: In a 2021 Phase 2 in type 2 diabetes, monthly infusions removed about a fifth of fat mass while adding lean mass and lowering HbA1c. BELIEVE showed that pairing it with semaglutide pushed weight loss to about 22% with most of it fat, and bimagrumab alone raised lean mass. It failed in inclusion body myositis. Interest is in muscle-sparing GLP-1 therapy; muscle cramps, acne, diarrhea, enzyme rises and a high dropout rate are the concerns.
46. Trial: active · Phase 2 · obesity with tirzepatide · NCT06643728 · Eli Lilly. Lilly's muscle sparing antibody finished dosing in January 2026 with results expected in 2026, after a planned Phase 2b was withdrawn in 2025. (checked 2026-10-23) [S3] (verified)

## Who shouldn't take it
47. People planning conception: Activin blockade alters FSH; class drug sotatercept impairs fertility. (inferred)
48. People on blood thinners or bleeding disorders: Class drug sotatercept causes bleeding and telangiectasia. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
49. Semaglutide: Semaglutide cuts appetite through the GLP-1 receptor but loses some muscle along with fat, while bimagrumab adds lean mass and removes fat. In the BELIEVE trial, the pair reached about 22% weight loss, mostly fat.
50. Tirzepatide: Tirzepatide drives large weight loss through GIP and GLP-1 receptors, with some muscle lost along the way. Bimagrumab is added to protect lean mass. BELIEVE tested it with semaglutide; this pair has not been tested.

## Where it comes from
51. A Novartis antibody (BYM338) that blocks activin type II receptors, cutting off the myostatin and activin signals that cap muscle growth; built for muscle-wasting disease, reborn as a fat-loss drug.
52. First studied for: Muscle wasting: sporadic inclusion body myositis, sarcopenia and hip-fracture recovery.
53. Name story: Started life as Novartis code BYM338, made with MorphoSys antibody technology; the '-umab' ending marks a fully human monoclonal antibody. Lilly renamed its programme LY3985863. [S4] (reported)
54. 2013: FDA breakthrough status for inclusion body myositis
55. 2016: Phase 2b/3 in inclusion body myositis fails
56. 2021: Phase 2 in type 2 diabetes: fat down 20%, muscle up
57. Jul 2023: Lilly buys Versanis for up to $1.9B
58. Sep 2025: Lilly stops tirzepatide combo trial in diabetes
59. 2026: BELIEVE published: 22% loss with semaglutide; tirzepatide trial ongoing
