# CJC-1295 with DAC
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/cjc-1295-with-dac
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: DAC:GRF, CJC-1295 DAC
2. Origin: Lab-modified natural chain
3. Sequence: YADAIFTQSYRKVLAQLSARKLLQDILSRK (30 amino acids). Position 2 is D-Ala; the last K carries the DAC (maleimide) linker that binds albumin; amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Unapproved research chemical. WADA-banned.

## Summary
5. CJC-1295 with DAC is a lab-modified GHRH analogue that prompts the pituitary to release growth hormone. A Drug Affinity Complex binds albumin and extends its action to about a week, so people use it for infrequent dosing aimed at recovery and body composition. Small studies show raised GH and IGF-1 for days, but there are no outcome results and users note water retention.
6. Punch line: The once-weekly GHRH analogue. An albumin-binding DAC keeps GH and IGF-1 raised for days; more bloat than no-DAC.
7. Used for: Higher GH and IGF-1 for recovery, body comp

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Endocrine & growth: Keeps GH and IGF-1 raised for days
9. Sleep & rhythms: Deeper sleep reported

## How it works
10. Prompts the pituitary to release GH · GHRH receptor · role Endocrine & growth
   CJC-1295 binds the GHRH receptor on the growth hormone cells of the pituitary, the same switch the brain's own GHRH flips. That raises cAMP inside the cell and releases stored growth hormone. Small human studies confirmed GH rises after a single dose.
11. Raises IGF-1 for up to two weeks · Hepatic IGF-1 · role Endocrine & growth
   Growth hormone travels to the liver and tells it to make IGF-1, the hormone that carries much of GH's growth and repair signal to muscle, bone and other tissue. In small human studies IGF-1 stayed raised for days after one dose, but there are no outcome data on recovery or body composition.
12. Binds albumin for about a week · Drug Affinity Complex · role Endocrine & growth
   The Drug Affinity Complex is a reactive chemical tag that bonds the peptide to albumin, the most common protein in blood. Bound to albumin, it escapes fast breakdown and kidney clearance, so it acts for about a week instead of minutes. Small human studies confirm the long action.
13. Raises baseline GH while pulses persist · Trough GH levels · role Endocrine & growth
   Because the peptide keeps the receptor engaged around the clock, GH between natural pulses stays higher than usual while the pulses still happen on top. The result is a steadier GH signal than the body normally produces, seen in small human studies. Users also report more water retention than with the no-DAC version.
14. GHRH tone deepens slow-wave sleep · Hypothalamic GHRH · role Sleep & rhythms
   In the hypothalamus GHRH does more than release GH. It also promotes slow-wave sleep, the deepest stage of non-REM sleep, so raising GHRH tone is expected to deepen it. Sleep has not been measured in the CJC-1295 studies themselves.
15. Mechanism overview: CJC-1295 is a GHRH analogue that binds the GHRH receptor on pituitary somatotrophs, raising cAMP and prompting growth hormone release. The Drug Affinity Complex lets it bond covalently to circulating albumin, stretching its life to about a week. GH then drives the liver to make IGF-1, which stays raised for up to two weeks. Natural GH pulses continue, but baseline GH between them rises, giving a more constant signal than the body normally produces. Hypothalamic GHRH tone also deepens slow-wave sleep.

## What has been tested
31. Endocrine & growth: GH and IGF-1 after one dose (human)
32. Fat metabolism: HIV lipodystrophy (trial)
33. Small human studies in 2006 confirmed GH and IGF-1 stay raised for days after one dose. A Phase 2 in HIV lipodystrophy was halted (reported). No efficacy results.
34. What it helps: Small human studies confirmed that a single dose keeps GH and IGF-1 elevated for days, allowing infrequent dosing. A trial in HIV lipodystrophy was reportedly halted, and there are no outcome results. Users report it for recovery and body composition, noting more water retention than the no-DAC version.
35. Trial: none · Phase 2 · HIV fat redistribution and growth hormone deficiency · ConjuChem. Last trials were around 2006; ConjuChem halted development after a participant death, and it never returned to the clinic. (checked 2026-10-23) [S3] (reported)

## Who shouldn't take it
36. People with active or recent cancer: Raises GH and IGF-1 for weeks per dose; cannot be switched off quickly. (inferred)
37. People with prediabetes or poorly controlled diabetes: Sustained GH raises insulin resistance. (inferred)
38. People with heart disease: A phase 2 trial was halted after a participant death (judged unrelated). (reported)
39. Drug-tested athletes: GHRH analogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
40. Ipamorelin: CJC-1295 raises the GHRH signal while ipamorelin works the ghrelin receptor, a second route to the same GH release, and together they give a bigger pulse than either alone. Ipamorelin is picked because it spares cortisol, prolactin and hunger. The pair has not been tested together in a trial.
41. GHRP-2: GHRP-2 drives GH through the ghrelin receptor and amplifies the brain's own GHRH pulses, so a long-acting GHRH analogue gives it more signal to amplify. The cost is GHRP-2's rise in hunger, prolactin and cortisol. This pairing is user logic, not trial data.
42. GHRP-6: GHRP-6 triggers a GH pulse through the ghrelin receptor and blunts somatostatin, the brake on GH, while CJC-1295 keeps the GHRH side raised. People choose it for bulking because it brings strong hunger. The pair itself has not been tested together.
43. Hexarelin: Hexarelin is the strongest GH releaser of the ghrelin-receptor group and also acts on heart tissue through CD36, while CJC-1295 supplies a steady GHRH signal. Its GH response fades with repeated use and it lifts cortisol and prolactin more. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
44. The same GHRH analogue with a Drug Affinity Complex added, which binds albumin and extends its action to about a week.
45. First studied for: A longer-acting GH-raising agent, for less frequent dosing.
46. Name story: CJC is reportedly the in-house prefix of its Canadian developer ConjuChem. DAC means Drug Affinity Complex: a chemical hook on an extra lysine that latches onto albumin in blood, stretching its life from minutes to days. [S4] (reported)
47. 2006: Small human studies: GH and IGF-1 raised for days per dose
48. Late 2000s: HIV lipodystrophy Phase 2 halted (reported)
49. Today: Weekly grey-market GH raiser; WADA-banned
