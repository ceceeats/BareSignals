# NA Semax Amidate
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/na-semax-amidate
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: N-acetyl Semax amide, Ac-MEHFPGP-NH2
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: MEHFPGP (7 amino acids). Same residues as Semax; acetylated start, amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Research chemical; not approved.

## Summary
5. NA Semax Amidate is Semax capped at both ends with an N-acetyl and a C-amide for longer action than plain Semax. It is used nasally for focus and drive, and it is assumed to act like Semax on BDNF and monoamines. The modified form has not been trialled itself, its case rests on stability logic, and it is one ingredient of the IllumiNeuro blend.
6. Punch line: Semax capped at both ends for longer action. A nasal focus spray users report as stronger than Semax.
7. Used for: Focus, drive and mental stamina

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Cognition: Focus and attention
9. Neural plasticity: Nerve growth signal
10. Neural protection: Neuron protection
11. Drive & mental stamina: More motivation reported
12. Mood (backed only by animal or user reports): Brighter mood reported

## How it works
13. Semax capped at both ends to last longer · N-acetyl, C-amide · role Cognition
   An N-acetyl cap on one end and an amide on the other shield the peptide from enzymes that trim short peptides from their ends. The aim is a longer working life than plain Semax, which users describe as stronger and longer lasting. That is stability logic and user report, not trial data for this form.
14. Assumed to act like Semax · ACTH(4-7) analogue · role Cognition
   Semax is the ACTH(4-7) fragment of the stress hormone ACTH, extended with Pro-Gly-Pro so it resists breakdown and lacks ACTH's hormonal action on the adrenals. The capped form is assumed to act the same way. Everything below comes from Semax studies.
15. Raises BDNF and TrkB in brain (Semax data) · BDNF/TrkB expression · role Neural plasticity
   Semax raises BDNF and its receptor TrkB in the brain, the growth factor signal that helps neurons survive and form new connections. That is the basis for its plasticity and cognition claims. It is shown in animal studies of Semax, not measured for the capped form.
16. Lifts dopamine and serotonin turnover (Semax data) · Monoamine release · role Drive & mental stamina
   Semax increases turnover of dopamine and serotonin, the monoamine messengers behind motivation, drive and mood. More turnover means these signals are released and recycled more actively. This comes from Semax animal work and fits user reports of drive, but is not measured for this form.
17. Shields neurons after stroke in models (Semax) · Anti-ischemic gene response · role Neural protection
   In stroke models, Semax switches on an anti-ischaemic gene response, a set of genes that help neurons survive when blood and oxygen supply is cut. That protects brain tissue around the injury. Semax has Russian human data in stroke, while the capped form has not been trialled.
18. Mechanism overview: This is Semax with an N-acetyl cap and C-terminal amide to slow breakdown. Semax is a fragment of ACTH(4-7) extended with Pro-Gly-Pro, and the capped form is assumed to act the same way. Semax data show it raises BDNF and its receptor TrkB in the brain, lifting plasticity, increases dopamine and serotonin turnover, and in stroke models triggers an anti-ischaemic gene response that protects neurons.

## What has been tested
34. Cognition: Relies on Semax's Russian data (human)
35. Shares Semax's Russian evidence; the modified form is not separately trialled.
36. What it helps: It relies on Semax's Russian human data in stroke and cognition. The modified form has not been trialled itself, and its case rests on stability logic. Users take it nasally for focus and drive and report it as more potent and longer lasting than plain Semax. It is one of the ingredients of the IllumiNeuro blend.
37. Trial: none. The N-acetyl amidated form has never been in a human trial; plain Semax is approved in Russia for stroke and cognition, with Russian trials not verifiable in open registries. (checked 2026-10-23) [S3] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
38. People with a history of seizures: Russian prescribing info for Semax lists convulsive disorders as a contraindication. (inferred)
39. People with acute psychosis or severe anxiety: Raises dopamine and serotonin turnover. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
40. NA Selank Amidate: Selank Amidate brings calm through GABA and enkephalin signalling, while Semax Amidate brings drive and focus through BDNF and dopamine. People pair them to balance stimulation with steadiness, and both are sold pre-mixed in Semax + Selank + PE-22-28 + Pinealon. The pair has not been tested together.
41. Pinealon: Pinealon is claimed to protect neurons through gene control and support melatonin, while Semax Amidate raises BDNF and drive. Both are sold pre-mixed in Semax + Selank + PE-22-28 + Pinealon. Pinealon's claims come from one group, and the pairing is user logic, not trial data.
42. PE-22-28: Both reach for brain plasticity from different angles. PE-22-28 blocks TREK-1 to excite serotonin neurons and grow hippocampal neurons in animals, while Semax Amidate raises BDNF and dopamine turnover. They are sold pre-mixed in Semax + Selank + PE-22-28 + Pinealon, but the pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
43. N-acetyl, amidated Semax: capped at both ends for longer action than plain Semax.
44. First studied for: Same neuroprotective/nootropic use as Semax, more stable.
45. Name story: "NA" (N-acetyl) and "Amidate" (C-terminal amide) are vendor shorthand for capping both ends, not a pharmacopeia name. Semax itself is ACTH(4-10) with a Pro-Gly-Pro tail, its name reportedly short for "seven amino acids". [S4] (inferred)
46. 2010s: Semax capped at both ends for longer action
47. Today: A stronger, longer Semax variant; research chemical only
