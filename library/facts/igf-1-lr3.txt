# IGF-1 LR3
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/igf-1-lr3
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: igf1 lr3 long r3, Long R3 IGF-1, LR3-IGF-1, Long arginine 3-IGF-1, IGF-1 Long R3
2. Origin: Lab-modified natural chain
3. Sequence: MFPAMPLSSLFVNGPRTLCGAELVDALQFVCGDRGFYFNKPTGYGSSSRRAPQTGIVDECCFRSCDLRRLEMYCAPLKPAKSA (83 amino acids). IGF-1 with position 3 swapped (E to R) and a 13 amino acid tail added; three internal bridges between C pairs
4. Legal and approval status: Unapproved research chemical (native IGF-1 is approved for a rare deficiency). WADA-banned (S2).

## Summary
5. IGF-1 LR3 is a lab-modified insulin-like growth factor 1 with an extra N-terminal extension and an arginine swap, so it avoids carrier proteins and lasts far longer. It was a cell-culture reagent that was adopted for muscle growth, acting through the IGF-1 receptor to drive protein building. It has no human safety data, and users flag hypoglycaemia as a real risk.
6. Punch line: IGF-1 tweaked to dodge carrier proteins and last longer. Users report fuller muscles; hypoglycemia is the risk.
7. Used for: Muscle growth and recovery

9. Muscle & performance: Fuller, bigger muscle reported
10. Tissue repair: Cell growth and repair

## How it works
11. Drives cell growth and protein building · IGF-1R, PI3K/Akt/mTOR · role Muscle & performance
   IGF-1 LR3 binds IGF-1R, a receptor tyrosine kinase on the cell surface, and switches on the PI3K, Akt and mTOR chain. That chain tells the cell to build protein and grow. It is a strong growth driver in cell culture, but LR3 itself has no human trials.
12. Dodges carrier proteins, so lasts far longer · Low IGFBP binding · role Endocrine & growth
   Most natural IGF-1 in blood is held by IGF-binding proteins, which limit how much reaches tissue. The arginine swap and added tail in LR3 stop most of that binding, so far more stays free and active, and for much longer. This is shown in biochemistry and cell work.
13. Pulls sugar into cells, can drop blood sugar · Insulin-like signalling · role Glucose & insulin
   IGF-1R is a close relative of the insulin receptor and shares much of its signalling, so cells pull more glucose out of the blood. That can drop blood sugar. Hypoglycaemia is a known effect of native IGF-1 as a drug, and LR3 users flag it as a real risk.
14. Spurs muscle stem cells to multiply · Satellite cells, MAPK · role Tissue repair
   Through the MAPK branch of IGF-1R signalling it pushes satellite cells, the stem cells parked along muscle fibres, to divide. Those new cells can fuse into damaged fibres during repair. This is shown in cell culture, while muscle gains in people rest on user reports.
15. Suppresses the body's own GH release · Pituitary feedback · role Endocrine & growth
   The pituitary reads high IGF-1 as a sign that GH is already high and releases less of its own, a negative feedback loop. This is textbook endocrinology for IGF-1 in general. In practice the body's own GH output drops while LR3 is in use.
16. Mechanism overview: IGF-1 LR3 is IGF-1 with an arginine swap at position 3 and a 13-amino-acid extension. Those changes stop most binding to IGF-binding proteins, so far more stays free and active for far longer. It binds IGF-1R, a tyrosine kinase that fires PI3K/Akt/mTOR to build protein and MAPK to drive proliferation, including muscle satellite cells. Its insulin-like signalling pulls glucose into cells and can drop blood sugar. High IGF-1 also feeds back on the pituitary and suppresses the body's own GH.

## Who shouldn't take it
39. Insulin or sulfonylurea users: IGF-1 lowers blood sugar directly; mecasermin label warns of hypoglycaemia. (inferred)
40. People with active or recent cancer: Mecasermin is contraindicated with active or suspected cancer. (inferred)
41. People with sleep apnoea or big tonsils: IGF-1 enlarges lymphoid tissue; label reports snoring and apnoea. (inferred)
42. Drug-tested athletes: IGF-1 and analogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Where it comes from
44. A lab-modified insulin-like growth factor 1 (Long R3 IGF-1): an extra 13-residue N-terminal extension and an arginine swap stop it binding its carrier proteins, so it lasts far longer and acts strongly.
45. First studied for: A research reagent for cell culture; adopted for muscle growth.
46. Name story: LR3 is short for Long R3: "Long" for 13 extra amino acids bolted to the front (83 instead of 70), "R3" for arginine (R) swapped in at position 3. Both help it slip past the binding proteins that normally mop IGF-1 up. [S6] (verified)
47. 1990s: Built as a long-lasting IGF-1 for cell culture
48. 2005: Native IGF-1 approved as mecasermin for severe deficiency
49. Today: Grey-market muscle builder; no human trials, WADA-banned
