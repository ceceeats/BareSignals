# Melanotan 1
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/melanotan-1
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: afamelanotide, Scenesse, CUV1647, EPT1647, NDP-MSH, NDP-α-MSH, [Nle4,D-Phe7]-α-MSH, Melanotan I
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: SYSLEHFRWGKPV (13 amino acids). Nle shown as L, D-Phe shown as F; acetylated start, amide end
4. Shown with an FDA approved badge
5. Legal and approval status: FDA- and EMA-approved (Scenesse) for EPP. Cosmetic tanning use is off-label/grey-market.

## Summary
6. Melanotan 1 (afamelanotide, Scenesse) is a lab-made, stabilized version of alpha-MSH that tells skin cells to make dark melanin. It is approved for erythropoietic protoporphyria, a painful sunlight sensitivity disorder, and is used off label for tanning. Users report fewer side effects than Melanotan 2, with little nausea and no spontaneous erections.
7. Punch line: Afamelanotide, approved as Scenesse for sun sensitivity in EPP. Tans skin with less nausea than Melanotan 2.
8. Used for: EPP light tolerance; tanning

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
9. Tanning: Darkens skin, protects from light
10. Skin & aesthetics: Approved for light sensitivity

## How it works
11. Tells skin cells to make melanin · MC1R · role Tanning
   Melanotan 1, or afamelanotide, binds MC1R, the melanocortin 1 receptor on melanocytes, the skin's pigment cells. This raises cAMP inside the cell and turns up melanin production. Skin darkening is shown in human studies.
12. Shifts pigment to dark eumelanin · Eumelanin synthesis · role Tanning
   MC1R signalling shifts pigment output from pale, reddish pheomelanin toward dark eumelanin. Eumelanin absorbs UV light better and gives deeper, more protective colour. This is textbook MC1R biology and fits the darkening seen in people.
13. Little sexual effect, unlike MT-2 · MC1R-selective vs MT-2 · role Tanning
   It is largely selective for MC1R and barely touches MC3R and MC4R in the brain, the receptors Melanotan II also hits. Those central receptors drive sexual arousal and appetite changes. So users report little nausea and no spontaneous erections, unlike MT-2.
14. Raises sunlight tolerance in EPP (approved) · Photoprotection · role Skin & aesthetics
   In erythropoietic protoporphyria, sunlight causes severe pain in the skin. Phase 3 trials showed people on afamelanotide had longer pain-free time in sunlight. This led to approval as Scenesse, given as a slow-release implant.
15. Calms UV-driven skin inflammation · MC1R anti-inflammatory · role Skin & aesthetics
   MC1R activation also dampens inflammation in the skin after UV exposure. This adds to the protection from the extra pigment itself. It is part of the established melanocortin pharmacology behind its EPP use.
16. Mechanism overview: Afamelanotide is a stabilised alpha-MSH analogue that is largely selective for MC1R on melanocytes. Activation raises cAMP and shifts pigment output toward dark eumelanin, darkening skin and adding photoprotection. MC1R signalling also calms UV-driven inflammation in the skin. Because it barely touches the central MC3R and MC4R that Melanotan II hits, it has little sexual or appetite effect. It is delivered as a slow-release implant in its approved form.

## Where it comes from
39. Afamelanotide, a stabilized analogue of alpha-MSH that drives melanin production, built at the University of Arizona.
40. First studied for: Protecting skin in people with the painful light-sensitivity disorder erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP).
41. Name story: Melanotan is literally melano + tan: a 1980s University of Arizona project to tan skin with minimal sun. The tanning dream became afamelanotide (Scenesse), now an approved implant for people with a rare light-sensitivity disorder (EPP). [S4] (verified)
42. 1980s: Built at the University of Arizona as a stable alpha-MSH
43. 2014: Approved in the EU as Scenesse for EPP
44. 2019: FDA approval as Scenesse implant
45. Today: Approved EPP drug; tanning use off-label and grey-market
