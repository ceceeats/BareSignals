# Semax + Selank + PE-22-28 + Pinealon
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/illumineuro
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: IllumiNeuro, IllumiNeuro Protocol, Illumineuro 46 mg, Illumineuro 48 mg, NA Selank + NA Semax + PE-22-28 + Pinealon
2. Origin: Blend of 4 peptides
3. Legal and approval status: US: not approved. Semax won an 8-5-1 PCAC vote in July 2026 (plain Semax, not the NA amidate); Selank, PE-22-28 and Pinealon have no US status. Russia: plain Semax and Selank approved; the NA forms and the blend are not. WADA: S0.

## Summary
4. IllumiNeuro is a four-peptide brain blend combining N-acetyl Semax amidate, N-acetyl Selank amidate, PE-22-28 (a spadin fragment) and Pinealon. People use it for focus, mood and brain support, combining Semax for BDNF and drive, Selank for calm, PE-22-28 for mood and Pinealon for brain protection. The blend itself has never been studied, and the N-acetyl amidate forms have no published human data.
5. Punch line: Four-peptide brain stack: Semax for focus, Selank for calm, PE-22-28 for mood, Pinealon for protection and sleep.
6. Used for: Focus, mood, brain support

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Cognition: NA Semax for focus and BDNF
8. Mood: PE-22-28 antidepressant in mice; Selank calm
9. Neural protection: Pinealon and Semax brain protection
10. Neural plasticity: PE-22-28 neurite outgrowth, Semax BDNF
11. Sleep & rhythms (backed only by animal or user reports): Pinealon circadian, vivid dreams
12. Drive & mental stamina (backed only by animal or user reports): Semax activating

## How it works
13. Raises brain growth factor · BDNF and TrkB up (Semax) · role Cognition
   Semax raises levels of BDNF, a growth factor for nerve cells, and of TrkB, the receptor it binds. More BDNF signalling favours new synapses and nerve cell survival. This is shown in animals for plain Semax; the capped NA amidate form has not been studied for it.
14. Calms anxiety · Enkephalinase inhibition, GABA-linked (Selank) · role Mood
   Selank slows enkephalinase, the enzymes that break down the body's own calming enkephalin peptides, and is linked to GABA signalling. The aim is calm without heavy sedation. Russian clinical data cover plain Selank in anxiety; the NA amidate version in this blend has no trial data.
15. Lifts mood in mice · TREK-1 channel block (PE-22-28) · role Mood
   PE-22-28, a short piece of spadin, blocks TREK-1, a potassium channel that keeps neurons quiet. With that brake eased, serotonin neurons fire more readily. Antidepressant-like effects and better stability than spadin are shown in mouse tests only.
16. Protects brain cells under stress · Pinealon, oxidative stress models · role Neural protection
   Pinealon is a three amino acid Khavinson bioregulator tested on neurons under oxidative stress, the damage caused by reactive oxygen. In those models it limited cell injury. The evidence comes from cell and animal work by one research group, with no human trials.
17. Mechanism overview: N-acetyl Semax amidate and N-acetyl Selank amidate are Semax (MEHFPGP) and Selank (TKPRPGP) capped at both ends, sold as longer-lasting. PE-22-28 (GVSWGLR) is the shortest active fragment of spadin and blocks the TREK-1 potassium channel, with antidepressant effects and better stability than spadin in mice. Pinealon (Glu-Asp-Arg) is a Khavinson bioregulator studied for neuron protection against oxidative stress. The blend stacks focus, calm, mood and protection signals.

26. Other ratio seen: Semax 10 · Selank 10 · Pinealon 20 · PE-22-28 10 = 50 mg (1 listing)
27. Version Common retail: NA Semax 20 · PE-22-28 10 · Pinealon 10 · NA Selank 6 = 46 mg
28. Why NA Semax Amidate is in it: The focus and drive engine
29. Why NA Selank Amidate is in it: Calm, to offset Semax
30. Why PE-22-28 is in it: A mood lane none of the others hit (TREK-1)
31. Why Pinealon is in it: Protection and sleep-rhythm signal
32. Mixing chemistry: Semax brings Met and His and PE-22-28 a Trp: oxidation is the watch point; keep cold and dark.
33. Mixing chemistry: Pinealon (Glu-Asp-Arg) and Selank are chemically quiet.
34. Mixing chemistry: All four are short (3 to 7 residues), so clumping risk is low.
35. Mixing chemistry: Often sprayed nasally, where the liquid's formulation is not disclosed.
36. Self-mixing: Separate vials let you drop PE-22-28 or Pinealon and titrate Semax on its own; four vials and four ratios is the trade-off.

## What has been tested
52. Never tested as a combination; the product's own page says there are no clinical studies on this four-peptide mix. Components: PE-22-28 in mice (Djillani 2017); Pinealon in Khavinson cell and animal work; Semax and Selank in Russian clinical use (as the non-acetylated forms).
53. What it helps: No study of the blend. PE-22-28: mouse antidepressant tests. Pinealon: cell and animal work. Semax and Selank: Russian clinical data for the plain forms, none for the NA amidates. User reports for the blend are not public.

## Who shouldn't take it
54. People with a history of seizures: Russian prescribing info for Semax lists convulsive disorders as a contraindication. (inferred)
55. People on benzodiazepines or other sedatives: Selank modulates GABA-A receptors; effects may stack. (inferred)
56. People with active or recent cancer: Marketed as gene-regulating and proliferative; effect on tumours untested. (inferred)
57. People with acute psychosis or severe anxiety: Raises dopamine and serotonin turnover. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
58. Semax + Selank: Semax + Selank is the two-peptide core of this blend in plain form, without PE-22-28 and Pinealon. People choose it to keep the focus and calm pair alone. Stacking both duplicates the same peptides and has not been tested.
59. Cerebrolysin: Cerebrolysin adds a pig brain peptide mix with nerve growth factor-like activity and dementia and stroke trial data, alongside this blend's BDNF, calm and mood signals. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
60. Dihexa: Dihexa works through a different growth signal, HGF and its receptor c-Met, while Semax here works through BDNF. People pair them for memory from two angles. Dihexa has rat data only, and the pair has not been tested together.
61. Epitalon: Epitalon is another Khavinson peptide, used for sleep rhythm and longevity, which overlaps with Pinealon's pineal signal here. Its own claims are unverified, and the pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
62. A four-peptide brain blend: N-acetyl Semax amidate, N-acetyl Selank amidate, PE-22-28 (a spadin fragment) and Pinealon (Glu-Asp-Arg).
63. First studied for: Marketed as a cognitive and mood blend: Semax for focus, Selank for calm, PE-22-28 for mood via TREK-1, Pinealon for brain and circadian support.
64. Name story: A product name: illuminate plus neuro. Not a scientific term. [S1] (inferred)
65. 1990s to 2000s: Semax and Selank approved in Russia
66. 2000s: Pinealon (EDR) developed in Khavinson's group
67. 2017: PE-22-28 shown to work in mice as a shortened spadin
68. 2020s: NA amidate versions of Semax and Selank sold
69. 2026: IllumiNeuro sold at 46 and 48 mg
70. Today: Sold as a nootropic blend, Sub-Q or nasal
