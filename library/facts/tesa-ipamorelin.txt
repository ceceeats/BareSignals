# Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/tesa-ipamorelin
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Tesa/Ipa, Tesa-IPA, Tesamorelin/Ipamorelin 5/5, Tesamorelin/Ipamorelin 10/3
2. Origin: Blend of 2 peptides
3. Legal and approval status: Tesamorelin: FDA-approved only as Egrifta for HIV lipodystrophy (EGRIFTA WR approved Mar 2025); compounded copies drew an FDA warning letter in Aug 2026. Ipamorelin: FDA Category 2, PCAC voted 0 to 12 against in Oct 2024. The blend is not approved anywhere. WADA: both prohibited (S2).

## Summary
4. This blend pairs tesamorelin, the only FDA-approved GHRH analogue, with ipamorelin, a selective ghrelin-receptor agonist. People use it to aim tesamorelin's belly-fat effect at a bigger growth hormone pulse through two routes. Tesamorelin alone cut visceral fat about 20% over 26 weeks in HIV lipodystrophy trials, but nothing is known about the pair. Users report fat loss over months, deeper sleep early, and water retention and joint stiffness.
5. Punch line: Approved belly-fat GHRH plus ipamorelin's second GH route. One nightly shot; the pricier cousin of CJC/Ipa.
6. Used for: Visceral fat loss, GH release, sleep

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Endocrine & growth: GHRH plus ghrelin route, bigger GH pulse
8. Fat metabolism: Tesamorelin's belly-fat effect
9. Sleep & rhythms: Deeper sleep from the ipamorelin side
10. Muscle & performance: Lean mass and recovery, reported
11. Skin & aesthetics (backed only by animal or user reports): Skin quality, reported
12. Organ function (backed only by animal or user reports): Liver fat, from tesamorelin data

## How it works
13. Full-length GHRH copy, stabilised at the front end · GHRH 1-44 with trans-3-hexenoyl cap · role Endocrine & growth
   Tesamorelin is the full 44 amino acid GHRH with a trans-3-hexenoyl cap that protects it from DPP-4 breakdown. It binds GHRH receptors in the pituitary and raises cAMP, prompting the body to release its own growth hormone. Shown in human trials.
14. Shrinks belly fat in trials · Visceral adipose tissue down · role Fat metabolism
   In phase 3 trials in people with HIV lipodystrophy, tesamorelin shrank visceral fat, the fat around the organs, by about 20% over 26 weeks while IGF-1 rose. Glucose and insulin did not change significantly in that trial. The data are from tesamorelin alone.
15. Presses the ghrelin button, a second route · GHS-R1a agonist · role Endocrine & growth
   Ipamorelin activates GHS-R1a, the ghrelin receptor, which signals through phospholipase C and calcium rather than cAMP. This gives a second, independent push on GH release. The effect is shown in animals; human trial data are limited.
16. Releases GH without cortisol or prolactin spikes · Selective GHS, animal data · role Sleep & rhythms
   Ipamorelin releases GH without raising cortisol or prolactin, unlike older GHRPs. That selectivity is shown in rats and pigs. Users report deeper sleep and vivid dreams early on, which fits GH pulses at night but is user-reported.
17. Together, a bigger pulse than either alone · GHRH + GHRP synergy · role Muscle & performance
   Because GHRH acts through cAMP and ipamorelin through calcium, the two routes add up to a larger GH pulse than either alone. This GHRH plus GHRP synergy is shown in healthy men. The tesamorelin and ipamorelin pair itself has not been tested.
18. Mechanism overview: Tesamorelin is human GHRH 1-44 with a trans-3-hexenoyl group on the N-terminus, which protects it from DPP-4 cleavage; it binds GHRH receptors and raises cAMP in somatotrophs. Ipamorelin binds GHS-R1a and signals through phospholipase C and calcium, releasing GH selectively in animals. The independent routes make GHRH plus GHRP synergistic. Tesamorelin keeps native Met27 and Asn8, so in solution it is more prone to oxidation and deamidation than Mod GRF 1-29.

28. Version Common retail, tesamorelin-heavy: Tesamorelin 10 · Ipamorelin 3 = 13 mg
29. Version Common retail, balanced: Tesamorelin 5 · Ipamorelin 5 = 10 mg
30. Why Tesamorelin is in it: The only GHRH analogue with phase 3 visceral-fat data
31. Why Ipamorelin is in it: Adds the ghrelin route, multiplying the pulse without cortisol or prolactin
32. Mixing chemistry: Tesamorelin keeps native Met27 and Asn8, the sites most prone to oxidation and deamidation; Mod GRF had them removed.
33. Mixing chemistry: The licensed maker's first version (Egrifta SV) had to be used immediately after mixing; it took a reformulation (EGRIFTA WR, 2025) with its own excipients to hold a vial for one week.
34. Mixing chemistry: Grey-market blends claim 4 to 6 weeks refrigerated; no published data supports that for tesamorelin in BAC water.
35. Mixing chemistry: At 44 amino acids tesamorelin has more structure for benzyl alcohol to disturb than short peptides.
36. Mixing chemistry: Discard if cloudy, gelled or particulate.
37. Self-mixing: Drawing tesamorelin and ipamorelin into one syringe right before injecting is the better-supported route; separate vials also let you fix the ratio (most guides want more ipamorelin than the 10/3 vial gives) and use the tesamorelin vial fast.

39. Half-life: per component
40. Most people take: Sized by the tesamorelin dose. 10/3 vial in 2 mL (5 mg/mL tesa, 1.5 mg/mL ipa): 10 units = 0.5 mg + 150 mcg, 20 units = 1 mg + 300 mcg. 5/5 vial in 2 mL: 10 units = 250 + 250 mcg. Fasted, before bed.

42. Liked / worked: Tesamorelin cut visceral fat ~20% in trials

## What has been tested
54. Fat metabolism: Tesamorelin, HIV visceral fat, phase 3 (human)
55. Endocrine & growth: GHRH + GHRP synergy, normal men (human)
56. Endocrine & growth: Ipamorelin GH release, rats and pigs (animal)
57. Sleep & rhythms: Deeper sleep, vivid dreams (users)
58. Never tested as a pair. Tesamorelin alone has phase 3 trials: 2 mg daily for 26 weeks cut visceral fat about 20% (27.8 cm²) in people with HIV lipodystrophy, with IGF-1 up 80%. Ipamorelin alone has animal selectivity data. GHRH plus GHRP synergy is shown in men.
59. What it helps: Strongest evidence of the GH blends for fat: tesamorelin alone cut visceral fat about 20% over 26 weeks in HIV lipodystrophy trials, with IGF-1 up 80% and no significant change in glucose or insulin in that trial. Nothing is known about the pair. Users report fat loss over months and better sleep early.

## Who shouldn't take it
60. Pituitary tumour, pituitary surgery or brain radiation: Label contraindication: needs an intact hypothalamic-pituitary axis. (verified)
61. People with active or recent cancer: Raises GH and IGF-1, which promote cell growth. (inferred)
62. People with prediabetes or poorly controlled diabetes: GH raises blood glucose and insulin resistance. (inferred)
63. Drug-tested athletes: GH secretagogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
64. CJC-1295 no DAC: CJC-1295 no DAC is a second GHRH copy on the same receptor as tesamorelin. Adding it gives overlap, not new synergy, as in the triple blend. The combination has not been tested.
65. Sermorelin: Sermorelin is another GHRH copy on the same receptor as tesamorelin. Adding it duplicates the GHRH side rather than adding a route. The pairing has not been tested together.
66. MK-677: MK-677 is an oral ghrelin mimic on the same receptor as ipamorelin, raising GH all day rather than in pulses. People add it for sleep and lean mass. It raises hunger, and the pairing has not been tested.
67. AOD-9604: AOD-9604 is a growth hormone fragment aimed at fat breakdown without GH receptor action, with rodent data only. People add it for fat loss. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
68. Tesamorelin, the only FDA-approved GHRH analogue (Egrifta), pre-mixed with ipamorelin, a selective ghrelin-receptor agonist. Sold in two splits: 5 + 5 mg (balanced) and 10 + 3 mg (tesamorelin-heavy).
69. First studied for: Built by the community to aim tesamorelin's proven belly-fat effect at a bigger growth hormone pulse, using the same GHRH plus GHRP synergy behind CJC/Ipa.
70. 1990: GHRP plus GHRH synergy shown in normal men
71. 1998: Ipamorelin described as the first selective GH secretagogue
72. 2007: Tesamorelin 2 mg daily cuts visceral fat ~20% in HIV trial
73. 2010: FDA approves tesamorelin (Egrifta)
74. 2024: PCAC votes against ipamorelin
75. 2025: EGRIFTA WR approved: reconstitute weekly, not daily
76. Today: Sold pre-mixed as 5/5 and 10/3
