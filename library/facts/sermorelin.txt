# Sermorelin
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/sermorelin
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Geref, Gerel, GRF 1-29, GHRH(1-29)NH2, sermorelin acetate
2. Origin: Fragment of a natural protein
3. Sequence: YADAIFTNSYRKVLGQLSARKLLQDIMSR (29 amino acids). Amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Previously FDA-approved, now discontinued as a product; compounded and grey-market use continues. WADA-banned.

## Summary
5. Sermorelin is a fragment of growth-hormone-releasing hormone, the first 29 amino acids and its active core, which prompts the pituitary to release its own GH in natural pulses. It was used for pituitary function testing and childhood GH deficiency, and users take it for sleep, recovery and body composition, often with a GHRP. Effects are smaller and slower than injected GH and need a working pituitary.
6. Punch line: The first 29 amino acids of GHRH. Gets the pituitary pulsing its own GH; gentler than injecting GH.
7. Used for: GH support, sleep, recovery

## How it works
13. Prompts the pituitary to release its own GH · GHRH receptor · role Endocrine & growth
   Sermorelin is GHRH 1-29, the shortest fully active piece of the body's GH-releasing hormone. It binds GHRH receptors on pituitary GH cells, raises cAMP and releases the pituitary's own stored GH. Human studies show it reliably raises GH, and it was used to test pituitary function.
14. Keeps natural GH pulses · Pituitary feedback intact · role Endocrine & growth
   Because it works upstream of GH, somatostatin and IGF-1 feedback still regulate the response. GH comes out in natural pulses rather than a flat high, which limits overshoot. The trade-off is that it needs a working pituitary to do anything.
15. Raises IGF-1 and lean mass modestly · GH, IGF-1 · role Muscle & performance
   Each GH pulse drives the liver to make IGF-1, which supports muscle and lean mass. Human studies show GH and IGF-1 rise, but gains are smaller and slower than with injected GH. It was used in childhood GH deficiency trials.
16. GHRH signalling deepens slow-wave sleep · Slow-wave sleep · role Sleep & rhythms
   GHRH signalling in the hypothalamus promotes deep, slow-wave sleep, the stage when most GH is released. This is established GHRH physiology. Users report better sleep on sermorelin, though sleep has not been its trial endpoint.
17. GHRH improved cognition in older adults, one RCT · GHRH, IGF-1 · role Cognition
   In one randomised trial in older adults, GHRH treatment improved cognitive scores, probably through GH and IGF-1 effects on the brain. It is a single trial, so treat it as an early human signal rather than proof.
18. Mechanism overview: Sermorelin is GHRH 1-29, the shortest fully active fragment of growth hormone releasing hormone. It binds GHRH receptors on pituitary somatotrophs, raises cAMP and triggers GH release. Because it acts upstream, somatostatin and IGF-1 feedback still regulate the response, so GH rises in natural pulses rather than as a flat high. GH then drives liver IGF-1, modestly lifting lean mass. Its half-life is minutes, so it needs frequent dosing. GHRH signalling also deepens slow-wave sleep.

## What has been tested
35. Endocrine & growth: Pituitary function test (human)
36. Endocrine & growth: Childhood GH deficiency (trial)
37. Used clinically for decades as a GH-deficiency diagnostic and therapy; it was FDA-approved (as Geref) before being discontinued for commercial reasons, not safety.
38. What it helps: Human studies show it reliably raises GH and IGF-1, and it was used for pituitary function testing and in childhood GH deficiency. ## Who shouldn't take it
40. People with untreated hypothyroidism: Prescribing info required thyroid correction first; low thyroid blunts response. (reported)
41. People with active or recent cancer: Raises GH and IGF-1, which promote cell growth. (inferred)
42. Drug-tested athletes: GHRH analogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
43. Ipamorelin: Sermorelin drives GH through the GHRH receptor and ipamorelin adds a clean ghrelin-receptor pulse, so the two pathways give a bigger but still natural GH release. People use it as an alternative to the CJC stack. The pair has not been tested together.
44. GHRP-2: GHRP-2 adds a strong ghrelin-receptor push to sermorelin's GHRH drive, so pulses are larger. The cost is more hunger, cortisol and prolactin than with ipamorelin. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
45. GHRP-6: GHRP-6 adds a ghrelin-receptor pulse and strong hunger to sermorelin's GHRH drive. People pair them when gaining weight and appetite is part of the goal. The pair itself has not been tested together.
46. Hexarelin: Hexarelin is the strongest ghrelin mimic and sermorelin adds GHRH drive, giving a large pulse. Hexarelin fades with repeat use and raises cortisol and prolactin more, so it suits short blocks. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
47. A fragment of growth-hormone-releasing hormone (the first 29 amino acids, the active core), which prompts the pituitary to release its own GH in natural pulses.
48. First studied for: A diagnostic test of pituitary function and a treatment for childhood GH deficiency.
49. Name story: The first 29 of growth hormone-releasing hormone's 44 amino acids, hence the old lab name GRF 1–29. '-relin' is the INN stem for hormone-release-stimulating peptides. FDA-approved as Geref, then dropped in 2008 for business reasons, not safety. [S5] (verified)
50. 1997: Approved as Geref for childhood GH deficiency and testing
51. 2008: Discontinued for commercial, not safety, reasons
52. Today: Compounded GH-optimisation peptide; WADA-banned
