# AOD-9604
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/aod-9604
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: AOD9604, Tyr-hGH(177-191), hGH fragment 176-191 (Tyr-modified), CAS 221231-10-3
2. Origin: Lab-modified natural chain
3. Sequence: YLRIVQCRSVEGSCGF (16 amino acids)
4. Legal and approval status: Never approved as a drug; the obesity program failed. Grey-market and supplement use only.

## Summary
5. AOD-9604 is a modified fragment of human growth hormone (residues 176-191 with a tyrosine added), built to keep the fat-burning part without the growth or blood sugar effects. People use it for fat loss and joint relief, and it is thought to boost fat breakdown. Its human obesity trial failed to beat placebo, so it was never approved, and users describe mild effects.
6. Punch line: Growth hormone's fat-burning tail plus a tyrosine, without growth or blood sugar effects. Used for fat and joints.
7. Used for: Fat loss and joint support

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Fat metabolism: GH's fat-burning tail
9. Bone & cartilage: Cartilage repair studied
10. Pain & sensory: Joint relief reported

## How it works
11. Claimed to boost fat breakdown · hGH 177-191 + Tyr · role Fat metabolism
   AOD-9604 copies residues 177 to 191 of growth hormone, the tail linked to GH's fat effects, plus an added tyrosine. In rodents it increases lipolysis, the breakdown of stored fat. In human obesity trials up to Phase 2b it did not beat placebo on weight.
12. Spares growth and blood sugar · No GH receptor action · role Fat metabolism
   Because it does not activate the GH receptor, it does not raise IGF-1 or blood sugar the way full growth hormone can. That keeps the fragment free of GH's growth and glucose effects. The trade-off is that its fat-loss effect in people has not been shown.
13. Blocks new fat storage in fat cells (rodents) · Lipogenesis inhibition · role Fat metabolism
   In rodent fat cells, AOD-9604 inhibits lipogenesis, the building of new fat from sugars and other fuels. Less new storage alongside more breakdown should shrink fat stores. This held in animals but did not translate into weight loss in human trials.
14. Claimed to aid cartilage repair (rabbit data) · Chondrocyte proliferation · role Bone & cartilage
   Rabbit studies suggest AOD-9604 encourages chondrocytes, the cells that build cartilage, to multiply, supporting cartilage repair. That led to its repositioning toward joint use. This is animal data only, with no human cartilage trials.
15. Mechanism overview: AOD-9604 is growth hormone residues 177 to 191 with an added tyrosine, built to keep GH's fat-burning tail. It does not activate the GH receptor, so it does not raise IGF-1 or blood sugar. Rodent studies show it boosts lipolysis and blocks new fat storage in fat cells. Rabbit data suggest it supports chondrocyte proliferation and cartilage repair. Its human fat-loss effect is claimed rather than shown.

## Who shouldn't take it
36. Drug-tested athletes: WADA has stated AOD-9604 is banned (S0). (verified)
37. People with prediabetes or poorly controlled diabetes: GH fragment; glucose effects in long-term use unclear. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
38. Tirzepatide: Tirzepatide drives weight loss through GIP and GLP-1 receptors, cutting hunger and improving how fat tissue handles fuel, with strong trial data. AOD-9604 is added for fat breakdown and joint support without touching blood sugar. The add-on is user logic, not trial data.
39. Semaglutide: Semaglutide does the heavy lifting on weight through GLP-1 effects on appetite and the stomach, proven in human trials. People add AOD-9604 hoping for extra fat breakdown and joint relief, though it failed alone in obesity trials. The pair has not been tested together.
40. Retatrutide: Retatrutide adds glucagon receptor action to GLP-1 and GIP, raising energy use and cutting liver fat. AOD-9604 is stacked on for fat breakdown and joints. The combination is user logic, and AOD-9604 did not beat placebo for weight on its own.

## Where it comes from
41. A modified fragment of growth hormone (residues 176-191 with a tyrosine added) built by Metabolic Pharmaceuticals in Australia to keep GH's fat-burning tail without its growth or blood-sugar effects.
42. First studied for: Obesity, as a fat-burning agent without GH's downsides.
43. Name story: AOD reportedly stands for 'Anti-Obesity Drug'; 9604 is a project number with no published meaning. It is the tail end of human growth hormone (residues 176 to 191) with a tyrosine added to the front. [S5] (reported)
44. 1990s to 2000s: GH fragment 176-191 built in Australia to burn fat without GH effects
45. 2007: Phase 2b obesity trial fails to beat placebo
46. 2014: Maker self-affirms GRAS food status (reported)
47. Today: Mild grey-market fat-loss and joint peptide
