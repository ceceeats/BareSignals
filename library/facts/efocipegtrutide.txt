# Efocipegtrutide
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/efocipegtrutide
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: HM15211, LAPS Triple Agonist, LAPS GLP/GIP/GCG
2. Origin: Lab-modified natural chain: triple agonist peptide linked to an antibody Fc fragment
3. Legal and approval status: Investigational, not approved anywhere: global Phase 2b in MASH completed July 2026 with data under analysis as of Oct 2026; FDA Fast Track for MASH, FDA/EMA orphan designations for PSC, PBC and IPF. As an unapproved drug it falls under WADA S0 (inferred).

## Summary
4. Efocipegtrutide is Hanmi's weekly glucagon, GIP and GLP-1 triple agonist attached to an antibody Fc fragment so it lasts a week. It is aimed at fatty liver disease with fibrosis rather than maximum weight loss, with the glucagon arm burning fat inside the liver. A 12-week early trial cut liver fat up to 81% against 6% on placebo. The larger MASH trial is unreported, and community use is negligible.
5. Punch line: Liver-first weekly triple agonist, Fc-tagged to last. Liver fat fell up to 81% in 12 weeks.
6. Used for: MASH, liver fibrosis

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Organ function: Clears liver fat, targets fibrosis
8. Inflammatory signaling: Calms liver inflammation, animal data
9. Fat metabolism: Modest weight loss
10. Appetite & satiety: Lower intake via GLP-1
11. Glucose & insulin (backed only by animal or user reports): Glucose control in animals
12. Neural protection (backed only by animal or user reports): Nerve protection in animals

## How it works
13. Glucagon arm burns fat inside the liver · Glucagon receptor, hepatocytes · role Organ function
   The glucagon arm activates glucagon receptors on liver cells, which shifts them toward burning fat and spending energy. Fat stored inside the liver is used up faster. In a short human trial, liver fat fell far more than body weight, which fits this liver-first action.
14. Directly calms scar-making liver cells, in animals · Hepatic stellate cell activation down · role Inflammatory signaling
   Hepatic stellate cells are the liver cells that switch on and lay down scar tissue when the liver is injured. Hanmi reports the drug directly holds back that activation. In animal models this meant less inflammation and fibrosis, but whether it reverses scarring in people awaits the Phase 2b biopsy results.
15. GLP-1 arm cuts appetite · GLP-1 receptor · role Appetite & satiety
   The GLP-1 arm activates GLP-1 receptors in the brain's appetite centres, so people feel less hungry and eat less. It also offsets glucagon's tendency to push blood sugar up. Weight loss in the human trial was modest, as the design is aimed at the liver more than the scale.
16. GIP arm aids insulin release and fat handling · GIP receptor · role Fat metabolism
   The GIP arm activates GIP receptors, which help the pancreas release insulin when glucose is high and influence how fat tissue stores fat. This rounds out the triple design alongside glucagon and GLP-1. Its separate contribution has not been isolated in human studies.
17. Fc fragment stretches action to a week · LAPSCOVERY Fc conjugation · role Organ function
   The peptide is chemically linked to a fragment of a human antibody, the Fc piece, using Hanmi's LAPSCOVERY platform. The larger size keeps it from being filtered out by the kidneys. That stretches its action to about a week, so it is given once weekly.
18. Mechanism overview: Efocipegtrutide is a single peptide that activates the glucagon, GIP and GLP-1 receptors, chemically linked to a human IgG Fc fragment (Hanmi's LAPSCOVERY platform) so it escapes kidney clearance and lasts about a week. Glucagon receptor signalling in hepatocytes raises fat oxidation and energy use in the liver, and Hanmi reports direct suppression of hepatic stellate cell activation, the cells that lay down scar tissue. GLP-1 receptor activity cuts appetite and offsets glucagon's glucose-raising effect, and GIP receptor activity aids insulin release and fat handling.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
19. Typical: 2–6 mg (Phase 2b) · once weekly · cycle Trial length (Phase 1b/2a 12 weeks) · route Sub-Q (verified)
20. Half-life: ~62–257 hours (dose-dependent)
21. Dose note: Only trial doses exist. At ~0.08 mg/kg the top Phase 1b/2a dose is roughly 6–8 mg for most adults, matching the Phase 2b top dose (inferred).
22. Most people take: Phase 2b tested 2, 4 and 6 mg weekly Sub-Q; Phase 1b/2a used 0.01–0.08 mg/kg weekly. No community dose exists; no oral form.
23. Phase 2b: 2, 4 or 6 mg · 1× weekly · trial length not stated · Sub-Q · for biopsy-confirmed MASH with fibrosis [S1] (verified)
24. Phase 1b/2a: 0.01–0.08 mg/kg · 1× weekly · 12 wk · Sub-Q · for obese adults with fatty liver [S2] (verified)

## What has been tested
39. Organ function: Liver fat in obese NAFLD, Phase 1b/2a (human)
40. Inflammatory signaling: Liver inflammation and fibrosis models (animal)
41. Fat metabolism: Body weight, Phase 1b/2a (human)
42. Neural protection: Neuroprotection posters (animal)
43. Phase 1 and a 12-week Phase 1b/2a in obese adults with fatty liver, then a global Phase 2b in biopsy-confirmed MASH with fibrosis (US and Korea, 2, 4 and 6 mg weekly), completed July 2026.
44. What it helps: Human evidence so far is a 12-week Phase 1b/2a in obese adults with fatty liver, where liver fat fell up to 81% against 6% on placebo with 2–5% weight loss. Animal models showed less liver inflammation and fibrosis, which semaglutide and tirzepatide barely touched. The Phase 2b biopsy trial in MASH finished in July 2026 and is unreported. There is no meaningful community use.
45. Trial: active · Phase 2 · MASH liver disease · NCT04505436 · Hanmi Pharmaceutical. Hanmi's once monthly triple agonist Phase 2b liver trial is active, not recruiting, with completion estimated mid 2026. (checked 2026-10-23) [S3] (reported)

## Who shouldn't take it
46. Personal or family history of MTC or MEN2: Causes thyroid C-cell tumours in rodents; boxed warning and contraindication on the label. (inferred)
47. Anyone booked for surgery, endoscopy or sedation: Food can stay in the stomach for days and be inhaled into the lungs under anaesthesia. (inferred)
48. People with arrhythmia or a fast resting pulse: The glucagon part of the drug pushes resting heart rate up. (inferred)

## Where it comes from
49. Hanmi's weekly glucagon/GIP/GLP-1 triple agonist, bolted to an antibody Fc fragment with its LAPSCOVERY platform so it lasts days instead of minutes; built for liver fibrosis rather than maximum weight loss.
50. First studied for: MASH with liver fibrosis; orphan designations for primary sclerosing cholangitis, primary biliary cholangitis and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
51. Name story: Coded HM15211 and branded LAPS Triple Agonist. The name reads as ef- (the Fc prefix Hanmi also uses in efpeglenatide and efinopegdutide), -peg- (linker) and -trutide (triple agonist), though no official breakdown was found. [S4] (inferred)
52. Late 2010s: HM15211 enters Phase 1 (NCT03374241)
53. 2020: Phase 1b/2a: liver fat down up to 81% in 12 weeks; two hyperglycemia cases
54. ~2020: Global Phase 2b in biopsy-confirmed MASH registered (NCT04505436)
55. May 2024: Independent monitors: no dose futile, all cohorts continue
56. Jul 2026: Phase 2b completed; results under analysis
