# Phenylpiracetam
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/phenylpiracetam
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Phenotropil, fonturacetam, carphedon, 4-phenylpiracetam
2. Origin: Not a peptide: racetam stimulant
3. Shown with an FDA approved badge
4. Legal and approval status: Prescription medicine in Russia (Phenotropil). Not approved in the US and not a legal dietary supplement there, though sold online. WADA prohibited in competition as a stimulant (listed as carphedon).

## Summary
5. Phenylpiracetam is piracetam with a phenyl ring added, made in Russia and sold there as Phenotropil. It is used for energy, focus and motivation, working as a dopamine and noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor with a stimulant edge. Russian studies report less fatigue after stroke and in asthenia, without modern placebo-controlled confirmation. Tolerance builds within days of daily use, and it is banned in sport.
6. Punch line: Piracetam plus a phenyl ring, sold in Russia as Phenotropil. A stimulant lift kept for hard days.
7. Used for: Energy, focus, motivation

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Drive & mental stamina: Stimulant lift, energy and motivation
9. Cognition: Sharper focus reported

## How it works
10. Blocks dopamine and noradrenaline reuptake · DAT and NET inhibition (R enantiomer) · role Drive & mental stamina
   The R enantiomer binds the dopamine transporter (DAT) and noradrenaline transporter (NET) and slows how fast those signals are cleared from the synapse. More dopamine and noradrenaline linger, which fits the quick stimulant feel and the fast tolerance users describe. Human data are older Russian clinical studies without modern placebo controls.
11. Racetam style support of signalling · AMPA and acetylcholine links (proposed) · role Cognition
   Like piracetam, it is proposed to tune AMPA receptors, which carry fast excitatory signals, and acetylcholine signalling, both central to learning and attention. This racetam side is much less well supported than the transporter action. Russian clinical reports of better cognition after stroke have not been confirmed in modern placebo controlled trials.
12. Mechanism overview: Phenylpiracetam adds a phenyl group to piracetam's pyrrolidone ring, which raises brain entry and adds stimulant activity. The R enantiomer inhibits dopamine and noradrenaline transporters, which fits the quick stimulant feel and fast tolerance. The racetam claims of AMPA and cholinergic modulation are less well supported. Human data are mostly Russian.

## What people report
17. Taken 100 to 200 mg in the morning, mostly as an occasional boost for hard days, exams or cold training rather than daily. Users describe energy, motivation and sharper focus that fade noticeably after a few consecutive days. Insomnia, overstimulation, irritability and headache appear mainly at high or late doses. ## What has been tested
28. Drive & mental stamina: Asthenia and fatigue (human)
29. Cognition: Stroke and cognitive decline (human)
30. Human evidence is older Russian clinical work in stroke, epilepsy, asthenia and stress, with limited independent replication. No Western placebo controlled trials.
31. What it helps: Russian clinical studies report less fatigue and better cognition in asthenia and after stroke, without modern placebo controlled confirmation. Users take it occasionally for energy and focus and note that the effect drops within days of repeated use.
32. Trial: none. No registered human trial found. (checked 2026-10-23) [S1]

## Synergies (paired with)
33. Bromantane: Phenylpiracetam slows dopamine clearance, while bromantane raises how much dopamine neurons make by switching on tyrosine hydroxylase. People pair them to work on drive from both the supply and the clearance side, but the pair has not been tested together.
34. Semax: Phenylpiracetam gives a fast stimulant lift through dopamine and noradrenaline transporters, while Semax is reported to raise BDNF and monoamine turnover in rodents. Users stack them for focus with a growth factor angle on top, which is user logic, not trial data.
35. Methylene blue: Methylene blue feeds electrons through the mitochondrial chain for cellular energy, while phenylpiracetam adds catecholamine drive. Methylene blue also blocks MAO-A, so the stack raises monoamines from two sides, a known interaction concern, and the pair has not been tested together.
36. NA Semax Amidate: NA Semax Amidate is a longer lasting Semax analogue assumed to share its BDNF and monoamine effects, while phenylpiracetam adds direct dopamine and noradrenaline reuptake block. People pair them for mental stamina, but the pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
37. Piracetam with a phenyl ring added, made in Russia and sold there on prescription as Phenotropil. The ring makes it far more stimulating than piracetam.
38. First studied for: Asthenia, fatigue and cognitive decline in Russia; it is said to have been developed for cosmonauts.
39. Name story: Piracetam with a phenyl ring bolted on; WADA lists it as carphedon, and fonturacetam is its INN. [S2] (reported)
40. 1980s: Developed in the Soviet Union from piracetam
41. 2000s: Registered in Russia as Phenotropil
42. Today: Russian prescription drug; grey market elsewhere; banned in sport
