# CBL-514
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/cbl-514
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: CBL514, CBL-514D (Dercum's formulation), Caliway fat-reduction injection
2. Origin: Not a peptide: small molecule (structure undisclosed)
3. Legal and approval status: Phase 3 as of Oct 2026: SUPREME-01 dosed its first participant Sep 2026 and SUPREME-02 IND cleared May 2026, results expected 2027. Not approved anywhere. Not named on the WADA list (inferred).

## Summary
4. CBL-514 is a first-in-class small-molecule injection from Taiwan's Caliway Biopharmaceuticals, not a peptide. It is aimed at non-surgical reduction of belly fat by triggering fat cells in the injected area to die by apoptosis. Company Phase 2b data show about three in four treated people improving a grade, against about one in five on placebo. All efficacy data come from the company, and injection-site pain is the main downside.
5. Punch line: Small-molecule injection that kills fat cells on the spot. Trials thinned belly fat; seen as a GLP-1 add-on.
6. Used for: Belly fat reduction, Dercum's disease

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Fat metabolism: Kills fat cells where injected
8. Skin & aesthetics: Smoother belly contour, cellulite
9. Pain & sensory: Less pain in Dercum's disease
10. Organ function: Less visceral fat on MRI

## How it works
11. Triggers fat cells to self-destruct · Adipocyte apoptosis, caspase-3 up · role Fat metabolism
   CBL-514 pushes fat cells into apoptosis, the orderly self-destruct program a cell runs on itself, and preclinical work showed caspase-3, the enzyme that carries out that program, rising. The cell is dismantled and cleared rather than just shrunk. In human trials this shows up as thinner, smaller fat layers at the injected site.
12. Tips cells toward death signals · Bax/Bcl-2 ratio up (preclinical) · role Fat metabolism
   Whether a cell lives or dies is partly set by the balance of Bax, which promotes death, against Bcl-2, which protects. CBL-514 shifted that balance toward Bax in preclinical studies, committing fat cells to apoptosis. This is lab and animal evidence for how it works, not something measured in people.
13. Kills cells without burning tissue · Non-necrotic, per company · role Skin & aesthetics
   Detergent-type fat dissolvers rupture cell membranes, which causes necrosis and inflammation in nearby tissue. CBL-514 is said to kill fat cells through apoptosis instead, so neighbouring skin and tissue are spared. That claim comes from the company; injection-site soreness is still the main reported downside in trials.
14. Fewer fat cells, so less regain in rodents · Combined with GLP-1/amylin drugs, preclinical · role Fat metabolism
   A fat cell that has died cannot be refilled, so fewer cells should mean less tissue to regain into. In rodents, adding CBL-514 to semaglutide or tirzepatide blunted weight regain. That is animal data only; regain after GLP-1 drugs has not been tested this way in people.
15. Mechanism overview: CBL-514 is an injected small molecule that drives adipocyte apoptosis, a programmed self-destruct in fat cells. In preclinical work it raised caspase-3 and the Bax/Bcl-2 ratio, the switches that commit a cell to apoptosis, and Caliway says it does this without necrosis of surrounding tissue, unlike detergent-type fat dissolvers that rupture membranes. Fewer fat cells means less tissue to refill, which is the basis for the company's claim that it blunts regain after GLP-1 drugs. The company reports no central nervous, heart or breathing effects.

## What has been tested
34. Fat metabolism: Abdominal subcutaneous fat, Phase 2b (human)
35. Organ function: Visceral fat on MRI, Phase 2 subgroup (human)
36. Pain & sensory: Dercum's disease pain, Phase 2 (human)
37. Fat metabolism: Weight regain with semaglutide/tirzepatide (animal)
38. About 10 completed company trials in 500+ people, including Phase 2b CBL-0204 (107 people) and CBL-0205 on belly fat, a Phase 2 in Dercum's disease and an MRI visceral-fat analysis; Phase 3 started 2026.
39. What it helps: Company Phase 2b data show about three in four treated people improving at least one grade on a belly-fat scale against about one in five on placebo, measurable fat thickness and volume loss within four weeks, and an MRI subgroup with visceral fat falling while placebo rose. A Phase 2 in Dercum's disease cut pain. Interest centres on non-surgical contouring and on pairing it with GLP-1 drugs; injection-site soreness is the main reported downside.
40. Trial: active · Phase 3 · belly fat reduction · NCT07526974 · Caliway Biopharmaceuticals. Two global Phase 3 fat dissolving injection trials (SUPREME-01 and 02) are starting in the US, Canada and Australia. (checked 2026-10-23) [S3] (verified)

## Who shouldn't take it
41. People on blood thinners: Multiple injections in fat; bruising. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
42. Semaglutide: Semaglutide shrinks fat stores by cutting appetite, while CBL-514 removes fat cells at the injection site. The idea is that fewer fat cells leave less room to regain after stopping. That is shown only in rodents; the pair has not been tested together in people.
43. Tirzepatide: Tirzepatide drives broad weight loss through appetite and insulin effects, and CBL-514 targets local fat cells for removal. People pair them for overall loss plus contouring. Regain was blunted in rodents only; the pair has not been tested together in people.
44. Eloralintide: Eloralintide works on fullness through the amylin system, while CBL-514 removes fat cells locally. The pairing follows the same logic as with GLP-1 drugs and the company has mentioned amylin drugs in its preclinical work. It is user and preclinical logic, not human trial data.

## Where it comes from
45. A first-in-class small-molecule injection from Taiwan's Caliway Biopharmaceuticals that kills fat cells in the injected area by apoptosis rather than burning or dissolving them.
46. First studied for: Non-surgical reduction of abdominal subcutaneous fat; also cellulite and Dercum's disease (painful fatty lumps).
47. Name story: A Caliway development code: 'CBL' appears to stand for the company (Caliway Biopharmaceuticals Lab), while 514 has no public explanation. It has no INN yet. [S4] (inferred)
