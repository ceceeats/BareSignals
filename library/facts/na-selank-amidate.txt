# NA Selank Amidate
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/na-selank-amidate
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: N-acetyl Selank amide, Ac-TKPRPGP-NH2, TP-7 (vendor label)
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: TKPRPGP (7 amino acids). Same residues as Selank; acetylated start, amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Research chemical; not approved.

## Summary
5. NA Selank Amidate is Selank capped at both ends with an N-acetyl and a C-amide so it resists breakdown and lasts longer. It is used nasally for calm and focus, and it is assumed to act like Selank. The capped form has never been trialled itself and rests on Selank's Russian data and stability logic. It is one ingredient of the IllumiNeuro blend.
6. Punch line: Selank capped at both ends to resist breakdown. A nasal calm reported as longer and slightly stronger.
7. Used for: Calm and focus without sedation

11. Sleep & rhythms: Better sleep reported

## How it works
12. Selank capped at both ends to last longer · N-acetyl, C-amide · role Mood
   An N-acetyl group on one end and an amide on the other block the peptidase enzymes that normally trim short peptides from their ends. The intent is a longer working life in the body than plain Selank. This is stability logic. The capped form has no mechanism or absorption data of its own.
13. Assumed to act like Selank · Tuftsin analogue · role Mood
   Selank is a synthetic analogue of tuftsin, a natural immune peptide, extended to make it more stable. The capped form is assumed to hit the same targets, so everything below comes from Selank studies. Whether the caps change how it binds has not been tested.
14. Claimed to boost GABA calming signal, as Selank · GABA-A allosteric modulation · role Mood
   Selank is reported to act as an allosteric modulator at GABA-A receptors, the brain's main calming channel, changing how strongly they respond to GABA rather than opening them directly. That fits the calm without sedation described in Russian human anxiety studies of Selank. The capped form is untested here.
15. Slows breakdown of calming enkephalins (Selank) · Enkephalinase inhibition · role Mood
   Selank inhibits enkephalinase, the enzymes that break down enkephalins, the body's own opioid-like calming peptides. With slower breakdown, enkephalins stay active at their receptors for longer. This comes from Selank lab work and is assumed, not shown, for the amidated form.
16. Shifts immune signalling (Selank data) · IL-6, interferon genes · role Immunity
   In Selank studies, it changes expression of immune genes including IL-6 and interferon-related genes, a link back to its tuftsin origin. That points to a shift in immune signalling alongside its brain effects. This is Selank data from lab and animal work, not measured for this form.
17. Raises BDNF in rat brain (Selank data) · BDNF expression · role Cognition
   Selank raised BDNF, a growth factor that supports neuron survival and new connections, in rat brain. Higher BDNF is linked to learning and resilience to stress. This is animal data for Selank only, and the capped form has not been measured.
18. Mechanism overview: This is Selank with an N-acetyl cap and a C-terminal amide, changes meant to resist peptidases and extend its life. It is assumed to act like Selank, a tuftsin analogue. From Selank data: it modulates GABA-A receptors allosterically for a calming effect, inhibits enkephalinase so natural enkephalins last longer, shifts immune genes such as IL-6 and interferon, and raises BDNF in rat brain. The modified form has no mechanism data of its own.

## Who shouldn't take it
38. People on benzodiazepines or other sedatives: Selank modulates GABA-A receptors; effects may stack. (inferred)
39. People on opioids: Selank slows enkephalin breakdown, prolonging the body’s own opioids. (inferred)

## Where it comes from
43. N-acetyl, amidated Selank: capped at both ends to resist breakdown, so it lasts longer than plain Selank.
44. First studied for: Same anxiolytic/nootropic use as Selank, in a more stable form.
45. Name story: Not a medical name: "NA" (N-acetyl) and "Amidate" (C-terminal amide) are vendor shorthand for capping both ends of Selank, chemically sealing it against enzymes. Selank itself is tuftsin with a Pro-Gly-Pro tail. [S4] (inferred)
46. 2010s: Selank capped at both ends for stability
47. Today: A longer-lasting Selank variant; research chemical only
