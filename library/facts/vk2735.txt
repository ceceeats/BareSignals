# VK2735
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/vk2735
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: VK-2735, Viking GLP-1/GIP dual agonist
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Legal and approval status: Investigational, not approved anywhere: injectable Phase 3 VANQUISH-1 and -2 fully enrolled (Nov 2025, Mar 2026), 78-week readouts not before late 2027 (reported); oral Phase 3 to start Q4 2026. As an unapproved drug it falls under WADA S0 (inferred).

## Summary
4. VK2735 is Viking Therapeutics' dual GLP-1 and GIP agonist, the same receptor pair as tirzepatide, developed as both a weekly injection and a daily pill. It is aimed at obesity and blood sugar control. In 13-week Phase 2 trials, injections gave up to 14.7% weight loss and the tablet up to 12.2%. Dropouts at the top tablet dose were high, and no community use is documented.
5. Punch line: Viking's tirzepatide-style GLP-1 and GIP dual, weekly shot or daily pill. Up to 14.7% lost in 13 weeks.
6. Used for: Obesity, type 2 diabetes

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Appetite & satiety: Strong hunger suppression
8. Fat metabolism: Up to 14.7% loss in 13 weeks
9. Glucose & insulin: Prediabetes back to normal
10. Heart & vessels: Lower blood pressure in trials

## How it works
11. Quiets hunger in the brain · GLP-1 receptor, hypothalamus · role Appetite & satiety
   VK2735 activates GLP-1 receptors in the hypothalamus and brainstem, the hunger and fullness centres. People feel less hungry and eat less. In 13-week human trials this produced weight loss with no plateau yet.
12. Boosts insulin when glucose is high · GLP-1R and GIPR, beta cells · role Glucose & insulin
   On pancreatic beta cells it activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, raising insulin only when glucose is high. In the VENTURE human trial, most people with prediabetes returned to normal glucose. The glucose dependence keeps lows uncommon.
13. GIP arm adds fat-tissue effects and may ease nausea · GIP receptor, adipose · role Fat metabolism
   GIP receptors in fat tissue and the brain are thought to add to weight loss and may soften GLP-1 nausea. This is the same logic as tirzepatide. GI side effects were still common in the trials, so any nausea benefit is unproven here.
14. Slows stomach emptying · GLP-1 receptor, gut · role Appetite & satiety
   GLP-1 receptors along the gut and its nerves slow how fast the stomach empties. Food stays longer, which adds to fullness and blunts sugar spikes after meals. It also accounts for much of the GI discomfort reported.
15. Lowers blood pressure with weight loss · Systemic, secondary · role Heart & vessels
   Blood pressure fell in the VENTURE human trial alongside weight loss. This is a secondary, systemic effect rather than a direct action on blood vessels. It is a measured trial finding, not a proven reduction in heart events.
16. Mechanism overview: VK2735 is a dual agonist of the GLP-1 and GIP receptors, the incretin pair also hit by tirzepatide. GLP-1 receptor activation in the hypothalamus and brainstem cuts hunger and slows stomach emptying, while GLP-1R and GIPR on pancreatic beta cells raise insulin only when glucose is high. GIP receptor signalling in fat tissue and the brain is thought to add to weight loss and may soften GLP-1 nausea. A long half-life of about a week supports weekly injection, and the same molecule is given as a tablet at roughly ten times the injected dose, implying low oral absorption.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
17. Typical: 2.5–17.5 mg weekly (inj); 30–120 mg daily (tablet) · weekly (Sub-Q) or daily (oral) · cycle Ongoing (Phase 3: 78 weeks) · route Sub-Q or oral tablet (verified)
18. Half-life: ~170–250 hours (Sub-Q)
19. Dose note: No community dosing exists; all figures are trial doses. Tablet doses are ~10× the injected dose, implying low oral absorption (inferred).
20. Most people take: Phase 3 uses 7.5, 12.5 or 17.5 mg Sub-Q weekly; Phase 2 used 2.5–15 mg. The oral tablet ran 15–120 mg daily, with 30 mg holding weight after a 90 mg start.
21. Phase 3 injection: 7.5, 12.5 or 17.5 mg · 1× weekly · 78 wk · Sub-Q · for VANQUISH obesity ± type 2 diabetes [S1] (verified)
22. Phase 2 injection: 2.5–15 mg · 1× weekly · 13 wk · Sub-Q · for VENTURE, adults with obesity [S2] [S3] (verified)
23. Oral tablet: 30–120 mg · 1× daily, titrated · 13 wk · Oral · for VENTURE-Oral; 15 mg did not beat placebo [S4] (verified)
24. Maintenance: induction dose spaced out · every 2 wk or monthly · after 21 wk weekly · Sub-Q · for maintenance study, kept 90–97% of loss [S5] (verified)

## What has been tested
41. Fat metabolism: Weight loss in obesity, VENTURE and VENTURE-Oral (human)
42. Glucose & insulin: Prediabetes normalisation, VENTURE (human)
43. Heart & vessels: Systolic blood pressure, VENTURE (human)
44. Appetite & satiety: Maintenance dosing after induction (human)
45. Phase 1 (injectable and oral), 13-week Phase 2 VENTURE (injection, 176 people) and VENTURE-Oral (tablet, 280 people), a maintenance-dosing study, and two 78-week Phase 3 VANQUISH trials in about 5,500 people.
46. What it helps: In 13-week Phase 2 trials, weekly injections gave up to 14.7% weight loss and the daily tablet up to 12.2%, with no plateau; about 78% with prediabetes returned to normal glucose and blood pressure fell. A maintenance study kept 90 to 97% of the loss on monthly or every-other-week dosing. GI side effects are common, and dropouts at the top tablet dose were high. No community use is documented.
47. Trial: active · Phase 3 · obesity · NCT07104500 · Viking Therapeutics. Phase 3 VANQUISH-1 finished enrolling in November 2025 and is ongoing. (checked 2026-10-23) [S6] (reported)

## Who shouldn't take it
48. Personal or family history of MTC or MEN2: Causes thyroid C-cell tumours in rodents; boxed warning and contraindication on the label. (inferred)
49. Anyone booked for surgery, endoscopy or sedation: Food can stay in the stomach for days and be inhaled into the lungs under anaesthesia. (inferred)
50. People relying on the contraceptive pill: Same GLP-1/GIP pair as tirzepatide, whose label warns. (inferred)

## Where it comes from
51. Viking Therapeutics' in-house GLP-1/GIP dual agonist, the same receptor pair as tirzepatide, picked from an internal series and run as both a weekly shot and a daily pill.
52. First studied for: Obesity, with and without type 2 diabetes.
53. Name story: VK is Viking Therapeutics' code prefix, shared with its thyroid-receptor drug VK2809. VK2735 was picked from an internal series of dual GLP-1/GIP agonists first shown in 2021 and has no generic name yet. [S7] (reported)
