# Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin + CJC-1295 no DAC
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/tesa-ipa-cjc
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Tesa/Ipa/CJC, Tesamorelin/Ipamorelin/CJC 6/3/3, triple GH blend
2. Origin: Blend of 3 peptides
3. Legal and approval status: Tesamorelin: FDA-approved only as Egrifta for HIV lipodystrophy. CJC-1295 and ipamorelin: FDA Category 2, PCAC voted against both in 2024, not removed in April 2026. The blend is not approved anywhere. WADA: all three prohibited (S2).

## Summary
4. This three-part growth hormone blend combines tesamorelin and CJC-1295 no DAC (two GHRH analogues) with ipamorelin, a ghrelin-receptor agonist. It is used for growth hormone release, visceral fat and sleep, since GHRH plus ghrelin routes give a bigger pulse. Evidence belongs to the parts, such as tesamorelin's visceral-fat trials. The triple has no data, and a second GHRH analogue adds cost and side effects without a clear extra mechanism.
5. Punch line: Triple GH stack: two GHRH analogues plus ipamorelin for a bigger pulse. Tesamorelin brings the visceral fat data.
6. Used for: GH release, visceral fat, sleep

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Endocrine & growth: Two GHRH routes plus the ghrelin route
8. Fat metabolism: Tesamorelin's visceral-fat effect
9. Sleep & rhythms: Deeper sleep, from its parts
10. Muscle & performance: Recovery and lean mass, reported for parts
11. Skin & aesthetics (backed only by animal or user reports): Skin quality, reported for parts
12. Tissue repair (backed only by animal or user reports): Recovery, reported for parts

## How it works
13. Full-length GHRH copy with fat-loss trial data · Tesamorelin, GHRH-R · role Fat metabolism
   Tesamorelin is the full 44 amino acid GHRH, the hormone that tells the pituitary to release growth hormone, with a cap that protects it from breakdown. It binds the GHRH receptor and raises cAMP. Phase 3 human trials show it cuts visceral fat in HIV lipodystrophy.
14. Short GHRH copy, lasts about 30 minutes · Mod GRF 1-29, GHRH-R · role Endocrine & growth
   Mod GRF 1-29 is the active first 29 amino acids of GHRH, modified to resist breakdown but still short-acting. It binds the same GHRH receptor and triggers a brief GH pulse. Its own human data are limited; the CJC-1295 trial used the long-acting DAC form.
15. Same receptor as tesamorelin, so overlap not synergy · Shared GHRH-R · role Endocrine & growth
   Tesamorelin and Mod GRF 1-29 both act on the GHRH receptor and raise the same cAMP signal. Two drugs on one receptor add at best and can saturate it, so they do not multiply each other. The second GHRH source brings unknown added benefit.
16. Presses the ghrelin button, selectively · Ipamorelin, GHS-R1a · role Sleep & rhythms
   Ipamorelin activates GHS-R1a, the ghrelin receptor, through a separate phospholipase C and calcium pathway. It releases GH selectively, without the cortisol and prolactin spikes older GHRPs cause. That selectivity is shown in rats and pigs, not in human trials.
17. GHRH plus ghrelin route gives a bigger pulse · GHRH + GHRP synergy · role Muscle & performance
   GHRH drives GH release through cAMP and ghrelin-type drugs through calcium, two independent routes into the same cells. Together they release more GH than either alone. This synergy is shown in a 1990 study in healthy men; the triple itself is untested.
18. Mechanism overview: Tesamorelin (GHRH 1-44 with an N-terminal trans-3-hexenoyl group) and Mod GRF 1-29 both agonise the GHRH receptor and raise cAMP; they differ in length, stability and half-life, but act at the same site, so combining them is additive at best. Ipamorelin acts at GHS-R1a via phospholipase C and calcium, and it is this second route that produces synergy with GHRH. The triple is therefore pharmacologically a GHRH + GHRP blend with two GHRH sources.

## What is inside the blend
19. Tesamorelin: 6 mg · GHRH signal with trial data for visceral fat
20. Ipamorelin: 3 mg · Ghrelin-receptor signal: the synergy partner
21. CJC-1295 no DAC (Mod GRF 1-29): 3 mg · Short GHRH pulse, cheaper and more stable than tesamorelin
22. Most common ratio: Tesamorelin 6 · Ipamorelin 3 · CJC-1295 no DAC 3 = 12 mg (9 of 9 listings (3 state the split, 6 sell 12 mg))
23. Version Common retail: Tesamorelin 6 · Ipamorelin 3 · CJC-1295 no DAC 3 = 12 mg
24. Why Tesamorelin is in it: The visceral-fat trial record
25. Why Ipamorelin is in it: The only part acting on a second receptor; the source of synergy
26. Why CJC-1295 no DAC is in it: Extra GHRH signal at lower cost; same receptor as tesamorelin, so overlap
27. Mixing chemistry: Tesamorelin keeps native Met27 and Asn8 and is the least stable part in solution; its licensed versions are used immediately (SV) or within a week (WR).
28. Mixing chemistry: Mod GRF and ipamorelin are the forgiving parts; the blend's usable life is set by tesamorelin.
29. Mixing chemistry: Two GHRH peptides of different lengths may separate differently on HPLC; ask for per-component mg, not one purity number.
30. Mixing chemistry: No stability data for the reconstituted triple. Discard if cloudy, gelled or particulate.
31. Self-mixing: Mixing three separate vials lets you size tesamorelin to its usual 1 mg without overshooting CJC, but adds needle passes; drawing into one syringe at the time of the shot is better supported than pre-mixing a vial.

## What has been tested
48. Fat metabolism: Tesamorelin, HIV visceral fat, phase 3 (human)
49. Endocrine & growth: CJC-1295 DAC, GH and IGF-1 rise (human)
50. Endocrine & growth: GHRH + GHRP synergy, normal men (human)
51. Endocrine & growth: Ipamorelin GH release, rats and pigs (animal)
52. Never tested as a combination, and no published protocol for it was found. Tesamorelin alone cut visceral fat about 20% in 26-week HIV trials; CJC-1295 (DAC form) raised GH and IGF-1 in healthy adults; ipamorelin released GH selectively in animals. Two GHRH analogues act on the same receptor, so their effects are not expected to multiply the way GHRH plus GHRP does.
53. What it helps: No data on the triple. The evidence that exists belongs to its parts: tesamorelin's visceral-fat trials, the CJC-1295 DAC GH and IGF-1 study, ipamorelin animal selectivity, and the 1990 human synergy study. Expect the pattern of a GHRH + GHRP blend, with unknown added benefit from the second GHRH analogue.

## Who shouldn't take it
54. Pituitary tumour, pituitary surgery or brain radiation: Label contraindication: needs an intact hypothalamic-pituitary axis. (verified)
55. People with active or recent cancer: Raises GH and IGF-1, which promote cell growth. (inferred)
56. People with prediabetes or poorly controlled diabetes: GH raises blood glucose and insulin resistance. (inferred)
57. Drug-tested athletes: GH secretagogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
58. Sermorelin: Sermorelin is another GHRH copy acting on the same receptor as tesamorelin and Mod GRF 1-29. Adding it makes a third GHRH source with more overlap, not synergy. The combination has not been tested.
59. MK-677: MK-677 is an oral ghrelin mimic that raises GH all day, on the same receptor as ipamorelin. Stacking it adds a steady background to the pulses. It raises hunger, and the pairing has not been tested together.
60. BPC-157: BPC-157 works on blood vessel growth and makes tendon cells more responsive to growth hormone in rats. People pair it with a GH blend for injury recovery. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
61. A three-part growth hormone blend: two GHRH analogues (tesamorelin and CJC-1295 no DAC, i.e. Mod GRF 1-29) plus the ghrelin-receptor agonist ipamorelin. Sold as 6 + 3 + 3 mg, 12 mg per vial.
62. First studied for: A seller-led extension of CJC/Ipa: tesamorelin is added for its belly-fat trial record, while keeping the cheaper Mod GRF.
63. 1990: GHRP plus GHRH synergy shown in normal men
64. 2006: CJC-1295 (DAC) raises GH and IGF-1 in healthy adults
65. 2007: Tesamorelin cuts visceral fat ~20% in HIV trial
66. 2010: Tesamorelin approved (Egrifta)

68. Today: Sold as a 6/3/3 mg seller blend
