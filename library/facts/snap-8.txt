# SNAP-8
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/snap-8
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: acetyl octapeptide, Acetyl Octapeptide-3, Acetyl Glutamyl Heptapeptide-3
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: EEMQRRAD (8 amino acids). Acetylated start, amide end (acetyl octapeptide-3)
4. Legal and approval status: Cosmetic ingredient; not a drug.

## Summary
5. SNAP-8 is acetyl octapeptide-3, an eight-amino-acid lab-made peptide and a longer relative of Argireline. It is used in cosmetic serums as a Botox-like wrinkle agent, and it interferes with the SNARE complex that muscles use to release their contraction signal. Studies show only a modest drop in wrinkle depth, often come from the maker, and penetration is poor.
6. Punch line: Argireline's longer cousin, a Botox-like serum peptide. It quiets the muscle signal behind lines; modest results.
7. Used for: Expression lines, forehead and eyes

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Skin & aesthetics: Softens expression lines

## How it works
9. Blunts the signal that contracts face muscles · SNARE complex · role Skin & aesthetics
   SNAP-8 interferes with the SNARE complex, the protein machinery that fuses acetylcholine-filled vesicles with the nerve ending. With that machinery less stable, the nerve's signal to contract reaches facial muscle more weakly. Topical cosmetic studies show a modest drop in wrinkle depth.
10. Mimics SNAP-25 and competes for its place · SNAP-25 N-terminus · role Skin & aesthetics
   Its eight amino acids copy the start of SNAP-25, one of the three core SNARE proteins. It competes with the real SNAP-25 for its place, so fewer working complexes form. The competition is mild, unlike botulinum toxin, which cuts SNAP-25 outright.
11. Less nerve-to-muscle release; fades when stopped · Reduced ACh exocytosis · role Skin & aesthetics
   Less acetylcholine release means expression lines around the eyes and forehead soften a little. Because nothing is cut, the effect fades once use stops. Studies are often manufacturer-run, skin penetration is poor and results fall far short of injected toxin.
12. Mechanism overview: SNAP-8 is an octapeptide copying the N-terminal end of SNAP-25, one of the SNARE proteins that fuse acetylcholine vesicles with the nerve membrane. By competing for SNAP-25's place, it destabilises SNARE assembly, so less acetylcholine reaches facial muscle and contraction softens. It works on the same machinery botulinum toxin cuts, but by mild competition, not cleavage, so the effect is small and fades when use stops.

## What has been tested
28. Skin & aesthetics: Wrinkle depth, topical (human)
29. Cosmetic studies (often manufacturer-run) report modest wrinkle-depth reduction; no rigorous independent trials.
30. What it helps: Topical cosmetic studies show a modest drop in wrinkle depth, mostly around the eyes and forehead. Those studies are often run by the ingredient maker, skin penetration is poor and results fall far short of injected botulinum toxin. Users apply it as a serum and describe the effect as subtle at best.
31. Trial: none. No registered trial; the only human data are the cosmetic supplier's own small wrinkle tests. (checked 2026-10-23) [S2] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
32. People with myasthenia gravis or neuromuscular disease: Interferes with SNARE nerve-muscle signalling, like a weak botulinum toxin. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
33. GHK-Cu: SNAP-8 softens expression lines by quieting muscle contraction, while GHK-Cu works on the skin itself, building collagen and elastin. People combine them in serums to treat both the movement and the skin quality behind wrinkles. The pairing is formulation logic, not trial data.
34. Argireline: Argireline is a shorter peptide from the same SNAP-25 mimic family, so it works on the same SNARE machinery. Pairing them stacks a mild anti-wrinkle effect, but they overlap rather than complement, and the pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
35. Acetyl octapeptide-3, a longer relative of Argireline, an eight-amino-acid peptide that interferes with the SNARE complex muscles use to release the signal that contracts them.
36. First studied for: A topical cosmetic 'Botox-like' wrinkle agent, to soften expression lines.
37. Name story: Named after SNAP-25 (synaptosomal-associated protein, 25 kDa), the nerve-ending protein whose N-terminal end it mimics. The 8 is its length: a two-residue extension of the six-residue Argireline. [S3] (verified)
38. 2000s: Built as a longer Argireline for expression lines
39. Today: Topical 'Botox-like' cosmetic ingredient; not a drug
