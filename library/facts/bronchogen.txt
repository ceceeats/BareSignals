# Bronchogen
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/bronchogen
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: bioregulator, AEDL, Ala-Glu-Asp-Leu
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: AEDL (4 amino acids)
4. Legal and approval status: Not an approved drug anywhere; sold as a Khavinson bioregulator.

## Summary
5. Bronchogen is a lab-made Khavinson bioregulator peptide for bronchial and lung tissue, closely related to Chonluten. It is used mainly by the longevity community for lung support, and it is claimed to tune bronchial genes and calm airway inflammation. Russian rat studies report restored airway lining, but there is no Western validation and user reports are very few.
6. Punch line: Khavinson bronchial bioregulator, Chonluten's sibling. Claimed to repair airway lining; evidence mostly Russian.
7. Used for: Bronchial and lung support

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Organ function: Bronchial lining support
9. Inflammatory signaling: Calms airway inflammation
10. Tissue repair: Airway repair claims

## How it works
11. Claimed to tune bronchial tissue genes · Khavinson group · role Organ function
   Bronchogen is claimed to act directly on DNA in bronchial cells, tuning which genes are active instead of binding a surface receptor. The aim is to support lung tissue in chronic lung conditions. This is the Khavinson group's claim and has no outside replication.
12. Claimed to restore airway lining in rats · Bronchial epithelium · role Tissue repair
   In the group's rat studies, damaged bronchial epithelium, the airway lining, was reported to recover. That lining traps and clears particles and germs, so restoring it matters for breathing. There is no Western validation of this.
13. Claimed to calm airway inflammation · Inflammatory markers · role Inflammatory signaling
   The group reports lower inflammatory markers in the airways after treatment. Less inflammation would mean less swelling and mucus in the bronchi. This is from Russian animal work only.
14. Mechanism overview: Bronchogen is a short Khavinson bioregulator peptide for bronchial tissue. The group claims it tunes bronchial gene expression, restores the airway lining in rats and lowers inflammatory markers. These claims come from a single research group without outside replication. Like other bioregulators, it is a short peptide said to act directly on DNA rather than through a surface receptor.

## What has been tested
30. Organ function: Chronic lung conditions (animal)
31. Russian studies only.
32. What it helps: Russian animal studies report support for lung tissue in chronic lung conditions, including restoration of damaged airway lining in rats. There is no Western validation. First-hand user reports are very few, leaving its practical effects largely undocumented. Its use outside Russia is driven mainly by the longevity community.
33. Trial: none. Khavinson bioregulator studied only in unregistered Russian clinical reports; no registry trial found in ClinicalTrials.gov, EU CTR, ISRCTN or ChiCTR, Russian registry unverifiable. (checked 2026-10-23) [S4] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
34. People with active or recent cancer: Marketed as gene-regulating; no human tumour safety data. (inferred)
35. People treating a diagnosed lung disease with it: No controlled trials show benefit; delays proven care. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
36. Epitalon: Epitalon is the general anti-aging bioregulator, claimed to act on telomerase and melatonin, while Bronchogen focuses on the bronchial lining. Longevity users run them in one course to pair a whole-body claim with a lung one. The pair has not been tested together.
37. Thymalin: Thymalin supports immune cell balance and germ clearance, while Bronchogen is aimed at restoring the airway lining. People pair them for chronic or repeated chest trouble, covering defence and tissue together. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
38. A Khavinson bioregulator peptide for bronchial/lung tissue (closely related to Chonluten).
39. First studied for: Respiratory support in chronic lung conditions.
40. Name story: Khavinson's naming system: organ plus '-gen'. Bronchogen targets bronchial epithelium and is listed as a 'bronchoprotective peptide'. AEDL is its one-letter code, Ala-Glu-Asp-Leu. [S5] (verified)
41. Late 20th c.: Khavinson bioregulator for bronchial tissue; a Chonluten relative
42. Today: Sold as a bioregulator; not an approved drug anywhere
