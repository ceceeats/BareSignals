# Ipamorelin
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/ipamorelin
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: NNC 26-0161, NNC-26-0161
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Sequence: AHAFK (5 amino acids). Aib shown as A, D-2-naphthylalanine shown as A, D-Phe shown as F; amide end. Weight is approximate
4. Legal and approval status: Unapproved research chemical. WADA-banned.

## Summary
5. Ipamorelin is a synthetic pentapeptide that mimics ghrelin to release growth hormone cleanly, without the hunger, cortisol or prolactin spikes of older GHRPs. People use it for sleep, recovery and fat loss, almost always stacked with CJC-1295. Human studies confirm a clean GH rise, but benefits beyond that are inferred from GH physiology, it failed a bowel recovery trial, and it is WADA-banned.
6. Punch line: The clean GH releaser: mimics ghrelin without hunger, cortisol or prolactin spikes. Nearly always paired with CJC.
7. Used for: GH release, recovery, sleep

## How it works
14. Mimics ghrelin to trigger a GH pulse · Ghrelin receptor, GHSR-1a · role Endocrine & growth
   Ipamorelin binds the ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a) on GH-making cells of the pituitary and in the hypothalamus. This sets off a GH pulse, amplifies the brain's own GHRH signal and blunts somatostatin, the brake on GH. Human studies confirm a clean GH rise.
15. Spares cortisol, prolactin and hunger · Selective GHS · role Endocrine & growth
   Older ghrelin mimics such as GHRP-2 and GHRP-6 also push out cortisol and prolactin and drive hunger. Ipamorelin is a selective secretagogue, so at standard use it barely moves those. In human studies this shows up as a GH rise with few side effects.
16. Raises IGF-1 downstream of GH · GH, liver IGF-1 · role Muscle & performance
   Each GH pulse signals the liver to make IGF-1, the hormone that carries most of GH's effects on muscle, fat and bone. Ipamorelin's benefits for lean mass are inferred from this GH and IGF-1 physiology, not from outcome trials in people.
17. Increases bone mineral content, in rats · GH, IGF-1 on bone · role Bone & cartilage
   GH and IGF-1 drive bone-building cells and bone growth. In rat studies ipamorelin raised bone mineral content, consistent with that route. No human study has shown a bone benefit, so this remains an animal finding.
18. Claimed to deepen sleep via night GH pulses · Slow-wave sleep, claimed · role Sleep & rhythms
   Most of the day's GH is released in pulses during deep, slow-wave sleep. Users claim that adding night-time GH pulses deepens sleep, but no trial has measured sleep on ipamorelin. Treat this as user report built on GH physiology.
19. Mechanism overview: Ipamorelin is a pentapeptide that activates the ghrelin receptor GHSR-1a on pituitary somatotrophs and in the hypothalamus. This triggers a GH pulse and amplifies GHRH while blunting somatostatin. It is selective: unlike GHRP-2 and GHRP-6, it barely raises cortisol, prolactin or hunger at standard doses. Each GH pulse drives liver IGF-1, which carries the downstream effects on lean mass, fat and bone. Pairing with a GHRH analogue such as CJC-1295 gives a larger pulse than either alone, because the two act on separate receptors.

## What has been tested
36. Endocrine & growth: GH release (human)
37. Digestive & gut: Recovery after bowel surgery (trial)
38. Endocrine & growth: GH release (animal)
39. Early human and animal studies showed clean GH release. Phase 2 trials for gut recovery after surgery found no benefit over placebo, and that development stopped.
40. What it helps: Human studies confirm a clean GH rise with few side effects. Rat studies showed more bone mineral content. Trials in bowel recovery after surgery found no benefit over placebo. Users report better sleep, recovery and fat loss, almost always stacking it with CJC-1295. Benefits beyond GH release are inferred from GH physiology rather than outcome trials.
41. Trial: none. Last trials 2008 to 2011 for gut recovery after surgery, which failed; never tested in humans since. (checked 2026-10-23) [S4] (reported)

## Who shouldn't take it
42. People with active or recent cancer: Raises GH and IGF-1, which promote cell growth. (inferred)
43. People with prediabetes or poorly controlled diabetes: GH raises blood glucose and insulin resistance. (inferred)
44. Drug-tested athletes: GH secretagogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
45. CJC-1295 no DAC: CJC-1295 no DAC works on the GHRH receptor, ipamorelin on the ghrelin receptor, so the two hit separate switches and give a larger GH pulse together. This is the classic GH stack, sold pre-mixed as CJC-1295 no DAC + Ipamorelin, but the pair has not been tested together in people.
46. CJC-1295 with DAC: The DAC version keeps GHRH tone raised for days, while ipamorelin adds sharp ghrelin-receptor pulses on top. People pair them for a higher baseline plus pulses. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
47. Sermorelin: Sermorelin is a short-acting GHRH that keeps natural pulses, and ipamorelin adds a ghrelin-receptor push from a second route. Together they aim for a bigger but still pulsatile GH release. The pair itself has not been tested together.
48. Tesamorelin: Tesamorelin brings the best human evidence in this family, for visceral and liver fat, and ipamorelin adds a separate ghrelin-receptor GH pulse. They are sold pre-mixed as Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin, but the combination has not been studied in trials.
49. BPC-157: BPC-157 targets local repair, blood vessel growth and tendon cell GH receptors, while ipamorelin raises the GH that those cells respond to. People pair them for injury recovery. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
50. TB-500 frag 17–23: The fragment is used for wound and tendon repair through actin and vessel growth, mostly on data from full Tβ4, while ipamorelin adds a systemic GH and IGF-1 lift. People stack them for recovery. The pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
51. A synthetic pentapeptide ghrelin-mimetic (GH secretagogue) designed to release GH cleanly, without the hunger, cortisol or prolactin spikes of older GHRPs.
52. First studied for: A selective GH-releasing agent for research and GH-optimization.
53. Name story: Novo Nordisk's NNC 26-0161, billed in its 1998 debut paper as "the first selective growth hormone secretagogue": it raises GH without the cortisol and prolactin bumps of GHRP-6 and GHRP-2. [S5] (verified)
54. 1998: Designed as a clean GH releaser without hunger, cortisol or prolactin spikes
55. 2000s: Phase 2 trials for gut recovery after surgery; no benefit, dropped
56. Today: The most popular GH-optimization peptide; unapproved, WADA-banned
