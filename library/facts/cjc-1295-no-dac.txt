# CJC-1295 no DAC
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/cjc-1295-no-dac
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Modified GRF (1-29), Mod GRF 1-29, tetrasubstituted GRF(1-29)
2. Origin: Lab-modified natural chain
3. Sequence: YADAIFTQSYRKVLAQLSARKLLQDILSR (29 amino acids). Position 2 is D-Ala; amide end
4. Legal and approval status: Unapproved research chemical. WADA-banned.

## Summary
5. CJC-1295 no DAC, also called modified GRF 1-29, is a GHRH analogue engineered for stability that stays short-acting without the DAC tail. People use it to raise GH in natural pulses, usually paired with a GHRP such as ipamorelin, for sleep, recovery and body composition. No human study exists for this exact molecule, so effects are inferred, though it is a very common grey-market GH peptide.
6. Punch line: Mod GRF 1-29. Sturdier GHRH without the DAC tail; short-acting, so GH rises in natural pulses.
7. Used for: GH release, often with ipamorelin

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Endocrine & growth: Raises natural GH pulses
9. Sleep & rhythms: Deeper sleep reported

## How it works
10. Prompts the pituitary to release GH · GHRH receptor · role Endocrine & growth
   CJC-1295 no DAC binds the GHRH receptor on pituitary GH cells, which raises cAMP inside the cell and releases stored GH. This is the same switch the body's own GHRH uses. No human study of this exact molecule exists, so the effect is inferred from related GHRH data.
11. Resists breakdown, still short-acting · Modified GRF 1-29 · role Endocrine & growth
   It is the active first 29 amino acids of GHRH with four swaps that resist DPP-4, the enzyme that quickly cuts natural GHRH. That makes it more stable, but it still clears within about half an hour, so it amplifies a pulse rather than holding GH up all day.
12. Amplifies GH pulses when paired with a GHRP · GHRH and GHS synergy · role Endocrine & growth
   GHRH opens the GH gate while ghrelin mimics such as ipamorelin push from a second receptor and blunt somatostatin. Acting together, the two pathways produce a much larger pulse than either alone. This synergy is well shown for GHRH plus secretagogues, but extrapolated for this molecule.
13. GHRH signalling deepens slow-wave sleep · Slow-wave sleep · role Sleep & rhythms
   GHRH signalling in the hypothalamus promotes deep, slow-wave sleep, and GH is mostly released during that stage. A GHRH analogue is expected to support it. This rests on GHRH physiology and user reports, with no sleep study of this exact peptide.
14. Mechanism overview: CJC-1295 without DAC, also called modified GRF 1-29, is the first 29 amino acids of GHRH, its active core, with four substitutions that resist DPP-4 cleavage and improve stability. It binds GHRH receptors on pituitary somatotrophs, raising cAMP and triggering GH release. Its half-life is about 30 minutes, so it amplifies a pulse rather than raising GH continuously, preserving the natural pattern. Combined with a ghrelin mimetic such as ipamorelin, the two pathways synergise into a much larger pulse. GHRH tone also deepens slow-wave sleep.

37. Drug-tested athletes: GHRH analogues are banned under WADA S2. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
38. Ipamorelin: CJC-1295 no DAC opens the GHRH gate and ipamorelin pushes through the ghrelin receptor with few side effects, so the pulse is larger than either alone. Sold pre-mixed as CJC-1295 no DAC + Ipamorelin, but the pair has not been tested together in people.
39. GHRP-2: GHRP-2 gives a strong ghrelin-receptor GH pulse and CJC-1295 no DAC adds GHRH drive from the other side. People pair them for a bigger pulse and accept more hunger, cortisol and prolactin. The pairing is user logic, not trial data for this molecule.
40. GHRP-6: GHRP-6 adds a ghrelin-receptor pulse and strong hunger, CJC-1295 no DAC adds GHRH drive. People pair them when bulking and eating more is the goal. The pair itself has not been tested together.
41. Hexarelin: Hexarelin is the strongest ghrelin mimic but fades with repeated use, and CJC-1295 no DAC adds GHRH drive to amplify its pulse. Used for short, hard GH blocks. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
42. Tesamorelin: Both act on the GHRH receptor, so they overlap rather than complement each other. They appear together in the pre-mixed Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin + CJC-1295 no DAC, where ipamorelin supplies the second pathway. The pair alone has not been tested.

## Where it comes from
43. A GHRH analogue (also called modified GRF 1-29 or CJC-1295 without the Drug Affinity Complex), engineered for stability but still short-acting without the DAC tail.
44. First studied for: Studied to raise GH in natural pulses, usually paired with a GHRP.
45. Name story: Properly called Modified GRF (1-29): the first 29 amino acids of growth hormone-releasing hormone with four swaps for stability. Selling it as 'CJC-1295' is a mislabel; the CJC code belongs to the DAC version, and even papers have confused the two. [S5] (verified)
46. 2000s: Stabilised GRF 1-29, also called modified GRF 1-29
