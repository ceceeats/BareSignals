# HMG
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/hmg
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: human menopausal gonadotropin, menotropins, menotrophin, Menopur, Pergonal (historic), Repronex, Humegon, Menogon
2. Origin: Made by the body: FSH and LH purified from postmenopausal urine
3. Shown with an FDA approved badge
4. Legal and approval status: FDA-approved as Menopur (menotropins, first US approval 1975); a biologic since Mar 2020. EMA lists the Menopur 1200 IU shortage as resolved (updated Apr 2026). WADA 2026 S2: prohibited.

## Summary
5. HMG (menotropins, Menopur) is a mix of FSH and LH extracted from the urine of postmenopausal women. It is approved to grow follicles for ovulation induction and IVF, and it is used in men with low gonadotropins to restart sperm production, usually added to hCG. Risks in women include ovarian hyperstimulation and multiple pregnancies, and male recovery can take up to two years.
6. Punch line: FSH plus LH purified from postmenopausal urine. Grows follicles for IVF and restarts sperm when hCG alone fails.
7. Used for: IVF follicle growth, male spermatogenesis restart

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Reproductive: Grows follicles, restarts sperm
9. Endocrine & growth: Supplies FSH and LH
10. Connection & desire: LH activity lifts testosterone
11. Mood: Mood swings during stimulation

## How it works
12. Grows egg follicles · FSH receptor, granulosa cells · role Reproductive
   The FSH in menotropins binds FSH receptors on granulosa cells, which line the egg follicles in the ovary. This recruits and grows several follicles at once. Its approved job is driving follicle growth in IVF cycles.
13. Drives sperm production with testosterone · FSH receptor, Sertoli cells · role Reproductive
   In the testes, FSH binds Sertoli cells, which nurse developing sperm. Together with high local testosterone, this drives spermatogenesis. Small studies in men support adding it to hCG when sperm does not return.
14. LH activity makes testosterone · LHCGR, Leydig and theca cells · role Endocrine & growth
   The LH activity in menotropins, mostly from hCG according to the label, binds LHCGR on Leydig and theca cells. These cells then make more testosterone and other androgens. In men it fills the gap hCG alone leaves by adding FSH beside the LH signal.
15. Estrogen climbs as follicles grow · Aromatase, estradiol · role Mood
   Androgens from theca cells are turned into estradiol by aromatase in the growing follicles. The more follicles grow, the higher estradiol climbs. Clinicians track this rise to time IVF and avoid overstimulation.
16. Mechanism overview: Menotropins is a purified mix of follicle-stimulating hormone and luteinizing-hormone activity from postmenopausal urine; the label notes most of the LH activity comes from hCG. FSH binds FSH receptors on ovarian granulosa cells to recruit and grow follicles, and on testicular Sertoli cells to support spermatogenesis. The LH component binds LHCGR on theca and Leydig cells, raising androgen and estradiol production. In men it fills the gap hCG leaves, since hCG supplies only LH-like signalling.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
17. Typical: 225 IU/day (IVF); 75 IU (men, off-label) · daily (IVF); every other day to 3×/week (men) · cycle ≤20 days per IVF cycle; months in men · route Sub-Q (label), IM (verified)
18. Half-life: FSH ~11–13 h
19. Most people take: IVF: 225 IU SC daily, adjust by ≤150 IU, max 450 IU/day, ≤20 days. Men: 75 IU every other day added to hCG if azoospermia persists (review protocol for FSH); community uses 75–150 IU 2–3×/week.

## What people report
20. Used by men coming off TRT or steroids, usually layered on hCG at 75 IU a few times a week to supply the FSH hCG lacks. First-hand reports are few.
21. Liked / worked: Multiple follicle growth for IVF
22. Liked / worked: Restores sperm when hCG alone fails
23. Liked / worked: Long fertility track record
24. What worked: Grows multiple follicles for IVF; first pregnancy from extracted gonadotropins in 1962. In men, adding FSH to hCG can restart sperm when hCG alone fails.
25. Went wrong / did not work: Ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome
26. Went wrong / did not work: Multiple pregnancies
27. Went wrong / did not work: Blood clots (label)
28. Went wrong / did not work: Slow male recovery, up to 2 years
29. What went wrong: Ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome, multiple pregnancies and blood clots in women (label). Male sperm recovery can take up to two years.
30. Most reported: Used on top of hCG for FSH
31. Most reported: Post-TRT fertility restarts
32. Most reported: Few detailed first-hand reports

## What has been tested
33. Reproductive: IVF and ovulation induction (human)
34. Reproductive: Sperm recovery added to hCG (human)
35. Decades of fertility use and IVF trials; in men, small studies adding FSH or hMG to hCG for azoospermia.
36. What it helps: Its approved job is growing multiple follicles in IVF cycles. In men with low gonadotropins, or after TRT and steroids, clinicians add FSH or hMG to hCG when sperm does not return on hCG alone. The male data are small studies and reviews. Community use is mostly men restoring fertility; reports are sparse.
37. Trial: active · IVF ovarian stimulation · Ferring and academic centres. Approved as Menopur; used as a standard arm in many ongoing IVF trials, though no specific recruiting hMG record was confirmed here. (checked 2026-10-23) [S1] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
38. Women with PCOS or prior ovarian hyperstimulation: These women overrespond and are most at risk of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome. (verified)
39. People with clotting history: Hyperstimulation thickens the blood and raises the risk of clots; label warns. (verified)
40. Pituitary tumour, pituitary surgery or brain radiation: Label contraindicates pituitary or hypothalamic tumours. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
41. HCG: HCG supplies the LH signal that keeps testosterone high inside the testes, and HMG adds FSH for Sertoli cells and sperm production. This is a clinical combination for men whose sperm does not return on hCG alone, backed by small studies and reviews.
42. Gonadorelin: Gonadorelin pulses the pituitary to release its own LH and FSH, while HMG supplies FSH and LH activity directly. They overlap in goal, so pairing them is user logic for fertility restarts, not trial data.
43. Kisspeptin-10: Kisspeptin-10 prompts the brain to restart GnRH and LH release, and HMG supplies FSH for sperm production at the testes. People use the logic of working top and bottom of the axis, but the pair has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
44. FSH and LH extracted from the urine of postmenopausal women, whose bodies pour these hormones out once the ovaries stop. Each 75 IU vial carries 75 IU FSH and 75 IU LH activity.
45. First studied for: Ovulation induction and IVF; also used to start sperm production in men with low gonadotropins.
46. Name story: Hormones from postmenopausal urine, first purified by Piero Donini in Italy. Early supply came from convents with a quiet papal blessing: about 100 nuns gave 30,000 litres for 100 mg. The brand Pergonal means "per gonadi", for the gonads. [S2] (reported)
47. 1940s–50s: Donini extracts FSH and LH from postmenopausal urine
48. 1962: First birth after extracted gonadotropins (Pergonal)
49. 1975: First US approval of menotropins
50. Mar 2020: Reclassified as a biologic; compounding cut off
51. 2023–2025: Menopur shortage in Europe, resolved (EMA)
