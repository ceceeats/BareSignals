# Adamax
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/adamax
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Semax derivative, adamantyl Semax, Ac-MEHFPGPAG-NH2 (adamantyl-modified)
2. Origin: Lab-modified Semax
3. Sequence: MEHFPGP (7 amino acids). Semax core; sellers claim an acetylated N-terminus and an adamantane on the C-terminus. Published structures disagree.
4. Legal and approval status: Not approved; no published research; listed by New Zealand's Medsafe in a June 2025 review of unscheduled peptides.

## Summary
5. Adamax is a seller-marketed version of Semax with an adamantane group added, said to make it longer lasting and stronger. Users take it for focus and calm. It is not approved and no published study of Adamax exists, since every cited reference is about Semax, and sellers disagree on what is in the vial.
6. Punch line: Semax with an adamantane group, sold as stronger and longer lasting. Taken for focus, calm; no study exists.
7. Used for: Focus, mood

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
8. Cognition: Focus and clarity reported
9. Neural protection: Borrowed from Semax claims
10. Mood: Less overwhelm reported
11. Drive & mental stamina: Mental energy reported

## How it works
12. Claimed to work like Semax, only longer · ACTH 4–7 core, melanocortin · role Cognition
   Adamax is sold as Semax, a fragment of ACTH, with an adamantane group added to make it last longer. The idea is that it acts on the same melanocortin pathway as Semax. No study has tested this in Adamax itself.
13. Claimed to raise brain growth factors · BDNF up, Semax data · role Neural protection
   Semax raises BDNF, brain-derived neurotrophic factor, a growth factor that supports neuron survival and new connections. Adamax is claimed to do the same. That claim rests entirely on Semax data, mostly from rodents.
14. Adamantane added for stability and brain entry · Adamantyl modification, claimed · role Cognition
   Adamantane is a bulky cage-shaped group, also used in P21, meant to slow breakdown and help the molecule cross into the brain. This is design intent, not a measured result. Published structures of Adamax disagree, and no paper characterises the molecule.
15. Mechanism overview: Adamax is sold as Semax (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro, from ACTH 4–10) with an adamantane group attached, the same bulky cage used in P21 to slow breakdown and help brain entry. The intended mechanism is Semax's: melanocortin-pathway signalling and raised BDNF. Published structures conflict on chain length, the end groups and where the adamantane sits, and no paper characterises the molecule.

## What has been tested
28. Nothing published on Adamax itself; all cited studies are on Semax.
29. What it helps: There is no published study of Adamax. Every reference sellers cite is about Semax. Users take it for focus and calm, and headaches and overstimulation come up at higher amounts.
30. Trial: none. No human trial in any registry; it exists only as a research chemical derived from Semax. (checked 2026-10-23) [S1] (inferred)

## Who shouldn't take it
31. People with a history of seizures: Semax parent carries convulsion warning; adamantane boosts brain entry. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
32. Semax: Adamax is marketed as a longer-lasting Semax, so pairing them is mostly doubling up on the same claimed pathway, melanocortin signalling and BDNF. Adamax has no published data, and the pair has not been tested together.
33. Selank: Semax-type peptides like Adamax are taken for drive and focus, while Selank is taken for calm through tuftsin and enkephalin effects. People pair them to balance stimulation with calm, but the pairing is user logic, not trial data.
34. P21: Both carry an adamantane group, and both are aimed at brain growth factors, Adamax through Semax-like BDNF claims and P21 through a CNTF mimic. Users stack them for memory and focus, but neither the pair nor Adamax alone has been studied.

## Where it comes from
35. A newer seller-marketed version of Semax with an adamantane group added, borrowing the trick used in P21.
36. First studied for: Marketed as a longer-lasting, stronger Semax.
37. Name story: Semax with an adamantane cage bolted to its tail end; 'Ada' is almost certainly from adamantane, the diamond-shaped hydrocarbon also behind amantadine and bromantane. Described as a designer analogue of Semax, with no published developer. [S2] (inferred)
38. 1980s–1990s: Semax developed in Russia from ACTH 4–10
39. 2020s: Adamax appears in research-peptide catalogs
40. June 2025: Named in New Zealand Medsafe's review of unscheduled peptides
41. Today: No published studies; structure disputed
