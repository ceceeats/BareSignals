# ATX-304
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/atx-304
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: O304, O-304, ATX304, ATX-304 Na salt
2. Origin: Not a peptide: small molecule
3. Legal and approval status: Investigational, not approved anywhere: Phase 1b done (2025), Phase 2 REWIRE-1 (muscle, lipids) and REWIRE-2 (weight loss) announced June 2026; MAD study restart pending after a July 2025 sponsor halt. As an unapproved drug it falls under WADA S0 (inferred); AMPK activators are also WADA S4 metabolic modulators (inferred).

## Summary
4. ATX-304 is an oral small molecule, not a peptide, that activates the cell's fuel sensor AMPK. It is pitched as an exercise mimetic that raises energy use rather than cutting appetite. A 2026 Phase 1b cut liver fat and raised resting metabolic rate 8% with little weight change. All human results are company press releases, and a dose-escalation study was paused by the sponsor in July 2025.
5. Punch line: Oral AMPK activator pitched as an exercise mimetic. Raises energy burn instead of cutting appetite; liver fat fell.
6. Used for: Liver fat, metabolic rate, insulin resistance

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Cellular energy: Raises cell energy use (AMPK)

9. Fat metabolism: Less liver and visceral fat
10. Muscle & performance: Better exercise capacity in mice
11. Organ function (backed only by animal or user reports): Liver fat down in Phase 1b

## How it works
14. Keeps the cell's fuel sensor switched on · AMPK, protected from dephosphorylation · role Cellular energy
   ATX-304 activates AMPK, the cell's fuel sensor, and protects its switched-on, phosphorylated form from being turned off. AMPK stays active longer, pushing cells to burn fuel. Shown in cell and animal work, with early human trials.
15. Activates AMPK without draining ATP · ATP-independent AMPK activation · role Cellular energy
   Most AMPK activation happens when cells run low on ATP, their energy currency. ATX-304 is said by its developers to activate AMPK without draining ATP, so cells do not have to be stressed first. This mechanism comes from developer studies.
16. Pulls glucose into muscle without insulin · GLUT4 uptake via AMPK · role Glucose & insulin
   Active AMPK moves GLUT4, the glucose transporter, to the muscle cell surface. Muscle can then pull in glucose without insulin, as during exercise. A phase 2a trial in type 2 diabetics on metformin lowered fasting glucose.
17. Burns more fat in mitochondria · Mitochondrial proton conductance, fatty acid oxidation · role Fat metabolism
   AMPK raises fatty acid oxidation and mitochondrial proton conductance, so cells burn more fat and release more energy as heat. A 2026 phase 1b in people with obesity and prediabetes cut liver fat and raised resting metabolic rate.
18. Widens small blood vessels · Microvascular perfusion · role Heart & vessels
   ATX-304 widens small blood vessels and raises microvascular perfusion, the blood flow through tiny vessels in tissue. Better flow brings more oxygen and glucose to muscle. The 2017 phase 2a in type 2 diabetics showed raised microvascular flow and lower blood pressure.
19. Mechanism overview: ATX-304 (O304) is an oral small molecule that activates AMPK, the cell's energy sensor, across all its isoforms. Its developers say it does this without lowering ATP, by protecting the active, phosphorylated form of AMPK from being switched off, and that it acts mainly outside the brain. Active AMPK pushes GLUT4 to the muscle surface for insulin-independent glucose uptake, raises fatty acid oxidation and mitochondrial proton conductance, and so lifts energy use the way aerobic training does.

## What has been tested
43. Glucose & insulin: Fasting glucose in type 2 diabetes, Phase 2a (human)
44. Organ function: Liver fat by MRI, Phase 1b (human)
45. Fat metabolism: Visceral fat and triglycerides, Phase 1b (human)
46. Cellular energy: Resting metabolic rate, Phase 1b (human)
47. Muscle & performance: Exercise capacity and heart function in aged mice (animal)
48. About 150 people in early trials, a 28-day Phase 2a in type 2 diabetics on metformin (2017), and a Phase 1b in 23 adults with obesity and prediabetes at 400 mg daily (reported 2026).
49. What it helps: A 2017 Phase 2a in type 2 diabetics on metformin lowered fasting glucose and blood pressure and raised microvascular blood flow. A 2026 Phase 1b in 23 people with obesity and prediabetes cut liver fat, visceral fat and triglycerides and raised resting metabolic rate 8%, with little weight change. In aged mice it reversed insulin resistance and improved heart function and exercise capacity. Biohackers use it as an exercise mimetic, often with SLU-PP-332.
50. Trial: active · Phase 1 · obesity with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes · 2024-514744-91-00 · Betagenon. A Phase 1 dose study in Germany was halted in July 2025, then amended in July 2026 to add another cohort. (checked 2026-10-23) [S3] (reported)

## Who shouldn't take it
51. Drug-tested athletes: AMPK activators are banned under WADA S4.4. (inferred)
52. Insulin or sulfonylurea users: AMPK activation pulls glucose into muscle, which can add to drug-induced lows. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
53. SLU-PP-332: SLU-PP-332 switches on ERR, the endurance gene receptors, while ATX-304 activates AMPK. Biohackers stack them as two exercise mimics from different angles. SLU-PP-332 has mouse data only, and the pair has not been tested.
54. Semaglutide: Semaglutide cuts appetite and weight through the GLP-1 receptor, while ATX-304 raises energy use and cut liver fat with little weight change. The pair covers intake and burn. It has not been tested together.

## Where it comes from
55. A Swedish oral pan-AMPK activator, born as O304 at Betagenon in Umeå and now run by Amplifier Therapeutics, a Cambrian Bio company; pitched as an exercise mimetic that raises energy use rather than cutting appetite.
56. First studied for: Type 2 diabetes (as O304), now obesity, prediabetes and cardiometabolic disease.
57. Name story: Born as O304 at Betagenon in Umeå, Sweden, and renamed ATX-304 under Amplifier Therapeutics, the Cambrian Bio company now developing it; the ATX prefix likely stands for Amplifier Therapeutics. [S4] (inferred)
58. 2016–2017: O304 Phase 2a TELLUS in type 2 diabetes: lower glucose and blood pressure
59. 2018: JCI Insight paper: O304 improves glucose and blood flow in mice and diabetics
60. 2021: Exercise-mimetic effects in aged mice (Communications Biology)
61. 2023: Renamed ATX-304 under Amplifier/Cambrian; Phase 1b starts
62. Jul 2025: Sponsor halts dose-escalation study
63. Jun 2026: Phase 1b: less liver and visceral fat, 8% higher metabolic rate; Phase 2 REWIRE planned
