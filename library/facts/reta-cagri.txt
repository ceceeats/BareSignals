# Retatrutide + Cagrilintide
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/reta-cagri
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: Reta/Cagri, Reta + Cagri blend, retatrutide/cagrilintide 12.5/2.5
2. Origin: Blend of 2 peptides
3. Legal and approval status: Neither part is approved anywhere. FDA says both cannot be compounded under federal law; retatrutide sellers drew warning letters (Sep 2025, Mar 2026, Aug 2026). Lilly plans to file retatrutide in Q1 2027; a CagriSema decision is expected Q4 2026. WADA: not named, falls under S0.

## Summary
4. This blend pairs retatrutide, Lilly's triple GLP-1, GIP and glucagon agonist, with cagrilintide, Novo's long-acting amylin analogue. Users combine them for weight loss, since the amylin side adds a second fullness route and quiets cravings retatrutide leaves behind. Both drugs have large human trials, but the pair has only a rat study. Stacked nausea and constipation are the main complaints.
5. Punch line: Triple agonist plus amylin. Cagrilintide adds a second fullness route, quieting cravings retatrutide leaves behind.
6. Used for: Weight loss, appetite and cravings

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Appetite & satiety: Two satiety routes: incretin and amylin
8. Fat metabolism: Glucagon side raises fat burning
9. Glucose & insulin: Lowers glucose and A1c
10. Reward & urges: Quiets cravings and food noise
11. Pain & sensory (backed only by animal or user reports): Knee pain eased in a retatrutide OA trial

## How it works
12. Hits three appetite and metabolism receptors · GLP-1R, GIPR, glucagon receptor agonist · role Appetite & satiety
   Retatrutide is one molecule that switches on three receptors: GLP-1 and GIP, the gut hormone receptors that cut hunger and lift insulin, plus the glucagon receptor. Together they drive large weight loss. Phase 2 and TRIUMPH-1 human trials show this for retatrutide alone.
13. Glucagon arm raises energy use and liver fat burning · Glucagon receptor · role Fat metabolism
   The glucagon receptor arm raises energy use and makes the liver burn more of its stored fat. This adds to weight loss from eating less, and is the main thing retatrutide has that semaglutide lacks. Shown in human trials of retatrutide alone.
14. Long-acting amylin copy: fuller sooner, slower stomach · Amylin and calcitonin receptors (DACRA) · role Appetite & satiety
   Cagrilintide is a long-acting copy of amylin that also activates calcitonin receptors. It makes people full sooner and slows stomach emptying, through a route separate from GLP-1. Human trials show weight loss alone and more when combined with semaglutide.
15. Acts on brain reward and craving circuits · Area postrema, hindbrain amylin signalling · role Reward & urges
   Amylin acts in the area postrema, a hindbrain zone outside the blood-brain barrier, to cut meal size and dampen reward-driven eating. This is why users report fewer cravings. Shown in human cagrilintide trials; the craving effect on this pair is user-reported.
16. Mechanism overview: Retatrutide is a single acylated peptide agonist at GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors; the glucagon arm raises energy expenditure and hepatic fat oxidation on top of incretin-driven satiety. Cagrilintide is a lipidated, long-acting amylin analogue that also activates calcitonin receptors (a dual amylin and calcitonin receptor agonist). Amylin signals through the area postrema and hindbrain to slow gastric emptying, reduce meal size and dampen reward-driven eating, a route separate from GLP-1, which is why amylin plus incretin pairs add weight loss in trials of cagrilintide with semaglutide.

## What is inside the blend
17. Retatrutide: 12.5 mg · Triple incretin plus glucagon: the main weight-loss driver
18. Cagrilintide: 2.5 mg · Amylin route: fullness, slower stomach, fewer cravings
19. Most common ratio: Retatrutide 12.5 · Cagrilintide 2.5 = 15 mg (7 of 8 listings)
20. Other ratio seen: Retatrutide 25 · Cagrilintide 5 = 30 mg (1 listing (second size))
21. Other ratio seen: Retatrutide 10 · Cagrilintide 2.5 = 12.5 mg (1 listing)
22. Version Common retail: Retatrutide 12.5 · Cagrilintide 2.5 = 15 mg
23. Version Larger vial: Retatrutide 20 · Cagrilintide 4 = 24 mg
24. Version Other seller splits: Retatrutide 10 · Cagrilintide 2 (also 10 + 2.5, 8 + 2) = 12 mg
25. Why Retatrutide is in it: Adds the glucagon arm and the strongest incretin signal; cagrilintide alone loses far less weight
26. Why Cagrilintide is in it: Adds the amylin route for meal size and cravings, which incretins leave partly untouched
27. Mixing chemistry: The CagriSema maker states cagrilintide and semaglutide cannot share one formulation: cagrilintide needs about pH 4, semaglutide about pH 7.4; their pen keeps them in two chambers.
28. Mixing chemistry: The licensed amylin drug pramlintide (pH 4) carries a label rule never to mix it with insulin, for the same reason.
29. Mixing chemistry: Retatrutide's own formulation pH is not public; as an acylated incretin like semaglutide it is likely soluble only near neutral pH, so a shared liquid is a compromise for at least one part.
30. Mixing chemistry: Cagrilintide is surface-reactive and forms high-molecular-weight aggregates in storage; dissolve by swirling, never shaking.
31. Mixing chemistry: A slight off-white tint is described as normal in blend vials; haze, particles or gel mean discard.
32. Self-mixing: Mixing the two reconstituted vials into one is the case most at odds with the licensed formulations (pH 4 vs neutral); same-day injections in separate syringes at separate sites is what most guides describe, and separate vials allow independent titration.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
33. Typical: 1–4 mg retatrutide + 0.2–0.8 mg cagrilintide · 1× weekly · cycle Ongoing, after slow titration · route Sub-Q (reported)
34. Half-life: per component
35. Most people take: Sized by the retatrutide dose. 12.5/2.5 vial in 2.5 mL (5 mg/mL reta, 1 mg/mL cagri): 20 units = 1 + 0.2 mg, 40 units = 2 + 0.4 mg, 80 units = 4 + 0.8 mg. Steps held at least 4 weeks; step back if GI effects are heavy.

## What people report
36. Weekly, Sub-Q. Common route: retatrutide alone first, then add cagrilintide once stable. On a 12.5/2.5 vial in 2.5 mL, 20 units = 1 mg reta + 0.2 mg cagri; 40 units = 2 + 0.4. Many use separate vials through titration and switch to the blend at maintenance. Reported ratios range from 5:1 down to 1:1 for heavy cravings.
37. Liked / worked: Each part has large human trials
38. Liked / worked: Pair beat either alone in rats, 2026
39. Liked / worked: Users report fewer cravings at lower reta doses
40. What worked: Users and guides report the cagrilintide side quieting cravings and emotional eating that persist on retatrutide, at lower retatrutide doses. Most community protocols stay around 2 to 4 mg retatrutide weekly with 0.4 to 0.8 mg cagrilintide.
41. Went wrong / did not work: No human trial of the pair
42. Went wrong / did not work: Stacked nausea, vomiting, constipation
43. Went wrong / did not work: Fixed ratio blocks separate titration
44. What went wrong: Stacked GI effects are the main complaint: nausea, vomiting, constipation, worst while titrating. Retatrutide adds dose-related skin tingling (dysesthesia, 5 to 21% in trials) and a higher heart rate; cagrilintide adds injection-site reactions (17% vs 3%) and anti-drug antibodies in about half of users. A fixed vial cannot titrate the two separately.
45. Most reported: Reta first, cagri added once stable
46. Most reported: 1 to 4 mg reta + 0.2 to 0.8 mg cagri weekly
47. Most reported: Separate vials to titrate, blend at maintenance
48. Most reported: Hold each step 4 weeks

## What has been tested
49. Fat metabolism: Retatrutide, phase 2 and TRIUMPH-1 (human)
50. Appetite & satiety: Cagrilintide alone, REDEFINE 1 (human)
51. Appetite & satiety: Retatrutide + cagrilintide, obese rats (animal)
52. Reward & urges: Cravings and food noise (users)
53. No human trial of the pair as of Oct 2026; one 2026 rat study reported more weight loss with the pair than either alone. Separately: retatrutide 12 mg weekly gave 24.2% loss at 48 weeks (phase 2) and 28.3% at 80 weeks (TRIUMPH-1); cagrilintide 2.4 mg alone gave 11.8% at 68 weeks (REDEFINE 1), 22.7% with semaglutide.
54. What it helps: Both halves have large human trials; the pair has only a rat study. Expect the GI profile of both, likely heavier during titration. Users report the amylin side mainly helps cravings and emotional eating, and lets them hold a lower retatrutide dose.

## Who shouldn't take it
55. Personal or family history of MTC or MEN2: GLP-1 receptor agonists cause C-cell tumours in rodents; trials exclude these people. (inferred)
56. Insulin or sulfonylurea users: Amylin analogues add to insulin; pramlintide carries a boxed warning for severe low blood sugar. (inferred)
57. People relying on the contraceptive pill: Contains GIP agonism like tirzepatide, whose label warns pill absorption drops after starts and dose increases. (inferred)
58. People with gastroparesis or severe slow digestion: Amylin slows the stomach further; pramlintide is contraindicated in gastroparesis. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
59. Semaglutide: Semaglutide is the GLP-1 drug cagrilintide was trialled with, giving 22.7% weight loss in REDEFINE 1. Adding it to retatrutide doubles up GLP-1 receptor action. That overlap has not been tested together.
60. Tirzepatide: Tirzepatide shares the GLP-1 and GIP receptors with retatrutide, so stacking them doubles up the same targets rather than adding a new one. People sometimes switch between them. The combination has not been tested.
61. Eloralintide: Eloralintide is another long-acting amylin drug, acting on the same fullness receptors as cagrilintide. Adding it doubles up the amylin side rather than adding a new route. The pairing has not been tested.
62. Amylin: Amylin here refers to pramlintide, the short-acting mealtime amylin drug. It overlaps with cagrilintide's amylin action. Stacking both duplicates one route, and the pairing has not been tested together.
63. 5-Amino-1MQ: 5-Amino-1MQ blocks the NNMT enzyme in fat cells, a different target from the appetite receptors here, and shrank fat cells in obese mice. People add it for fat loss. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
64. Lilly's triple agonist retatrutide (GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors) pre-mixed with Novo's long-acting amylin analogue cagrilintide. Most sold at a fixed 5 to 1 ratio, usually 12.5 + 2.5 mg per vial.
65. First studied for: A grey-market copy of the CagriSema idea (amylin plus an incretin), using the strongest incretin in development instead of semaglutide. Users add the amylin side for cravings and food noise that retatrutide leaves behind.
66. 2021: Cagrilintide alone: 10.8% weight loss at 26 weeks, phase 2
67. 2023: Retatrutide phase 2: 24.2% at 48 weeks
68. 2025: REDEFINE 1: CagriSema 22.7% at 68 weeks
69. May 2026: TRIUMPH-1 topline: retatrutide 28.3% at 80 weeks
70. 2026: Rat study: pair beats either alone
71. Today: Sold pre-mixed 5:1, mainly 12.5 + 2.5 mg

## Deep read (as of 2026-10)

### What has and has not been studied as a pair [mixed]
72. No human trial of retatrutide with cagrilintide has been located, and none is registered in the sources reviewed (verified as absent). The evidence people lean on is a different pairing: cagrilintide with semaglutide. In REDEFINE 1, 3,417 adults took the combination, either drug alone or placebo for 68 weeks; adherent participants lost 22.7% on the combination, 16.1% on semaglutide, 11.8% on cagrilintide alone and 2.3% on placebo [4,5] (verified). Gastrointestinal effects hit 80% on the combination against 40% on placebo [4].
73. That result does not transfer automatically. Semaglutide acts on one receptor; retatrutide acts on three and already produces far larger losses on its own, so there is less room left for a second drug to add, and the glucagon arm brings effects semaglutide does not have (inferred). The single 2026 rodent paper located for this read tested retatrutide alone, about 25% lower body weight than obese controls over four weeks, not the pair [9] (verified). A rodent study of the pair itself could not be traced.

### Each component, one level deeper than the headline [people]
74. Retatrutide. The phase 2 trial enrolled 338 adults for 48 weeks; at 12 mg weight fell 24.2% against 2.1% on placebo [1] (verified). Heart rate rose with dose, peaking around week 24 and easing afterwards; cardiac arrhythmia adverse events reached 14% in some dose groups against 2% on placebo, mostly mild [1] (verified). TRIUMPH-1, 2,339 adults over 80 weeks, gave 28.3% at 12 mg on treatment and 25.0% by intention to treat, against 2.2% and 3.9% on placebo [2] (verified). At 12 mg, 11.3% stopped for adverse events, and a skin-sensation disturbance called dysesthesia appeared in 12.5% against 0.9% [2] (verified), a signal the older incretin drugs did not show.
75. Cagrilintide. Its phase 2 trial randomised 706 adults for 26 weeks; weight fell 10.8% at 4.5 mg against 9.0% on liraglutide and 3.0% on placebo [3] (verified). Injection-site reactions rose with dose to 43%. Anti-drug antibodies appeared in 46 to 73% of participants by week 26, increasing with dose and time; most cross-reacted with the body's own amylin, few were neutralising, and they did not appear to blunt weight loss [3] (verified).

### The antibody finding and what it means for a vial [mixed]
76. That cagrilintide provokes antibodies in most people, even as a pharmaceutical-grade product in a controlled trial, is the most under-discussed fact in this pairing (verified for the trial product). Antibodies that cross-react with native amylin are, in principle, antibodies against one of the body's own hormones. In the trial they were mostly non-neutralising and harmless over 26 weeks [3].
77. Material outside that supply chain adds unknown impurities and aggregates, which are the usual drivers of immune responses to peptides (inferred). A second peptide in the same solution adds its own (inferred). Nobody has measured antibody rates with research-grade cagrilintide, or with either peptide sharing a vial.

### The fixed ratio and the titration problem [mixed]
78. The common 5 to 1 mass ratio lines up roughly with the top trial doses of each, about 12 mg of retatrutide and 2.4 mg of cagrilintide (inferred from [2,4]). The trials, though, escalated each drug on its own schedule: retatrutide from 2 mg, stepping every four weeks [2]. In a blend, every step moves both at once, so the dose of the more nauseating component cannot be held while the other is raised (inferred).
79. Side effects stack on overlapping routes. At 12 mg, retatrutide's nausea rate was 42.4% against 14.8% on placebo [2]; cagrilintide alone gave nausea in 20 to 47% [3] (verified for each alone). Their sum in one person is unmeasured.

### Where the law stands, October 2026 [people]
80. The FDA states plainly that retatrutide and cagrilintide cannot be used in compounding under federal law, because neither is part of an approved drug or has been found safe and effective for any condition [6] (verified). It has sent warning letters to telehealth companies marketing retatrutide, to ingredient distributors selling it to compounders, and to outsourcing facilities repackaging it [6] (verified). In August 2026 the developer filed six lawsuits against sellers and reported more than 200 referrals to regulators and prosecutors [7] (verified).
81. Sport: neither is named on the 2026 Prohibited List, but with no government approval both fall in the catch-all non-approved class, prohibited at all times [8] (inferred application, verified wording). If a cagrilintide product is approved, that would change for that component (inferred).

### Open questions [mixed]
82. Does adding amylin to a triple agonist add meaningful weight loss, or mostly side effects? Untested in people.
83. Does the pair push heart rate or arrhythmia further than retatrutide alone? Unknown.
84. What are antibody rates with research-grade material? Unmeasured.
85. Is there an interaction between dysesthesia and the amylin route? Nobody has looked.
