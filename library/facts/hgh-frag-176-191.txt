# HGH frag 176–191
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/hgh-frag-176-191
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: hGH fragment 176-191, GH(176-191), somatotropin (176-191), Fragment 176-191
2. Origin: Fragment of a natural protein
3. Sequence: YLRIVQCRSVEGSCGF (16 amino acids). Same chain as AOD-9604
4. Legal and approval status: Unapproved research chemical.

## Summary
5. HGH frag 176-191 is the last stretch of the growth hormone molecule, the part linked to fat metabolism, isolated to keep fat breakdown without GH's growth effects. People use it for fat loss, sometimes injected locally, and animal work shows it raises fat burning. Human weight loss has not been shown, and it is an unapproved research chemical with mild reported effects.
6. Punch line: The fat-burning end of growth hormone, cut free of its growth effects. Often injected into stubborn fat; mild.
7. Used for: Fat loss, often local injection

## How it works
11. Keeps GH's fat-burning, drops growth effects · GH C-terminal fragment · role Fat metabolism
   The fragment is the C-terminal tail of growth hormone, residues 176 to 191, the stretch linked to fat metabolism. It does not switch on the GH receptor in the usual way, so the growth signals that build tissue are left out. What remains is a fat-focused piece, studied mainly in animals.
12. Spares blood sugar · No IGF-1 rise · role Endocrine & growth
   Because it skips normal GH receptor activation, it does not raise IGF-1, the liver hormone behind most of GH's growth effects. Full GH can push blood sugar up and blunt insulin action, and this fragment avoids that. That is the reasoning behind its design, with human data coming mostly from its relative AOD-9604.
13. Raises fat breakdown and burning in obese mice · β3-adrenergic upregulation · role Fat metabolism
   In obese mice it raises the number of beta-3 adrenergic receptors on fat cells, the receptors that tell fat cells to release stored fat. More receptors meant more lipolysis and more fat burned for energy. This is animal work, and human weight loss has not been shown.
14. Mechanism overview: This is the C-terminal 176 to 191 region of growth hormone, the part linked to fat metabolism. It does not activate the GH receptor in the usual way, so it does not raise IGF-1 or disturb blood sugar. In obese mice it upregulates beta-3 adrenergic receptors on fat cells, increasing lipolysis and fat oxidation. Its human data come mostly from its close relative AOD-9604.
