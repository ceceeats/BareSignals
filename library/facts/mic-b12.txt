# MIC + B12
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/mic-b12
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: MIC B12, Lipo-B, Lipo B12, lipotropic B12 shot, MICC, LC120, LC216
2. Origin: Blend: amino acids + vitamin (lipotropic injection)
3. Legal and approval status: US: not FDA-approved as a combination; made by 503A compounding pharmacies on prescription. Cyanocobalamin injection alone is approved for B12 deficiency. Grey-market LC120 and LC216 vials are sold for research use. Not on the WADA list.

## Summary
4. MIC + B12 is the classic lipotropic injection, combining methionine, inositol and choline with vitamin B12. Clinics sell it as a weekly fat-burning and energy shot, on the idea that the trio helps the liver move fat out. B12 clearly helps people who are deficient, but no trial shows meaningful weight loss from the shot, and extra B12 does nothing measurable at normal levels.
5. Punch line: The classic lipotropic shot: methionine, inositol and choline with B12. A weekly diet companion; fat loss unproven.
6. Used for: Energy and fat-loss support during a diet

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Fat metabolism: MIC trio claimed to move fat out of the liver
8. Cellular energy: B12 for red cells and fuel use
9. Organ function: Choline and methionine for the liver
10. Neural protection: B12 keeps nerves healthy
11. Skin & aesthetics (backed only by animal or user reports): High B12 can cause acne-like breakouts

## How it works
12. Lets the liver package and export fat · Choline → phosphatidylcholine, VLDL · role Organ function
   Choline becomes phosphatidylcholine, which the liver needs to package fat into VLDL particles for export. Without enough choline, fat builds up in the liver, shown in animals and people. Extra choline helps when intake is low; there is no evidence it moves more fat on a normal diet.
13. Methyl donor for the liver · Methionine → SAM · role Organ function
   Methionine is converted to SAM, the main methyl donor. SAM drives the PEMT pathway, a second route to phosphatidylcholine, so it supports liver fat export too. These lipotropic effects are shown mainly in animals on deficient diets.
14. Lipid and insulin signalling · Inositol, phosphoinositides · role Fat metabolism
   Inositol is the building block of phosphoinositides, signalling lipids that relay insulin's message inside cells. That links it to sugar and fat handling. Its best human evidence is oral use in polycystic ovary syndrome, not injected use for fat loss.
15. Needed for red cells and nerves · B12: methionine synthase, methylmalonyl-CoA mutase · role Cellular energy
   B12 is a cofactor for methionine synthase, which recycles homocysteine into methionine and keeps DNA and red cell production going, and for methylmalonyl-CoA mutase in odd-chain fat breakdown. Injections clearly correct deficiency in human trials; extra B12 adds little when levels are normal.
16. Protects nerves when B12 is low · Myelin maintenance · role Neural protection
   B12 is needed to maintain myelin, the insulating layer around nerves. When B12 runs low, nerves can be damaged, causing numbness and weakness. Treating deficiency protects nerves in people; there is no evidence of extra nerve benefit when levels are already normal.
17. Mechanism overview: Methionine, inositol and choline are classed as lipotropes, nutrients that prevent or reverse hepatic fat accumulation. Choline is required for phosphatidylcholine synthesis and VLDL secretion; methionine supplies S-adenosylmethionine for the PEMT pathway, which also makes phosphatidylcholine. Inositol is a phosphoinositide precursor involved in insulin signalling. Vitamin B12 is a cofactor for methionine synthase, which regenerates methionine from homocysteine, and for methylmalonyl-CoA mutase in odd-chain fat metabolism. The pairing is biochemically coherent, but lipotropic effects are shown mainly in deficient animals, and no controlled human trial measures fat loss from the combined injection.

## What is inside the blend
18. Methionine: 25 mg per mL · Methyl donor for liver fat handling
19. Inositol: 50 mg per mL · Insulin and lipid signalling
20. Choline: 50 mg per mL · Lets the liver export fat
21. Vitamin B12 (cyanocobalamin): 1 mg per mL · Energy and nerves; the only part with approved use
22. Most common ratio: Methionine 25 · Inositol 50 · Choline 50 · B12 1 = 126 mg/mL (3 of 6 pharmacy/clinic formulas (+1 guide))
23. Other ratio seen: Methionine 25 · Inositol 50 · Choline 50 · B12 0.33 ("MICC") = ~125 mg/mL (2 pharmacies)
24. Other ratio seen: Methionine 25 · Choline 50 · B12 1, no inositol (Lipo-B) = 76 mg/mL (1 pharmacy)
25. Version Common pharmacy: Methionine 25 · Inositol 50 · Choline 50 · B12 1 = 126 mg/mL
26. Version LC120: Methionine 15 · Choline 50 · L-Carnitine 50 · Dexpanthenol 5 = 120 mg/mL
27. Version LC216 (Lipo-B): L-Carnitine 20 · Arginine 20 · Methionine 25 · Inositol 50 · Choline 50 · B6 25 · B5 25 · B12 1 = 216 mg/mL
28. Why Methionine is in it: Methyl groups for the liver
29. Why Inositol is in it: Insulin and lipid signalling
30. Why Choline is in it: Liver fat export
31. Why Vitamin B12 is in it: Energy and nerves when B12 is low
32. Mixing chemistry: Ready-made liquid in a multi-dose vial; nothing to reconstitute.
33. Mixing chemistry: B12 is red and light-sensitive; keep the vial in its box.
34. Mixing chemistry: Cyanocobalamin is the usual B12; some clinics swap methylcobalamin, which is less stable to light.
35. Mixing chemistry: LC120 has no B12 at all despite the 'Lipo' label: it is a carnitine formula.
36. Mixing chemistry: Don't use if cloudy or particles appear.
37. Self-mixing: Not mixed by the user; it comes ready. Some people draw it with a separate B12 or Lipo-C vial in one syringe, which adds needle passes and untested contact.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
38. Typical: 1 mL (≈1 mg B12) · 1× weekly (some 2×) · cycle 4–12 weeks · route IM (reported)
39. Half-life: per component
40. Most people take: 1 mL intramuscular once a week is the usual clinic dose, sometimes twice a week in supervised weight-loss programs, reassessed every 4 to 8 weeks. LC120 and LC216 vials are dosed the same way, 1 mL at a time.

## What people report
41. 1 to 2 mL IM once a week, some twice, over 4 to 12 weeks. Reports: injection-site soreness, nausea, mild stomach upset, headache, a metallic taste at higher methionine doses, acne-like breakouts from B12. Often paired with a GLP-1 or phentermine program.
42. Liked / worked: Fixes low B12 and its tiredness
43. Liked / worked: Weekly shot helps diet adherence
44. Liked / worked: Cheap and widely compounded
45. What worked: People with low B12 feel more energy, and that is real. Clinics report weekly shots help adherence during a diet.
46. Went wrong / did not work: No trial shows weight loss from the shot
47. Went wrong / did not work: B12 adds nothing if levels are normal
48. Went wrong / did not work: Oral B12 works as well for levels
49. What went wrong: No good trial shows meaningful weight loss from the shot. Reviews say weight change on it comes from diet and exercise. Extra B12 does nothing measurable when levels are already normal.
50. Most reported: 1 mL IM weekly
51. Most reported: Sore site, nausea, headache
52. Most reported: Acne-like breakouts from B12
53. Most reported: Paired with GLP-1 programs

## What has been tested
54. Cellular energy: B12 injection for deficiency (human)
55. Organ function: Choline and fatty liver (animal)
56. Fat metabolism: MIC + B12 for weight loss (none)
57. No controlled trial of MIC + B12 as a product. B12 injections are approved for deficiency. Choline and methionine have lipotropic (liver fat) effects mostly in animals; a Cochrane review found oral 1,000 mcg B12 raises blood levels as well as injections.
58. What it helps: B12 injections clearly help people who are deficient. The MIC trio's fat-moving effects are shown in deficient animals, not in people on a normal diet. No trial measures weight loss from MIC + B12, and guides credit any loss to diet.

## Who shouldn't take it
59. People with homocystinuria or high homocysteine: Methionine is the direct precursor of homocysteine. (inferred)
60. People with Leber's hereditary optic neuropathy: Cyanocobalamin can speed optic nerve damage. (verified)
61. People with cobalt allergy: Cobalt sits at the centre of the B12 molecule; the label contraindicates use. (verified)
62. People with very low potassium: Treating deficiency can drop potassium fast. (verified)

## Synergies (paired with)
63. Lipo-C: Lipo-C shares choline and methionine with MIC + B12 and adds carnitine and B1 and B5 for fuel use. People combine or alternate them during diets. Neither has a weight loss trial, and the pair has not been tested together.
64. Vitamin B12: Standalone B12 just raises the B12 dose already in MIC + B12. It matters only if someone is deficient, where injections clearly work. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
65. L-Carnitine: L-carnitine adds the fat shuttle into mitochondria, which MIC + B12 lacks, with small weight loss effects in oral trials. Clinics pair them for fat loss support. The pair has not been tested together.
66. Semaglutide: Semaglutide does the main weight loss work through the GLP-1 receptor, with large human trials. MIC + B12 is added for liver fat handling and B12 while eating less. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.
67. Tirzepatide: Tirzepatide drives weight loss through GIP and GLP-1 receptors, with large human trials. MIC + B12 is added as a nutrient layer during the deficit. The pair has not been tested together.
68. Glutathione: Glutathione is the main cellular antioxidant and is used for skin brightening. Clinics bundle it with MIC + B12 in wellness injection menus. The two work on different systems, and the pairing has not been tested.
69. NAD+: NAD+ carries electrons for energy production and feeds repair enzymes, while MIC + B12 supports liver fat export and B12 status. Clinics pair them for energy. The pairing is user logic, not trial data.

## Where it comes from
70. The classic 'lipotropic' shot: methionine, inositol and choline (MIC) with vitamin B12. The common formula per mL is methionine 25, inositol 50, choline 50 mg and B12 1 mg (1,000 mcg). Grey-market 'LC' vials add carnitine and more B vitamins.
71. First studied for: A weight-loss clinic and med-spa staple in the US since at least the 2000s, sold as a weekly 'fat-burning' energy shot.
72. Name story: MIC is the initials of methionine, inositol and choline; B12 is the vitamin added. 'Lipo-B' is the same shot, named for lipotropic plus B12. 'LC' codes like LC120 and LC216 give total mg per mL. [S1] (reported)
73. 1930s: Choline found to stop fatty liver in animals: the 'lipotropic' idea
74. 1948: Vitamin B12 isolated
75. 2000s: MIC + B12 shots spread through US weight-loss clinics (inferred)
76. 2018: Cochrane: oral and injected B12 raise levels about equally
77. Today: Compounded MIC + B12; grey-market LC120 and LC216
