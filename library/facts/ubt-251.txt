# UBT-251
Page: https://baresignals.co/library/#/p/ubt-251
Checked against Library v68, exported 2026-10-25

## Identity
1. Also called: UBT251, triple G agonist, GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple agonist
2. Origin: Lab-designed
3. Legal and approval status: Investigational as of Oct 2026. China: phase 3 started Sep 2026 (obesity, 600 people; T2D UNIGUIDE-1 and -2). Global (Novo): phase 1b/2a in obesity, data 2027; global T2D phase 2 planned 2H 2026. Not on the WADA list by name.

## Summary
4. UBT-251 is a triple agonist of GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors from United Biotechnology in China, the same class as retatrutide. It is developed for obesity and type 2 diabetes, and Novo Nordisk licensed rights outside Greater China in March 2025. Chinese phase 2 trials cut weight up to 19.7% in 24 weeks. Data are short and limited to Chinese adults, and nobody outside trials uses it.
5. Punch line: China's retatrutide rival, a GLP-1, GIP, glucagon triple. Novo licensed it; up to 19.7% lost in 24 weeks.
6. Used for: Obesity, type 2 diabetes

## Roles (what it is used for, strongest first)
7. Fat metabolism: Up to 19.7% loss in 24 wk
8. Glucose & insulin: HbA1c down up to 2.16 points
9. Appetite & satiety: GLP-1/GIP hunger suppression
10. Heart & vessels: Lower blood pressure and lipids
11. Organ function (backed only by animal or user reports): Liver and kidney trials cleared

## How it works
12. Quiets hunger, slows stomach · GLP-1 receptor · role Appetite & satiety
   GLP-1 receptor activation in the brain's appetite centres cuts hunger, and in the gut it slows stomach emptying. People eat less and feel full longer. This is the core of the weight loss seen in the Chinese Phase 2 human trials.
13. Boosts insulin response, may ease nausea · GIP receptor · role Glucose & insulin
   GIP receptor activation on pancreatic beta cells adds to insulin release when glucose is high. GIP signalling is also thought to soften the nausea GLP-1 drugs cause, though that has not been shown for this drug specifically. In the diabetes trial, HbA1c fell more than on semaglutide.
14. Raises energy use and liver fat burning · Glucagon receptor · role Fat metabolism
   Glucagon receptor activation raises energy use and pushes the liver to burn its stored fat. This is why triple agonists are also aimed at fatty liver. Glucagon can raise blood sugar, which the GLP-1 and GIP arms offset.
15. Weekly dosing · Long-acting modification (undisclosed) · role Fat metabolism
   A long-acting chemical modification, not publicly disclosed, keeps the peptide in the body for about a week. That allows once-weekly injections. The exact design is unpublished.
16. Mechanism overview: UBT251 is a long-acting synthetic peptide that activates three receptors. GLP-1 receptor agonism cuts appetite, slows gastric emptying and raises glucose-dependent insulin. GIP receptor agonism adds insulin secretion and may soften GLP-1 nausea. Glucagon receptor agonism raises energy expenditure and drives fat burning in the liver, which is why triple agonists are also aimed at fatty liver; it can push glucose up, which the other two arms offset.

## Dosage (community reported unless tagged)
17. Typical: 2–6 mg · once weekly · cycle Trial only (24 wk) · route Sub-Q (verified)
18. Half-life: Long-acting, weekly (not published)
19. Dose note: Titration steps for the 2 and 6 mg arms not published. Outliyr lists no protocol.
20. Most people take: Trial only: 2, 4 or 6 mg weekly Sub-Q for 24 weeks; one 4 mg arm started at 0.5 mg and another at 1 mg. No community protocol exists.
21. Phase 2 obesity: 2–6 mg · 1× weekly · 24 wk; 0.5 or 1 mg start in 4 mg arms · Sub-Q · for Chinese adults with obesity [S1] [S2] (verified)
22. Phase 2 T2D: 2–6 mg · 1× weekly · 24 wk · Sub-Q · for Chinese adults with T2D [S3] (verified)

## What has been tested
34. Fat metabolism: Obesity, phase 1b and phase 2 (China) (human)
35. Glucose & insulin: Type 2 diabetes, phase 2 vs semaglutide (human)
36. Heart & vessels: Blood pressure and lipids (secondary) (human)
37. Phase 1b in China (36 people, 12 weeks), two Chinese phase 2 trials in obesity (205) and type 2 diabetes (211), then Chinese phase 3 from Sep 2026; Novo runs a global phase 1b/2a.
38. What it helps: In Chinese phase 2 trials it cut weight up to 19.7% in 24 weeks without a plateau, and in diabetes lowered HbA1c more than semaglutide 1 mg with twice the weight loss. Blood pressure, waist and lipids improved. Side effects were mostly GI. It is a retatrutide-class drug with far less data, and nobody outside trials is using it.
39. Trial: active · Phase 2 · obesity · Novo Nordisk. After 19.7 percent weight loss in a Chinese Phase 2, Novo is running a global Phase 1b/2a with data due 2027. (checked 2026-10-23) [S4] (reported)

## Who shouldn't take it
40. Personal or family history of MTC or MEN2: Causes thyroid C-cell tumours in rodents; boxed warning and contraindication on the label. (inferred)
41. Anyone booked for surgery, endoscopy or sedation: Food can stay in the stomach for days and be inhaled into the lungs under anaesthesia. (inferred)
42. People with arrhythmia or a fast resting pulse: The glucagon part of the drug pushes resting heart rate up. (inferred)

## Synergies (paired with)
43. Retatrutide: Retatrutide hits the same three receptors, GLP-1, GIP and glucagon, so the two are alternatives in the same class. Retatrutide has far more data. Stacking two triple agonists has no logic behind it and has not been tested.
44. Semaglutide: Semaglutide works only on GLP-1, while UBT-251 adds GIP and glucagon arms. It was the comparator in the diabetes trial, where UBT-251 lowered HbA1c more. They are alternatives, not a tested combination.

## Where it comes from
45. A triple agonist of GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors from The United Laboratories' subsidiary United Biotechnology in China, the same class as retatrutide. Novo Nordisk licensed rights outside Greater China in March 2025.
46. First studied for: Obesity and type 2 diabetes; China also cleared trials in fatty liver and chronic kidney disease.
47. Name story: UBT is United Biotechnology, the TUL subsidiary that discovered it, and 251 is its compound number. No INN has been published as of Oct 2026. [S5] (inferred)
48. 2024: Chinese phase 1b: 15.1% loss in 12 weeks at top dose
49. Mar 2025: Novo licenses ex-China rights ($200M upfront, up to $1.8B milestones)
50. Feb 2026: China phase 2 obesity: up to 19.7% in 24 weeks
51. Mar 2026: China phase 2 T2D: beats semaglutide 1 mg on HbA1c and weight
52. Sep 2026: First patients dosed in Chinese phase 3 (obesity and T2D)
53. Today: Global Novo data due 2027
