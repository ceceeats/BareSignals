Achilles

The thick tendon joining the calf to the heel. Covers mid-portion tendinopathy, insertional tendinopathy at the heel, partial tears and full rupture.

Researched 3 to 10 Nov 202640 sourcesVersion 1
Tissue repairPain & sensoryMuscle & performance

What it is. Pain and thickening in the tendon above the heel, worst in the morning and at the start of a run, or a sudden tear that feels like a kick in the calf. Adult tendon core barely renews itself, which is why it heals slowly.7

What studies found. Loading exercise is the treatment: 80% of people treated with exercise alone were fully recovered at 5 years.9 BPC-157 and TB-500 have only rat Achilles studies, most from one lab; the one independent rat study found TB-500 clearly helped and BPC-157 only leaned that way.23

What people report. 31 people on Reddit: 15 better, 12 partly better, 2 no change, 2 re-tears. Three say a surgeon or physio called their rupture recovery weeks ahead of schedule, the strongest kind of report here.33

Watch out. Ciprofloxacin-type antibiotics triple rupture risk for about 2 months, more with steroid tablets.6 BPC-157 and TB-500 are banned in sport at all times, prescription or not.35

1What the Achilles problem is

Four problems, one name

  • Mid-portion tendinopathy: pain and a thickened, sometimes knobbly tendon 2 to 7 cm above the heel. The most common type in runners. Stiff in the morning, sore at the start of a run, may warm up, then worse the next day.1,2
  • Insertional tendinopathy: pain right where the tendon meets the heel bone, often with calcium deposits. Here the tendon is also squeezed against the bone when the ankle bends up, so deep heel drops off a step usually make it worse. Its evidence is thin: 9 trials, very low confidence overall.3
  • Partial tear: some fibres torn inside an already worn tendon. Few good trials exist for this.
  • Full rupture: a sudden pop, often during push-off in a middle-aged recreational player, with a feeling of being kicked in the calf. Ruptures in Finland almost doubled from 1997 to 2019, rising most in older adults.5

Who gets it

About 1 in 20 recreational runners reported Achilles symptoms around one running event; having had it in the past year raised the odds about 6 times.4 Padel, pickleball and basketball players see it often; NBA players took about 11 months to return from a rupture.40

A scan is not the same as the pain

In most good studies, a tendon that looks better on ultrasound did not feel better, and the other way round. A scan "looking worse" does not mean the tendon is failing.14

Why tendons heal slowly

  • The core is old. Carbon-14 left in the air by 1950s and 60s bomb tests showed the Achilles core still carries the carbon of a person's first 17 years. Muscle samples were constantly renewed. (28 tendon samples, only 4 muscle.)7
  • The middle is short of blood. The mid-section, where most ruptures happen, has the poorest blood supply of the tendon.2
  • The outside listens to load. Training raised collagen building around the tendon 5 to 7 fold. Across 27 studies, how heavy the load was mattered more than whether the exercise was lowering, lifting or holding.8
  • Repair is scar first. The body lays down weaker type III collagen, then slowly swaps it for type I as fibres line up with load, over 12 months or more. Rat peptide studies measure only the first 2 to 4 weeks of this.23

What happens without peptides

Human

Most get better with loading. In 34 people treated with exercise alone, 80% were fully recovered at 5 years (65% symptom free, 15% had one new episode). 20% still had symptoms.9 Any peptide story has to beat that.

RCT

Rest is not needed. Runners who kept running and jumping, keeping pain within limits, ended the year the same as those who rested for 6 weeks.10

RCT

Ruptures heal without surgery too. In 554 people, function at a year was the same with or without surgery. Surgery cut re-tears from 6.2% to 0.6% but added nerve injuries.19

2What studies found

Read this first: no peptide has ever been tested on a human Achilles. Every BPC-157 and TB-500 Achilles result is in rats, and most come from the Zagreb group that discovered BPC-157. Collagen is the only peptide-like option with an Achilles trial in people.

BPC-157 · rats only

BPC-157

Library card
  1. 2003 Rats

    A cut Achilles rejoined stronger by day 14.

    Rats with a fully cut Achilles were given BPC-157 daily into the belly. By 2 weeks their tendons took more force to break, were stiffer, walked better and had a smaller gap. Zagreb group.21

  2. 2006 Rats

    Tendon back onto bone, and less steroid damage.

    With the Achilles cut off the heel bone, BPC-157 improved healing back onto the bone and lessened the harm done by a steroid. Zagreb group.22

  3. 2026 Rats

    The first outside lab: leaned better, not proven.

    A Turkish group repaired the Achilles in 32 rats. BPC-157 tissue looked numerically better and the tendon was a bit stronger, but neither reached significance. The authors still read it as positive and call the study exploratory; about 8 rats per group.23

What it does in the tendon: more new blood vessels, and tendon cells that crawl out and survive stress better, through a cell-anchor signal (FAK-paxillin). It did not make the cells multiply.22 The catch: it lasts under 30 minutes in rat and dog blood, and no study has tested injecting near the tendon against injecting anywhere.27

TB-500 · one rat study, positive

TB-500

Fragment card · Full Tβ4 card
  1. 2026 Rats

    Stronger tendons and better organised collagen.

    In the same Turkish study, TB-500 for 4 weeks gave clearly stronger repaired tendons and better tissue scores, with more mature, lined-up collagen. Adding BPC-157 on top added nothing.23

Know what's in the vial: TB-500 is a 7 amino acid piece (residues 17 to 23) of the 43 amino acid protein thymosin beta-4, the part that holds actin, which cells use to crawl. Some vials sold as "TB-500" hold the full protein instead. Full Tβ4 has human trials in eyes, heart and skin, none in tendon.31

Collagen · the one human Achilles trial

Collagen peptides

Library card
  1. 2019 RCT

    Better scores in the first 3 months, then placebo caught up.

    20 people with Achilles tendinopathy did a calf programme; those starting with collagen peptides gained 12.6 points on the 100-point Achilles score against 5.3 on placebo. After switching, the late group caught up. Small and industry-made.28

  2. 2017 8 men

    Gelatin plus vitamin C doubled a collagen-building marker.

    15 g of vitamin C rich gelatin an hour before 6 minutes of skipping doubled a blood marker of collagen building. A marker, not healing.29

BPC-157 in people, all of it

Review

3 studies, 30 people, none in tendon. A 2026 review found 16 knees, 12 bladders and 2 IV safety cases, all low quality, all from one clinician at a US clinic that sells it.24

Review

Surgeons: not for routine use. An October 2026 orthopaedic review says there is no validated dose, no human kinetics and no long-term safety data.25 A sports medicine review flags social media as amplifying placebo.26

Trial

The first real test starts 2027. A university trial of daily BPC-157 for 90 days after rotator cuff surgery, against salt water, starts in January. No Achilles trial is registered.32

Seen by a surgeon or physio

Reports where a clinician measured or judged the healing count most, because they compare against hundreds of other patients.

BPC-157

"About 3 weeks ahead." Surgeon at the 7-week check after repair: no lumps, good tension, 10 double heel raises and bent-knee calf raises with 15 kg. The poster says the cause can't be proven.33

Stack

"About 8 weeks ahead." Physio at 16 weeks after surgery, on BPC-157 + TB-500 + GHK-Cu + CJC/ipamorelin, with physio and diet.33

Stack

"Unusually fast." A jiu-jitsu athlete after surgery: shoes at week 8, normal walking and heel raises by month 3, training by month 5, competing at month 6. Also took low-dose growth hormone and did intensive physio.33

How to read these: shoes around week 8 and full weight in a boot by week 6 are close to what modern early-loading rupture plans aim for anyway.20 "Ahead of schedule" depends on whose schedule. Three reports is promising, not proof.

Outside Reddit

Across 14 runner, triathlon, hunting and sports forums, only one person gave a first-hand Achilles peptide account: a visible partial tear, pain mostly gone by 5 months on BPC-157 + TB-500 nasal spray then local injections, which he credited mostly to cycling and time off running. Local injections changed nothing he could notice; the nasal spray dried his sinuses. Two clinician pages read listed BPC-157 as not recommended.34

Collected 3 to 10 Nov 2026, one person per count, seller posts left out. Three of the better rupture reports came from threads with supplier comments, so they may be promotional.

4Pairings and the same problem elsewhere

Pairings · what is used together

  • BPC-157 + TB-500 (the Wolverine stack): by far the most common, in 24 of the 31 Reddit reports. In the one rat study that tested both, TB-500 did the work and adding BPC-157 added nothing.23,33 Rats
  • Plus GHK-Cu, CJC/ipamorelin or growth hormone: in the best rupture stories. Real growth hormone raised tendon collagen building in healthy young men,30 but the peptides that release it have no tendon data, and MK-677 carries heart failure and blood sugar warnings.31
  • Collagen with vitamin C before loading: the one pairing with human marker data.29 Human
  • Peptides with rehab: every good report had rehab alongside, so the two can't be separated.

The same problem, other places

  • Tennis elbow: the same tendon biology in an arm, with its own case page.
  • Knee: jumper's knee is the patellar tendon version; ACL and meniscus are on the knee page.
  • Tendons, the shared base: loading rules, injections and the peptide evidence for every tendon.
  • Muscle injury: calf strains sit next to the Achilles and heal much faster.
  • Plantar heel pain sits just below and is often confused with insertional Achilles pain.

5Every option, with numbers

What works best

  • Heavy slow calf raises 3 times a week did as well as twice-daily heel drops, and more people stuck with them (92% vs 78% of sessions). Both held at a year.11 RCT
  • Load enough, and supervised: unsupervised home heel drops were no better than sham upper-body exercise.13 What predicted recovery was doing the sets, reps and added weight, not just showing up. RCT
  • Heel lifts in the shoe beat heel drops by 9.6 points at 12 weeks, just under what patients notice.12 RCT
  • Shockwave eased Achilles pain more than placebo in pooled trials, a moderate effect.18 Meta
  • Isometric holds (about 45 seconds at 70% of max) are a gentle way to load a painful tendon. The famous "instant pain relief" came from 6 volleyball players and failed to repeat in Achilles.38,39

What did not beat placebo

  • PRP: 54.4 vs 53.4 points at 6 months in 240 people. Also no help after rupture.15 RCT
  • High-volume injection: no better than sham at 12 months in 192 people; the steroid version helped at 6 weeks and was worse by 26.16 RCT
  • Cortisone: one trial found 17.7 points more improvement at 6 months with no harm at 2 years;17 others warn of re-flares and rupture. Contested.

Rupture

A functional brace with early weight bearing matched a cast in 540 people.20 Surgery or not gave the same function at a year; surgery means fewer re-tears, more nerve and wound problems.19

6Warnings and fine print

Banned in sport

BPC-157 is named under S0 and TB-500 and thymosin beta-4 under S2 on the WADA list, banned in and out of competition. GH peptides (CJC-1295, ipamorelin, MK-677) are banned too. A doctor's prescription does not protect you: no exemption is possible for an unapproved drug.35

From 1 January 2027 the list names peptides as a group and warns that unnamed peptides can still be banned.36 A US triathlete got 4 years after a whistleblower, without a positive test.

When to get checked

  • A pop with sudden weakness: can't stand on tiptoe on that side. A rupture needs seeing within days.
  • Achilles pain while on, or just after, ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin or similar: rupture risk is about 3 times higher for 60 days, up to about 20 times with steroid tablets.6
  • Pain in both tendons with no clear cause, or a hot swollen joint.

Before you trust a vial or a number

  • Not approved anywhere. A draft US policy (comments closed 13 Nov 2026) would let licensed US pharmacies make BPC-157 and TB-500 under 10 conditions. That is not approval and does not cover research vials.37
  • No human dose exists for tendon healing. Rat studies used 10 µg/kg BPC-157 and 60 µg/kg TB-500 a day into the belly.23
  • Feeling better is not stronger. Pain relief can tempt people back to sprinting and jumping before the tendon has remodelled, which takes a year.
  • Tell your surgeon about anything you take around an operation.

Usual questions

Does BPC-157 heal an Achilles?

In rats with a cut tendon, mostly yes, from one lab; an outside lab saw only a lean in that direction. Never tested in a human tendon. Reddit users mostly report feeling better, nearly always alongside rehab.21,23,33

BPC-157 or TB-500 for the Achilles?

In the only rat study that compared them head to head, TB-500 gave the clearer result, and adding BPC-157 added nothing. One small study.23

Should I inject near the tendon?

No study has compared local with belly injections. BPC-157 is cleared from rat blood within 30 minutes and travels the whole body. One forum user noticed no change from local shots.27,34

Can I keep running?

Often yes: keeping pain within limits gave the same 1-year result as resting 6 weeks.10

How long will it take?

Trials run 12 weeks of loading with gains to a year; 80% were recovered at 5 years. NBA players returned about 11 months after a rupture.9,11,40

Is collagen worth taking?

It is the only peptide-type option with an Achilles trial in people: small, early gains in 20 people. Low risk, legal in sport.28

Will it show up in a drug test?

A urine test for BPC-157 exists, but nobody knows how long it stays detectable in people. Bans also come from investigations, not only tests.35

Do statins cause Achilles rupture?

The large UK study found no link, and statin users' tendons looked the same on ultrasound.6
