Acne

Blackheads, red spots, deep cysts, adult and hormonal acne, PCOS acne, and the lookalikes (fungal "acne", rosacea), plus the marks and scars spots leave behind. Every way people treat it, why each works, and what peptides have and have not shown.

Researched 3 to 20 Nov 202671 sourcesVersion 2 · preview
SkinInflammatory signallingEndocrine & growth

What it is. A spot is a pore going wrong in four ways at once: the oil gland pumps too much oil, dead skin cells plug the opening, a skin bacterium everyone carries thrives inside, and the immune system overreacts. Every treatment on this page works by switching off one or more of those four.43

What studies found. Dummy creams alone cut spots 35 to 60% in 12 weeks, so only the gap counts. Isotretinoin, the one drug that hits all four, gave the biggest gap of anything: about 58% more spots gone than placebo across 179 trials.5 No peptide has a proper acne trial; the closest is an EGF ointment that improved spots in 36 people while plain ointment on the other half of the face did not.53

What people report. Of about 42 people who put copper peptide (GHK-Cu) on their skin, half said red or brown marks and scars looked better, mostly while also using peels or acids; about 1 in 8 broke out, and nobody said it cleared active acne.68 GLP-1 users split both ways.57

Watch out. Itchy, same-size bumps in heat and humidity are often a yeast, not acne: acne creams fail and steroid creams feed it.37 Isotretinoin, spironolactone and most retinoids are not for pregnancy.67

If you take one thing from this page: match the treatment to the driver. Blackheads are a plug problem (retinoids), red spots add bacteria and inflammation (benzoyl peroxide, azelaic acid, antibiotics with BPO), jawline and period flares are an oil-and-hormone problem (spironolactone, the pill, clascoterone), and itchy same-size bumps are a yeast. No peptide switches off any driver as well as these.

1What acne is

Which one is it

Comedonal. Blackheads (open plugs) and whiteheads (closed), rough texture, little redness.

Forehead, nose, chin · plug is the main driver

Inflammatory. Red, tender bumps (papules) and pus-topped spots (pustules).

Cheeks, jaw · bacteria plus inflammation

Nodulocystic. Deep, painful lumps that last weeks. The highest scar risk: severe acne raised the odds of scarring about 9 times in Thai patients.41

Isotretinoin territory · see a doctor early

Adult or hormonal. Jawline, chin and neck, flaring before periods. Half of women in their 20s and a quarter in their 40s report acne (men 43% and 12%).4

Women 20s to 40s · oil driven by androgens

PCOS-linked. The hormonal pattern plus irregular cycles, extra body hair, scalp thinning, weight around the middle. Needs blood tests; androgens and insulin both push the oil gland. PCOS case

Spironolactone, the pill, metformin, GLP-1s

Fungal lookalike (Malassezia folliculitis). Small, same-size, itchy bumps on the forehead, hairline, chest and upper back, worse with heat, sweat, tight clothes, antibiotics or steroid creams, and ignoring acne creams. Itch made it 7.4 times more likely, and upper-back spots about 9 times.37 Big for Bali: hot, humid weather and occlusion are named risk factors.70

Lookalike · a skin smear confirms it · antifungalsMalassezia is a yeast that feeds on skin oil in sweat-soaked follicles; antibiotics clear its bacterial competitors and steroid creams lower local defences.

Rosacea lookalike. Flushing, a red background and bumps with no blackheads, usually over 30; heat, spice and alcohol set it off. Its redness is driven partly by fragments of the skin's own peptide LL-37.50

Lookalike · different treatments

Marks vs scars. Flat brown marks (PIH) and red or pink marks (PIE) are not scars and fade over months. Scars change the texture: ice-pick, boxcar and rolling dents, or raised scars. Marks and scars

Brown marks: up to 85% of darker skin types

Drug-induced acne (steroids, testosterone, some growth hormone and peptide use) tends to show as all-same-stage bumps on the chest and back.

Southeast Asia note

  • Heat flares it. In tropical Delhi, 40.4% of 171 acne patients worsened in summer against 6.4% in winter, and flares tracked monthly heat and humidity.40
  • Think fungal first if it itches. 28.8% of 320 people diagnosed with acne in a Bangkok clinic also had fungal folliculitis.37
  • Brown marks last. 58.2% of 324 acne patients across 7 Asian countries had brown marks; in 22.3% they lasted 5 years or more. Over half of Jakarta women aged 20 to 30 had them.39
  • Scars are common. 52.8% of young Chinese adults with acne in Singapore and Malaysia, and 61.3% of Thai acne patients, had scars; picking raised the risk.41
  • Rules in Indonesia: the drug regulator treats tretinoin, hydroquinone and steroid skincare as prescription items.71 No Bali or Indonesia acne prevalence study was found.

Pills

Dummy pills work too. Placebo pills cut inflamed spots 26.4% at 3 months and 34.2% at 6 months.2

Teens

Most teen acne fades. Over 80% of teenagers get acne; it carries on past 25 in 3% of men and 12% of women seen in clinic.3 A typical age it fades was not found in a long-term study.

Adults

Ask adults and it's far higher. In 1,013 adults asked about any spots: 50.9% of women vs 42.5% of men in their 20s, 26.3% vs 12.0% in their 40s. The gap is definitions: acne needing care vs any spots.4

Stress

Exams made it worse in 22 students, tracking how stressed they felt (r = 0.61), even after sleep and diet.69

How to read the numbers below: "+44%" from the 179-trial analysis means 44% more of the spots disappeared than on placebo. "Clear or almost clear" is the stricter goal trials use; 20 to 30% of people on a good cream reach it, against 7 to 25% on dummy.5

3Peptides in depth

Read this first: no peptide has a controlled trial with acne spot counts as the goal. The nearest thing is EGF, a growth-factor peptide sold in serums, in one 36-person split-face trial. Two antimicrobial peptides that reached big skin trials lost to their own plain gel. The real peptide story here is marks and scars, and a few peptides that may make acne worse.

GHK-Cu · marks and scars, not spots

GHK-Cu (copper peptide)

Library card · + KPV
Human skin · scarsAcne: none

Spots leave red marks, brown marks and dents long after they heal. GHK-Cu works like a reset message telling older or damaged skin to rebuild the way younger skin does, switching on both collagen building and the clean-up of old collagen.47 In 30 Asian women with acne scars, a gel containing a copper peptide cut a dermatologist's scar score by 27% in 45 days.45 That study had no comparison group, and no trial has tested GHK-Cu on active acne.

How it may help

  • Rebuilds and clears collagen at once, the remodelling a dented scar needs. Human skin47
  • Calms scar-forming cells: lowers TGF-β1 in scar fibroblasts in the lab. Cells47
  • Damps inflammation in lab models; never tested on an acne spot. Cells47
  1. 1998 20 people

    The "beats retinol" collagen study is real, but small.

    Healthy thigh skin, 1 month, biopsies. People with any rise in new collagen: copper peptide 7 of 10, vitamin C 5 of 10, melatonin 5 of 10, tretinoin 4 of 10. Copper and tretinoin were in different groups, never tested on the same person, and nobody had acne. Often misquoted online as "12 weeks, 70% more collagen".44

  2. 2025 30 women

    Scar score down 27% in 45 days.

    A gel of copper tripeptide-1 plus L-carnitine, hesperidin and hyaluronic acid, twice daily: scar score 6.87 to 5.03, no side effects. Open label, no control, co-authors from the product maker, and four actives at once, so the copper peptide's share is unknown.45

  3. 2026 Injected

    Injected: no human data, early harm reports.

    A cosmetic dermatologist calls the cream science "very solid", about vitamin C level, and advises against injecting. A longevity clinic reported self-injectors with injection-site infections and raised liver enzymes and copper (cause unconfirmed).46

EGF · the only controlled peptide result

EGF (sh-Oligopeptide-1)

No Library card yet
Human outcome · 36 people

An inflamed spot is a small wound, and EGF is the signal skin uses to close wounds. It is the growth-factor peptide sold in serums as sh-Oligopeptide-1. In 36 people with mild to moderate acne, EGF ointment on one half of the face and plain ointment on the other: spots and scars fell on the EGF side from week 4 but not on the plain side, and biopsies showed less of the inflammation signals NF-κB, IL-1α and IL-8, with more collagen.53 One small, single-blind trial; the abstract gives no head-to-head figure between the two sides.

Verdict: worth watching, not proven. Closest any peptide gets to an acne result.

LL-37 and omiganan · lost to plain gel

Antimicrobial peptides

LL-37 card
Negative · 2 big skin trials

The obvious idea: a peptide that kills bacteria should kill acne bacteria. But acne skin already makes extra LL-37, the body's own antimicrobial peptide, and in rosacea its broken fragments cause the redness.43,50 Fat-cell precursors around the pore make their own version when C. acnes is present, and retinoids switch that off.43

  1. Omiganan 263 people

    Rosacea phase 3: equal to plain gel.

    12 weeks: inflamed bumps fell 19.8 on omiganan vs 20.9 on plain gel; 24.6% vs 25.6% improved two grades. Its 4 acne trials (2000 to 2016, about 977 people) never posted results.51

  2. LL-37 148 people

    Leg ulcers: no healing benefit.

    LL-37 cream in a phase 2b trial did not beat placebo overall; a hint in large ulcers only.50

  3. GDP-20 60 people

    On top of isotretinoin, unclear.

    Granulysin-derived peptide gel added to low-dose isotretinoin: blackheads 21.4 to 4.4, red spots 6.1 to 1.4, but the abstract gives no comparison with isotretinoin alone and does not say it was randomised.52

Verdict: adding LL-37 is not supported and could inflame skin prone to rosacea. Zero first-hand reports found.

KPV, BPC-157 · lab and rats only

KPV and BPC-157

KPV · BPC-157 · GLOW · KLOW
KPV · CellsBPC-157 · Animal

Red spots are an alarm that keeps ringing. KPV, the last three amino acids of the tanning hormone α-MSH, acts like the off switch for that alarm without the tan, damping NF-κB.48 In human skin cells and a 3D skin model it cut IL-1β and cell death from pollution damage. No human skin study exists, cream or injected.48

BPC-157: rat wounds, deep burns and diabetic ulcers healed faster; no human skin, scar or acne data, and its one human safety study never posted results.49 GLOW/KLOW blends (GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500, ± KPV): one 8-week blog diary, still frequent breakouts.68

Verdict: plausible on paper, untested on acne. Zero non-seller first-hand reports for either.

GLP-1 drugs · drug or weight loss?

GLP-1 drugs

Semaglutide · Tirzepatide · Retatrutide · GLP-1s case
Human · weak, mixed

Insulin and IGF-1 push the oil gland, so drugs that lower insulin should calm it. But fast fat loss also shifts sex hormones, which may unmask androgens. GLP-1 drugs have no acne trial, and the data point both ways.59

  1. 2026 41 people

    17 of 41 with acne felt better.

    A survey designed with dermatologists, 1,226 GLP-1 users: acne better in 41% (could be 27 to 57%), hidradenitis 87%, dark velvety skin patches 75%. Self-report, no control.57

  2. 2025 Records

    More acne diagnoses in obese women, not men.

    A US database study found more new acne diagnoses in non-diabetic obese women on GLP-1s. Its numbers sit behind a bot check and are unread here; the authors note the individual rise was small.58

  3. 2025 34 RCTs

    84,243 people: no skin signal except hair loss.

    Hair loss was 3.4 times more likely (about 6 vs 1 case per 1,000 people a year); acne was not named. A 45-paper review found no acne signal and blamed weight-loss hormone shifts for new acne.59

  4. 2025 58 women

    PCOS: 1 in 3 with acne improved

    on semaglutide over about a year, with 11.4% weight loss.62 In hidradenitis, 16 of 20 on tirzepatide hit the response target at 24 weeks (open label).61

Drug or weight loss?

DrugWeightBothUnknown

Review authors lean to weight; for acne itself it is still unknown. A 2-year semaglutide cohort that reported improvement is left out here: its tables grade every person for both acne and hidradenitis, most were diabetic, and its side-effect totals do not add up.60

GH peptides and IGF-1 · the one that can cause it

GH peptides, IGF-1

MK-677 · Ipamorelin · CJC-1295 · Tesamorelin · IGF-1 LR3
IGF-1 · human harm signalGH peptides · theory

IGF-1, the growth factor that growth hormone raises, pushes the same oil-gland switch as androgens. The cleanest human clue: people born unable to make IGF-1 almost never get acne (1 of 13), and when women were given IGF-1 at high doses, 3 of 6 got acne that cleared when the dose was cut.42

GH-releasing peptides: no trial lists acne. MK-677 for 2 years in older adults: more appetite (67% vs 36%), swelling and muscle pain, not acne; tesamorelin's label: rash 4% vs 2%, no acne; CJC-1295 raised IGF-1 1.5 to 3 times with no skin events in the abstract.56 Those trials enrolled older or ill adults, whose oil glands are far quieter than a 25-year-old's.

What people report · 4 people

31

The reverse of what the biology predicts, but tiny and confounded (tretinoin, zinc, another compound, one 16-year-old).68

Verdict: expect more oil, especially stacked with testosterone. Blood sugar rises on MK-677.

Melanotan · moles, not spots

Melanotan, afamelanotide

Melanotan II · Melanotan I · Afamelanotide
3-person pilotAcne harm: claimed, no rate

Melanotan II also touches a receptor on oil glands (MC5R) that raises oil, so acne is often listed as a side effect, but no study gives a rate. The licensed cousin afamelanotide reduced spots in all 3 people with acne in a small pilot, and its FDA label does not list acne.54

Real skin risks: new or changing moles 4% vs 2% and skin darkening 4% vs 0% on the label in 6 months; melanoma from existing moles in case reports. About 26 user reviews never mention acne; moles, freckles and mouth pigment are the complaints.54,55,68

What they said

  • GHK-Cu, marks: post-acne redness fading, brown marks lighter, rolling scars "slowly filling" over a year with needling. One said red marks were 40% down. Most were also using peels, glycolic, lactic or BHA.68
  • GHK-Cu, breakouts: painful cyst-like pimples; daily chin breakouts; breakouts at night with retinol that stopped when the copper moved to mornings; open pores then rough skin after a month.68
  • GLP-1: "not even whiteheads" on tirzepatide for two women; jawline acne after moving to a higher dose for another. Rosacea cleared after 10 years for one; psoriasis patches gone for several.68
  • MK-677: mild acne cleared over 8 months for one user, alongside zinc, berberine and another compound, while fasting sugar rose from 80 to 95.68

Seen by a doctor

  • Copper peptide blend: dermatologist-graded scar score down 27% in 30 women, no control.45
  • Tirzepatide: investigators graded 16 of 20 hidradenitis patients as responders.61

5Pairings and related

Pairings · what is used together

  • Retinoid + benzoyl peroxide: the base pair; together they beat each alone from week 1.6 RCT
  • Any antibiotic + benzoyl peroxide: always.Antibiotics kill only the bacteria they can reach, leaving the resistant ones to multiply; benzoyl peroxide kills by oxidation, which bacteria cannot adapt to. 22% of acne bacteria already resist clindamycin and 29% erythromycin; benzoyl peroxide stops resistance.11
  • Isotretinoin before laser for scars: in Thai patients, brown marks after laser were 24% on isotretinoin vs 42% off it.66
  • GHK-Cu + peels, acids or needling: where nearly all good marks reports come from. GHK-Cu + retinol at the same time links with breakouts; people split them morning and night.68
  • GLP-1 + spironolactone for PCOS acne. Starting a GLP-1 and a new supplement in the same fortnight makes it impossible to tell which caused a flare.
  • Collagen or "hair, skin, nails" powders: check for biotin before blaming the collagen or the GLP-1.68

The same problem, other places

  • PCOS: hormonal acne with cycle and insulin problems; spironolactone, the pill, metformin and GLP-1s.
  • Hair loss (full case page): the same androgens shrink scalp follicles while they enlarge oil glands.Androgens act on the same hair-follicle unit: in the scalp they shorten hair growth; in the oil gland next to it they drive oil.
  • Skin, Hair & Nails: the Goal page for firmer, smoother, even-toned skin, with where each compound acts and how strong the proof is.
  • Hidradenitis ("acne inversa"): a different disease with plugged follicles in skin folds; GLP-1 data are strongest here.61
  • Rosacea: looks like acne, driven partly by LL-37 fragments.50
  • GLP-1s: every effect beyond weight loss.
  • Acne research notes: every study and number with its tag.

6Marks and scars

Marks fade · protect first

Up to 85% of people with darker skin get brown marks after inflamed spots, and they can bother people more than the acne. Women, darker skin, severe acne, sun and picking raise the risk.39

  • Sunscreen daily: called non-negotiable for brown marks.39
  • Azelaic acid: treats spots and fades brown marks at once; usable in pregnancy.14
  • Retinoids: tretinoin helped brown marks in at-risk patients.7
  • Tranexamic acid: micro-injections and low-power laser both lightened post-acne marks in a 25-person split-face trial; laser a little better.66
  • Copper peptide: the largest community signal for red and brown marks, always alongside acids or peels.68
  • Don't pick: the most changeable risk factor in Asian studies.39

Scars · combinations win

  • Fractional laser (CO2, erbium) often beat plain microneedling across 27 studies (1,137 people); more brown-mark risk in darker skin.66
  • RF microneedling: close to laser with less pigment risk; favoured for darker skin.66
  • Subcision frees tethered rolling scars; with CO2 laser it was among the most consistent pairs in 56 trials.66
  • TCA CROSS for ice-pick scars: over 70% better in 8 of 10, uncontrolled.66
  • Insulin or PRP with needling: insulin gave more "marked" responses, low certainty.65

A second 2026 network analysis found no treatment reliably ranks first. Several work; none is clearly best.66

Options, in order of evidence

  1. Mild, blackheads or a few red spots: adapalene (or another retinoid) plus benzoyl peroxide; azelaic acid if marks, sensitive skin or pregnancy. Judge at 12 weeks.5,6 RCT
  2. Moderate: a double or triple combination gel; add doxycycline for 3 to 4 months, always with benzoyl peroxide and a retinoid.17,18 RCT
  3. Adult women, hormonal pattern: spironolactone or the combined pill, judged at 6 months; clascoterone cream (also for men).21,22,16 RCT
  4. Severe, scarring, or two failed rounds: isotretinoin; low dose gives similar 24-week results with fewer side effects.24,25 Meta
  5. PCOS or insulin resistance: add metformin or address weight and insulin, including GLP-1s where prescribed.23,62
  6. Add-ons with some data: zinc, omega-3, a low-glycaemic diet for 10 to 12 weeks; a 12-week dairy break as a personal test.30,32,34
  7. Itchy, uniform bumps: get a smear and treat the yeast instead.38
  8. Peptides, honestly: EGF serum as an experiment; topical GHK-Cu for marks alongside sunscreen and acids. None replaces a retinoid or benzoyl peroxide.53,68

Usual questions

Does GHK-Cu clear acne?

No trial says so. About half of 42 users said marks, redness or scars looked better, usually with peels or acids; about 1 in 8 broke out; nobody said it cleared active acne.68

Is there any peptide cream with a real acne result?

One: EGF ointment, 36 people, against plain ointment on the other half of the face, single blind: spots and scars fell on the EGF side and not on the plain side over 12 weeks.53

Is the "copper peptide beats retinol" study real?

Yes, but it was 1 month, 20 people, thigh skin, counting people with any collagen rise (7 of 10 vs 4 of 10), and copper and tretinoin were never tested on the same person.44

Is injected GHK-Cu better than cream?

There is no human data for injecting it. Dermatologists say cream is the evidence-based route, and a clinic has seen infections and raised liver enzymes and copper in self-injectors.46

Will semaglutide or tirzepatide fix my acne?

Unclear. 4 in 10 users with acne in one survey felt better; a database found more acne diagnoses in obese women; large trial pools show no acne signal; forums split 2 better, 3 worse. Rosacea, psoriasis and hidradenitis improve more consistently.57,58,59

Do GH peptides cause acne?

IGF-1 itself does at high doses (3 of 6 women, cleared on cutting the dose). GH-peptide trials list no acne, but they studied older adults.42,56

Does melanotan cause acne?

Often claimed, never measured: no study gives a rate, the licensed cousin's label doesn't list it, and about 26 user reviews never mention it. Moles are the real skin risk.54,55

Can LL-37 or KPV kill or calm my acne?

LL-37's best skin trial (148 ulcer patients) showed no benefit, and its fragments drive rosacea. KPV has only skin-cell data. Neither has a single non-seller user report.48,50

Does milk cause acne?

Linked, not proven: a glass a day raised the odds about 40% in 14 observational studies, with skim at least as strong as whole. No trial of cutting dairy exists.35

Does whey protein cause acne?

Less than assumed. A 6-month blinded trial of 30 g whey daily in 49 young Thai men with acne found no worsening, though case reports describe chest and back flares that cleared on stopping.36

Does sugar or white bread matter?

Two small trials found low-glycaemic diets cut spots and shrank oil glands in 10 to 12 weeks; pooled results were mixed. A modifier, not a cause.34

Could my "acne" be fungal?

If the bumps itch, look the same size and sit on the forehead, hairline, chest or back, yes: 28.8% of acne patients in a Bangkok clinic had it. It needs an antifungal.37,38

Is a "purge" real?

Purging is a retinoid idea: faster skin turnover brings plugs up in the first weeks. GHK-Cu does not clearly speed turnover, so new breakouts in new places are more likely irritation or clogging.

Does isotretinoin cure acne for good?

For most. In nearly 20,000 US patients, 22.5% needed acne treatment again and 8.2% a second course; women relapsed more, and a higher total dose lowered relapse.24

What works best for acne scars?

Combinations: laser with subcision or PRP; microneedling is gentler for darker skin. No single treatment is proven best.66

Warnings

  • Isotretinoin and pregnancy: birth defects in 35% or more of exposed pregnancies. Two forms of contraception from 1 month before to 1 month after, with pregnancy tests; wait a month after the last dose before trying to conceive.67
  • Spironolactone: not for pregnancy (it can affect a male baby's development); headaches 20% vs 12%.22 Tazarotene, clascoterone and tetracycline antibiotics are also no in pregnancy.8,16
  • Antibiotics: never clindamycin or erythromycin alone on skin, and keep pills to 3 to 4 months, always with benzoyl peroxide. Doxycycline makes skin burn faster in tropical sun. Minocycline can cause lupus and permanent blue-grey skin.11,19
  • Steroid creams on "acne": can turn a fungal infection into a worse one.70
  • Melanotan: new or changing moles, melanoma reports, priapism, brain swelling (PRES), muscle breakdown; unlicensed tanning products vary in dose and purity. Get moles checked twice a year.54,55
  • Unregulated injectables: research-labelled peptides are not made for people; purity, dose and sterility are unchecked, and copper peptides can arrive with free copper. Injecting adds infection and lump risk and has no human acne or scar data for any peptide.46
  • GH peptides: raise blood sugar; MK-677 had a heart-failure signal in one trial. Biotin in skin and hair powders is a common suspected breakout trigger.56,68
  • See a doctor for deep painful lumps, scarring, sudden acne in an adult woman with extra hair or missed periods, or acne after starting testosterone or steroids.
