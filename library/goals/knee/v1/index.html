Knee

ACL tear, meniscus tear, jumper's knee (patellar tendon), MCL sprain, knee osteoarthritis and recovery after knee surgery. One page because people use the same peptides for all of them.

Researched 3 to 10 Nov 202642 sourcesVersion 1
Tissue repairBone & cartilagePain & sensory

What it is. Six different knee problems that heal very differently. Ligament outside the joint (MCL) heals by itself in weeks; the ACL inside the joint rarely bridges, because joint fluid dissolves the healing clot.9

What studies found. About half of young adults with a torn ACL who started with rehab never needed surgery, with the same results at 11 years.1 Collagen peptides have the best human knee data of anything called a peptide, from 35 trials.24 BPC-157's only human knee data is one phone survey with no comparison group.20

What people report. Outside Reddit, 10 of 12 people on BPC-157 or TB-500 said their knee felt better, and all 10 collagen users did. No single diagnosis reaches 5 people, and no doctor or scan confirmed a peptide effect.38

Watch out. Over half of people get relief from a salt-water knee injection in arthritis trials, so "it worked" needs a comparison.16 A knee that locks, gives way or swells fast after a pop needs a doctor.

1What the knee problem is

Which one is it

  • ACL tear: a pop on landing, cutting or a sudden stop, fast swelling within hours, and a knee that gives way when turning. Many can walk within days, which fools people into thinking it is minor. In jiu-jitsu, matches with heel hooks had about 12 times the knee injury rate.30
  • Meniscus tear: pain on the inside or outside joint line, swelling the next day, catching. True locking, where the knee gets stuck and won't straighten, is the one sign that changes the plan toward surgery.5
  • Jumper's knee: pain just below the kneecap on jumping, landing, squats and stairs. 32% of elite basketball players and 45% of volleyball players had it.11 Over 15 years, half of affected athletes quit their sport because of it.35
  • MCL sprain: pain on the inside of the knee after a blow or a knee pushed inward: tackles, guard passes, padel lunges. Grade III often comes with an ACL tear; elite players with both returned at about 12 months.12,34
  • Osteoarthritis: wear and remodelling of cartilage and bone; pain on loading, short morning stiffness, swelling. 44% of young adults had it on X-ray 11 years after an ACL tear, and a rebuilt ACL did not clearly prevent it.1,14 Losing meniscus is the most consistent risk.13
  • After surgery: a rebuilt ACL takes many months to turn into ligament-like tissue; most are cleared for sport at 9 to 12 months.4 Surgery itself keeps joint inflammation higher for months than rehab alone.15

The MRI trap

In 991 people over 50, meniscus tears showed on MRI in 19% to 56%, and 61% of those with a tear had no knee pain that month. After 40, a tear on a scan is often not the cause of the pain.7

Why the ACL doesn't knit

  • No bridge ever forms. 23 torn human ACLs examined under the microscope went through the usual stages of repair, but at no point did tissue join the two torn ends; a lining grew over each stump instead.9
  • The clot gets dissolved. Fluid in an injured knee carries more clot-dissolving plasmin than the blood does, made inside the joint.9
  • Give it a scaffold and it can heal. A protein sponge stitched between the torn ends (BEAR) gave the same 2-year results as reconstruction in 100 teens, but more needed a second operation, 14% vs 6%.10
  • Cartilage has no blood vessels and few cells, so adult damage is mostly not replaced.

What happens without peptides

RCT

Torn ACL, rehab first. 121 young adults: rehab first or early surgery scored the same at 2, 5 and 11 years (a 1.6-point gap on a 100-point scale; about 9 is a change people notice). 52% of the rehab group were eventually reconstructed, so about half never were.1

RCT

Meniscus tear, physio first. In 321 people aged 45 to 70, 16 physio sessions did as well as keyhole surgery over 2 years; 29% later chose surgery.5 Trimming a worn meniscus equalled fake surgery,6 and in 18 to 40 year olds surgery was not clearly better than exercise.36

Meta

Arthritis and placebo. Across 73 trials, salt-water knee injections gave real relief at 1 to 6 months, with more than half responding.16 MCL sprains: 70 to 98% do well with exercise, back in 3 to 12 weeks.12

What this means for stories: a knee that "felt better within weeks" is the expected course for MCL sprains, meniscus flares and arthritis flares. Only a comparison group can separate a peptide from that.

2What studies found

Read this first: no research peptide has a controlled knee trial. The real human knee data belongs to collagen peptides and GLP-1 drugs. BPC-157 has one uncontrolled phone survey; everything else is rats.

BPC-157 · one survey, rats

BPC-157

Library card
  1. 2021 16 people

    14 of 16 said knee pain improved.

    A Florida hormone clinic injected 17 knees (one 4 mg shot in most; 4 people also got thymosin beta-4) and phoned 16 of them 6 to 12 months later. 14 said pain was clearly better, and 9 said relief lasted 6 months or more. Mixed causes, no scores, no scans, no comparison group, and the clinic sells it.20

  2. 2010 Rats

    A cut MCL healed better, by any route.

    Rats with a cut MCL healed with better function, strength and tissue over 90 days whether BPC-157 was injected, rubbed on as cream or put in drinking water. Zagreb group.21

  3. 2018 Rats

    Less arthritis after a wrecked knee.

    Rats with cut ACL and MCL and a removed meniscus walked better with less arthritis at 4 and 8 weeks; starting late helped less. 5 rats per group, a thesis, not a published paper.21

Missing: no rat or human study of BPC-157 in an ACL, meniscus or patellar tendon. No knee trial is registered.22

TB-500 and GHK-Cu · thin

TB-500, GHK-Cu

TB-500 · Tβ4 · GHK-Cu
  1. 2021 4 people

    TB-500: the 4 people in the survey above.

    3 of 4 given BPC-157 plus thymosin beta-4 improved. No cartilage, ACL or meniscus study of TB-500 exists, even in animals.20

  2. 2015 72 rats

    GHK-Cu: a tighter rebuilt ACL, gone when shots stopped.

    Weekly GHK-Cu shots into the knee made a rebuilt rat ACL less loose and stiffer at 6 weeks. By 12 weeks, after stopping, there was no difference, and pull-out strength and walking never differed.23

Collagen · the best human peptide data

Collagen peptides

Library card
  1. 2024 35 trials

    Small to moderate pain relief in knee arthritis.

    35 randomised trials, 3,165 people: pain and function improved more than control, with no more side effects. Moderate to high certainty.24

  2. 2021 180 people

    Young active knees hurt a bit less.

    5 g a day for 12 weeks in 18 to 30 year olds with activity knee pain: pain fell 21.9 vs 15.6 points out of 100, and doctors' scores agreed. About 6 points more than placebo is small. An earlier 139-athlete trial found the same. Industry-linked.25

GLP-1 drugs · big pain drops, mostly weight

GLP-1 drugs

Semaglutide · Retatrutide · GLP-1s case
  1. 2024 407 people

    Semaglutide: knee pain down 41.7 vs 27.5 points.

    In knee arthritis with obesity, 68 weeks of semaglutide cut weight 13.7% and pain 41.7 points out of 100, against 27.5 on placebo. Mostly through weight loss.26

  2. 2026 574 people

    Retatrutide: published, smaller than the press release.

    In the knee group of the main retatrutide trial (NEJM, Sept 2026), pain fell 3.6 points out of 10 on the top dose against 1.9 on placebo, with about 25% weight loss. The earlier company release from a separate knee trial claimed a 75.8% drop; that one is still not peer reviewed, and 12 to 18% stopped for side effects.27

Drug or weight? A 2026 review found liraglutide gave extra weight loss without extra knee relief, so these are not proven painkillers apart from the weight. A tirzepatide trial testing whether people avoid knee replacement reports in 2027.28

What they said

  • Jumper's knee: painless bodyweight squats and no night pain after a month of daily BPC-157 near the tendon, alongside eccentric squats, massage and a week off; he says he can't credit it alone. Another man's tendon pain eased each time he used it over 8 years, except with one batch from a different source.38
  • Meniscus flare: settled in about 3 weeks on oral BPC-157, against about 3 months for the other knee earlier, while also on curcumin and glucosamine.38
  • Arthritis: TB-500 alone "really helped knee pain".38
  • No change: 2 months of BPC-157 into the knee, where a steroid shot later helped more; and a knee after meniscus surgery that improved later with rest.38
  • Collagen: mostly women on Mumsnet with "creaky" or painful knees, taking it for 1 to 2 years: "pain has gone", "90% reduced", and one whose knee twinged again when she ran out.39

Seen by a doctor

Two surgeon remarks: a man with both knees replaced on BPC-157 + TB-500, whose surgeon and physio said he healed faster than most, and a broken knee on collagen with a surgeon noting the speed. Both were also doing physio and other care, and neither credited the supplement alone.38 Physios interviewed in 2026 said some patients feel better and they credit part of it to rehab time and placebo; one physio says relief often fades.40

4Pairings and the same problem elsewhere

Pairings · what is used together

  • BPC-157 + TB-500: the most common knee pairing. In the one survey, 3 of 4 on both improved against 11 of 12 on BPC-157 alone: too few to compare.20 16 people
  • Peptide with a cortisone, PRP or hyaluronic shot at the same visit is offered by clinics, with no data. Cortisone every 3 months for 2 years thinned cartilage with no extra relief.19
  • Collagen with vitamin C before loading: doubled a collagen-building marker in a small study.29 Human
  • GLP-1 with exercise: both arms of the semaglutide trial got activity advice, so exercise is part of every result.26
  • Growth hormone after ACL surgery: real GH in 19 men raised thigh strength 29% over placebo in a pilot, with no change in patient scores. The peptides that release GH have no knee data.32 Pilot

The same problem, other places

  • Achilles: the same tendon biology lower down, with its own case.
  • Tennis elbow: tendon pain in the arm.
  • Tendons, the shared base: jumper's knee loading plans, shockwave and cortisone in full.
  • Muscle injury and Shoulder: where the first BPC-157 trials are running.
  • GLP-1s: every effect beyond weight loss, knee pain included.

5Every option, with numbers

What works best

  • Supervised, progressive rehab for every problem here; give it 12 weeks before judging; it is first line in every guideline.37 Rehab first is a trial-backed choice for a torn ACL and a meniscus tear without locking.1,5 RCT
  • After ACL surgery, wait and test. Re-injury risk fell about 51% for each month return was delayed, up to 9 months; equal thigh strength on both legs was the other clear protector.3 About 1 in 4 under-25s who return to sport tear an ACL again.4
  • Weight loss for arthritis, including GLP-1 drugs where prescribed.26 RCT
  • Collagen peptides, 5 to 10 g a day in trials: low risk, small benefit.24 Meta
  • Surgery when needed: a locked knee, repeated giving way despite rehab, a grade III MCL that is unstable, or no progress after 3 to 6 months.2

What did not beat placebo

  • Hyaluronic acid shots: 169 trials; in the big ones, 2 points out of 100 better than placebo, with more serious side effects.17 Meta
  • PRP for arthritis: same pain and cartilage as salt water in 288 people.18 RCT
  • Trimming a worn meniscus: same as fake surgery.6 RCT
  • Bracing a torn ACL to heal it: unproven. One comparison found 70% of braced knees still unstable at 2 years vs 2.5% after surgery; its developers dispute the method, and a 180-person trial reports around 2029.8

6Warnings and fine print

See a doctor

  • The knee is locked and can't straighten.
  • Fast swelling within hours of a pop, or the knee gives way.
  • A hot, red, swollen knee with fever, especially after any injection: joint infection is an emergency.
  • Calf swelling after knee surgery or weeks in a brace (a clot).

Banned in sport

BPC-157 (S0), TB-500 and thymosin beta-4 (S2) and GH peptides are banned at all times; from 2027 peptides are named as a group. Collagen is food, and semaglutide and tirzepatide are only monitored.41 The BPC-157 hamstring trial excluded drug-tested athletes for this reason.

Before you trust a vial or a number

  • Injecting into a joint carries infection risk even with medical products; unregulated vials add sterility and identity risk. Injection into a joint is also not a route in the draft US pharmacy policy.42
  • Pain relief is not strength. A peptide that dulls pain can tempt a return to pivoting sport before strength tests are passed, the biggest re-injury risk.3
  • Around surgery: tell the surgeon and anaesthetist. GLP-1 drugs slow the stomach, which matters under anaesthesia; no peptide has surgical data.
  • Pentosan, sometimes grouped with peptides, is linked to eye damage after years of daily use for bladder pain; short knee courses have not been studied for this.31 Its injected knee trial measured joint-fluid markers, not pain.33
  • MK-677 has heart failure and blood sugar warnings.22

Usual questions

Do I need surgery for a torn ACL?

Not necessarily. In two trials about half of people who started with rehab never needed surgery, with the same results at 2, 5 and 11 years. Younger, very active people with a knee that keeps giving way more often end up rebuilt.1,2

Does BPC-157 heal an ACL or meniscus?

Unknown. It has never been studied in either, even in rats. The one human knee study was a phone survey with no comparison group, and in arthritis trials more than half respond to salt water.20,16

Can a torn ACL heal by itself?

Sometimes on MRI: about half of rehab-only knees looked continuous at 2 years. At 11 years that group scored worse than rebuilt knees, though in only 24 knees, and had the least arthritis.1

When can I play padel, basketball or jiu-jitsu again?

Most are cleared at 9 to 12 months after ACL surgery. 81% return to some sport, 65% to their old level and 55% to competition.3,4

My MRI shows a meniscus tear. Is that why it hurts?

Maybe not. Over 50, tears are common on MRI and 61% of people with one had no knee pain that month.7

Is collagen worth it for knees?

It has the best human data of any peptide here: small to moderate relief in 35 trials, and about 6 points out of 100 more than placebo in young active people. Low risk.24,25

Do GLP-1 drugs help knee arthritis?

For people with obesity, yes for pain, mostly through weight loss. Neither semaglutide nor retatrutide is proven to rebuild cartilage.26,27

Is cortisone bad for my knee?

Shots every 3 months for 2 years thinned cartilage more than salt water with no extra relief. One shot for a flare was not tested there.19
