Anxiety

And the depression question alongside it. Two Russian nasal peptides, Selank and Semax, get sold as a benzodiazepine you can't get hooked on and an antidepressant without the wait. This page holds both up against the drugs they're compared to, and against doing nothing.

What it is. Anxiety is normal; an anxiety disorder is worry or dread that stays on when the threat is gone and starts costing you sleep, focus and days. Generalized anxiety disorder is the everyday version of it.

What studies found. One 62-person Russian trial found Selank matched a low-dose benzodiazepine for anxiety without the sedation, memory loss or dependence.1 That is the whole human case, and it has never been repeated outside Russia.7 Semax is a focus drug with almost no human mood data.8

What people report. Of Selank reports off vendor pages, most say the same thing: calm without being knocked out, often within days.9 A clear minority feel nothing, and a few feel more anxious, not less. Semax reports are about focus, and it can tip into over-stimulation.

Watch out. Benzodiazepines carry the FDA's strongest warning: dependence can form in days to weeks, even as prescribed.6 The peptides are not approved or quality-checked outside Russia, and a single Selank dose has set off racing heart and worse anxiety in some people.9 Lasting anxiety or depression needs real care, not a nasal spray.

1What anxiety is

Signs

Everyone feels anxious. It becomes a disorder when the worry won't switch off, runs ahead of anything real, and starts taking things from you.

  • Worry you can't steer for most days over months, about several things at once (generalized anxiety).
  • Body signs: a racing heart, tight chest, restlessness, a wired-but-tired feeling, broken sleep, a stomach that knots.
  • It narrows your life: you avoid places, calls or decisions to keep the feeling down.

When it is more than anxiety

Panic that hits in minutes, dread tied only to social situations, or low mood and loss of interest that ride alongside it. Where low mood leads, the question becomes depression, lower down this page.

How it behaves without treatment

Meta

A dummy pill helps about a third. Across anxiety-disorder trials, roughly 37% of people on placebo respond and 24% reach remission, and generalized anxiety sits at the high end of that.4 Any "it worked for me" has to clear that bar first.

Review

It rises and falls on its own. Sleep, stress, caffeine, a hard week: all move anxiety, so one calm day proves nothing. The fair test is weeks, scored the same way, not a single good morning.

RCT

Talking therapy holds up. Cognitive behavioural therapy beats a dummy treatment across anxiety disorders, and its gains tend to outlast a pill you stop taking.5 It is the benchmark the drugs and peptides are really competing with.

2What studies found

How they work, where it counts

Anxiety is the brain running hot. The calm comes from one brake: a receptor called GABA-A, a gated channel that lets negatively charged chloride into a neuron and makes it harder to fire. Tap through to see what each option does to that brake.

Resting, no GABA. The gate is shut, chloride stays out, and the neuron fires freely. Plenty of this at once is the body of anxiety: the brain running hot with no brake on.
GABA opens the brake. The brain's own calming messenger binds two spots on the receptor and the gate opens. Chloride flows in, the neuron is harder to fire. This is calm as the body normally makes it, no drug involved.
A benzo leans on the brake. It doesn't open the gate itself. It parks in a separate notch and makes GABA's own opening stronger and more frequent, so more chloride pours in. Fast, heavy calm. Lean on it daily and the brain turns its own brake down to compensate, so you need more for the same effect: that is tolerance and dependence.
Selank works around the receptor. It doesn't jam the gate open like a benzo. It nudges the GABA system more gently and tops up serotonin and dopamine, so the calm is lighter and slower and no dependence has shown up. The honest catch: its exact handhold is still not pinned down, which is why it sits above the receptor here, not inside it.

Simplified. The GABA sites and the benzodiazepine notch are real and well mapped; Selank's exact grip on the system is not, so it is drawn acting around the receptor rather than in it.

Benzodiazepines · the thing Selank is sold against

Diazepam, alprazolam (Xanax), clonazepam, medazepam. They open the brain's main calming channel, the GABA-A receptor, wider. How it helps: fast and reliable, calm within an hour. How it hurts: that same brake wears a groove. Tolerance builds, the dose creeps, and the body comes to need it.

FDA

The strongest warning there is. In 2020 the FDA put a boxed warning on every benzodiazepine for abuse, addiction, physical dependence and withdrawal. Dependence can form in days to weeks, even taken exactly as prescribed, and stopping suddenly can bring on withdrawal including seizures.6

Label

Meant for the short run. Most benzodiazepines are recommended for weeks, not months, yet are often taken far longer.6 They work; the problem is what happens after.

SSRIs, SNRIs and the depression question

Sertraline, escitalopram, venlafaxine and the rest are the first-line drugs for both generalized anxiety and depression. They work slowly, over weeks, by nudging serotonin, and you don't get hooked the way you do on a benzo, though stopping fast brings its own discontinuation effects.

Meta

They beat placebo, modestly. In generalized anxiety, antidepressants raise the response rate over a dummy pill by a clear but not dramatic margin, and most people stay on them without trouble.5 Decades of independent trials sit behind that number.

Plain truth

For depression, no peptide is close. Semax gets talked about as an antidepressant, but the human evidence for mood is thin and mostly animal work and conference abstracts.8 If low mood is the main problem, the honest comparison is an antidepressant and therapy, not a nasal peptide.

Selank · the anxiety peptide

Selank

Library card

A synthetic seven-amino-acid cousin of a natural immune peptide (tuftsin), approved in Russia as nasal drops for generalized anxiety and neurasthenia, not approved anywhere in the West.

How it is said to work: it touches the same GABA calming system a benzodiazepine does, but as a gentle modulator rather than a blunt amplifier, and it also nudges serotonin and dopamine. That mixed, lighter touch is the stated reason it calms without sedating or building dependence.3

RCT

Matched a benzo in 62 people. In patients with generalized anxiety and neurasthenia, Selank gave anxiety relief similar to the benzodiazepine medazepam, and added a mild lift in focus and energy the benzo didn't.1 Small, open to bias, and done by the group tied to the drug.

Rat

Calm without the benzo baggage. In stressed rats, Selank matched or helped diazepam, and the authors report its effect comes without the amnesia, withdrawal or dependence benzodiazepines bring.2,3

Check

The half-life line is shaky. The "2 minutes in the blood" figure often quoted is from a dish, not a person, and the felt effect plainly lasts much longer.9 A reminder of how thin the real data is.

Semax · the focus peptide, not the calm one

Semax

Library card · vs Selank

A fragment of the stress hormone ACTH with a stabilising tail, a registered Russian drug for stroke and cognition. Same program as Selank, different job.

How it is said to work: it is a nootropic, aimed at focus, memory and drive, and is claimed to raise BDNF, a brain growth signal, though that rise is shown in rats and unconfirmed in people.8 It is not an anxiolytic, and for some it does the opposite.

Reports

People take it for clarity, not calm. Across 85 reports, the steady themes are focus, memory and energy; the steady side effect is over-stimulation, often with coffee or a stimulant, and at higher doses that shows up as anxiety and irritability.9

Human

Thin where it counts. Most human Semax work is small, Russian, and often only a conference abstract; there is very little showing it lifts mood or focus in a controlled way.8

Consistent

Calm without being switched off. The one thing 25-plus people agree on: anxiety eased without the fog or sedation of a benzo, often within minutes to a few days.9

Honest

For a real share, nothing. About 1 in 5 off vendor pages felt no effect at all, even at higher doses. It is not a sure thing.9

Watch

A few got the opposite. Headaches, and in a handful of people more anxiety, racing heart or bad sleep, sometimes from a single dose.9

Semax, and the aggregators

Reports

Semax is a focus story. Of 85 reports, the agreement is on clarity and drive, not calm, and the repeated downside is feeling wired, worse with stimulants.9

Aggregator

One large dataset leans positive. On SelfAssay, Selank's most logged benefit across 241 reports is anxiety relief, named by 91 people; anxiety also shows up as a side effect for a few.10 Self-selected, so read it as a direction, not a rate.

Flag

One source looks seeded. Another site shows Selank at 3% favourable with benefit counts higher than its user count, a broken pattern we don't trust and have left out of the numbers above.9

4Pairings and the same problem elsewhere

What people pair it with

  • Selank plus Semax is the common stack: Selank for calm, Semax for focus. People report it reads as balanced, though the effects get hard to tell apart.
  • Alongside an SSRI or a benzo some use Selank as a top-up; a few report it took the edge off benzo-withdrawal stress. No trial backs this, and mixing anything with those drugs is a doctor's call.
  • With magnesium, glycine or an adaptogen like ashwagandha, which at least have their own small human trials for anxiety.

6Before you try anything

Warnings

  • Benzodiazepines are prescription-only for a reason. The FDA's boxed warning covers dependence, which can form in days to weeks even as prescribed, and withdrawal that can include seizures. Never start, change or stop one without a doctor, and never stop abruptly.6
  • SSRIs and SNRIs take weeks and shouldn't be stopped suddenly. They also interact with other serotonin drugs. A doctor manages this, not a nasal spray on the side.
  • The peptides are not approved or quality-checked outside Russia. What is in the vial is not guaranteed. A single Selank dose has triggered racing heart, worse anxiety and bad sleep in some people, and Semax can over-stimulate, more so with stimulants or coffee.9
  • Lasting anxiety or depression is a medical problem. If it is taking your sleep, focus or days, or low mood is part of it, see a doctor. Peptides are not a substitute for care.

?Questions people ask

Is Selank as good as Xanax?

One 62-person trial found it matched a different benzodiazepine for anxiety, without the sedation or dependence.1 That is a real but single result, done by the drug's own group and never repeated independently. "As good as" is not established; "promising" is fair.

Will Selank get me hooked like a benzo?

No dependence or withdrawal has been reported for it in animal or human work, which is its main selling point over benzodiazepines.2,3 But the human safety data is thin, so "none reported" is not the same as "proven safe long-term".

Does Semax help anxiety or depression?

Semax is a focus and cognition drug, not a calming one, and for some people it adds anxiety rather than easing it.9 Its antidepressant reputation rests on very little human evidence.8 For low mood, an antidepressant and therapy are the real options.

How fast does Selank work?

Reports range from minutes to about five days for a steady effect, most often within the first few days.9 If a week or two does nothing, it is probably not going to.

Can I take Selank with my SSRI or benzo?

People do, and some report it helps, but there is no trial on the combination and these are serious drugs. This is a conversation with your prescriber, not a thing to layer on quietly.

Is any of this FDA approved?

No. Selank and Semax are approved in Russia and sold as nasal drops there; neither is approved by the FDA or any Western regulator, and the human evidence has never been replicated to Western standards.7
