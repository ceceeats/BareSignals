Hair

Pattern hair loss in men and women, shedding after illness, a crash diet or a GLP-1 drug, alopecia areata, PCOS hair loss, recovery after a hair transplant and scarring loss. One page because people reach for the same peptides for all of them.

HairEndocrine & growthImmunitySkin & aesthetics

What it is. Hair grows in cycles, and nearly every kind of hair loss is a cycle going wrong. In pattern loss, DHT shortens each growing phase until the follicle comes back smaller and the hair turns to fuzz; after illness, a crash diet or a GLP-1 drug, many follicles drop into rest at once and fall out about 3 months later.1,2

What studies found. The proven tools are medicines: finasteride held men's gains for 5 years while placebo kept thinning, and minoxidil adds about 13 hairs per cm² in women.3,4 No research peptide used on its own has a placebo-controlled hair trial. The best result with one in it: 7 men whose bald top scalp shrank from 40% to 7.5%, with copper peptides tattooed in together with minoxidil and dutasteride.5

What people report. GLP-1 shedding is the one consistent story: among 73 forum posters, shedding stopped once weight held steady, sometimes faster with low-dose oral minoxidil.6 Of 10 people using copper peptides on the scalp, 7 saw no change; the rest describe thicker existing hair, rarely new hair in bald spots.7

Watch out. Thymosin beta-4, the parent of TB-500, grew hair in mice but slowed human scalp follicles in a dish.8,9 Sudden patches, burning, pain or scarring need a dermatologist: scarring loss is permanent.10

1What the hair problem is

Which one is it

The type changes everything, so match the signs first. A healthy scalp loses 50 to 100 hairs a day.2

Pattern loss, men

Signs: temples recede and the crown thins, slowly, over years; under a scope hairs are of mixed thickness.15 Why: in follicles with the genes for it, DHT shortens every growing phase, and each cycle the follicle comes back smaller until thick hairs become fine fuzz.1 A scalp hair grows for 2 to 6 years before it rests, so each shrinking step takes years to show. That is why pattern loss creeps, and why treatment is judged over months and years, not weeks.2

Pattern loss, women

Signs: a widening part with the front hairline kept. It affects nearly half of women at some point and is often missed. Shedding that drags past 6 months can look just like it.15

Shedding after a trigger

Signs: all-over shedding, handfuls in the shower, a thinner ponytail, but no bald patches. It shows about 3 months after illness, fever, surgery, giving birth, a crash diet, low iron or protein, a new medicine or big stress; after COVID the median was 2 months (range 1 to 11) in 181 people.2,16 A shock can push up to half of growing follicles into rest at once. They sit there about 3 months before the hair drops, so by the time the brush fills, the cause is usually forgotten. Regrowth then comes at about 1 cm a month.2,17

GLP-1 shedding

Signs: the same shedding, starting 2 to 3 months into semaglutide or tirzepatide. Mostly from fast weight loss, not the drug poisoning follicles: women and people losing more than 10% of body weight shed most. It can also unmask pattern loss that was already there.18,14

Alopecia areata

Signs: round, smooth bald patches, sometimes in the eyebrows or beard; it can spread to the whole scalp or body, and the course is unpredictable. The follicle's immune shield breaks and immune cells attack the bulb. Over 40% carry thyroid antibodies.19,20,21

PCOS

Signs: female pattern loss driven by extra androgens, often with acne and extra body hair. Patchy loss in PCOS is a different problem worth checking: in 1,249 women with PCOS, alopecia areata was rare (0.24%) and thyroid autoimmunity common (20.3%).22

After a hair transplant

Signs: most moved hairs fall out within about 6 weeks, rest about 3 months, start growing at months 3 to 5, and the result is judged at 12 to 18 months.17 Native hair near the grafts can shed too (shock loss): 23 of 621 patients: 14 of 67 women against 9 of 554 men (30 times the odds).23

Scarring loss

Signs: patches with burning, itch, pain or scarring, as in lichen planopilaris or frontal fibrosing alopecia. The stem cells are destroyed, so the loss is permanent. Transplants into scar take at first (80 to 100% at 6 to 12 months) but fall below 41% after year two.10,13

Inside the follicle

A scalp has about 100,000 follicles; 85 to 90% are growing and 5 to 10% resting at any time.2 Tap a part, play the cycle, or tap a treatment to light up where it acts.

Growing phase (anagen)

Lasts 2 to 6 years and holds 85 to 90% of follicles. How long it lasts sets how long hair can get; the size of the dermal papilla sets how thick.2,24

Shrink and rest (catagen, telogen)

The lower follicle pulls up in 2 to 3 weeks, then rests about 3 months with the old hair still anchored. A shock pushes many follicles here at once, up to half, which is the shedding that comes 3 months later.2

Shedding (exogen)

A new hair starts below and the old club hair falls out: 50 to 100 a day is normal. A white bulb on a fallen hair is this resting root, not a sign of damage.2,13

Pattern loss (miniaturisation)

DHT binds the receptor in papilla cells, which send slow-down signals (TGF-beta). Each growing phase is shorter and the follicle comes back smaller, until thick hairs are fine, short fuzz. The stem cells survive, which is why the loss can be slowed or partly reversed.1,13

Hair shaft

The visible part, dead keratin. Length depends on how long the growing phase lasts; thickness on the size of the bulb and papilla.2,24

Oil gland and goosebump muscle

Oil glands run on androgens, which ties hair to acne and PCOS. The thin pink band is the muscle that makes hair stand up.13

Bulge stem cells

A ledge below the oil gland where the follicle's stem cells and pigment stem cells live. They rebuild the lower follicle every cycle. In pattern loss they survive but stop being switched on; in scarring loss they are destroyed, so that loss is permanent.13

Matrix and pigment cells

Cells here divide faster than almost any in the body to build the shaft, which is why chemotherapy hits hair. Pigment cells colour the hair while it grows and are refilled from the bulge each cycle; when they run out, hair greys.13

Dermal papilla

The follicle's control room: a cluster of cells in the bulb that signals the rest. Its size sets hair thickness. Minoxidil acts here by opening potassium channels, not just by adding blood flow.24,25

DHT receptor

Testosterone becomes DHT through the enzyme 5-alpha-reductase type 2, and DHT binds the androgen receptor in papilla cells. Medicines act at three points: make less DHT (finasteride, dutasteride), block the receptor (clascoterone, spironolactone, pyrilutamide), or break the receptor down (GT20029, in trials).1,26

Wnt switch and CXXC5 brake

Wnt is the follicle's grow switch and keeps the papilla active. CXXC5 is a brake protein that holds it back, and it is turned up in shrunken human follicles. PTD-DBM was built to release this brake.27

Immune shield

The bulb hides from the immune system using local signals such as alpha-MSH, TGF-beta and IL-10. In alopecia areata the shield breaks and T cells attack, using signals that pass through JAK enzymes; JAK pills block them.19

Blood supply

Vessels feed the growing bulb through the papilla. Balding scalp shows less blood support and low-level inflammation around follicles.13

2What happens without treatment

The course on its own

RCT

Pattern loss keeps going. Men on placebo kept thinning year after year over 5 years.3 Yet in short trials placebo groups gain 4.6 to 10.1 hairs per cm² in 12 to 24 weeks. A product with no comparison group that reports "5 more hairs per cm²" may be showing exactly this placebo gain. It is also why "539% better than placebo" can come from a small real difference: the placebo number is tiny to begin with.26,28

Human

Shedding mostly recovers. After a trigger it usually settles within 3 to 12 months, once the cause is fixed.2,14 Shedding that lasts past 6 months is called chronic and can hide early pattern loss.15

RCT

Alopecia areata is unpredictable. Patches can regrow on their own, which is why single success stories prove little. In severe cases, only 0.8 to 6% on placebo reach near-full scalp in 24 to 36 weeks.30,29

3What studies found

Proven options, with numbers

47 RCTs

Minoxidil on the scalp. In women, about twice as many saw moderate or marked regrowth as on placebo, and about 13 more hairs per cm²; 2% and 5% worked about the same. Scalp itch and dryness hit 75% of users in one trial.4,39 The only approved drug for women.15

RCT

Minoxidil pills, low dose. Matched the lotion in a 75-person, 32-week trial (density up 47.5% vs 48%), and at 1.35 to 4.05 mg thickened hair fibres by 3 to 6 µm while placebo lost 2 µm. In 1,404 patients only 1.7% stopped for side effects.39,24,40 After a newspaper article in August 2022, first-time US prescriptions rose 2.4 times in men and 1.7 times in women within 8 weeks, while only 3 randomised trials existed by 2024.25

RCT

Finasteride 1 mg (men). In 1,553 men, gains held on every measure over 5 years while placebo kept losing hair. In women it did no better than placebo (30 of 67 vs 33 of 70). A topical 0.25% spray matched the pill (20.2 vs 21.1 more hairs per cm²) but still lowers blood DHT; none is FDA-approved.3,4,41

Small

Dutasteride. In a small non-randomised 6-month study it showed no measurable change while oral minoxidil groups improved. Six months is short for a DHT blocker.42

Phase 3

Clascoterone lotion (pending). Two trials in 1,465 men beat a plain lotion by 168% and 539%, but no actual hair counts are out. Some reaches the bloodstream; no hormonal side effects reported. US filing early 2027; no trial in women.28

Observ.

Spironolactone (women). Never tested against placebo. In 80 women, 44% regrew, 44% held and 12% kept losing; in 128 women in 2026, 54% improved at least one grade over 24 months.43 Not for pregnancy.

12 RCTs

Microneedling with minoxidil. In 631 people, hair counts rose clearly more than minoxidil alone (a large effect); needle depth and device did not matter. Ranked the best add-on for women.44,45

9 RCTs

PRP. Same density and terminal hairs as minoxidil in 451 people, though people were more satisfied with it. PRP plus a growth factor plus minoxidil ranked top overall: 35 more hairs per cm² than minoxidil alone.45

RCT

Laser or light. A laser comb gave about 17 more hairs than a sham device in two women's trials; added to minoxidil it gave nothing extra in 4 trials.4,46

RCT

JAK pills (alopecia areata). Near-full scalp: baricitinib 4 mg 39% and 36% vs 6% and 3% at 36 weeks; ritlecitinib 23% vs 2%; deuruxolitinib 8 mg 29 to 33% (12 mg up to 41%) vs about 1% at 24 weeks. Relapse after stopping is common.30,47,29,20

Surgery

Hair transplant. Most studies report 85 to 95% of grafts surviving in normal scalp (93.1% at 9 months in one, 81% in a plain-surgery control arm in another). Serious complications were zero in 2,896 patients.48,23

Coming

New androgen blockers. Pyrilutamide lotion: 666 men gained 15.3 hairs per cm² against 4.7 on placebo at 24 weeks. GT20029 passed phase 2. Setipiprant failed.26

Read this first: no research peptide used on its own (GHK-Cu, TB-500, BPC-157, KPV, PTD-DBM, thymosin alpha-1) has a controlled human hair trial. Cosmetic peptide blends have small trials against a plain serum. Everything else is mixes, animals, human follicles in a dish, or theory.13,35

GHK-Cu and copper peptides · mixes, rats

GHK-Cu

Library card · GHK, no copper

Pattern loss is a shrinking problem: each cycle the follicle comes back smaller. GHK-Cu is best read as a reset message that carries copper to tired tissue, and copper peptides have been sold for hair since the 1990s. The best human result is 7 men whose bald top scalp shrank from 40% to 7.5%, but the copper went in together with minoxidil and dutasteride. On its own it has never had a controlled hair trial.5,49

  1. 2025 7 men, mixed

    Tattooed mix: bald area 40% down to 7.5%.

    Men aged 28 to 55 who had not done well on a year of standard treatment had minoxidil 0.5%, dutasteride 0.1% and copper peptides 1.2% driven into the scalp with a tattoo machine, once a month for 5 months. 5 of 7 regrew more than 10% of the top scalp, graded by 4 blinded dermatologists from photos. No scarring or infection.5 No control group, looked back at records, and no hair counts under a scope. An earlier series of the same mix without copper gained 10% against 26.5% here, but the number of sessions, needle depth and copper all changed at once. The senior author advises a compounding pharmacy.5

  2. 2018 1,000 people, open

    A clinic cocktail: pull test better in 83%.

    Copper tripeptide plus thymosin beta-4 and growth factors, injected every 3 weeks 8 times. Hair counts rose at a year, with no comparison group, in a product made by the clinic.50

  3. 1993 Rats

    "As good as 5% minoxidil", traced.

    The source is a 1993 review: a coded copper peptide (PC1031) enlarged follicles on fuzzy rats, "similar to" topical minoxidil. The minoxidil strength is not given, the peptide is not named as GHK-Cu, and the authors worked for its developer.31

  4. 1995 Cells

    "Blocks DHT", traced to plain copper.

    In engineered cells, free copper ions blocked the skin type of 5-alpha-reductase at 1.9 µM, and the scalp type only at 19.2 µM, 10 times more. Zinc and cadmium worked too. No study tests GHK-Cu on this enzyme, and forum users injecting it saw no change in DHT on their labs.38,13

Missing: injected GHK-Cu for hair has no human study. A cosmetic dermatologist told NPR he would not recommend injections; a nurse practitioner reported injection-site reactions, infections and liver problems in people self-injecting, cause unclear.51 On the skin, an expert panel judged copper cosmetic peptides safe as used.13

Thymosin beta-4, TB-500 · mice yes, human follicles no

Thymosin beta-4

Tβ4 · TB-500 · Thymulin

Every new hair starts with stem cells waking up in the bulge. Thymosin beta-4 works like a scaffolding crew that moves cells, and in NIH mice and rats it pulled those stem cells into action and sped hair growth. Human scalp follicles in a dish went the other way: 10 to 20% less hair and an early switch into the shrinking phase. TB-500, the fragment people inject, has never been tested on hair.8,9

  1. 2004 Rats, mice

    Faster growth by moving stem cells.

    Made by the descendants of bulge stem cells during growth; added at tiny doses it made those cells move and mature. A mouse bred to make extra grew more hair, and in goats it raised cashmere output.8,13

  2. 2012 Human follicles

    Three thymus peptides, three results.

    Scalp follicles from a UK transplant clinic, 2 to 3 donors each.9

PTD-DBM · mice only

PTD-DBM

Library card

In shrunken human follicles a brake protein, CXXC5, is turned up and holds back the Wnt grow switch. PTD-DBM is a decoy that peels the brake off: in mice, wounds regrew hair and even new follicles faster, more so with valproic acid. It has never been tested in people; its lab moved on to a small molecule that has passed only a phase 1 safety check.27,33

Claim to doubt: a 2025 review says PTD-DBM "demonstrated clinical efficacy", but no human trial exists in any registry we searched.33 Forums: 0 better, 1 partly (thicker existing hair), 2 no change; users found it unstable and hard to keep cold.7

KPV and thymosin alpha-1 · patches, no data

KPV, Tα1

KPV · Thymosin alpha-1

Alopecia areata starts when the follicle's immune shield fails. KPV is the tail end of alpha-MSH, one of the shield's own guards: an off switch for an alarm that keeps ringing. Its cousin K(D)PT kept pigment flowing in stressed human follicles in a dish, an idea for white regrowth after patches. Nobody has tested KPV for hair growth, in animals or people.19,34

Thymosin alpha-1 boosts the immune system, while alopecia areata is an overactive one, so the logic is unclear. In human follicles in a dish it slightly slowed growth.9 One forum user given 10 shots by an immunologist saw no change and new psoriasis patches.7 The nearest real immune-rebalancing idea, low-dose IL-2, has a completed trial in 44 people.52

AHK-Cu · human follicles in a dish

AHK-Cu

Library card

AHK-Cu is GHK-Cu's lesser-known cousin and the only copper peptide with human follicle data. At tiny doses it lengthened human hair follicles kept alive in a dish, made papilla cells divide more and lowered cell-death signals. No animal or human trial followed.32

Forums: 1 partial (some temple regrowth with a dermaroller and other topicals), 2 no change after 5 to 6 months; another user had flushing and facial puffiness.7

Cosmetic peptide serums · small trials

Peptide serums

Redensyl, Capixyl, Procapil type

These lotions mix cosmetic peptides (acetyl tetrapeptide-3, biotinoyl tripeptide-1, GHK) with plant extracts. They are the only peptides with trials against a plain serum, and the effects are small.35,36

  1. 2026 14 trials

    Graded B, as blends.

    A review of 14 human trials gave blends with these peptides a B grade. Blends can't show what any single peptide does.35

  2. 2020 32 people

    Matched 3% minoxidil, no placebo.

    Terminal hairs up 8.3% on the acetyl tetrapeptide-3 blend vs 8.7% on minoxidil. Without a placebo group, both could be near the placebo gain.36

  3. 2020 44 people

    More growing hairs vs resting.

    A Redensyl blend raised the growing-to-resting ratio from 2.25 to 6.02 over 24 weeks; the plain-serum result is not in the abstract.36

Coming: an 80-person blinded trial against 2% minoxidil, including women, completes 30 Nov 2026.36 Greying: an "anti-grey" peptide raised pigment markers in 15 men, with no grey-hair count.53

Exosomes · a pilot, case reports

Exosomes

Not peptides; often sold alongside
  1. 2025 20 men

    9.5 vs 1.5 hairs gained over saline.

    A plant-extract exosome formula, 4 sessions, measured at 16 weeks. A 120-man trial is still recruiting, so results are not close.37

  2. 2025 3 cases

    Severe patches improved, not cleared.

    Two women who had failed JAK pills improved by 12 to 20 points on a 100-point bald score after one session; a man's beard patches regrew and darkened from week 6. Patches can regrow alone, and a 2026 review found no randomised trial.37

Regulator: no exosome product is approved, and an FDA warning letter cites serious adverse events after exosome treatment.37

BPC-157, GH peptides, collagen · no hair data

Borrowed claims

BPC-157 · Ipamorelin · MK-677 · Collagen
  • BPC-157: zero hair studies; the claim is borrowed from rat wound and blood vessel work. After his own transplant a podcast host credited BPC-157, TB-500 and a copper shampoo; a transplant clinic advises not starting any injectable in the first 10 days and telling the surgeon everything.13,54
  • GH peptides: no study on hair counts. Two forum users said hair got thicker: one on ipamorelin with CJC-1295 for 12 weeks, one on MK-677 alongside finasteride, minoxidil and microneedling.7
  • Collagen peptides: in a 6-month trial in women after menopause, collagen slowed shedding, but calcium with vitamin D did the same, so it is not collagen itself.55
  • Biotin: helps only if you lack it, which is rare, and 5 to 20 mg a day can fake thyroid test results.21

GLP-1 drugs · a cause of shedding

Semaglutide, tirzepatide

Semaglutide · Tirzepatide · GLP-1s case

Losing weight fast is a shock to the body, and follicles answer the way they answer illness: many drop into rest together. Across 34 trials with 84,243 people, hair loss was about 3 times the placebo rate. In plain numbers that is 3 in 100 on Wegovy against 1 in 100 on placebo, and about 6 in 100 on the top tirzepatide dose.11,12

  • Drug or weight? Both, mostly weight. Tirzepatide's diabetes trials, with less weight loss, logged 0.25%; semaglutide under 2 mg a week is rarely linked.12,56 In 2026 a BMJ study of people with diabetes found 1.37 times the risk against SGLT-2 drugs, which also cause some weight loss, and 1.68 times against DPP-4 drugs. The signal shrank after a statistical check for hidden bias, and the absolute risk was low: about 6.9 against 5.0 cases per 1,000 people a year.57
  • Real life looks worse than trials: 3 to 7% in trials against over 70% in some self-selected surveys, which count differently.18
  • The other direction: some reports describe pattern and scarring loss improving on GLP-1s, perhaps through metabolism and less inflammation.18
  • What dermatologists do: check iron (ferritin), vitamin D, zinc and thyroid, start minoxidil, and begin pattern-loss treatment before the GLP-1 if it is already there.14

6Pairings and related

What is used together

  • Finasteride with minoxidil (men) and microneedling with minoxidil (women) ranked top in a network comparison of trials.45 Meta
  • Peptides go on top, never alone. The real-world stack is minoxidil plus a DHT blocker, then microneedling or PRP, then copper peptides; the best peptide study was itself a mix.5,13
  • Copper peptides with microneedling: needles push more copper into the skin; a dilute solution showed none at 9 hours.13
  • JAK pills with oral minoxidil: 168 of 202 people on tofacitinib for patches took both; 56% reached half regrowth.61
  • GLP-1 with pattern-loss treatment: start minoxidil early if pattern loss is already there.14
  • Transplant with medicine: finasteride or minoxidil to keep the native hair, and tell the clinic about anything injected.54,13

The same problem, other pages

  • PCOS: the androgen side of female hair loss, with spironolactone and GLP-1s.
  • GLP-1s: every effect beyond weight loss, shedding included.
  • Acne: the same androgens drive oil glands; clascoterone started as an acne cream.
  • Skin: GHK-Cu, collagen and the copper peptides on skin.
  • GHK-Cu card and hair research notes: every study and count behind this page.

Lashes and greying

A prescription eye drop, bimatoprost, grew lashes in 77% vs 18% on a plain drop at 4 months.62 No peptide has reversed grey hair in a controlled study.53

7Options, in order of evidence

  1. Find the type first.Pattern, shedding, patches or scarring change everything. For all-over shedding, doctors check ferritin, thyroid, vitamin D and zinc.14
  2. Pattern loss, men.Finasteride (pill or spray) with minoxidil (lotion or low-dose pill); microneedling as an add-on. Clascoterone pending, filing 2027.3,45,28
  3. Pattern loss, women.Minoxidil lotion (the only approved drug) or low-dose pills; microneedling; spironolactone off-label, never tested against placebo.15,44,43
  4. Shedding, GLP-1 included.Fix the trigger, eat enough protein and iron, lose weight more slowly; it usually settles in 3 to 12 months; minoxidil if it drags.14,6
  5. Alopecia areata, moderate to severe.JAK pills: 20 to 40% near-full regrowth in 6 to 9 months. Baricitinib is the only treatment with high-certainty evidence.30,29
  6. Transplant.Keep native hair on medicine; PRP at surgery has weak but positive data.48,13
  7. Peptides, experimental.Copper peptides on the scalp (low risk, weak evidence, best with microneedling); cosmetic blends (small trials); TB-500 (mice yes, human follicles no); PTD-DBM (mice, unstable); BPC-157, KPV and thymosin alpha-1 (no hair data).13

8Usual questions

Is it normal to lose 100 hairs a day?

Yes. 50 to 100 a day is normal shedding from follicles ending their rest phase.2

Will my GLP-1 hair loss stop?

Usually. Trichologists expect 3 to 6 months of shedding and fullness can take about a year. On forums it stopped once weight held steady. Pattern loss underneath will not fix itself.14,6

How likely is hair loss on Ozempic, Wegovy or Mounjaro?

About 3 times the placebo rate: about 3 in 100 on Wegovy vs 1 in 100 on placebo, and 5 to 7 in 100 on high-dose tirzepatide. Mostly from fast weight loss; a small drug effect can't be ruled out.11,12,57

Do copper peptides regrow hair?

Not proven. No controlled trial of GHK-Cu alone. Blends containing GHK have small trials. The "as good as 5% minoxidil" line comes from a 1993 rat result with a developer's coded peptide, and "blocks DHT" from a 1995 study of plain copper.35,31,38

Does TB-500 grow hair?

It did in mice. In human scalp follicles in a dish, thymosin beta-4 cut growth by 10 to 20% and pushed follicles into the shrinking phase early. TB-500 itself was never tested.8,9

Is oral minoxidil safe?

In 1,404 patients only 1.7% stopped for side effects: extra hair in 15%, fluid retention 1.3%, a fast heart 0.9%. Fluid around the heart is extremely rare, often linked to dosing errors.40,58

Oral or topical minoxidil?

Similar regrowth in a 32-week trial. The pill avoids the scalp itch that hit 75% on the lotion, but grows hair elsewhere in about 15%.39,40

Does finasteride ruin your sex life?

Sexual side effects were 1.57 times placebo across 15 trials, mostly reversible; a small group report lasting symptoms after stopping.59,63

Is the new clascoterone lotion hormone-free?

Some of it reaches the bloodstream. Phase 3 reported no hormonal side effects. Real hair counts are not out yet.28

Is spironolactone worth it for women?

Never tested against placebo. In observational studies 44 to 54% improve and most of the rest hold steady.43

What works for alopecia areata?

JAK pills: 23 to 41% reach near-full scalp at 24 to 36 weeks vs 1 to 6% on placebo, with a boxed warning. Thymosin alpha-1 has no study in people and slightly slowed human follicles in a dish.29,9

What is PTD-DBM?

A peptide that releases the Wnt grow switch. It worked in mice and was never tested in people; its successor molecule passed only a safety trial.27,33

Do biotin or collagen help?

Biotin only if you lack it, and it can fake thyroid results. Collagen slowed shedding in one trial, but calcium with vitamin D did the same.21,55

Is shedding after a transplant normal?

Yes: grafts shed within about 6 weeks and regrow from month 3 to 5. Native-hair shock loss hits about 4%, far more in women.17,23

Do BPC-157 or TB-500 help a transplant heal?

No study in transplant patients. One clinic advises not starting them in the first 10 days unless your surgeon plans it, and telling the clinic about anything you inject.54

Is anything new coming?

Two androgen-blocking lotions from China: pyrilutamide (about 11 more hairs per cm² than placebo at 24 weeks) and GT20029 (phase 2). Clascoterone filing is planned for early 2027.26,28

9Warnings and fine print

See a dermatologist

  • Sudden round patches, or loss with burning, pain, itch or scarring: scarring loss is permanent.10
  • Shedding that lasts more than 6 months.15
  • Patchy loss with PCOS or thyroid disease: it may be alopecia areata.22,21

The medicines

  • Finasteride, dutasteride: sexual side effects 1.57 times placebo. FDA reports show more depression and anxiety reports in young men on hair doses, though an observational meta-analysis found no clear link (1.07). Screen mood before and during.59,63
  • Oral minoxidil: fluid retention 1.3 to 10%; fast heart or dizziness under 5%; early shedding in 16 to 22% is expected. Not with heart lining disease or uncontrolled blood pressure.58
  • Pregnancy: finasteride, dutasteride, spironolactone and oral minoxidil are avoided. Topical finasteride can pass to partners and children by touch.41,13
  • JAK pills: boxed warning for serious infections, death, cancer, heart events and clots; more relevant over 65.29,64

Peptides and unregulated products

  • Research peptides (GHK-Cu, TB-500, PTD-DBM, BPC-157) are not approved drugs; purity and stability vary, PTD-DBM especially.13
  • Thymosin beta-4 and alpha-1 both nudged human follicles toward shedding in a dish.9
  • Injected GHK-Cu: no human safety data; injection-site reactions and infections are reported.51
  • Exosomes: unapproved biologics; the FDA has warned of serious adverse events.37
  • Compounded topical finasteride: caused reports like the pill's side effects, many lasting after stopping.41
  • Melanotan: depigmented patches reported in a patient with alopecia areata.65
  • Biotin: stop 24 to 72 hours before thyroid or hormone blood tests.21
  • After a transplant: avoid unknown injectables near fresh grafts in the first 10 days, and don't spray anything that runs over healing cuts without asking.54,7

Trials to watch

  • Peptide serum vs 2% minoxidil (80 people, women included, Bangkok): completes 30 Nov 2026. No placebo arm, so it can only show "about as good as minoxidil" or not.36
  • Cord exosome injections vs saline (120 men, Beijing): still recruiting; its 24-week result can't arrive by its listed date.37
  • Clascoterone lotion: US and EU filing planned for early 2027; real hair counts still to be published.28
  • GT20029 in phase 3 and pyrilutamide heading for approval in China.26

Words on this page

Anagen, telogen
The growing phase (2 to 6 years) and the resting phase (about 3 months) of a follicle.2
Miniaturisation
The follicle shrinking a little each cycle in pattern loss.1
Terminal, vellus
Thick, coloured hairs vs the fine fuzz shrunken follicles make.1
Hairs per cm²
Hairs counted in a small marked patch of scalp; "+13" means 13 more in each square centimetre than the placebo group.4
SALT score
How much of the scalp is bald, from 0 to 100%. "SALT 20 or less" means at least 80% covered.30
Times placebo
A ratio: "3 times placebo" for something that hits 1 in 100 on placebo means about 3 in 100 on the drug.11,12
