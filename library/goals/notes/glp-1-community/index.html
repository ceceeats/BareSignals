GLP1 Community v1

First-hand reports of effects BEYOND weight loss, from outside Reddit. Collected 3 Nov 2026 (all pages accessed that day). What changed: first version.

How to read this

  • Sources: Mumsnet (weight-loss-injections, conception, alcohol support boards), HealthUnlocked (Endometriosis UK), diabetes.co.uk, Mayo Clinic Connect, Straight Dope boards, Drugs.com reviews, LessWrong/GreaterWrong self-experiment blogs, news features quoting named users (Boston Globe, NBC, NY Post, Today, Yorkshire Evening Post, Guardian readers, Dazed, SCMP, GMA, NYT via Futurism), published survey and social-media studies, one telehealth company survey.
  • Excluded: Reddit (other tool), vendor pages, MESO-Rx/AnabolicMinds, secondhand ("my friend"), people not yet on the drug. No suppliers named.
  • Tags: [reported] = community or news self-report. [verified] = primary data (published study, preprint, conference abstract, label). Preprints and abstracts are flagged as such.
  • "Weight first?" column: did the change start before meaningful weight loss. This is the key test for "drug or weight".
  • Counts are separate people, deduplicated by username across threads (some Mumsnet regulars post in many threads, e.g. CobaltRewind, Caffeineneedednow, HansHolbein, SilenceInside, MooBaggage; each counted once per claim).
  • CONSISTENT = 5+ separate people.
  • Big bias warning: about 85% of community reports here are Mounjaro (tirzepatide) users on UK Mumsnet, mostly women 35 to 60. Threads are self-selected by title ("unexpected positive side effects", "feeling depressed"), so both positive and negative threads overcount their own direction. Doctor or test confirmation is rare (under 5% of reports).

1. Alcohol cravings

Separate first-hand reports: about 58 less/aversion, about 8 no change or more. CONSISTENT. Drugs: mostly Mounjaro, also Wegovy, Ozempic, Saxenda (liraglutide), retatrutide. What worked [reported]: - Self-described "functioning alcoholic" at 60 to 80 units/week now has one G&T on Saturdays, no weeknight craving. Another at 50 to 60 units/week with morning cravings dropped to 5 to 10 units. Mounjaro. Mumsnet alcohol support: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/alcohol_support/5415397-mounjaro-alcohol-addiction - Woman drinking about 4 bottles of wine a week lost the craving from the first dose, at only 2.5 to 5 mg. Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5512586-do-weight-loss-injections-switch-off-alcohol-noise - Leeds retiree (Julie Lord, 56) whose GP had flagged harmful drinking went from a bottle a night to half a glass within a week on Wegovy, with little weight lost. Cravings came back about 6 weeks after stopping. Yorkshire Evening Post, about Aug 2026: https://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/health/i-thought-i-was-taking-wegovy-to-lose-weight-i-never-expected-it-to-stop-my-drinking-8923501 - Retired engineer on Ozempic went from about 15 drinks/week to 5; "takes effort to finish a drink". Boston Globe, 3 Feb 2025: https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/02/03/business/obesity-drugs-ozempic-wegovy-glp-drinking-alcohol-gambling-online-shopping - Two posters say the effect PERSISTED after stopping (teetotal 9 months after a 6-month course; 2 months off). Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5535564-glp-1-has-turned-me-into-a-normal-drinker - Speed: often from dose 1 or within the first week. Weight first? Yes in several (Julie Lord, first-dose reports). - Form of the effect splits two ways: (a) "the want is gone" and (b) physical intolerance (one glass makes them drunk or sick, worse hangovers). Diabetes.co.uk tirzepatide user and a retatrutide self-experimenter both describe (b). Clinician-observed [reported]: MGH obesity doctor Chika Anekwe says about 10 of her patients volunteered they drink less (Boston Globe above). Primary data [verified]: Scientific Reports, 28 Nov 2023 (Bickel lab, Virginia Tech). Reddit mining found 1,580 alcohol posts from 962 users, 71.7% describing reduced craving/use; separate survey n=153 showed fewer drinks and lower binge odds on semaglutide or tirzepatide vs controls. https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1030586 Negatives: one Wegovy user drinks more (wine is her main calorie source); one says desire crept back in maintenance; 4 no change in one thread; one reports drinking more often on Mounjaro. Relapse on dose taper reported by one heavy drinker (5415397).

2. Mood up (calmer, happier, less self-loathing)

Separate reports: about 30 better. CONSISTENT. Also see 3 (mood down), which is just as consistent. Drug: mostly Mounjaro, also Saxenda, Wegovy. What worked [reported]: - Thread "Anyone else calmer on Mounjaro": 10 of 11 first-hand posters calmer, quieter self-critical voice, deeper sleep. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5196127-anyone-else-notice-they-are-calmer-on-mounjaro - "Improved mental health": 10 better, 3 worse. One calmer and less irritable within a week, before much weight loss; one less anxious "from day 1" at BMI 29.5. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5387163-improved-mental-health - Type 2 diabetic on NHS tirzepatide describes euphoria and urges to sing within days of dose 1, still there at 15 weeks. diabetes.co.uk, Oct 2025: https://www.diabetes.co.uk/forum/threads/positive-side-effects-from-tirzepatide-mounjaro.210496/ - Self-harm urges much reduced (one poster). Mumsnet 5277004. - Antidepressant reduction: one poster halved fluoxetine (40 to 20 mg), another reducing antidepressants after endo improvement. Neither says a doctor confirmed. Weight first? Several say yes (days to 1 week). Many tie it to relief from food guilt, which is weight-adjacent. Negatives: see section 3.

3. Mood down, flat, anhedonia ("Ozempic personality")

Separate reports: about 40 flat/low/numb. CONSISTENT. Clinician-observed: yes, several doctors. What was reported [reported]: - "Low mood/feeling nothing": 10 of 13 flat or numb, none with prior depression stated; 3 recovered after dropping dose. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5472351-mounjaro-and-low-moodfeeling-nothing - "Wondering if Mounjaro is making me depressed": 10 of 15 worse ("flat, lacklustre, no Christmas excitement"). https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5226923-im-starting-to-wonder-if-mounjaro-is-making-me-feel-depressed - "Complete loss of mojo": 8 of 10 flat/low motivation; one improved after dropping 5 to 2.5 mg. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5538646-complete-loss-of-mojo-anyone-else - Florida woman (51) on Zepbound 15 mg lost joy in sunsets and sport over 2 years; joy back within 2 weeks of dropping to 12.5 mg. Boston Globe, 16 Apr 2026: https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/04/16/lifestyle/ozempic-personality-dull-joy/ - Podcaster Dave Knapp stopped following his team after 3 years on Mounjaro; drive returned on lower-dose Zepbound. NBC Washington/TODAY, 19 May 2026: https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/health/ozempic-personality-patient-troubling-glp-1-mental-health-impact/4106119/ - Two people say Wegovy kept their mood and focus while Mounjaro flattened them. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5516424-anyone-found-wegovy-improves-functioning-but-mounjaro-helps-more-with-weight-loss - Retatrutide self-experimenter at BMI 20: strong productivity at 1 mg, then apathy by week 7. GreaterWrong: https://www.greaterwrong.com/posts/J8nvbu5AFqR5ysSDR/a-9-week-trip-on-retatrutide Pattern: dose-linked, usually starts weeks to months in, commonly lifts within weeks of a dose cut. Some feel a dip on day 1 to 2 after each shot. Clinician-observed [reported]: Spencer Nadolsky (obesity doctor) sees it, says most resolve within weeks of dose reduction, uses bupropion for persistent cases, compiling about 100 case studies with Zak Krumm (U Florida) (Boston Globe; MindSite News 22 Apr 2026 https://mindsitenews.org/2026/04/22/could-glp-1-be-dulling-your-joy/). Psychiatrist Farid Sabet-Sharghi sees patients feeling "less alive" at higher doses (KevinMD, 30 Dec 2025 https://kevinmd.com/2025/12/glp-1-psychological-side-effects-a-psychiatrists-view.html). Christopher McGowan calls it uncommon; far more patients report better mood. Primary data [verified, secondhand via news]: Lancet analysis of about 95,000 people linked semaglutide to lower risk of worsening depression/anxiety (Boston Globe summary, not read in original). Novo says anhedonia is not a listed adverse reaction; Lilly has no data. Weight first? Not applicable; this rises with dose and time.

4. Food noise quiet

Separate reports: 15+ in my sample (I did not target it; it is in almost every thread). CONSISTENT. [reported] Examples: ADHD poster quieter head on day 2 (Mumsnet 5194885); Zepbound user cut online browsing and "freed mental capacity" (Boston Globe); Guardian reader with PCOS freed from food preoccupation (Guardian readers, 25 Mar 2023 via https://uk.news.yahoo.com/unbearable-side-effects-cravings-curbed-070042955.html). [verified] JAMA Network Open 2026 (Hunt et al., qualitative, n=30, interviews Jul to Sep 2025): majority reported a significant drop in food noise and compulsive eating. https://www.news-medical.net/news/20260608/GLP-1-users-say-the-drugs-quiet-food-noise-but-do-not-replace-lifestyle-change.aspx [verified, conference poster, not peer reviewed] Top 100 #FoodNoise TikToks (Penn State, ECO 2025): about half mention medication, 92% of those GLP-1s, 71% are patient testimonies. https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/how-much-noise-is-there-about-food-noise-on-tiktok Negative: a psychiatrist worries quieting food noise can mask eating disorders in young patients (KevinMD).

Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5277004-unexpected-positive-side-effects-of-mj - RA and fibromyalgia pain dropped within a week of the first shot; RA now in remission; rheumatologist "interested". Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5221961-reduction-in-pain-do-we-know-why - Joint stiffness eased from day 2 with about 7 lb lost by day 9. Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5118838-what-other-benefits-have-you-found-from-taking-mj - Zepbound user: "within a few days all my joint pain went away"; a Wegovy patient no longer needed extra RA medication. NYT, 18 Jun 2024 (via Futurism/Yahoo): https://au.news.yahoo.com/doctors-surprised-patients-start-semaglutide-170020809.html - Pain returned after stopping in several (hip, morphine-level chronic pain). https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5530726-anyone-using-mounjaro-mainly-for-pain-and-inflammation-relief?page=1 NEW sub-claim, plantar fasciitis: 5 separate people say long-standing plantar fasciitis vanished, two within days and one says it had persisted through earlier weight loss (5277004, 5301591 https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5301591-other-advantages-to-taking-mounjaro?page=3, 5468982). Strongest "not just weight" signal in this file. Weight first? Yes in at least 7 reports (day 1 to 1 week). Clinician/test: one inflammation marker 8.53 to 0.7 after about 11 months and 7 stone lost (weight-confounded) https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5528979-has-anyone-found-weight-loss-injections-seem-to-reduce-inflammation?page=2 Primary data [reported via clinic page, needs Lilly source]: retatrutide TRIUMPH-4 in knee osteoarthritis, about 75% pain-score drop, 1 in 8 pain-free, about 71 lb lost at 68 weeks (May 2026) https://www.clinicalnutritioncenter.com/research/retatrutide-phase-3-weight-loss-knee-pain-denver. [verified, conference abstract, no numbers given] ACR 2025: fewer RA flares on DMARD plus GLP-1 or SGLT2 vs DMARD alone. Negatives: 4 of 5 Straight Dope users with arthritis or general aches saw no change (Mar 2026, https://boards.straightdope.com/t/anti-inflammatory-effects-of-wegovy/1028793); arthritis no change at 10 mg; EDS pain unchanged; knee flare; new night cramps; Mayo Connect thread of neuropathy and joint pain on Mounjaro that eased on stopping (https://connect.mayoclinic.org/comment/1257759).

6. IBS and gut (excluding usual GI side effects)

Separate reports: 11 better. CONSISTENT. [reported] IBS of 30 years "seemingly gone" by week 2; IBS gone in 5 days with calmer mood; up to 10 diarrhoea episodes a day resolved; bowels regular for the first time in 20 years (5277004, 5530726, 5301591). One stopped a PPI after 7 years (reflux) (Mumsnet 5201047 https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5201047-things-the-jab-has-helpedcured-thats-not-weight-loss). Weight first? Yes for the 5-day and 2-week reports. Negatives: one swapped IBS for constipation; IBS returned off drug (2); nausea, constipation and reflux are common label side effects; retatrutide blogger had reflux and vomiting.

7. Asthma (and NOT hayfever)

Separate reports: 15 asthma better, 3 no change. CONSISTENT. Hayfever: 0 better, 8 no change, 1 worse. [reported] Stopped all inhalers after one week, none in 6 months (do not copy without a doctor); reliever used once in 18 months; asthma better early "before weight loss"; repeated chest infections and hospital stays stopped over 15 months, consultant now reviewing tests. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5408732-glp-1s-to-treat-asthma and https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5512633-mounjaro-and-asthmaallergies Fun fact angle: lung airway inflammation improves in many reports while pollen allergy does not change at all. Inferred: points to a metabolic/airway effect, not an anti-allergy effect. No peak flow or doctor-confirmed reductions reported.

8. Sleep, snoring, apnoea

Separate reports: about 14 better, about 6 worse or no change. CONSISTENT. [reported] Apnoea greatly reduced within 3 weeks in someone who had apnoea at a lower weight before (5201047); snoring stopped before weight loss (5468982); teeth grinding and snoring down (5118838). [verified, from label knowledge, recheck] Zepbound is FDA-approved for moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnoea with obesity (SURMOUNT-OSA, Dec 2024). Negatives: insomnia early (several), sleep down to 4 hours, poorer sleep on retatrutide, apnoea unchanged after a stone lost.

9. ADHD symptoms and focus

Separate reports: about 22 better, about 9 no change or worse. CONSISTENT, but mixed. What worked [reported]: - Thread "ADHD? The noises turned down": 6 of 6 improved, diagnosed ADHD poster noticed a quieter head and less sound sensitivity on day 2. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5194885-other-positive-effects-of-mj-adhd-the-noises-turned-down - "Discussion about ADHD": 7 of 10 better; one diagnosed on Elvanse says thoughts more ordered within a week, no weight lost yet. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5278916-can-we-have-a-discussion-about-adhd-mounjuro - One says Saxenda and Wegovy helped her ADHD but Mounjaro did not (5516424). Negatives: in "ADHD and WLI", 3 of 6 said focus got WORSE (lost food dopamine, sought it elsewhere), 2 no change, one on Elvanse with no change but binge eating stopped. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5346190-adhd-and-wli. A LessWrong commenter with ADHD on dextroamphetamine saw no executive change on oral semaglutide. Pattern: impulsivity and "mental noise" improve more often than executive function. Weight first? Often yes (days to 1 week). No psychiatrist confirmation found.

10. Impulse shopping, gambling, nicotine, other compulsions

Shopping: 11 better, 1 no change, 1 rebound after stopping. CONSISTENT. Gambling: 2. Nicotine/smoking: 3 better, 1 no change. Not consistent yet. [reported] Zepbound user cut Amazon browsing (10 to 12 hours a week) and purchases by at least 80%; Wegovy user lost interest in $3,000 to $4,000/month slot play and cut nicotine pouches 15 to 5 a day (Boston Globe, Feb 2025). Mumsnet: less online shopping and scratchcards from month 1 (5118838); quit smoking 6 days after starting with no cravings (5118838); "nicotine addiction ended overnight" (5201047). Nail biting and hair twiddling slowed (one). Excess codeine dropped (one). Morphine tapered over 3 months in a deeply infiltrating endo patient (see 11). [verified] Wharton/Penn MRI study of GLP-1 and spending decisions started Oct 2026, no results yet (CBS, 8 Oct 2026).

11. Endometriosis pain

Separate reports: 8 better, 1 too early. CONSISTENT (just). What worked [reported]: - First period on Mounjaro at about 10% of usual endo pain (5221961). - 4 of 4 in "Mounjaro endometriosis short cycles": first pain-free period since 17 after one cycle; first pain-free period ever at month 10 (says it is not the weight); deep endo patient off morphine after 15 years over about 3 months; cycle lengthened 23 to 29 days. https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5119709-mounjaro-endometriosis-short-cycles - Clinician-overseen case: tirzepatide at a very low dose after excision, less bloating, brain fog and flares, back to in-person work; surgeon Chandra Spring-Robinson. NBC Connecticut, 19 Aug 2026: https://www.nbcconnecticut.com/news/health/beyond-the-scale-endometriosis-patients-report-relief-after-using-glp-1s/3766426/ - Stage 4 patient says Ozempic halted endo growth and shortened/lightened periods (no scan cited). Rescripted, 14 Jul 2026: https://rescripted.com/posts/glp-1-and-endometriosis-will-your-ozempic-prescription-calm-your-endo Primary data [verified, preprint, not peer reviewed]: GLEAM survey (Baudoin, van Bakel, Research Square, 14 Jul 2026), n=227 with endo/adenomyosis on GLP-1: 63% met the improvement criterion, 84% improved in at least one domain. Improvement was NOT linked to amount of weight lost (OR 1.02 per % lost); normal-BMI, under-5%-loss subgroup 12 of 14 improved; higher BMI improved less. https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10323555/v1 Weight first? Yes in 2 forum cases plus the GLEAM pattern. Negatives: one Endometriosis UK poster on Mounjaro plus Ryeqo had near-continuous bleeding and GP suspected an interaction (HealthUnlocked https://healthunlocked.com/endometriosis-uk/posts/151907050/ryeqo-and-the-never-ending-period-mounjaro). No worse-pain reports found.

12. PCOS cycles and symptoms

Separate reports: about 10 better, 3 no change. CONSISTENT. [reported] Periods back after about 10 years plus spots and unwanted hair gone (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5325025-pcos); regular cycles and hair slowly improving; regular 28-day cycle at 6 months; two Texas women with regular cycles for the first time, one now ovulating, prescribed by a doctor who had "a couple of women with PCOS" on it (GMA, 6 Jun 2023 https://ws.goodmorningamerica.com/wellness/story/women-struggle-pcos-find-ozempic-mounjaro-99729389). PCOS monthly pain largely gone. Weight first? Mostly no: most had lost 2 to 6 stone. One period returned after only 7 lb. Negatives: classic PCOS with no natural periods, 15 kg lost, still no return (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5551843-has-anyone-with-classic-pcos-no-natural-periods-had-their-cycle-return-on-mounjaro); acne and facial hair unchanged at 12 weeks; mild hirsutism unchanged.

13. Period changes (all women)

Separate reports: about 30 changed. Direction is MIXED; consistent only as "periods change". [reported] Returned after years (implant, pill or menopause), lighter, cycles shortened, less painful (5214812 https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5214812-anyone-on-mounjaro-had-their-periods-stop-or-change; 5406115; 5474816). Negatives: missed or stopped (6), heavier or more painful (3), breakthrough bleeding on the pill or mini pill (3), irregular bleeding. A post-menopausal woman had a full period at 5 mg; another had a bleed and an endometrial biopsy (clear) (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5228577-mounjaro-can-reverse-menopause). Safety line: any post-menopausal bleed needs a doctor. [verified] Nature Health, 10 Apr 2026 (Penn, Sehgal/Guntuku): 400,000+ Reddit posts from about 70,000 users; nearly 4% described reproductive symptoms (irregular cycles, heavy or intermenstrual bleeding). https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1123370

14. Fertility and surprise pregnancies

Separate reports: 7 pregnancies (5 surprise or after infertility). CONSISTENT (just). [reported] Pregnant 2 months into Ozempic while on the pill, with a history of fertility issues (Dazed, 25 Mar 2024 https://www.dazeddigital.com/beauty/article/62257/1/ozempic-users-are-getting-pregnant-despite-being-on-birth-control); woman who thought she could not have more children conceived on Ozempic then Mounjaro (SCMP/Bloomberg); pregnant after about 6 weeks, healthy 12-week scan, doctor not concerned (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/conception/5185639-has-anyone-fallen-pregnant-on-mourjano); PCOS woman conceived after one dose (later miscarried) (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/conception/5197465-mounjaro-and-pcos-ttc). Two mechanisms show up: restored ovulation (PCOS, weight) and reduced oral contraceptive absorption. [verified, label knowledge, recheck wording] The Mounjaro/Zepbound label advises women on oral contraceptives to switch to non-oral or add a barrier method for 4 weeks after starting and after each dose increase. Negatives: 5 years TTC, 14 kg lost in 3 months, no pregnancy yet; Wegovy user's cycles shortened but no pregnancy yet (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/conception/5336661-weight-loss-jabs-infertility).

15. Libido

Up: 5 (2 Mumsnet, 1 Mumsnet perimenopausal, 2 NY Post). Down: 2 plus psychiatrist observation. CONSISTENT for "up" (just), weak. [reported] Libido up on Mounjaro including one whose libido had been suppressed by sertraline (Mumsnet https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5178342-things-i-dont-want-to-do-since-starting-mounjaro?page=2); 53-year-old post-menopausal woman, libido up at 6 weeks; Zepbound user now sex about 4 times a week after a dose increase; Mounjaro user with depression-linked low libido saw a large increase (NY Post, 13 Feb 2025 via https://www.aol.com/wild-ways-ozempic-wreaking-havoc-182512362.html). Negatives: nurse on compounded tirzepatide lost libido the first week, fixed by a dose cut (same article); possible mild drop (one Mumsnet). Weight first? One yes (6 weeks), others after major loss.

16. Hot flushes / menopause symptoms

Better: 8. Worse: 2. CONSISTENT. [reported] Night sweats gone in week 1 before losing 2 lb, despite years of HRT not helping; "cool as a cucumber from the first dose" (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5269448-menopause-hot-flushes-have-disappeared). NEW sub-claim, feeling cold: 6+ people (Mumsnet, diabetes.co.uk, retatrutide blogger). [verified] Penn Nature Health study lists temperature effects (chills, feeling cold, hot flashes) as underreported. Negatives: hot flashes returned during weight loss in a post-menopausal woman; flushes more noticeable at 12.5 mg.

17. Migraine and headache

Better: 10. Worse or no change: 10. MIXED, no clear signal. [reported] Migraines stopped in week 1 before weight loss; 27 migraines in 10 weeks the year before vs 4 on Mounjaro; lifelong 2 to 3 a month gone for 4 months (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5393784-mj-seemingly-cured-my-migraines-anyone-else; https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5325902-migraines-mounjaro). Negatives: debilitating migraines leading to stopping; migraines a few days after each shot; new vestibular migraine; headache is a common label side effect. IIH patient: no change after 2 doses (neurologist supportive).

18. Psoriasis, eczema, skin

Psoriasis better: 6. CONSISTENT. Eczema better: 3, no change 1. Acne better 5, worse 4 (mixed). [reported] Psoriasis "almost fully cleared" (5301591); "dramatic" and "nearly cleared" (5528979); Mounjaro gave a boost to existing psoriasis treatment (Straight Dope). Venous and atopic eczema largely cleared at 4 to 6 weeks (diabetes.co.uk). Severe seborrhoeic dermatitis improved so much she barely uses the dermatologist cream (5301591). Acne negatives: acne and spotting back on the mini pill; jaw spots since starting (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/weight-loss-injections/5199229-mounjaro-has-stopped-the-mini-pill-from-working). [verified, conference] Hidradenitis suppurativa, semaglutide, retrospective n=30 (EADV 2024, Lyons, Dublin): flares every 8.5 weeks to every 12; DLQI 13 to 9; only 6 kg lost; confounded by biologics and metformin. https://www.healio.com/news/dermatology/20240925/semaglutide-improves-hidradenitis-suppurativa-outcomes

19. Swelling and fluid (ankles, lipoedema)

Better: 6. CONSISTENT. [reported] Hot swollen ankles and feet resolved in 2 to 4 weeks with BP down (5221961, 5201047); hand, wrist and ankle swelling gone (5528979). Lipoedema leg pain eased, with large weight loss.

20. Autoimmune and chronic illness (mixed bag)

About 9 reports, too varied to call consistent. [reported] RA remission (rheumatologist interested); MS standing tolerance and pain better at 10 mg, neurologist credits partly weight; long Covid near normal (2); fewer colds (1); levothyroxine dose reduced (1); DVT leg swelling and heat reduced (1). ME/CFS: 2 no change. Inflammatory arthritis: 1 no change. Crohn's: 1 just starting.

21. Hair loss (negative claim)

Hair loss: 9 reports, regrowth within months reported by 1. CONSISTENT as a NEGATIVE.
