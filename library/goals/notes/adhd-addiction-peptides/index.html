ADHD Addiction Peptides v1

Case page research for baresignals.co, seen from the ENDPOINT: which peptides people report helping ADHD and addiction, and what studies back them. Compiled 3 Nov 2026. What this file adds to ADHD Research v1 and Addiction Research v1: the community side (Reddit excluded), plus studies those files missed (marked NEW). Tags: [verified] = read in the primary abstract, registry entry or the original page. [reported] = secondary coverage or a forum post (all forum posts are [reported] by nature). [inferred] = my reasoning. [speculated] = plausible, untested. Source rules applied: own use only, no vendors, no supplier names, no MESO-Rx/AnabolicMinds, no Reddit (including Reddit mirror sites, which came up often and were skipped). Doses appear only where a study used them. Sources used this pass: Mumsnet (8 threads), LongeCity (7 threads), Skool communities (3 posts), BenzoBuddies, Autism UK community, Substack (2), LessWrong, Drugs.com reviews, Understood.org, NPR/KFF Health News, PBS NewsHour, VICE, AlterNet, Science-Based Medicine, WebMD, podcast pages (Attia/Lembke, Peptalk), PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, bioRxiv.

0. Headline findings

  1. GLP-1 drugs are the only "peptide" with both a big community signal and real trial data, and the signal is much stronger for drinking than for ADHD. Mumsnet alcohol threads ran about 30 improved vs 4 no change vs 1 worse. Mumsnet ADHD threads ran about 11 improved vs 6 mixed vs 5 no change vs 7 worse (counting the Mounjaro "flat/tired" posts). [reported, my tallies]
  2. NEW big study: US veterans with type 2 diabetes, GLP-1 vs SGLT-2 drugs (the honest comparator): new alcohol use disorder HR 0.82, opioid 0.75, cocaine 0.80, nicotine 0.80, cannabis 0.86; in people who already had a substance use disorder, SUD death HR 0.50 and overdose HR 0.61. [verified, Cai 2026, BMJ, PMID 41781010, doi:10.1136/bmj-2025-086886] This partly answers the Dai 2025 "no benefit vs SGLT2" concern in Addiction v1.
  3. NEW patient survey: of 1,179 people newly started on a GLP-1, 31.1% said they drank less and 6.0% said they drank more. [reported via news coverage of Uscher-Pines 2026, JAMA Psychiatry, PMID 42873517; abstract not available in PubMed] Plain meaning: about 1 in 3, not "everyone".
  4. NAD+ IV, the addiction-clinic favourite, has no controlled trial. The only human data are two uncontrolled 50-patient case series by one group selling a related protocol, plus a 7–8 rat abstract. Patient stories split: dramatic success stories in clinic and press features, and documented failures (seizure in withdrawal, relapse) in investigative reporting. [verified + reported, section 2]
  5. Semax is the peptide ADHD people talk about most outside Reddit, and reports are mostly positive (focus, drive, "clean" stimulation), but almost none come from diagnosed ADHD users, and one LongeCity user said it "potentiates any stimulant" on Vyvanse, which matches the rodent amphetamine data. [reported + verified animal]
  6. Selank, BPC-157, DSIP, Cerebrolysin, Dihexa, P21, Pinealon, Epitalon, MOTS-c, SS-31, kisspeptin: essentially zero non-Reddit first-hand reports for ADHD or addiction outcomes. Most discussion is vendor copy. [verified absence for searches run]

1. ADHD: peptide by peptide (what worked first)

1a. GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide)

What improved, in their words (paraphrased): "head quieter", "calmer", "less chaotic mind", "more organised", less impulsive, fewer urges for stimulation, less impulse shopping and social media, food noise gone. [reported] What did not: executive function and procrastination were the most common "no change" items; several said impulse control improved but task-starting did not. [reported] This matches Johns Hopkins psychiatrist David Goodman's view that GLP-1s may help impulsivity but not attention or executive function. [reported, Understood.org, updated 19 May 2026] Worse: flatness, low motivation, exhaustion, "dopamine-seeking increased", one poster "neurotransmitters out of whack" until going below the starting dose. A separate Mumsnet low-mood thread had 8 of 13 posters describing flatness on Mounjaro, 5 of whom improved after a dose cut or stopping. [reported] Retatrutide user on LessWrong: motivation strongly up at a mid dose, strongly down at the next step up, back on dose reduction. [reported] Matches Nadolsky 2026 (3 tirzepatide cases, flatness at the top dose easing at ≤10 mg). [verified, PMID 42518361, in Addiction v1] Speed: "head quieter" often within the first weeks; Wegovy OP reported change in week 1. Flatness tended to appear around month 2 or at dose steps; one at month 9. [reported] With stimulants: one Elvanse user called the combination "remarkable"; one Wegovy user on Elvanse said ADHD unchanged but binge eating stopped; one stopped Concerta while on Ozempic and executive function dropped. Pharmacy-article quotes (Reddit-sourced, so only noted): one cut Vyvanse because it felt "too intense", one said Adderall lasted longer and hurt sleep. [reported] No study of GLP-1 + stimulant interaction exists. Clinician cautions: stacked appetite loss and weight loss; absorption change thought unlikely for slow-release ADHD pills. [reported, Understood] Studies: no ADHD trial registered (ClinicalTrials.gov search 3 Nov 2026: 0 GLP-1 ADHD trials). [verified] Genetics shows no ADHD link. [verified, PMID 40141382, ADHD v1] Plain read: a real pattern of "quieter head, less impulsive eating and spending", mostly in women with ADHD and obesity, set against an equally real pattern of flatness on tirzepatide at higher doses. Not an ADHD treatment. [inferred]

1b. Semax (and NA-Semax amidate)

Community, non-Reddit: LongeCity 6 threads (~20 first-hand reports), Skool 1, Substack 1.

Thread First-hand Positive Mixed No effect Negative
"Semax experiences?" p1 (2014) 8 5 2 0 1 (flat, drowsy, detached)
"Semax experiences?" p2 7 users / 8 reports 5 2 1 0
"Semax review – 2 weeks" (on Vyvanse) 1 1
"Semax review" 1 1
"Semax, NA-Semax or NA-Semax amidate?" 1 1
"First time 0.1% Semax: bad, then awesome" 1 1 (panic, pounding heart, nausea for ~1 h after a high first dose, then calm clear focus)
"Consensus on Semax" (NA-Semax amidate) 1 user, 2 posts 1 (mild, "maybe placebo")
"NA Semax amidate + NA Selank" first time 1 1 (flow, confidence; brief anxiety)
Skool post (woman, years on ADD meds, now off them) 1 1
[all reported]
  • Totals: about 22 first-hand reports: ~16 positive, ~5 mixed, ~1 no effect, ~1 negative. [reported, my count] Diagnosed ADHD/ADD: only 3 (Vyvanse user, Skool woman, one "ADD symptoms did not worsen" coffee user). One more self-described attention difficulty (no effect at low dose, positive at higher). [reported]
  • What improved: focus, clarity, recall, verbal fluency, drive, mood lift, stress resistance; "alertness without the anxiety racetams give". [reported]
  • How fast: nasal: minutes ("near-instant", "within 10 minutes"), lasting 4–9 hours. Injected (under the skin): subtler, builds over a week. [reported] Fits rodent BDNF rise at 20–90 min. [verified, PMID 18756821, ADHD v1]
  • Negatives: insomnia (3), irritability (2), anxiety (2–3, including one on an SSRI), late-day sleepiness and vivid dreams, depersonalisation (1), panic after a high first dose (1). [reported]
  • With stimulants: the Vyvanse user said Semax boosted his stimulant without the usual irritability and that it "will potentiate any stimulant". One user taking Ritalin had anxiety (anxiety disorder). One user said it reduced his wish for other stimulants. [reported] Animal data: Semax before amphetamine raised mouse activity to 261% vs 182% amphetamine alone. [verified, Eremin 2004, PMID 15188751]
  • MAOI: one LongeCity user combined it with a low dose of the MAOI tranylcypromine plus NAC and reported calm with motivation loss and detachment. [reported] No MAO data exist for Semax; Wikipedia's sourced summary lists no MAO action. [verified absence] Semax inhibits enkephalin-breaking enzymes (IC50 ~10 µM). [reported, Kost 2001 via Wikipedia]
  • NA-Semax amidate specifically: 2 reports, both mild-positive, no potency comparison anywhere. [reported] Still no published data. [verified absence]
  • NEW study: Semax (60 nmol/kg daily, injected) reversed stress-induced anhedonia and restored hippocampal BDNF in rats. [verified, Inozemtseva 2024, Eur J Pharmacol, PMID 39442746, doi:10.1016/j.ejphar.2024.177068] Relevance: the "motivation" reports, not attention. A 2025 Alzheimer-mouse study showed cognition gains. [verified, PMID 41479572]

1c. Selank

  • Community: LongeCity 3 reports (anxiety down, mood up; one "little effect" by injection; one combined with NA-Semax). Substack comments: 1 helped racing thoughts, 1 no effect after a full cycle. [reported] No diagnosed-ADHD reports found outside Reddit. [verified absence]
  • NEW study: RCT-labelled, 70 anxiety patients: Selank added to phenazepam reduced the benzodiazepine's side effects (attention and memory impairment, sedation, emotional blunting) during and after withdrawal of the tranquiliser. [verified abstract, Medvedev 2015, Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr, PMID 26356395, doi:10.17116/jnevro20151156133-40; Russian-only full text] Relevance: cognitive side of anxiety, and benzo tapering (see 2d).

1d. Cortexin (NEW, missed in ADHD v1)

  • Russian polypeptide brain extract, injected. Chutko 2026: 40 children (12–14) with sluggish cognitive tempo, compared with 30 combined-ADHD and 30 healthy children; Cortexin 10 mg intramuscular every other day for 10 injections. Inattention, working memory, fatigue and reading improved in 82%, with EEG changes. No placebo arm. [verified abstract, PMID 42899917, doi:10.17116/jnevro202612609152]
  • Same group: children with ADHD plus problematic internet use had worse inattention and impulsivity (n=43 vs 40). Cortexin is a keyword, but no treatment result is in the abstract. [verified, PMID 41661012]
  • Community: none found. [verified absence]

1e. Cerebrolysin

  • Community: 1 recalled parent account (husband injected as a child in Eastern Europe, "woke up"); autism, not ADHD. [reported]
  • NEW study, autism not ADHD: 36 children, 18 intramuscular injections at 0.1 mL/kg over 2 months, no control group: CARS autism score 40.6 to 36.1 (−11%); 4 of 40 dropped for agitation and irritability. [verified, Ashrafi 2019, Iran J Pediatr] Plain read: same pattern as the ADHD studies: open-label, response near natural change plus therapies.

1f. Oxytocin

  • Registry: MGH adult ADHD cognitive control trial (NCT03136263, n=24, completed Nov 2020) still has no posted results; a second Israeli trial (NCT03788902, n=90) studied oxytocin levels and methylphenidate in ADHD children. [verified registry] Community: none. [verified absence]

1g. Dihexa, P21, Pinealon, DSIP, BPC-157, Epitalon, MOTS-c, SS-31, kisspeptin

  • No non-vendor first-hand ADHD reports found; search results were almost all seller blogs or AI-written "personal experience" pages, which I discarded. [verified absence] One LongeCity user stacked P21 with NA-Semax and NA-Selank and credited the stack with jitter-free focus. [reported] PubMed: no ADHD or attention study for any of them. [verified]

[reported, my tallies]
  • What changed: "alcohol noise gone", take-or-leave, stopping after 2–3 drinks, wine tastes "vile" or too sweet, feeling ill or drunk faster, worse hangovers, fewer hangovers because less drinking. Ex-heavy drinkers in the counts: ~50 units/week, ~2 bottles of wine/week, ~5 cases of beer/week, a bottle of vodka a day (PBS). [reported]
  • How fast: day one to week 3 in most who gave a time; "instant, days" for craving in the PBS crack-cocaine case. [reported] Fits trial data that the drink-size effect shows within weeks (Hendershot 2025). [verified, Addiction v1]
  • After stopping: split. One teetotal 9 months after stopping Mounjaro, one still uninterested 2 months off, one OP whose alcohol and food noise returned within a month off, one keeps desire away with low doses every 2–3 weeks. [reported] No trial has follow-up after stopping. [verified, Addiction v1 gap]
  • With recovery meds: none of the Mumsnet posters used naltrexone or AA alongside; one naltrexone user recommended it as the cheaper option. PBS cases combined GLP-1 with counselling. [reported]
  • Clinician-observed (count most, but note interest):
  • Caron Treatment Centers (Pennsylvania): more than 600 patients prescribed GLP-1s off-label over 2 years; medical lead, himself sober, calls it "not a silver bullet". [reported, PBS NewsHour 7 Oct 2026] Clinic with a treatment programme to sell, flag.
  • Rhode Island transitional-housing pilot (~18–20 residents): higher self-rated well-being and lower cravings, some relapsed and restarted. [reported, PBS + Wyoming Public Media Mar 2026] No outcome data published.
  • Stanford addiction psychiatrist Anna Lembke: one treatment-resistant patient on off-label semaglutide had "complete cessation" of craving; she warns of substitution, as after gastric bypass. [reported, Attia podcast page, auto-transcript]
  • Surveys (NEW to these files):
  • Uscher-Pines 2026 (JAMA Psychiatry, 1,179 new GLP-1 users): 31.1% drank less, 6.0% more. [reported]
  • Matero 2024 (JAMA Netw Open, ~14,000 WeightWatchers members on any anti-obesity drug including metformin): ~3,400 of ~7,500 drinkers (about 45%) cut down; heaviest drinkers far more likely to cut down; drop also seen on metformin. [reported via Henry Ford press release and NPR; PubMed record not found by my searches] Plain read: part of the "GLP-1 effect" is being in a weight programme.
  • Tyndall 2026, NIH All of Us (15,447 GLP-1 users): current users scored 5–11% lower on AUDIT-C than future users, driven by drinking frequency, not drinks per occasion or binges. [verified, PMID 42423024, doi:10.1111/acer.70357] Note: this is the opposite of the trial pattern (fewer drinks per drinking day). Population differences likely. [inferred]
  • Retatrutide (NEW): rats trained to recognise alcohol: semaglutide, tirzepatide and retatrutide all blunted alcohol's "felt" effect; retatrutide only at its top tested dose (0.3 mg/kg) and without slowing responses. Drinking not measured. [verified, bioRxiv preprint 2025, doi:10.1101/2025.04.17.649402; published version in Psychopharmacology reported, doi:10.1007/s00213-025-06854-3] This is the "less buzz" story people describe. One retatrutide blog promoting it was a seller page and is excluded.

2b. GLP-1 drugs: smoking, vaping, other compulsions

  • Community: 1 Mumsnet vaper said no change on Mounjaro; shopping less (1), fizzy-drink cravings down (3 of 4), food urges down (3). Smokers in the Hendershot trial cut cigarettes. [reported + verified]
  • NEW: veterans BMJ cohort, nicotine use disorder HR 0.80. [verified, PMID 41781010]
  • Gambling: a 2026 review lists GLP-1s among investigational drugs with no trial data. [verified, Bissig 2026, PMID 42147309]

2c. Amylin drugs (cagrilintide, salmon calcitonin) (NEW)

  • Rats: salmon calcitonin (an amylin-receptor agonist) added to ongoing dulaglutide cut alcohol intake in both sexes; started together, intake fell then tolerance developed. [verified, Aranäs 2024, PMID 39639536]
  • Rats: salmon calcitonin into the paraventricular thalamus cut alcohol intake in males (P=0.0048) but not females (P=0.90), and reduced alcohol-induced dopamine release. [verified, Aranäs 2026, PMID 41763298]
  • Novo Nordisk trial NCT06409130 (semaglutide, cagrilintide and a third agent in alcohol liver disease plus drinking, n=270) completed Nov 2025, results still not found. [verified registry, Addiction v1]
  • Community: none found. [verified absence]

2d. Selank

  • Community: BenzoBuddies thread on adding Selank to a clonazepam taper: 0 users actually tried it. [reported] No alcohol or nicotine reports found. [verified absence]
  • NEW animal: Selank blocked alcohol-induced hyperactivity and the expression of behavioural sensitisation in mice, similar to naloxone. [verified, Kolik 2016, PMID 27878720]
  • Human relevance: the phenazepam add-on RCT (section 1c) is the closest human withdrawal-adjacent data. [verified]

2e. BPC-157

  • Community: no first-hand non-Reddit report of BPC-157 for cravings or withdrawal; one recovery-coach podcast guest used it only for dental healing. [reported]
  • NEW animal (Zagreb group): in mice, BPC-157 countered acute alcohol intoxication (anaesthesia, hypothermia, 25% deaths) and reduced withdrawal seizures after 13 days of drinking. [verified, Blagaic 2004, PMID 15381050; 2005, PMID 16369461] It also blunted morphine pain relief in mice (relevant if anyone uses it alongside opioid pain control). [verified, PMID 20388962]
  • Still no study of drug seeking, craving or relapse. [verified absence]

2f. DSIP

  • Only the 1984 uncontrolled Swiss series (107 withdrawal inpatients). [verified, Addiction v1] One recovery podcast mentioned DSIP only for sleep during opioid withdrawal. [reported] FDA advisers rejected it for compounding July 2026. [reported, ADHD v1]

2g. NAD+ (IV), the addiction-clinic claim

Claims (clinic and clinic-adjacent, commercially interested): cravings gone in days, 70–90% craving-free by day 5–10, withdrawal symptoms down 70–80% on day 1, 7% relapse. [reported, KFF Health News/NPR 2019; AlterNet 2014] Typical course 10–15 days of infusions, about $15,000 in one Indiana clinic. [reported] What the published human data are: - Blum 2022 and Blum 2024: the same 50 poly-drug patients, infusions of NAD+ plus enkephalinase-inhibiting amino acids and B vitamins, at least 7 infusions over ~4 weeks. Craving, anxiety and depression Likert scores fell (P<10⁻⁹ for craving); 40 of 40 urine tests midway were negative. No control group, counsellor-collected self-ratings, NAD+ mixed with other ingredients, and authors linked to a company selling a related product. [verified, PMID 36118157, doi:10.2174/2666082218666220527114427; PMID 39949994] The 2024 paper states ~1,000 infusions in 900 patients without serious side effects. [verified] - Rats (LSU, 7 + 8 rats): IV NAD+ cut oxycodone-withdrawal disruption from 4 days to 2. Conference abstract. [verified abstract] - 1960s–1980s: an informal AA trial (20 of 30 freed of anxiety/depression) and a claimed 11,000-patient basis for a 1961 patent, neither verifiable. [reported, AlterNet] - ClinicalTrials.gov: no NAD+ trial for any addiction or withdrawal outcome. [verified, searched 3 Nov 2026] First-hand reports (non-Reddit): - Positive (4+): VICE writer (one infusion, ~9 days of better energy and mood, no alcohol cravings in his quit week; chest tightness and headache during the drip); 2 anonymous patients in the same piece (codeine, crack, heroin withdrawal easier); a former footballer (fortnightly drips, a year dry, mood and energy up; press account only); 5 clinic-supplied patients in AlterNet. [reported] - Negative (3): Indiana patient had hallucinations and seizures in withdrawal, hospitalised 3 days, relapsed at ~3 months; a second had a seizure when cut off from alcohol, drank again, DUI arrest; a man in the clinic's own promo video said it did not work and was later jailed. [reported, KFF Health News/NPR 23 Aug 2019] - Infusion side effects named: chest tightness, headache, flu-like feelings. [reported] Expert view: "no published evidence to show that NAD therapy has any benefit in the treatment of addiction" (pharmacist reviewer); addiction psychiatrists called the claims unrealistic and the brain-scan brochures "totally bogus". [verified page, Science-Based Medicine, Dec 2021; KFF] A UK addiction specialist was "very impressed with the short-term results" and called for outcome studies. [reported, VICE 2016] Plain read: NAD+ IV has the strongest marketing signal in addiction and the weakest evidence signal of anything here. The most serious documented harm is not NAD itself but alcohol or benzodiazepine withdrawal handled without standard medical detox. [inferred]

2h. Oxytocin, kisspeptin, Semax, Cerebrolysin, Epitalon, MOTS-c, SS-31, Pinealon, Dihexa, P21

  • Oxytocin: the 100-person AUD RCT was null (Addiction v1). No non-Reddit first-hand reports. [verified + verified absence]
  • Semax: no addiction study; no first-hand addiction reports outside Reddit. [verified absence]
  • The rest: no addiction study in PubMed and no community reports. [verified absence]
  • Blends: recovery-coach podcast (Peptalk ep. 33): guest in recovery from alcohol and stimulants describes blending low-dose GLP-1s for clients in recovery, reports retatrutide dimmed libido and mood in some clients, and advises 30 days sober before adding peptides. Hosts are peptide-business affiliates. [reported, flag commercial]

3. Interactions and warnings found this pass

  • Semax + stimulants: one user reported stimulant potentiation on Vyvanse; rodent data show amphetamine effect amplified (261% vs 182%). Expect more stimulation, insomnia, anxiety. [reported + verified animal; human size speculated]
  • Semax + MAOI: one user report (calm, flat, detached); no pharmacology data. [reported; verified absence]
  • Semax first dose: one LongeCity report of a ~1-hour panic, racing heart and nausea after a high first dose. [reported]
  • GLP-1 + stimulants: additive appetite loss; some users report stimulants feeling stronger or lasting longer; untested. [reported]
  • GLP-1 flatness: 8 of 13 Mounjaro posters in one thread, mostly easing with dose cuts; 5 Mounjaro "worse" posts vs 2 Wegovy "better" in another. [reported] Relevant to ADHD users, whose baseline motivation is already fragile. [inferred]
  • Unsupervised GLP-1 and eating disorders: a 21-year-old woman given tirzepatide by an online service fell from 47 to 41 kg, developed starvation ketosis, and was then diagnosed with anorexia nervosa. [verified, PMID 42243968] ADHD carries a 3.8x eating-disorder risk (ADHD v1).
  • NAD+ IV in withdrawal: documented seizures and hallucinations when alcohol/benzodiazepine withdrawal was treated with NAD alone. Alcohol withdrawal needs medical detox. [reported, KFF]
  • BPC-157 + opioid pain relief: blunted morphine analgesia in mice. [verified animal]
  • Retatrutide: heart rate rose ~15 bpm above baseline in one user's 9-week log. [reported]
