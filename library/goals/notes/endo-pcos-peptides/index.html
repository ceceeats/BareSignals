Endo PCOS Peptides v1

Community and study pass for the endometriosis and PCOS goal pages on baresignals.co. Compiled 3 Nov 2026 (Bali). What changed: new file. It builds on Endometriosis Research v1 and PCOS Research v1 and does not replace them. Reddit was not used. MESO-Rx and AnabolicMinds were not used. No vendor or supplier pages were used as evidence. Work done: about 30 web searches, about 40 page fetches, 16 PubMed queries plus 4 metadata pulls, 3 trial registry queries. Tags: [verified] = I read it in the primary source this pass (abstract, registry, patent text or the forum post itself). [reported] = a secondary source says it, or it is a self-report I could not check. [inferred] = my reasoning from verified facts. [speculated] = plausible but untested. How to read the counts: one person counts once, even when they posted in several threads. "First-hand" means the poster describes their own use. Forum posters are self-selected, unblinded and on other treatments too.

0. One-screen summary

Endometriosis - GLP-1 drugs are the only "peptide" with a real community signal. On Mumsnet, HealthUnlocked, news features and podcasts I counted 9 first-hand users: 7 better, 1 no change, 1 worse. Most were on tirzepatide (Mounjaro). Reported gains: period pain (two "first pain-free period" reports), bloating, flares, brain fog, fatigue. Several said the change came within the first cycle. None had a scan or surgery confirming any lesion change. [reported] - A new survey this pass (GLEAM preprint, Jul 2026, n = 227): 63% met the primary endpoint for improvement and 84% reported improvement in at least one symptom area. Improvement did not track with how much weight people lost. In normal-BMI people who lost under 5% of their weight, 12 of 14 improved. This is a separate survey from the McMaster (Leonardi) survey in Endo v1, and the two should not be merged. [verified, preprint abstract] - Thymosin alpha-1 has an industry-run phase 3 trial (missed in v1). It is registered in India (CTRI/2023/06/054279), and the results so far appear only in the sponsor's patent: n = 120, pain score 6.35 → 1.88 vs 5.88 → 2.5 with standard care alone. It has not been peer reviewed, and the company sells the drug. [verified as patent text] - LDN: 1 first-hand patient story (it is also a promotional charity's page), 1 clinician case, and a pain clinic cohort where 66.7% of pelvic pain patients responded (subgroup size not given, endometriosis not named). Practical warning from the forums: naltrexone blocks opioid painkillers around surgery. [reported/verified as tagged] - BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, GHK-Cu, LL-37, blends: zero first-hand endometriosis reports found anywhere outside Reddit. The blood-vessel growth concern stands. [verified absence]

PCOS - GLP-1 again dominates. I counted 21 first-hand users (Mumsnet, a TV news feature, a Substack). Almost all reported weight loss. On cycles, 5 said periods came back or became regular, 1 had a single bleed then nothing, 1 reported worse period pain and 1 had early spotting. On skin and hair, 3 improved and 2 did not change. No one had a doctor-confirmed ovulation result. [reported] - New study this pass (missed in PCOS v1): a 2026 meta-analysis of 3 RCTs (n = 252) found that GLP-1 drugs (± metformin) vs metformin tripled natural pregnancy (OR 3.10) but did not improve IVF pregnancy. [verified] PMID 42893679 - Naltrexone (full dose, 50 mg) has older small PCOS trials that v1 missed: cycles improved in 8 of 10 women, and it restored clomiphene response so that 9 of 27 conceived. This is not LDN, but it is the best human data for any opioid blocker in PCOS. [verified] - MOTS-c, NAD+/NMN, SS-31: rodent or cell data only. No PCOS user reports found. GH peptides (CJC/ipamorelin, MK-677, tesamorelin): no PCOS reports found, and clinicians flag rising blood sugar and A1c. [verified/reported]

Surveys - GLEAM (Baudoin and van Bakel; Research Square preprint dated 14 Jul 2026; pre-registered on OSF; anonymous international survey; n = 227 with endometriosis or adenomyosis who had used a GLP-1) [verified, abstract page]: - 63% (95% CI 57 to 69) met the primary endpoint: better on the global rating plus at least one symptom area. - 84% improved in at least one area. - Weight lost was not linked to improvement (OR 1.02 per % lost, p = 0.22). - Higher starting BMI meant less improvement (OR 0.94 per BMI unit). - Normal BMI with under 5% weight loss: 12 of 14 improved (descriptive only). - Per-symptom percentages (pain, bowel, bladder, fatigue) were not on the page I could open. The authors call it exploratory, with self-selection and recall bias. - McMaster / Leonardi survey (in Endo v1: 64.6% improved at least one symptom, 33.7% had complete resolution of one, 60.3% relapsed after stopping) [reported]: a recruitment poster on endometriosisnetwork.com confirms the study exists (ages 18 to 55, diagnosed or suspected). n is still not found. Two different surveys, both preprint level. [verified existence]

Studies (new this pass) - Peritoneal fluid of women with endometriosis had lower GLP-1 (and ghrelin, glucagon, visfatin) than women with fibroids (n = 54 vs 30). Macrophage markers correlated. [verified] Krasnyi 2022, PMID 36142272, doi:10.3390/ijms231810361. This is the "single non-replicated" study the tirzepatide hypothesis paper rests on (Endo v1, PMID 42449952). - No GLP-1 endometriosis trial is registered on ClinicalTrials.gov (searched 3 Nov 2026). [verified absence] PubMed has no animal lesion study of semaglutide, liraglutide or exenatide in endometriosis. [verified absence] - JAMA Netw Open 2026 (Yen et al., n = 444,820): GLP-1 plus progestin was linked with lower endometrial cancer risk (HR 0.34) and fewer hysterectomies in benign uterine disease. Endometriosis was not studied. Do not cite it as endometriosis evidence. [verified] - Reviews (2026) say GLP-1 drugs act on PI3K/AMPK and inflammation in endometriosis, mostly preclinical. [verified] PMID 42789930, PMID 41798441

Risks seen in the community - Oral hormone absorption: endometriosis is often treated with oral progestins or Ryeqo. Three posts link starting tirzepatide to breakthrough bleeding or worry about the pill (posts 6 and 9 above; a Mumsnet OP with an endometrioma kept in check by desogestrel). The tirzepatide label advises backup contraception with oral contraceptives after starting and after each dose increase (PCOS v1). [verified label point via PCOS v1; inferred extension to progestin suppression] - Bowel: slowed gut emptying can mimic or worsen endometriosis bloating and constipation. [reported, Brighten, Seckin]

1.2 Thymosin alpha-1 (new, missed in v1)

  • Phase 3 trial, India: open-label, randomised, two arms, thymosin alpha-1 + standard care vs standard care alone, women aged 18 to 49 with laparoscopy- or imaging-confirmed endometriosis up to stage III. Registry CTRI/2023/06/054279. A mirror shows "active, not recruiting" with 0 enrolled and no results, so the record may be incomplete. [verified, registry mirror]
  • Results appear in the sponsor's Indian patent application 202321069111 (filed Oct 2023, published 2025, not yet examined) [verified, patent text]:
  • n = 120. Dose 1.6 mg under the skin twice weekly for 90 days, then 3 months of follow-up.
  • Pain VAS: 6.35 → 1.88 on thymosin + standard care vs 5.88 → 2.5 on standard care alone. The labels on the page are muddled, so this needs checking against the filing.
  • Period pain, painful sex and painful bowel movements are said to improve more on thymosin. Non-period pelvic pain improved on thymosin (p = 0.023) but not on standard care alone (p = 0.135).
  • No safety results shown.
  • Bias flags: the sponsor is the drug's manufacturer, the trial was open-label, the results are in a patent, and it has no peer review or PubMed record (PubMed: 0 hits for thymosin alpha-1 + endometriosis). [verified]
  • Related: a 2026 retrospective series (n = 102) from the same company with a fertility clinic gave thymosin alpha-1 for recurrent implantation failure. 60.8% became pregnant, with no control group and no endometriosis subgroup. [verified] IJRMS Jun 2026. A US registry trial of thymosin alpha-1 for implantation failure also exists (NCT07675980; not opened). [reported]
  • Community: 0 first-hand endometriosis reports. [verified absence]
  • [inferred] This is the strongest-looking human signal for any classic "research peptide" in endometriosis, and also the least independent. Grade it C- pending publication.

1.3 Low dose naltrexone (not a peptide)

  • First-hand: "Shauna" (LDN Research Trust interview, Mar 2020) had endometriosis + PCOS with infertility. She built up from 0.5 mg to 5 mg, felt nauseous, and settled at 2.5 mg. She reported less pelvic inflammation, far more stamina at work and no more frequent colds. She conceived about 2 months in, while also on ovulation drugs. hCG blood tests rose normally. She had migraines and nausea on dose increases. The host charity promotes LDN and runs a prescriber marketplace. [reported]
  • Clinician-observed: Dr Monica Minjeur (Cycle Wisdom podcast, Apr 2025) described one endometriosis patient whose PMS and pain eased over 3 cycles alongside other changes, and 3 patients with unexplained infertility who conceived on LDN. She says LDN does not cure endometriosis and excision remains definitive. She cites no studies. [reported]
  • Clinic blog: Dr Brighten calls it "life-changing" for endometriosis and adenomyosis, with no data or endometriosis studies cited. [verified page Jan 2025, updated Oct 2026]
  • Closest study: a retrospective pain clinic cohort (Ontario, n = 93) found 53.8% responded overall and 66.7% in the pelvic pain subgroup. Subgroup size was not given and endometriosis was not named. Half had side effects (nausea 18, fatigue 7, vivid dreams 6, insomnia 6). 25 stopped for side effects and 21 for no effect. [verified] Aalto et al., J Pain Res 2025:18:6637
  • No-change / no-data: three Endometriosis UK HealthUnlocked LDN threads contained only questions, no outcomes. One reply searched the literature and found no endometriosis study. [verified]
  • Worse / safety: one HealthUnlocked poster on naltrexone woke from endometriosis-related surgery in severe pain that took a long time to control, and the anaesthetist suspected naltrexone was still active. [reported] [inferred] Naltrexone blocks opioid painkillers, so anyone on LDN should tell their surgical team and follow their stop-before-surgery advice. This belongs on the page.
  • Trials: the only RCT (NCT03970330) was terminated at n = 9 (Endo v1). PubMed has 0 LDN + endometriosis studies. [verified]

1.4 BPC-157

  • Community: 0 first-hand endometriosis reports on HealthUnlocked, Mumsnet, Substack, biohacker blogs or podcasts. Searches surfaced only seller SEO pages (excluded) and spam. [verified absence]
  • Nearest anecdote: a Substack author felt "no difference" on oral BPC-157 (not used for endometriosis). [reported] On a Huberman Lab episode (Jun 2026), the host relayed a user whose vascular spots (spider angiomas) got worse on BPC-157, and the guest physician called a tumour-growth concern via VEGF "plausible" but context-dependent. [reported, episode summary] [inferred] A blood-vessel lesion flaring is the same direction of worry as endometriosis lesions.
  • PubMed BPC-157 + uterus/ovary/endometrium: 5 hits, none about endometriosis. One rat study used the ovarian veins as collateral routes after a vena cava blockage and showed VEGFR gene changes in the ovarian vein. [verified] PMID 29510201
  • Risk stands as in Endo v1: VEGFR2 upregulation vs lesions that need VEGF-driven vessels. Grade E! [inferred]

1.5 TB-500 / thymosin beta-4

  • Community: 0 reports. [verified absence] Risk as in Endo v1: the body's own Tβ4 is raised in adenomyosis and drives endometriosis cell invasion. Grade E!

1.6 KPV, GHK-Cu, LL-37, Larazotide, ARA-290, blends (KLOW, GLOW, Wolverine)

  • Community: 0 endometriosis reports for any of them. The blend searches returned only seller and spam pages. [verified absence]
  • PubMed: KPV / melanocortin + endometriosis: no therapeutic study. [verified]
  • [inferred] KLOW, GLOW and Wolverine contain BPC-157 and TB-500 (GLOW and KLOW also contain GHK-Cu), so they carry the blood-vessel concern several times over.

1.7 Oxytocin, PT-141, melanotan

  • Oxytocin: no therapeutic community use for endometriosis. A daily-life study (n = 66, chronic pelvic pain with endometriosis) found no link between salivary oxytocin and pain. Stress was linked to pain. [verified] PMID 41188592. Oxytocin antagonists are the research direction (Endo v1). [inferred] Taking oxytocin would go the wrong way for adenomyosis.
  • PT-141: no endometriosis reports. [inferred] It could matter for painful sex and low desire, but it is untested here.

1.8 Selank, Semax, DSIP, Epitalon, MOTS-c, SS-31, NAD+, glutathione, AOD-9604, tesamorelin, CJC/ipamorelin, amylin drugs

  • No endometriosis first-hand reports or condition studies found in this pass. [verified absence by search]
  • GH secretagogues: GH and IGF-1 may stimulate existing growth (a physician on Huberman Lab, 2026). No endometriosis data. [reported; risk speculated]
  • Context study: a Jackson Laboratory / UConn map of human lesions (Nature Communications, Oct 2026) found pain nerves clustered next to activated macrophages near lesion glands, a neuro-immune loop. No peptide or GLP-1 was tested. [verified, JAX news] A 2026 review names CGRP, substance P and the NPS/NPSR1 system as non-hormonal targets. [verified] J Endometr Uterine Disord 2026, doi:10.1016/j.jeud.2026.100186

  • New study: a meta-analysis of 3 RCTs (4 reports, n = 252) of GLP-1 (± metformin) vs metformin [verified] Kar et al., Eur J Obstet Gynecol Reprod Biol Oct 2026, PMID 42893679, doi:10.1016/j.ejogrb.2026.115499:

  • Natural pregnancy: OR 3.10 (1.74 to 5.49), moderate certainty.
  • IVF pregnancy: OR 1.01.
  • Live birth was not reported.
  • Reviews (2026): GLP-1 benefits in PCOS are mostly via weight and metabolism; adolescent data are limited. [verified] PMID 42654342, PMID 41386097
  • Registry: no PCOS trial of retatrutide, cagrilintide or orforglipron found. [verified absence]

2.2 Naltrexone (full dose, studied) and LDN (talked about)

  • Studies, full dose 50 mg/day, missed in PCOS v1 [verified]:
  • Fruzzetti 2002 (n = 10 obese PCOS, 6 months, open controlled): cycles improved in 80%, BMI 29.9 → 26.1, free testosterone, androstenedione and DHEAS fell. The authors could not separate the effect from weight loss. PMID 12009347
  • Ahmed 2008 (n = 30 clomiphene-resistant obese PCOS): 3 of 30 ovulated on naltrexone alone. With clomiphene added, 19 of 27 ovulated and 9 of 27 conceived (7 term births). Insulin, LH, LH/FSH ratio and testosterone fell. PMID 18641399, doi:10.1093/humrep/den273
  • Hadžiomerović-Pekić 2010 (RCT n = 29, 3 months): ovulation and androgens improved equally on metformin, naltrexone, or pill + prednisolone. PMID 20356587
  • Fulghesu 2001 (n = 10): with pulsatile GnRH, ovulation went from 60% to 90% (not significant), with 2 pregnancies. PMID 11508781
  • Naltrexone raised LH pulse frequency in PCOS (1992). PMID 1555686
  • Clinician-observed (LDN): Dr Minjeur uses LDN for PCOS insulin resistance, mood, infertility and recurrent loss. She reports that about 10% of her PCOS patients get more irregular cycles on LDN, and that she sometimes switches them to 25 to 50 mg. No data published. [reported]
  • Community first-hand: 1 (Shauna, endometriosis + PCOS; see 1.3). No PCOS-only LDN reports found. [reported]
  • [inferred] The human data are for 50 mg, not 1.5 to 4.5 mg LDN, in obese, insulin-resistant women. Do not present them as LDN evidence.

2.3 MOTS-c

  • Community: 0 first-hand PCOS reports. Searches found generic biohacker posts (energy, CGM numbers) and seller pages only. [verified absence]
  • A peptide podcast (May 2026) hosted by a functional medicine practitioner who sells peptides recommends MOTS-c and tirzepatide for PCOS (now PMOS). No patient numbers, no side effects discussed. [reported, commercial]
  • Studies: human levels conflict (PCOS v1). No treatment trial. [verified]

2.4 NAD+ precursors (NMN), SS-31, glutathione

  • NMN in letrozole + high-fat-diet PCOS rats (2026, two papers): reversed insulin resistance, high testosterone, irregular cycles and cystic ovaries. [verified] PMID 42892420; PMID 41742258. It improved PCOS mouse egg quality in the lab dish. [verified] PMID 40678956
  • SS-31 protected ovarian cells in aged mice (ovarian ageing model, not PCOS). The authors suggest PCOS as a future use. [verified] PMID 41840657
  • Glutathione: promoted alongside tirzepatide on the same commercial podcast. No PCOS outcome data seen. [reported]
  • Community: 0 PCOS reports for NAD+, NMN, SS-31 or glutathione injections. [verified absence]

2.5 GH peptides (CJC-1295/ipamorelin, MK-677, tesamorelin, AOD-9604)

  • Community: 0 first-hand PCOS reports. [verified absence]
  • A telehealth clinic that sells peptide therapy (May 2026) notes that GH secretagogues affect insulin sensitivity. It advises baseline IGF-1 and fasting insulin before use and says sex-specific trial data are limited. [verified page; commercial]
  • A physician on Huberman Lab (Jun 2026) said GH secretagogues can reduce insulin sensitivity and raise A1c, advised being "lean and healthy enough" first, and noted people combine them with GLP-1s to offset this. [reported, summary]
  • [inferred] PCOS involves insulin resistance in up to about 80% (per the clinic page; PCOS v1 has guideline figures). Peptides that worsen glucose push the wrong way. Grade E! for PCOS.

2.6 Kisspeptin

  • Registry: a completed phase 1 at MGH gave continuous kisspeptin to try to dampen the reproductive axis, with PCOS among the conditions (NCT05971849, n = 8, ended Apr 2024). Results were not opened. [verified registry listing] This is the logical direction for LH-driven PCOS, the opposite of the IVF trigger use in PCOS v1. [inferred]
  • Community: 0 reports. [verified absence]

2.7 Oxytocin, PT-141, melanotan, GHK-Cu (hair), Epitalon, Selank, Semax, DSIP, BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, blends

  • Oxytocin: a systematic review (5 human, 3 animal studies) found blood oxytocin is lower in PCOS. No treatment trial. [verified] PMID 38920985. An oxytocin receptor gene variant is linked to PCOS in Italian families. [verified] PMID 37013781
  • GHK-Cu for PCOS hair loss: hair-loss forum search pages returned no readable thread with women or PCOS outcomes. [inaccessible / absence]
  • Epitalon: a Substack author (perimenopausal, not PCOS) reports better sleep. No hormone tests. [reported]
  • PCOS reports for the rest: 0 found. [verified absence]
