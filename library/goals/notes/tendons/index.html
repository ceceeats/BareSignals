1. Shared base page: section outline (common to all tendons)

1.1 What a tendon is and why it heals slowly

  • Tendon core essentially not renewed in adults; muscle turns over continuously (Heinemeier 2013, DOI; 28 tendon vs 4 muscle samples). [verified]
  • The tendon's outer layer does respond: training raised collagen synthesis around the Achilles 5 to 7 fold (Langberg 2001) and injured tendons responded more than healthy ones (Langberg 2007, n = 6). [verified, see Achilles v2]
  • Poor blood supply at classic injury zones: Achilles midsection markedly hypovascular (Chen 2009, DOI). [verified] Equivalent primary data for the elbow, patellar and cuff zones not gathered. [gap]
  • Healing phases: inflammation days 0 to 7, proliferation weeks 1 to 6 (type III collagen), remodelling from week 6 for 12+ months (type III to type I). [reported, general literature]

1.3 Loading principles (all tendons)

  1. Load is the treatment. Stiffness gains depend on load intensity, not contraction type (Bohm 2015, 27 studies). [verified]
  2. There is a threshold. Only high-strain Achilles training (~4.6%) adapted the tendon (Arampatzis 2007). [verified]
  3. Progressive and pain-monitored. Keep activity if pain stays within limits; same 12-month result as rest (Silbernagel 2007). The common rule (up to 5/10 during, settled by next morning) is [reported].
  4. 12 weeks minimum, gains to 12 months.
  5. Isometrics are a tolerable way to start loading a painful tendon; instant pain relief is contested (Rio 2015 n = 6 vs O'Neill 2019, Holden 2020, Clifford 2020, Soliman 2026). [verified]
  6. Supervision and dose matter: unsupervised home eccentrics no better than sham (Janssen 2026); doing the prescribed sets and weight predicted results, attendance did not (Kenny 2025). [verified, see Achilles v2]
  7. Scans do not equal symptoms. Imaging change rarely tracks recovery (Malliaras 2013); patellar gains were not explained by strength, thickness or vessel changes (Deng 2024, DOI). [verified]
  8. Fear of movement slows recovery: kinesiophobia correlated with poorer heel-rise recovery at 5 years (Silbernagel 2011, DOI). [verified]

1.4 Injections and procedures (cross-tendon pattern)

  • Cortisone: better short term, worse long term in tennis elbow (1-year recovery 83% vs 96% placebo; recurrence 54% vs 12%; Coombes 2013 JAMA, DOI) and patellar (gains lost at 6 months; Kongsgaard 2009, DOI) [verified]; Achilles mixed (Johannsen 2022 positive; Inject 2026 negative at 26 weeks) [verified]. Not recommended directly into weight-bearing tendons because of rupture risk; plantar fascia rupture and fat pad atrophy risk (AFP 2026 review, PMID 42202347). [verified]
  • PRP: null in Achilles (de Vos 2010, Kearney 2021, PATH-2) and patellar (Scott 2019: neither PRP type beat saline with HSR rehab; DOI) [verified]. After cuff repair, biologic augmentation (PRP or patch) cut re-tears 26.7% to 14.8% but without clinically meaningful function gains (Checketts 2026, 20 RCTs, DOI). [verified]
  • Shockwave: beat placebo for elbow, cuff and Achilles but not patellar (SMD -0.15, CI crosses zero) (Guo 2026 NMA, 65 RCTs, 3,921 people, DOI). [verified]
  • High-volume injection: no better than sham (Inject 2026). [verified]
  • Blood flow restriction: promising adjunct in upper-limb tendons, not consistently better than active rehab (Khan 2026, 20 reports, DOI). [verified]

  • US: HHS interim enforcement discretion (28 Oct 2026) for licensed 503A compounding of BPC-157 (SC/IM/oral/topical) and TB-500 (SC/IM) under 10 conditions; not FDA approval; FDA rulemaking pending; comments close ~13 Nov 2026. [reported, see Achilles v2 6.1]

1.7 PK and "start low, go slow"

BPC-157 half-life under 30 min (rats, dogs); IM bioavailability 14 to 19% rats, 45 to 51% dogs (He 2022). Human IV pilot: 2 people, 10 mg then 20 mg, no lab changes (Lee 2025). Animal tendon doses: BPC-157 10 µg/kg/day ip; TB-500 60 µg/kg/day ip. No human tendon dose. [verified, see Achilles v2 section 7]

1.8 Systemic risk factors (all tendons)

Fluoroquinolones (Achilles rupture ~3x for 60 days, up to ~20x with oral steroids; absolute risk small); oral steroids; age; previous tendinopathy; higher BMI linked to worse structure; statins not linked in the best data (Morales 2019; de Sá 2018). [verified]

1.9 Warnings and fine print (doctor liked this; keep identical on every page)

Unproven in humans; product identity, purity and sterility unregulated; banned in sport; not a substitute for loading; see a clinician for sudden pain, a "kick" sensation, inability to push off or lift the arm (possible rupture). [inferred from above]

3. Where the literature agrees vs differs across tendons

Agrees [verified across trials above] - Exercise and loading are first line everywhere; most add-ons do not beat exercise alone (cuff NMA, Achilles injections, patellar PRP). - Cortisone trades short-term relief for worse or relapsing longer-term results (elbow, patellar, plantar risk; Achilles mixed). - Placebo or sham procedures match surgery or injections more often than expected (FIMPACT, Inject, Kearney, Scott).

Differs - Shockwave: works vs placebo in elbow, cuff, Achilles; not patellar (Guo 2026). [verified] - Best loading style: patellar favours a staged progression over eccentrics (Breda 2021); Achilles shows HSR = eccentrics (Beyer 2015) and heel lifts close to both (Rabusin 2020). [verified] - Surgery: helpful for re-rupture prevention in Achilles rupture; not better than physio for small degenerative cuff tears or impingement. [verified] - Peptide animal data exist almost only for Achilles. [verified absence for others in PubMed searches this pass]
