Gut

IBS, inflammatory bowel disease (ulcerative colitis, Crohn's), gastritis and ulcers, reflux, SIBO and the idea of a "leaky gut". BPC-157 was first found in stomach juice, which is why people try it here.

Researched 3 to 10 Nov 202641 sourcesVersion 2
Digestive & gutInflammatory signalingImmunity

What it is. The gut lining is one cell thick, stitched together at the top like seams. IBS hurts without visible damage; colitis and Crohn's are real ulcers that can scar. A "leaky gut" can be measured in some people, but it is not a diagnosis on its own.3

What studies found. Peptides can heal a gut: linaclotide is an approved peptide pill for IBS with constipation, and teduglutide regrows lining in short bowel.11,19 BPC-157 began as a stomach-juice protein, but its one patient trial, an enema in 53 people with colitis, did not clearly beat placebo.14 KPV works like an off switch for an inflammation alarm, and so far only in mice.17

What people report. In US medical records, 14% of BPC-157 users took it for reflux, gastritis or bowel disease.39 KPV users rank gut first in tracking apps, though its Reddit pool is harsher. GLP-1 drugs carry the strongest forum signal: 20 of 21 Mumsnet users with IBS say it improved.32,40

Watch out. Blood in the stool, weight loss or waking at night with pain need a doctor, not a vial. Never swap colitis or Crohn's medicine for an untested one.

1What the gut problem is

Which one is it

  • IBS: belly pain tied to bowel habit with nothing visible on a scope. Types: constipation (IBS-C), diarrhoea (IBS-D) or mixed. About 1 in 8 people develop it after a bad bacterial gut infection.1
  • IBD: ulcerative colitis (colon only, from the rectum up) or Crohn's (anywhere, patchy, can tunnel through the wall). Measured with a stool test (calprotectin) and a scope.
  • Gastritis and ulcers: stomach lining inflamed or broken through, mainly from H. pylori, painkillers like ibuprofen, or alcohol. This is where BPC-157's rat work began.13
  • Reflux: acid backs up into the gullet. Up to 40% still have symptoms on acid-blocking pills.2
  • SIBO: too many bacteria in the small intestine, found with a breath test; can follow long-term acid blockers or gut surgery.7

Endometriosis and the gut

Endometriosis roughly triples the odds of IBS, and about 1 in 4 women with it meet IBS criteria. Endometriosis on the bowel causes painful bowel movements and is often mislabelled as IBS.8,37 See the Endometriosis case.

Leaky gut: what is real

  • Real in some groups. Across 66 studies, a leaky barrier showed up in 17 to 50% of post-infection IBS and 37 to 62% of IBS-D, but only 4 to 25% of IBS-C. Leakier guts went with more pain in 9 of 13 studies.3
  • The zonulin blood test doesn't work. Most commercial kits measure other proteins, not zonulin. The one study with a truly specific test found zonulin lower in IBS.4
  • "Leaky gut causes everything" (fatigue, autism, every skin problem) is not shown; those links mostly used the same faulty kits.4
  • The gut makes its own peptides. LL-37 sits in the mucus and kills bacteria; vitamin D and fibre-fed bacteria switch it on.20,21

What happens without peptides

RCT

About 1 in 3 IBS patients improve on a dummy. In the big rifaximin trials, 31.7% felt adequate relief on placebo.9 Any gut story has to beat that.

Meta

Diet and the brain move IBS a lot. Low FODMAP halved the chance of not improving; gut-directed hypnotherapy or CBT cut the number still unwell by about a third.5,6

Human

IBS comes and goes. Flares follow food, stress, periods and infection, so a few good weeks prove little. IBD flares and settles too, which is why trials use scopes and stool tests, not feelings.

2What studies found

Read this first: peptides already work in the gut, but they are approved drugs. Linaclotide and plecanatide treat IBS with constipation; teduglutide treats short bowel. The research peptides people buy (BPC-157, KPV, LL-37, TB-500) have one weak patient trial between them.

Approved gut peptides · the proof it can work

RCT

Linaclotide. A 14 amino acid peptide swallowed once a day that barely gets absorbed: it pulls water into the bowel and calms pain nerves. In 804 people with IBS-C, 33.7% responded against 13.9% on placebo over 26 weeks. Diarrhoea made 4.5% stop.11

Pooled

Plecanatide. Same receptor, copied from the body's own uroguanylin. Better stools and frequency than placebo at 12 weeks across 4 trials and 4,815 people.12

RCT

Teduglutide (GLP-2). Grows gut lining. In short bowel, 16 of 35 needed at least 20% less IV feeding against 1 of 16 on placebo; 3 came off IV feeding entirely.19 In Crohn's, better numbers that were not a proven win.

Why this matters: a swallowed peptide can work in the gut when it acts on the lining and doesn't need to reach the blood.

BPC-157 · born in the gut, one trial

BPC-157

Library card

An ulcer heals only if blood can reach it. BPC-157, a 15 amino acid piece of a protein first found in human stomach juice, acts like a foreman calling new blood vessels to damaged lining, and in rats it healed ulcers, colitis and surgical joins. It was developed in the 2000s as a colitis drug (PL 14736) and never made it past one small trial.13,14

  1. 2005 53 people

    Colitis enema: not clearly better than placebo.

    People with mild to moderate ulcerative colitis had an 80 mg BPC-157 enema or placebo daily for 2 weeks. Disease scores fell 3.2 points against 1.6, but the gap could have been chance. Dropouts for worsening colitis were 3 vs 2. Only ever a meeting abstract; the FDA called it inadequate.14

  2. 1990s on Rats

    Ulcers, colitis, fistulas, joins after surgery.

    Alcohol and painkiller stomach damage, colitis, short bowel, many surgical joins and fistulas healed in rats, almost all from one Zagreb lab. An independent 2026 review calls it short, single-dose studies with unclear bias: worth testing, not established.13,15

Oral or injected? Its developers say it survives stomach juice for over 24 hours. Nobody has measured it in human blood after swallowing; a 42-person oral study from 2015 never posted results. For the gut, oral makes more sense than for tendons, because the target is the lining itself, as with linaclotide. Unproven.14

KPV · mice only

KPV

Library card

In colitis, the gut's inflammation alarm keeps ringing after the threat has passed. KPV, the last three links of the hormone α-MSH, is the off switch for that alarm, and an inflamed colon pulls it into its cells through a transporter that switches on during colitis. It calmed two kinds of mouse colitis when swallowed. No human trial exists.17

  1. 2008 Mice Cells

    Swallowed, it calmed colitis.

    The three-amino-acid tail of α-MSH is carried into gut and immune cells by PepT1, a transporter that switches on in inflamed colon. Tiny amounts shut down the NF-κB inflammation switch, and it eased two kinds of mouse colitis given in drinking water.17

  2. 2017 Mice

    Packed in nanoparticles, it healed more.

    Wrapped in hyaluronic acid particles and swallowed, it healed mouse colitis better than the carrier alone.18

No human gut trial exists. The draft US pharmacy policy lists KPV for oral and topical use only.38

Larazotide · the cautionary tale

Larazotide

Library card

An oral peptide built to close the gut's seams in celiac disease. In early tests it stopped gluten raising leakiness by 70%; in a 342-person trial only the lowest dose helped; pooled, it didn't change leakiness; the big trial was stopped in 2022 because the drug-placebo gap was too small.16 "Seals a leaky gut" in early data didn't survive a big trial.

GLP-1 drugs · the biggest signal, both ways

GLP-1 drugs

Semaglutide · Tirzepatide · GLP-1s case
  1. 2026 300 people

    Colitis remission 67% vs 25%.

    150 people with ulcerative colitis on a GLP-1 against 150 matched: symptom remission at 12 weeks 66.7% vs 25.3%, not explained by weight; in those scoped, 58% vs 38% healed lining. One centre, looking back.22

  2. 2026 4,000+ pairs

    But no fewer serious relapses.

    In stable IBD, starting semaglutide or tirzepatide didn't change hard relapse (steroids, hospital, surgery) over a year: 7.9% vs 7.9%. Nearly half stopped the drug within a year. Both can be true: calmer symptoms without a change in disease course.23

  3. 2026 13,330 people

    IBS: slightly fewer new gut complaints.

    GLP-1 users had a little less new diarrhoea (8.9% vs 10.6%), bloating and belly pain than matched non-users. Small gaps, coded records.24

Why it might help IBS-D: it slows the gut, which is also why constipation is the common cost, and why IBS-C could get worse.32

Glutamine and others

RCT

Glutamine, post-infection IBS-D. In 106 people with diarrhoea after a gut infection and a proven leaky gut, 5 g three times a day for 8 weeks: 80% responded against 6% on placebo, and leakiness normalised. But the placebo was whey protein, which can upset IBS, the 6% placebo rate is unusually low, and nobody has repeated it.25 No benefit in Crohn's.

Mice

LL-37. The body's own version, and a piece of it, reduced colitis in 4 mouse models. No human gut trial.21

Mice

Thymosin alpha-1, VIP. Only animal or review mentions for the gut. Too much VIP, from a rare tumour, causes severe watery diarrhoea.26

Search

TB-500, collagen. No gut healing studies found. Collagen in 15 women raised GLP-1 and cut the next meal by about 10%, with no gut change.27

In doctors' notes · the largest real-world look

A US analytics firm searched 15 million medical records and found 1,039 people whose doctors wrote down that they used BPC-157. Gut problems were the second most common reason, after pain.39

148 people

Gut reasons: 14% of users took it for reflux, bowel disease or gastritis. The study does not split these further.

180 people

Route: where recorded, oral use (17%) was a little more common than injection (14%).

354 people

What doctors saw, all uses: 79% improved, 16% no change, 5% worse among those with a response written down. Gut problems were among the side effects too (1.3% of users).

No gut-only outcome numbers and no comparison group. It shows that doctors see BPC-157 used for the gut, not whether it works there.

4Pairings and the same problem elsewhere

Pairings · what is used together

  • BPC-157 + KPV: the most marketed gut stack. Never tested together, not even in mice.15
  • Glutamine + low FODMAP: the one tested pairing: 88% vs 60% improved by at least 45% in 50 people.28 RCT
  • GLP-1 + an IBD biologic: being tested in two large trials, results 2028.23 Trial
  • Vitamin D and LL-37: vitamin D switches on the gut's own LL-37; in colitis, higher levels went together.21

The same problem, other places

  • Endometriosis: triples the odds of IBS and can sit on the bowel.8
  • PCOS: a weaker link to IBS.8
  • Period pain: bowel and womb cramps overlap, and GLP-1 constipation can feel like period pain.
  • Bladder pain (interstitial cystitis): one of the three human BPC-157 studies.
  • GLP-1s: every effect beyond weight loss.

5Every option, with numbers

IBS

  • Get the diagnosis right first: a stool calprotectin test separates IBS from IBD; celiac, bile acid diarrhoea and bowel endometriosis can all look like IBS.
  • Low FODMAP with a dietitian: the chance of not improving roughly halved, and it was the only diet that beat usual eating for bloating.5 Meta
  • Peppermint oil: 1 in 4 extra people improved; more reflux.10 Meta
  • Gut-directed hypnotherapy or CBT: about a third fewer still unwell.6 Meta
  • By type: linaclotide or plecanatide for IBS-C; rifaximin for IBS-D (40.7% vs 31.7% relieved).9,11

IBD, ulcers, reflux

  • IBD: specialist care with biologics or newer pills; the best of these work for 1 extra person in every 3 to 9 treated over a year.29 GLP-1s as an add-on when weight or diabetes is also a problem are being studied.
  • Ulcers and gastritis: test for H. pylori, stop ibuprofen-type painkillers, acid blockers.
  • Reflux: acid blockers; weight loss helps many.
  • Post-infection IBS-D with a proven leaky gut: ask about glutamine, the one trial-backed option.25

6Warnings and fine print

See a doctor, not a vial

  • Blood in the stool or black stools, vomiting blood.
  • Weight loss without trying, anaemia, fever.
  • Waking at night with pain or diarrhoea.
  • Symptoms starting after 50, or bowel cancer or IBD in the family.
  • Trouble swallowing.

Untreated IBD can scar, perforate and raise bowel cancer risk. No evidence supports swapping IBD medicine for a peptide.

GLP-1 drugs and the gut

  • Rare but real: against an older weight drug, GLP-1s carried higher risk of stomach paralysis (about 3.7 times), bowel obstruction and pancreatitis, with wide uncertainty.30
  • Tell your IBD team before starting: 2 of 16 forum users flared, and one wondered if slow emptying cut absorption of her oral colitis medicine.32
  • Before a scope or anaesthetic, say you're on one: a slow stomach raises the risk of breathing in stomach contents.31

What is in the vial

  • Most single vials hold what they say, but not all: in 6,441 independent lab tests, 3.5% of BPC-157 samples and 10% of TB-500 samples did not contain the peptide at all.41
  • TB-500 is the weak link: only 7.5 to 22.5% of TB-500 samples passed both purity and amount, and many vials sold as TB-500 are full thymosin beta-4.41
  • Blends are harder still: none of 8 classifiable BPC-157 + TB-500 blend tests passed purity (May to September 2026).41
  • Endotoxin was measurable in 15% of 243 samples tested for it. A no-change report can mean a weak vial, not a weak peptide.41

Before you trust a vial or a number

  • Not characterised: the FDA says BPC-157 impurities, clumping and endotoxin are unknowns; rat and dog injection studies shifted clotting tests and liver markers.14
  • Growth signals: teduglutide grows lining, so it is watched for polyps; any pro-growth peptide in someone with polyps or bowel cancer history is a theoretical worry.
  • Banned in sport: BPC-157 at all times.36
  • Not approved: a draft US policy would let licensed pharmacies make BPC-157 and KPV under 10 conditions, including proof that oral forms are absorbed in people, which doesn't exist yet.38

Usual questions

Has BPC-157 been tested in gut disease?

Once: 53 people with colitis, 2 weeks of enemas, not clearly better than placebo. Everything else is rats.14

Oral or injected for the gut?

Untested in people either way. Gut peptide drugs that work, like linaclotide, are swallowed and act on the lining, so oral is the logical route for gut use, but nobody has shown BPC-157 works that way in humans.11,14

Is leaky gut real?

Yes, measurably, in some people with IBS-D, post-infection IBS, celiac and IBD. It is a feature, not a diagnosis.3

Should I get a zonulin test?

No. Most commercial kits don't measure zonulin.4

What is KPV?

A three-amino-acid piece of α-MSH that calms colitis in mice when swallowed, riding a gut transporter. No human data.17

Do GLP-1s help colitis or Crohn's?

Mixed. One study found far more symptom remission; a larger one found no fewer serious relapses. Forum users mostly report calmer bowels, but 2 of 16 flared.22,23,32

Does glutamine heal the gut?

One strong trial in a narrow group (diarrhoea after infection, with a proven leaky gut), never repeated. Some users get cramps and loose stools.25,34

Does BPC-157 help reflux or gastritis?

Only rat studies. In US medical notes, reflux, gastritis and bowel disease together were the second most common reason doctors recorded for using it, with no gut-only results. Two forum users felt nothing from oral BPC-157 for gastritis.39,33

Does KPV help colitis?

It calmed colitis in mice. In the Library's KPV log, one person's bleeding colitis settled over weeks on injections, but the Reddit KPV pool is harsh, with nearly half of accounts led by a side effect.17,40

BPC-157 plus KPV?

The most marketed gut stack. Never tested together, not even in mice.15

How long before people notice gut changes?

KPV users describe changes from days 3 to 14; GLP-1 IBS users from hours to about 3 months.40,32

Can these make my gut worse?

Yes: KPV users report headache and flu-like feelings, oral glutamine gave 3 of 8 people cramps and loose stools, and 2 of 16 colitis users flared on a GLP-1.40,34,32

Will a stool test show if it worked?

For colitis and Crohn's, yes: calprotectin and scopes are how trials judge healing. No research peptide user found has shared a before-and-after result.33

Are my peptides even real?

Mostly, but 3.5% of BPC-157 samples in independent testing held none of it, and blends failed purity often. A no-change gut may be a weak vial.41

Is there an approved peptide for IBS?

Yes: linaclotide and plecanatide, for the constipation type.11,12
