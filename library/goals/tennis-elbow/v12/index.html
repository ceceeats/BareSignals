Tennis elbow

Also called lateral epicondylitis · lateral elbow tendinopathy. Golfer's elbow is its inner-side twin.

Researched 1–2 Nov 202657 sourcesVersion 12
Tissue repairPain & sensoryInflammatory signaling

What it is. Pain on the outer elbow bump when you grip. It is wear where the wrist extensor tendon meets the outer elbow. About 9 in 10 are better within a year with no treatment,27 so any treatment has to beat that.

What studies found. In rats, BPC-157 and TB-500 healed cut tendons faster and stronger. In people, collagen injections closed 8 of 13 elbow tendon tears, and 14 of 16 had less knee pain after BPC-157. Human elbow trials of BPC-157 or TB-500 have not been done yet.

What people report. Of 47 on Reddit, 18 clearly better and 17 partly; on forums and blogs, 12 of 15 better. Most noticed it within 2 to 5 weeks, usually alongside rehab.

Watch out. Cortisone wins for 6 weeks, then comes back in half of people. Peptides are banned in tested sport, and what is in a vial has never been checked against the studies.

1What tennis elbow is

Signs, look-alikes and what is going on inside

Pain on the outer elbow bump when gripping, lifting a kettle or shaking hands. Doctors press there while you lift the back of your hand against resistance (Cozen's test, catches 91%).32 Grip is often weaker with the arm straight than bent. Smokers get it about 3 times as often, and jobs or sports that are both forceful and repetitive about 5 times.26

Often mistaken for it

  • Pain further down the forearm (3 to 5 cm): a trapped nerve (radial tunnel).
  • Clicking or giving way, after a fall or many steroid shots: loose joint.
  • Neck pain with it, tingling: a nerve from the neck.
  • Inner elbow: golfer's elbow, the mirror version, 3× rarer.26

Most tennis elbow is B. It is failed healing with low-grade immune signals, not a classic inflammation, which is why doctors now say tendinopathy instead of tendinitis.31 In painful tendons the new vessels arrive with nerves, and as people recover the vessels shrink back.16,19

2What studies found

Read this first: a cut tendon is not a worn one. Almost every tendon study below cuts a healthy rat tendon and measures how fast it knits back. A fresh cut switches on the body's full repair sequence (swelling, new cells, new collagen), so a peptide only has to speed up healing that is already happening. Tennis elbow is the opposite problem: healing that started and stalled, leaving frayed collagen and extra vessels and nerves.31,43 That is not easier or harder in one simple way, but it is different, so a fast-healing rat tendon is a promising sign, not proof it restarts a stalled one.

BPC-157 · what studies found

BPC-157

Library card

How it may help

  • Tendon cells move into the damage. It switches on FAK and paxillin, the signal tendon cells use to crawl into an injury. Shown in cells
  • Tendon cells respond more to growth hormone. It raises the number of growth hormone receptors on them, so the hormone the body already makes works harder. Shown in cells
  • More blood supply where it is healing. It raises VEGF, the build-a-vessel signal, in injured tendon but not in healthy cells. Shown in rats
  • Protection from oxidative damage. It did not make tendon cells grow by itself, but it reversed the damage from an oxidative stress molecule (4-HNE). Shown in cells
  • Where tendon meets bone. The Zagreb group also showed healing at the tendon-to-bone joint and lower inflammation during Achilles repair. Tennis elbow sits at that same kind of joint. Shown in rats

Every study, oldest first

  1. 2003

    A cut Achilles rejoined completely within 14 days.

    Rats had the Achilles cut through, leaving a gap, then got BPC-157 or salt water daily. Untreated tendons healed poorly. With BPC-157 the repair took more force to tear and was stiffer, closer to a normal tendon, and the rats walked better from the first days. Under the microscope there were more repair cells and more collagen, and the gap closed until the tendon was whole again. In a dish it did not make tendon cells grow on its own, but it blocked a damage molecule (4-HNE) that stops them growing. A caution on speed: rats heal far faster than people, so 14 days in a rat does not mean 14 days in a person.1

    Rat · Zagreb lab · J Orthop Res

  2. 2006

    A tendon torn off the bone grew back onto it.

    In rats, a tendon cut from the heel bone does not reattach by itself. With BPC-157 it did: over 21 days it held more load and was stiffer, the collagen fibres were better organised, there was more type I collagen (the strong kind healthy tendon is made of) and a better blood supply. A steroid shot (methylprednisolone) made healing worse, and adding BPC-157 largely cancelled that harm. Why it matters here: tennis elbow sits exactly where tendon meets bone.2

    Rat · Zagreb lab · J Orthop Res

  3. 2008

    Less swelling and more new vessels in the first 4 days.

    This study looked at days 1 to 4, before collagen is even laid down. BPC-157 cut the flood of inflammatory cells, grew more new blood vessels, and the rats used the leg better on every day measured. The steroid also cut inflammation, but it blocked new vessels and the rats were no better. The lesson: calming inflammation alone does not speed healing; the tendon also needs a blood supply.3

    Rat · Zagreb lab · Inflamm Res

  4. 2009

    It grows vessels where tissue is healing, not everywhere.

    On blood-vessel cells in a dish BPC-157 did nothing. In injured rat muscle and tendon it raised VEGF, the body's main build-a-vessel signal, and vessel growth was "adequately modulated": enough to heal, not a tangle. In plain terms, it seems to amplify the repair signal an injury already sends rather than force vessels to grow.4

    Rat + cells · Zagreb lab · J Physiol Pharmacol

  5. 2011

    Tendon cells crawled out faster and survived stress.

    An independent lab in Taiwan put pieces of rat Achilles in a dish. With BPC-157, tendon cells grew out of the pieces faster and moved more, and the higher the dose, the more they moved. It did not make them multiply, but more of them survived a burst of hydrogen peroxide, the kind of chemical stress an inflamed injury produces. It worked by switching on FAK and paxillin, the grip-and-pull proteins cells use to travel. Repair depends on cells reaching the damaged spot.5

    Cells · Chang Gung University · J Appl Physiol

  6. 2014

    Tendon cells became more responsive to growth hormone.

    Of all the genes BPC-157 changed in tendon cells, the growth hormone receptor rose the most, and it rose with dose and time. More receptors means the growth hormone the body already makes has a bigger effect: when it was added, the BPC-157 cells multiplied more. This is the lab basis for pairing BPC-157 with growth-hormone peptides; the pairing itself has not been tested.6

    Cells · Chang Gung University · Molecules

  7. 2021

    Knee injections: 14 of 16 people had less pain.

    A Florida clinic phoned 16 patients who had BPC-157 injected into a painful knee 6 to 12 months earlier. 11 of the 12 who had BPC-157 alone said the pain improved significantly, and 3 of the 4 who also had Tβ4. No comparison group and no scans, so it shows people felt better, not why.8

    Human · Altern Ther Health Med

  8. 2026

    Head to head: BPC-157 vs TB-500 vs both.

    An independent lab in Turkey cut and repaired rat Achilles tendons and treated them for 4 weeks, with 4 tendons per group tested for strength. Force needed to tear: untreated 26.9 N, BPC-157 37.2 N, TB-500 37.4 N, both together 34.5 N. Both single peptides made the repair about 40% stronger; only TB-500 reached statistical significance, and the pair added nothing.7

    Rat · Bağcılar hospital, Istanbul · Jt Dis Relat Surg

TB-500 & Tβ4 · what studies found

TB-500

Fragment card · Full Tβ4 card

TB-500 is a 7 amino acid piece (17–23) of Thymosin Beta-4; many vials sold as TB-500 are the full 43 amino acid Tβ4, and most research is on the full one.

How it may help

  • Cell movement. It holds onto actin, the protein cells use to crawl, so repair cells can move into a wound more easily. Shown in cells
  • Collagen laid down, thicker and in line. More collagen in healing skin, and thicker, evenly spaced collagen in a healed ligament. Shown in rats and mice
  • Clearing the old tissue. It raises MMP enzymes that cut away damaged matrix so new collagen can replace it. Shown in mice
  • New vessels and calmer inflammation, as with BPC-157. Shown in animals
  • The catch: nearly all of this is from full Tβ4 (43 amino acids), not the 7 amino acid fragment sold as TB-500. An October 2026 orthopaedic review says no one has shown the two are equivalent.11

Every study, oldest first

  1. 1999

    Skin wounds closed up to 61% faster.

    Full Tβ4, put on or injected near full-thickness wounds: new skin covered 42% more of the wound by day 4 and 61% more by day 7, wounds shrank at least 11% more, with more collagen laid down and more new vessels. Even 10 picograms made skin cells migrate 2 to 3 times more in a dish.53

    Rat · NIH · J Invest Dermatol

  2. 2003

    The 7-piece stretch behind TB-500 does the vessel work.

    Testing pieces of Tβ4, the seven amino acids at positions 17 to 23 (LKKTETQ, the stretch TB-500 copies) made vessel cells move and sprout as well as the whole protein; pieces missing it did nothing. TB-500 adds a small chemical tag to this stretch, which was not tested here.54

    Cells · NIH · FASEB J

  3. 2003

    Healing restarted in old and diabetic mice.

    In 26-month-old mice and diabetic mice, where wounds heal slowly, Tβ4 increased wound contraction and collagen laid down. In the old mice the 7-piece stretch alone worked about as well as the whole protein.55

    Mice · NIH · Wound Repair Regen

  4. 2006

    It switches on the clean-up crew.

    On day 2 after wounding, Tβ4 raised MMP-2 and MMP-9 several-fold. MMPs are the enzymes that cut away damaged matrix so new collagen can be laid in its place. All of this activity came from the 17 to 23 stretch.56

    Mice + cells · NIH · J Cell Physiol

  5. 2013

    A torn knee ligament healed with thicker, evenly spaced collagen.

    A knee ligament was cut and 1 µg of Tβ4 in fibrin glue was placed in the gap. At 4 weeks the fibre bundles were evenly spaced (untreated ones were irregular), the collagen fibrils were significantly thicker, and the healed ligament with its bone ends was significantly stronger.9

    Rat · Harbin Medical University · Regul Pept

  6. 2014

    Healing tendons make their own Tβ4.

    In cut rat Achilles tendons, healthy rats had Tβ4 in the healing tissue; diabetic rats, which heal badly, had significantly less Tβ4 and VEGF there. It suggests Tβ4 is part of the body's own tendon repair kit.57

    Rat · Karolinska Institutet · Cell Tissue Res

  7. 2019

    Stem cells turned into tendon-like cells.

    Human stem cells grown on a tendon-like scaffold releasing Tβ4 for 28 days moved more, multiplied more and switched on tendon genes, on top of what the scaffold alone did.10

    Cells · Mater Sci Eng C

  8. 2026

    Strongest repair in the head-to-head.

    In the same Achilles study as BPC-157 above, TB-500 needed 37.4 N to tear vs 26.9 N untreated, the only group to reach significance, with better collagen scores. 4 tendons per group were tested, and which form was in the vial was not reported.7

    Rat · Jt Dis Relat Surg

  9. 2026

    First human trial of the TB-500 fragment.

    80 adults with heart disease, against placebo, now recruiting. Heart, not tendon, but it will give the first human safety data for the fragment.13

    Trial · ClinicalTrials.gov

Collagen · the peptide with elbow data

Human · 2025

Collagen injections into torn elbow tendons. 13 people with partial tears: pain 7.5 → 2.5 out of 10 in a month, 8 of 13 tears closed on ultrasound. No control group.41 Curr Health Sci J

Human · 2026

Injection + shockwave + loading, 10 people. Pain and function scores both down about 31 of 50 points by day 51, grip up about 4 kg.20 Life

RCT · 2025

Oral collagen + vitamin C, 45 people. Three groups of 15. The one doing exercise, shockwave and the supplement had the best grip and function at 30 days.17 Front Rehabil Sci

RCT · 2016

15 g gelatin + vitamin C an hour before loading. Doubled a blood marker of new collagen. The reason to take it before rehab.15 Am J Clin Nutr

RCT · 2019

Achilles: collagen on top of calf training. Score up 12.6 points in 3 months vs 5.3 on placebo, 20 people.16 Nutrients

Same tendon wear, different joint. Tennis elbow's closest twins attach to bone too: golfer's elbow, gluteal, hamstring, insertional Achilles.

Other compounds worth knowing

  • Collagen peptides or gelatin with vitamin C, about an hour before loading. The best human data here. 15 g taken an hour before exercise doubled a blood marker of new collagen being made,15 and in Achilles patients collagen on top of calf training improved function faster than placebo.16 Library card
  • GHK-Cu. Helps remodel collagen in skin and wounds, but there are no direct tendon studies yet. GHK-Cu + BPC card
  • Growth hormone peptides (CJC-1295, ipamorelin, MK-677). A 2026 sports medicine review found modest human effects at best, and MK-677 carries a heart failure signal.12
  • KPV. Anti-inflammatory, so it fits poorly with tennis elbow, which is mostly wear rather than inflammation.

What has strong human evidence is still progressive loading: slow, heavy forearm work. Peptides would at best be an add-on.33

Pooled across 24 trials, 89% of people given placebo or nothing were better at a year, and symptoms halved every 2.5 to 3 months however long they had had them.27 About 1 in 10 still in pain at 6 months ends up having surgery.47

Why this matters: about 1 in 3 are better by 6 weeks with nothing at all, so a treatment has to beat that.

Padel and racquets

Across 8 studies and 2,022 players, the elbow was the most injured body part.40 Review In Portugal, 69% of padel players had been injured at least once, the elbow in 18%, and players coached by an instructor had fewer elbow injuries.46 Human

If you're trying it

  • Expect some better, not fixed. About 1 in 4 on Reddit felt nothing.
  • Give it 4 to 8 weeks. Most who improved did by then; a few took up to 3 months.
  • Keep loading. Nearly every success did slow forearm work too; it is the one thing with solid human evidence.
  • When the pain drops, build back slowly. The tendon can lag behind how it feels.
  • Track one number: pain when gripping, 0 to 10, once a week. Change one thing at a time.
  • See someone if the pain sits further down the forearm, tingles, or the joint clicks or gives way.

6Warnings and fine print

Before you trust a number

  • Mostly one lab. 175 of 230 BPC-157 papers on PubMed list the same Zagreb author (searched 2 Nov 2026).52 Independent labs in Taiwan and Turkey did see tendon effects too.5,7
  • Tiny rat groups. The BPC vs TB-500 tendon test rests on 4 tendons a group.7
  • Intact TB-500 did not speed healing in a dish; one of its breakdown pieces (Ac-LKKTE) did.51
  • No human tendon study. The FDA's 2026 review looked and found none, so it did not judge tendon use.48
  • Side effects reported to the FDA: an injection-site reaction, shortness of breath, and skin and gum darkening after a week on a "research only" BPC + TB-500 blend, which came back on restarting. The FDA could not say BPC-157 caused them.48
  • Immune reaction. The FDA cannot rule one out with injected peptides that carry impurities or clump.48
  • The vial is not the study. No study has checked that peptides sold online match what was tested. A study tells you what happened to the people in it, not what will happen to you.

Five checks for any claim

Is there a link to a study? Was it in people? Was there a group that got nothing? How many, and who measured? Has another lab repeated it?

Had a cortisone shot?

In people. In a blinded trial, 83% had recovered a year after cortisone vs 96% after a placebo shot, and it came back in 54% vs 12%.29 RCT In another, 47 of 65 people helped early by the shot relapsed.30

In rats. The steroid methylprednisolone made tendon-to-bone healing worse, and BPC-157 largely reversed that. Early on, the steroid also blocked new vessels; BPC-157 kept them.2,3 Rat

No human study of a peptide after a cortisone shot exists yet.

Banned in sport?

Is BPC-157 banned?

Yes, at all times, for anyone tested under WADA rules. It sits in S0, the group for drugs no health authority has approved, and has been named there since the 2022 list.21,22 Tβ4 and its fragments are banned too.

Why do athletes take it?

To come back from tendon and muscle injuries faster, or to keep training through overuse pain.

Why is it counted as cheating?

A substance goes on the list when it meets two of three tests: it can enhance performance, it can harm health, or it goes against the spirit of sport.23 Healing faster means more training, and with no human safety data and unknown purity, the health test is met too.

Can an athlete get an exemption?

Unlikely. USADA says permitted alternatives exist.21

What do critics say?

That it is a repair aid, not a performance drug. When ultra-runners admitted using it, supporters said they used it to heal, not to win.24

Who does this apply to?

Only people tested in competition. Padel's federation publishes the WADA list, and grappling events under WADA rules test for it.25 A weekend player is not bound by it.
