Tennis elbow

What it is. Wear in the tendon that holds the wrist extensors to the outer elbow. The collagen frays and new vessels and nerves grow in. Very little inflammation.

What people try. BPC-157 most, often with TB-500 or the full Tβ4, next to collagen and rehab. 9 of 10 elbow accounts we logged felt better, most by week 2 or 3.

What studies show. Every tendon study is in rats or cells, and almost all on a freshly cut tendon, not a worn one. No human elbow or tendon trial yet.

Watch out. Banned in tested sport. Vial quality varies. What has the strongest human evidence is still progressive loading.

What it actually is

Most tennis elbow is B: wear, not a fire. Doctors now call it a tendinopathy, not a tendinitis, because the tissue shows collagen breakdown and new blood vessels with very little inflammation.20

Those new vessels matter. In painful tendons they arrive with nerves, and as people improve, the vessels shrink back.16,19

Tennis elbow = outer elbow (gripping, backhand, padel). Golfer's elbow = inner elbow (pulling, climbing, BJJ grips). Same idea, different tendon.

The open question

Both peptides push vessel growth, which suits a fresh tear. A worn tendon already has too many painful vessels. Nobody has tested whether these peptides make that better or worse.4,16 BPC-157 "modulating" rather than forcing vessels is the most hopeful clue so far.

BPC-157 · how it may help

BPC-157

Library card

A 15 amino acid piece of a stomach protein. In rats it is the most studied tendon peptide there is.

  1. Tendon cells crawl into the damage.FAK–paxillin · cell migration Cells
  2. Tendon cells hear growth hormone louder. It raises their growth hormone receptors.GHR · JAK2 Cells
  3. Vessels grow where healing needs them. No direct effect in a dish; in the body it modulates VEGF.VEGF · angiogenesis Rat
  4. Shields tendon cells from oxidative stress.HNE, H₂O₂ protection Cells

Studies · tap to open

RatCut Achilles healed stronger and fully rejoined2003

Daily BPC-157 after the tendon was cut: higher load before failure, better walking, more collagen and a smaller gap by day 14. Tendon cells were protected from an oxidative molecule, but did not grow faster on their own.

Staresinic, J Orthop Res · 1

RatTendon healed back onto bone, and undid steroid damage2006

Achilles cut from the heel bone, which does not heal by itself in rats. BPC-157 restored it, with more type I collagen. The steroid methylprednisolone made healing worse; BPC-157 largely reversed that.

Krivic, J Orthop Res · 2

RatLess inflammation and more vessels, early2008

Days 1 to 4 after the cut. BPC-157 lowered inflammation and raised new vessels, and function returned sooner. The steroid lowered inflammation too, but also blocked vessels and did not speed recovery.

Krivic, Inflamm Res · 3

RatVessels modulated, not just pushed2009

No direct vessel growth in cell culture. In healing muscle and tendon, VEGF and vessel markers were adjusted toward a more orderly repair.

Brcic, J Physiol Pharmacol · 4

CellsTendon cells moved and survived stress2011

Rat Achilles tendon cells spread and migrated faster and survived hydrogen peroxide, through the FAK and paxillin pathway. It did not make them multiply directly.

Chang, J Appl Physiol · 5

CellsMore growth hormone receptors on tendon cells2014

Growth hormone receptor was one of the most raised genes. Adding growth hormone then made the treated cells multiply.

Chang, Molecules · 6

RatBPC vs TB-500 vs both on a cut Achilles2026

8 rats per group, 4 weeks. BPC-157 trended better but did not reach significance on total scores. TB-500 was the only group with significantly stronger tendons. Together: no extra benefit.

Biçer, Jt Dis Relat Surg · 7

HumanKnee injections, chart review2021

17 patients, 16 reached by phone. 11 of 12 on BPC-157 alone said their knee pain improved, 14 of 16 overall. No control group and no scoring tools. The only human musculoskeletal data so far.

Lee, Altern Ther Health Med · 8

TB-500 & Tβ4 · how it may help

TB-500

Fragment card · Full Tβ4 card

Two different things share this name. TB-500 is a 7 amino acid piece (17–23) of Thymosin Beta-4. Many vials sold as TB-500 are the full 43 amino acid Tβ4. Most research is on the full one.

  1. Holds actin so repair cells can move. Actin is what cells use to crawl into a wound.G-actin binding Cells
  2. Grows new vessels at the injury.Angiogenesis Animal
  3. Calms inflammatory signals.TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6 Cells
  4. Lines collagen up. Evenly spaced fibre bundles in healing ligament and tendon.Collagen organisation Rat

Studies · tap to open

RatStrongest tendons of all groups2026

Cut Achilles, 4 weeks. Only the TB-500 group reached significantly higher load before failure, with better collagen scores. The paper calls it "synthetic Tβ4 (TB-500)", so which molecule is still unclear.

Biçer, Jt Dis Relat Surg · 7

RatKnee ligament healed stronger2013

Full Tβ4 placed in the gap of a cut knee ligament (MCL). At 4 weeks: evenly spaced fibre bundles, thicker collagen fibrils and better mechanical strength.

Xu, Regul Pept · 9

CellsStem cells turned tendon-like2019

Full Tβ4 released from a nanofibre yarn made human stem cells move, multiply and switch on tendon genes. Lab tissue engineering, not a treatment.

Wu, Mater Sci Eng C · 10

TrialFirst human trial of the fragment itself2026

80 adults with stable heart disease, dose steps vs placebo, recruiting, results expected after Feb 2027. Heart, not tendon, but the first human safety data for the 7 amino acid piece.

NCT07487363 · 13

Fragment and full Tβ4 have never been tested against each other. An October 2026 orthopaedic review says no one has shown they are the same.11

What else helps the elbow

Loading · the strongest human evidence

Slow, heavy, progressive forearm work. Every peptide here is at best an add-on to it, and 2026 orthopaedic reviews say peptides should not replace it.11,12

Collagen + vitamin C, about an hour before loading

15 g of vitamin C enriched gelatin an hour before exercise doubled a blood marker of new collagen.15 For tennis elbow, a supplement with collagen, vitamin C and hyaluronic acid plus rehab and shockwave gave the best grip and function at 30 days.17 For Achilles, collagen on top of calf training improved faster than placebo.16

Vitamin D

Lower vitamin D was linked with worse elbow function 12 weeks after shockwave. Observational, one study.18 Worth a blood test.

Weaker fits

  • GHK-Cu remodels collagen in skin and wounds; no tendon study found.
  • GH boosters (CJC-1295, ipamorelin, MK-677): modest human effects at best, MK-677 has a heart failure signal.12
  • KPV is anti-inflammatory, which fits a wear problem poorly.

Together?

People say yes. Several elbow accounts only turned around once TB-500 was added, and the pair is the most common stack in these reports.

The one study says no. In the 2026 rat Achilles study, the pair did no better than either alone.7 The authors think both peptides may land on the same repair pathways.

One rat study is not the last word. We are checking which molecule it used before it changes anything on the cards.

Had a cortisone shot?

Many tennis elbows get a steroid injection first. In rats, the steroid methylprednisolone made tendon-to-bone healing worse, and BPC-157 largely reversed that.2

Early on, the steroid lowered inflammation but also blocked new vessels and did not speed recovery. BPC-157 lowered inflammation and kept the vessels.3

Rat data only. No human study of BPC-157 after a cortisone shot exists yet.

Banned in sport?

Is BPC-157 banned?

Yes, at all times, for anyone tested under WADA rules. It sits in S0, the group for drugs no health authority has approved, and has been named there since the 2022 list.21,22 Tβ4 and its fragments are banned too.

Why do athletes take it?

To come back from tendon and muscle injuries faster, or to keep training through overuse pain.

Why is it counted as cheating?

A substance goes on the list when it meets two of three tests: it can enhance performance, it can harm health, or it goes against the spirit of sport.23 Healing faster means more training, and with no human safety data and unknown purity, the health test is met too.

Can an athlete get an exemption?

Unlikely. USADA says permitted alternatives exist.21

What do critics say?

That it is a repair aid, not a performance drug. When ultra-runners admitted using it, supporters said they used it to heal, not to win.24

Who does this apply to?

Only people tested in competition. Padel's federation publishes the WADA list, and grappling events under WADA rules test for it.25 A weekend player is not bound by it.

Still unknown

  • Does any peptide help a worn tendon, not just a freshly cut one?
  • Do they grow more painful vessels in an elbow that already has too many?
  • Does a recent flare respond faster than years of wear? Reports hint yes.
  • Which molecule was the "TB-500" in the 2026 rat study?
  • Any human data after a cortisone shot?
  • How do tennis, padel and basketball players report it? Sweeps under way.
