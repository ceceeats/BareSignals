Tennis elbow

Also golfer's elbow (inner side) · lateral epicondylitis · lateral elbow tendinopathy

What it is. Wear where the wrist extensor tendon meets the outer elbow. The collagen frays, new vessels and nerves grow in. Painful gripping is the sign.

On its own. About 9 in 10 are better within a year with no treatment, and symptoms halve every 2.5 to 3 months.27 Any treatment has to beat that.

What peptides have shown. In rats, BPC-157 and TB-500 rebuild cut tendons faster and stronger. In people, collagen injections closed 8 of 13 elbow tendon tears. Human elbow trials of BPC-157 or TB-500 have not been done yet.

Watch out. Cortisone wins for 6 weeks, then comes back in half of people. Peptides are banned in tested sport. What works best in people is still progressive loading.

What it actually is

Most tennis elbow is B. It is failed healing with low-grade immune signals, not a classic inflammation, which is why doctors now say tendinopathy instead of tendinitis.31 In painful tendons the new vessels arrive with nerves, and as people recover the vessels shrink back.16,19

Is it tennis elbow?

Pain on the outer elbow bump when gripping, lifting a kettle or shaking hands. Doctors press there while you lift the back of your hand against resistance (Cozen's test, catches 91%).32 Grip is often weaker with the arm straight than bent. Smokers get it about 3 times as often, and jobs or sports that are both forceful and repetitive about 5 times.26

Could be something else

  • Pain further down the forearm (3 to 5 cm): a trapped nerve (radial tunnel).
  • Clicking or giving way, after a fall or many steroid shots: loose joint.
  • Neck pain with it, tingling: a nerve from the neck.
  • Inner elbow: golfer's elbow, the mirror version, 3× rarer.26

Pooled across 24 trials, 89% of people given placebo or nothing were better at a year, and symptoms halved every 2.5 to 3 months however long they had had them.27 About 1 in 10 still in pain at 6 months ends up having surgery.47

Why this matters: "gone after 5 weeks" happens to many people with nothing. A treatment has to do better than that, and faster.

What worked for them

Of 18 people on Reddit who clearly got better

  • Felt it in about 5 weeks (middle of the group); a few in days, a few at 3 months.
  • Near the elbow did better than the belly: 4 of 7 better vs 2 of 6.
  • BPC-157 alone worked as often as stacks: 6 of 11 vs 12 of 36.
  • 8 of 18 named rehab or kept training alongside; the rest did not say.

If you're trying it

  • Expect some better, not fixed. About 1 in 4 on Reddit felt nothing.
  • Give it 4 to 8 weeks. Most who improved did by then; a few took up to 3 months.
  • Keep loading. Nearly every success did slow forearm work too; it is the one thing with solid human evidence.
  • When the pain drops, build back slowly. The tendon can lag behind how it feels.
  • Track one number: pain when gripping, 0 to 10, once a week. Change one thing at a time.
  • See someone if the pain sits further down the forearm, tingles, or the joint clicks or gives way.

BPC-157 · what studies found

BPC-157

Library card

How it may help: repair cells crawl in Cells · cells respond more to growth hormone Cells · vessels grow where healing needs them Rat · cells shielded from oxidative stress Cells

Rat · 2003

Cut Achilles healed stronger and fully rejoined. By day 14: higher breaking load, better walking, more collagen, smaller gap.1 J Orthop Res

Rat · 2006

Tendon healed back onto bone, and undid steroid damage. A tendon cut from the heel bone does not heal alone in rats; with BPC-157 it did, with more type I collagen. It largely reversed the harm of a steroid. Tennis elbow is also a tendon-to-bone problem.2 J Orthop Res

Rat · 2008

Less inflammation and more vessels, from day 1. Function returned sooner. A steroid cut inflammation too, but blocked vessels and did not speed recovery.3 Inflamm Res

Rat · 2009

Vessels modulated, not just pushed. No vessel growth in a dish; in healing tendon, VEGF was tuned toward orderly repair.4 J Physiol Pharmacol

Cells · 2011

Tendon cells moved faster and survived stress. Through the FAK–paxillin pathway; they did not multiply on their own.5 J Appl Physiol

Cells · 2014

More growth hormone receptors on tendon cells. Adding growth hormone then made the cells multiply.6 Molecules

Rat · 2026

BPC vs TB-500 vs both. Independent lab, 8 rats a group, 4 weeks. BPC-157 trended better, TB-500 was significantly stronger, together added nothing.7 Jt Dis Relat Surg

Human · 2021

Knee injections, 16 patients. 11 of 12 on BPC-157 alone said pain improved, 14 of 16 overall. No control group.8 Altern Ther Health Med

TB-500 & Tβ4 · what studies found

TB-500

Fragment card · Full Tβ4 card

TB-500 is a 7 amino acid piece (17–23) of Thymosin Beta-4; many vials sold as TB-500 are the full 43 amino acid Tβ4, and most research is on the full one.

How it may help: holds actin so repair cells can move Cells · new vessels at the injury Animal · calmer inflammatory signals Cells · collagen lines up Rat

Rat · 2026

Strongest tendons of all groups. Cut Achilles, 4 weeks: only TB-500 reached significantly higher breaking load, with better collagen scores. Which form was used is unclear.7 Jt Dis Relat Surg

Rat · 2013

Knee ligament healed stronger. Full Tβ4 in a cut knee ligament: evenly spaced fibres, thicker collagen, better strength at 4 weeks.9 Regul Pept

Cells · 2019

Stem cells turned tendon-like. Full Tβ4 made human stem cells move, multiply and switch on tendon genes.10 Mater Sci Eng C

Trial · 2026

First human trial of the fragment. 80 adults with heart disease, vs placebo, recruiting. Heart, not tendon, but first safety data.13 ClinicalTrials.gov

Collagen · the peptide with elbow data

Human · 2025

Collagen injections into torn elbow tendons. 13 people with partial tears: pain 7.5 → 2.5 out of 10 in a month, 8 of 13 tears closed on ultrasound. No control group.41 Curr Health Sci J

Human · 2026

Injection + shockwave + loading, 10 people. Pain and function scores both down about 31 of 50 points by day 51, grip up about 4 kg.20 Life

RCT · 2025

Oral collagen + vitamin C, 45 people. With exercise and shockwave, best grip and function at 30 days of three groups.17 Front Rehabil Sci

RCT · 2016

15 g gelatin + vitamin C an hour before loading. Doubled a blood marker of new collagen. The reason to take it before rehab.15 Am J Clin Nutr

RCT · 2019

Achilles: collagen on top of calf training. Score up 12.6 points in 3 months vs 5.3 on placebo, 20 people.16 Nutrients

Vitamin D: lower levels went with worse elbow function 12 weeks after shockwave (62 people).18 Worth a blood test. Collagen Library card

Had a cortisone shot?

In people. In a blinded trial, 83% had recovered a year after cortisone vs 96% after a placebo shot, and it came back in 54% vs 12%.29 RCT In another, 47 of 65 people helped early by the shot relapsed.30

In rats. The steroid methylprednisolone made tendon-to-bone healing worse, and BPC-157 largely reversed that. Early on, the steroid also blocked new vessels; BPC-157 kept them.2,3 Rat

No human study of a peptide after a cortisone shot exists yet.

Padel and racquets

Across 8 studies and 2,022 players, the elbow was the most injured body part.40 Review In Portugal, 69% of padel players had been injured at least once, the elbow in 18%, and players coached by an instructor had fewer elbow injuries.46 Human

Banned in sport?

Is BPC-157 banned?

Yes, at all times, for anyone tested under WADA rules. It sits in S0, the group for drugs no health authority has approved, and has been named there since the 2022 list.21,22 Tβ4 and its fragments are banned too.

Why do athletes take it?

To come back from tendon and muscle injuries faster, or to keep training through overuse pain.

Why is it counted as cheating?

A substance goes on the list when it meets two of three tests: it can enhance performance, it can harm health, or it goes against the spirit of sport.23 Healing faster means more training, and with no human safety data and unknown purity, the health test is met too.

Can an athlete get an exemption?

Unlikely. USADA says permitted alternatives exist.21

What do critics say?

That it is a repair aid, not a performance drug. When ultra-runners admitted using it, supporters said they used it to heal, not to win.24

Who does this apply to?

Only people tested in competition. Padel's federation publishes the WADA list, and grappling events under WADA rules test for it.25 A weekend player is not bound by it.
