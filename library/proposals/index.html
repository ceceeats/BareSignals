Merge or keep separate?

My recommendation: merge. BPC-157's six mechanisms map onto its roles one to one in your data (each mechanism already carries a role), so every effect gets its reason with no repetition. A separate How it works column reads like a second list of the same claims.

Keep it separate only for compounds with one strong mechanism behind many effects (Semaglutide, MOTS-c). The card can switch automatically: merge when mechanisms match roles, show a one-line "How it works" otherwise.

B. Full page with tabs

Same six groups, but only one shows at a time. The page fits on one laptop screen, with no long scroll. Good for returning readers who want one thing; weaker for a first read, because people don't see what's inside the other tabs.

C. No separate full page: the card grows

The card above is the top. Below it, the same six sections sit closed, as one-line rows with a short preview ("Dosing: 3 community doses, 1 watch note"). Tap a row and it opens in place. One place to read everything, nothing duplicated, and the list stays where you left it.

Side by side

A · Six boxes

+ Everything visible, clear order, easy to screenshot a box.

+ Closest to what exists, lowest risk.

− Still a long page on phones (about half today's length).

B · Tabs

+ Fits one screen, very tidy.

− Hides content; people miss tabs.

− Harder to share one link to a section.

C · Card grows

+ One place, no duplication between card and full page.

+ Fewest clicks.

− Biggest rebuild; the laptop side panel gets long.
