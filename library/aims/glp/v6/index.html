GLPs

GLP-1, GIP, glucagon and amylin. Ozempic, Mounjaro, retatrutide, cagrilintide, eloralintide, brenipatide, and how people pair them.

Goal · Weight & appetiteResearched 5 Nov 2026Version 1

What it is. Four meal hormones, each with its own switch in the body. GLP-1 quiets hunger, GIP helps insulin and fat storage, glucagon burns fuel in the liver, amylin ends meals sooner. Each drug copies one or more of them.

What studies found. The more hormones a drug copies, the more weight goes in trials: about 15% on semaglutide, 20% on tirzepatide, 28% on retatrutide 12 mg, 20% on semaglutide plus cagrilintide.

What people pair. A GLP drug with an amylin drug (cagrilintide or eloralintide), because they act on different brain cells and add up. Adding tesamorelin is the exception: on forums, the scale stalls.

Watch out. A quarter to two fifths of the weight lost can be lean tissue. Protein and strength training are what keep it muscle. Pairing two appetite drugs also doubles the stomach side effects.

1The four hormones

Your gut and pancreas release these around meals. Each one turns on its own receptor, like a key in its own lock. The natural hormones last minutes. The drugs are built to last about a week, so one weekly shot keeps the lock turned.

GLP-1

Made in the gut after eating, and in the brainstem

Tells the pancreas to release insulin only when blood sugar is high. Slows the stomach. Turns down the hunger centre in the brain and the dopamine reward response to food, which is why "food noise" goes quiet.

Feels like: not thinking about food, small meals feel enough, cravings and urges go quiet.

GIP

Made in the upper gut after eating

Also boosts insulin when sugar is high. Works on fat tissue, helping fat cells store fat safely instead of letting it spill into the liver and muscle. In animals it calms the brainstem's nausea centre, one reason tirzepatide's nausea isn't worse than semaglutide's despite more weight loss.14

Feels like: nothing on its own. It makes GLP-1 stronger and gentler.

Glucagon

Made in the pancreas when fasting

The "use stored fuel" hormone. Tells the liver to release sugar, burn its own fat and take in amino acids. In a drug, it's added to burn liver fat and raise the energy you use. On its own it raises blood sugar and heart rate, so it's always paired with GLP-1.

Feels like: people on retatrutide describe feeling warmer and a faster resting pulse.18

Amylin

Made in the pancreas with insulin, after meals

Not a GLP at all, a separate hormone family. It acts on the brainstem's fullness centre, a spot outside the brain's protective barrier. Meals end sooner, the stomach slows, and glucagon stays down after eating.9

Feels like: full after a few bites. Hunger may still be there, the meal just stops.

3Full vs not hungry vs appetite suppressant

"Appetite suppressant" gets used for four different feelings. They come from different places, which is why two drugs can both "kill appetite" and feel nothing alike.

  • Not hungry. No drive to start eating, food doesn't cross your mind.Hunger centre · mainly GLP-1
  • Full sooner. You start a plate and stop halfway because you're done.Brainstem fullness centre · mainly amylin, also GLP-1
  • Staying full. Breakfast still sits there at 3 pm.Slower stomach · GLP-1 and amylin
  • Quiet urges. Snacking, the evening wine, the "just one more" all lose their pull.Dopamine reward circuit · mainly GLP-1

Old-school appetite suppressants like phentermine or tesofensine are a fifth kind. They raise brain stimulants (noradrenaline, dopamine), so hunger drops because you're wired, often with a faster heart and worse sleep. GLP and amylin drugs work through the body's own fullness signals instead.

Ozempic vs Mounjaro vs retatrutide

Ozempic (semaglutide) is one key: GLP-1. Mounjaro (tirzepatide) adds GIP, and is tuned more toward GIP than GLP-1. Head to head, tirzepatide beat semaglutide 20.2% to 13.7% over 72 weeks.20 Retatrutide adds a third key, glucagon, and leans hardest on GIP: in lab tests it was about 12 times stronger at GIP than at GLP-1, and about 90 times stronger than at glucagon.21 It reached 28% on 12 mg at 80 weeks, where 45% of people lost 30% or more.5 Glucagon is the reason it also clears liver fat so strongly.

Where brenipatide fits

Brenipatide copies the same two hormones as tirzepatide, but Lilly built it for the brain's reward and inflammation pathways, not weight. It's in phase 3 for alcohol use disorder (two 56-week trials, 2,200 people) and for depression. In its first trial, skin tingling showed up in 15% of people on it and none on placebo.12 13

Brenipatide and the reward circuit

Why would a weight drug change drinking? Alcohol makes dopamine cells deep in the midbrain (the ventral tegmental area) fire, and they flood a spot called the nucleus accumbens. That flood is the "this feels good, do it again" signal. GLP-1 and GIP receptors sit right on that circuit. Brenipatide is built to reach it.

How GLP-1 drugs work on this circuit comes from animal studies; in people, semaglutide cut heavy drinking days in a trial. Brenipatide's own alcohol trials (RENEW-ALC-1 and 2, 56 weeks, 2,200 people) are still running, so its effect in people isn't known yet.13 15 Plausible

5Cagrilintide vs eloralintide

Both copy amylin, so both work on fullness, not hunger. The difference is how picky they are. Amylin's lock is the calcitonin receptor with a helper protein attached (three helpers make three amylin locks: AMY1, AMY2, AMY3). Without a helper, it's a plain calcitonin receptor, which amylin drugs can also hit. Cagrilintide hits all of them. Eloralintide prefers AMY1.

Cagrilintide

Novo Nordisk. Turns on amylin receptors and the plain calcitonin receptor too.

Alone in trials

About 11.5% weight loss at 68 weeks.3

Main pairing

Semaglutide (CagriSema), under FDA review.

Eloralintide

Lilly. Built to favour one amylin receptor, AMY1: in lab tests it was about 12 times more active there than at the plain calcitonin receptor.19

Alone in trials

9% at 1 mg up to 20% at 9 mg over 48 weeks, close to semaglutide alone. Nausea and fatigue were the most common side effects; a slower step-up helped.1

Main pairing

Tirzepatide (EloraTZP), phase 3 from late 2026.

The idea that eloralintide is gentler because it skips the calcitonin receptor is still a theory. No trial has put the two side by side, and the trials differ in length and people.17 Plausible

6Pairings

The reason pairing works: GLP drugs mostly act on the hunger centre and reward circuit, amylin drugs on the brainstem fullness centre. Different cells, so the effects add up instead of overlapping. Two of these pairings have trials. The rest are community stacks with no human data on the pair.

Sema + Cagri Trial · FDA review

20.4% together14.9% sema alone11.5% cagri alone3% placebo

CagriSema, 3,417 adults, 68 weeks.3 The pair beat either drug alone, though not by the full sum of both. Stomach side effects hit 80% against 40% on placebo. About a third of the weight lost was lean tissue.7 Head to head against tirzepatide alone (REDEFINE 4, 84 weeks), CagriSema lost: 23.0% against 25.5%.22 Filed with the FDA in December 2025; no decision announced as of 5 Nov 2026.4

Tirz + Elora Trial · phase 3 next

23.3% together14.8% tirz 15 mg alone

EloraTZP, 367 adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes, 48 weeks.2 People with diabetes lose less on every drug, so this likely runs higher without diabetes. The cost: up to 27% of people on the highest combination stopped for side effects, against 3% on tirzepatide alone.

Tirz + Cagri Community

Same logic as EloraTZP, using Novo's amylin drug instead of Lilly's. No trial exists, because each company only pairs its own drugs.

What people report: of 8 people on one forum, 5 say it helped, mostly to break a stall or quiet late-week hunger. One lost 100 lb in a year and wrote that before adding it they were starving, and now sometimes forget to eat. 3 say it worked at first, then faded within weeks even as they raised the dose. Tiredness in the first two weeks eased for those who mentioned it.27

Reta + Cagri Community

All four hormones at once. Retatrutide covers hunger, insulin and liver fat; cagrilintide adds the brainstem "meal ends" signal. Sold as a factory blend (Reta + Cagri). No human trial of the pair, and a rat study some sites cite could not be found.

What people report: of 11 people, 7 say adding a small cagrilintide dose (mostly 0.25 to 1.7 mg) quieted food noise or restarted loss, one reaching a 33% total loss. 2 felt nothing at 0.25 to 0.5 mg. A common tip: retatrutide's own appetite effect often only arrives around 6 to 8 mg, so people let it build before stacking. Several call cagrilintide "very strong", and one stopped after six months for nausea.28 29

Reta + Elora Community

The newer version of Reta + Cagri, swapping in the pickier amylin drug. No trial of the pair.

What people report: of 11 people using eloralintide, mostly on top of tirzepatide or retatrutide, 8 say it helped, often breaking stalls of months (one stalled 5 to 6 months, then lost about 12 kg). It builds slowly: 4 people felt little for the first 2 to 5 weeks, then a strong effect, which fits its long half-life. Constipation and tiredness come up most. Is it better than cagrilintide? Only 1 person compared the two directly (cagrilintide did little, eloralintide broke the stall), so that stays a first signal, not a finding.30 23

Reta + Tesa Community

A different idea: not more appetite control, but body shape. Tesamorelin tells the pituitary to release more growth hormone. In its trial (HIV patients with belly fat), deep visceral fat fell about 18% at 12 months, and came back fast after stopping.11 People add it hoping to target belly fat and keep muscle while retatrutide drives weight down. Whether it protects muscle on a GLP drug has never been tested.

What people report: all 5 people who posted about it said the scale stalled or went up, often with ipamorelin added too, which raises hunger. Two said they looked leaner or lost some belly fat anyway; others restarted loss by raising retatrutide or dropping the growth hormone peptides. So any gain shows as shape, not weight, and nobody posted scans. Growth hormone can also push blood sugar up and cause water retention and joint aches.28

Reta + Tesa + Cagri Community

The full stack: retatrutide for the three meal hormones, cagrilintide for fullness, tesamorelin for growth hormone. No trial of any part together. The logic people give is that growth hormone peptides raise hunger (especially when ipamorelin is added), so they add cagrilintide to hold appetite down while tesamorelin works on belly fat.

What people report: too few posts to count. One person raised their cagrilintide dose when they started tesamorelin and ipamorelin, for exactly this reason. Given that all 5 Reta + Tesa users saw the scale stall, judge this stack by waist and body shape, not weight.28

7Why drinking loses its pull

GLP-1 receptors sit on the brain's dopamine reward circuit, the same circuit that makes alcohol, sugar and nicotine feel good. In animals, GLP-1 drugs blunt the dopamine surge alcohol causes. So the drink still works chemically, it just stops feeling worth it. In people, the best trial (108 adults with alcohol use disorder and obesity) cut heavy drinking days by 41 points on semaglutide against 26 on placebo.15 16 Likely

Amylin may do the same. An amylin-type drug cut alcohol's dopamine release in mice and cut drinking about 39% in heavy-drinking rats.10 No human data yet. Plausible More on cravings and the 65 effects beyond weight on the GLP-1s Case.

8The real goal: lose fat, keep muscle

The scale is the easy part. What people actually want is less fat and the same muscle. On every drug, part of the weight lost is lean tissue (muscle, organ, water), measured by body scans:

Tirzepatide ~25%Orforglipron ~27%CagriSema ~33%Retatrutide ~35%Semaglutide ~40%

Different trials, different people, so these aren't a ranking.7 Dieting without a drug also loses roughly a fifth to a third as lean. Lean tissue isn't all muscle, so the real muscle loss is smaller than these numbers.

The four US obesity and nutrition societies' 2025 advice for people on these drugs:8

  • Protein 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg a day while losing weight, about 80 to 120 g a day. Hard to reach when you're rarely hungry.
  • Strength training at least 3 times a week, plus 150 minutes of moderate cardio. Cardio alone protects muscle less.
  • Eating too little. Below about 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,800 kcal (men), vitamins and minerals run short: iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc, B12, vitamin D among them.
  • Signs you're short: tiredness beyond normal, hair shedding, dry itchy skin, weakness, slow healing, easy bruising.

Stacks aimed at building muscle (growth hormone peptides and others) will get their own Goals page.

Warnings

  • Pairing two appetite drugs stacks the side effects. On the highest tirzepatide plus eloralintide dose, more than 1 in 4 people stopped.2
  • Community pairings (Tirz + Cagri, Reta + Cagri, Reta + Elora, Reta + Tesa, Reta + Tesa + Cagri) have no human safety data as a pair.
  • Retatrutide: skin tingling or burning (dysesthesia) in 12.5% of people on 12 mg against 0.9% on placebo; vomiting in 25% against 5%.24 Heart rate rose with dose in its phase 2 trial, peaked around week 24, then came back down; community reports mention a faster resting pulse.25 18 Brenipatide showed tingling in 15% too.
  • Very low intake means muscle, hair and nutrient losses. Protein first at every meal.
  • Known class warnings on the labels: pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, dehydration from vomiting, and not for pregnancy or a family history of medullary thyroid cancer.
  • Cagrilintide dosing: one secondhand forum report of hours of vomiting after a 3.75 mg first dose; people who use it start at 0.25 mg and step up. Taking a full tirzepatide dose and a high cagrilintide dose on the same day caused vomiting for another.27
  • Growth hormone (tesamorelin) can raise blood sugar, cause swelling and joint aches; gains reverse after stopping.11
  • Retatrutide, eloralintide, cagrilintide and brenipatide are not approved anywhere. Grey-market vials vary in what's inside. See how peptides are made.

?Quick answers

Is cagrilintide a GLP?

No. Cagrilintide and eloralintide copy amylin, a different hormone made with insulin. They're often lumped in with GLPs because they're paired with them.

Why does Mounjaro feel gentler than Ozempic for some people?

One reason may be GIP, which calms the nausea centre in animal studies. Tirzepatide's nausea rates in trials are similar to semaglutide's even though weight loss is bigger.

Is retatrutide just a stronger Mounjaro?

It adds glucagon, which works on the liver rather than appetite. That's why it clears liver fat strongly and why people feel warmer, but also why heart rate can rise.

Why add an amylin drug instead of raising the GLP dose?

A higher GLP dose pushes the same cells harder, with more nausea. An amylin drug works on different cells, so it adds fullness without doubling the same signal.

Do these drugs stop people drinking?

They reduce how much and how often for many, without forcing it. The strongest trial showed fewer heavy drinking days, not full abstinence. Brenipatide is the first one tested specifically for alcohol.

What happens when you stop?

In the semaglutide trial that followed people after stopping, people had lost 17.3% and regained 11.6 points of it within a year, about two thirds, because the hormones' effect ends when the drug does.26 Tesamorelin's belly fat loss also reversed quickly.
