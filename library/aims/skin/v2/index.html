Skin, Hair & Nails

Firmer, smoother, even-toned skin, a tan, stronger nails. What each one is in the body, which compounds reach it, and how they measure up against sunscreen and a retinoid.

Goal · Skin, hair & nailsResearched 20 Nov 2026Version 2Preview

What it is. Firmness lives in the dermis, where fibroblasts spin collagen and a copper enzyme welds it. Colour lives in pigment cells. Nails and hair are dead keratin pushed out by living factories you never see.

What studies found. Daily sunscreen stopped measurable skin ageing over 4.5 years1 and tretinoin improved 14 of 15 faces.2 The best peptide result is a cream: GHK-Cu cut wrinkle volume 55.8% against its base in 40 women.15 Nothing injected for skin has a human outcome study.

What people report. Copper serum: 11 of 19 reviewers better, 3 worse.77 Argireline: 7 of 13 saw mild softening.79 Oral collagen: 12 saw no skin change for every 8 who did, yet 7 of 11 said nails got stronger.80 Anecdotes are not proof.

Watch out. A melanotan tan darkens moles and has melanoma case reports.42 GLOW and KLOW have never been studied as blends. Anything for nails needs 4 to 6 months to show.53

3The proven baseline

Skin ages steadily with no treatment, and plain moisturiser alone improves hydration and fine-line scores in most cosmetic trials. That is why the trials below compare against a base cream or placebo, and why every peptide should be read against this list.

Daily sunscreen Large trial
903 adults under 55 in Australia, 4.5 years. The daily group showed no detectable increase in skin ageing and 24% less ageing than people who used it when they felt like it.1 The only thing proven to stop visible ageing adding up.
Tretinoin Trials
Prescription retinoid. Double-blind, 16 weeks: 14 of 15 faces improved on tretinoin vs 0 of 15 on the plain base, with changes visible under the microscope. The price is irritation.2
Retinol Small trial
The cosmetic cousin. 28 women aged 40 to 61, 12 weeks: barrier and hydration better at both strengths; the top layer thickened by 12.4 µm at 0.247% in a 5-person substudy, with more redness at the higher strength.3
Vitamin C 5% Trial
Beat its base over 6 months on neck and arms: better overall score, denser surface texture, fewer deep furrows and elastic tissue repair under the electron microscope.4 Modest, real, slow.
Tranexamic acid Meta-analysis
For melasma. Across 6 trials and 599 patients, the best dose studied was 250 mg three times a day for 12 weeks.5 As a 3% cream it matched 4% hydroquinone in 20 patients over 8 weeks, and seemed gentler.6
Microneedling Review
21 studies, 723 patients: 83% satisfied, redness in 6.8%, scaling in 1.7%. No standard outcome measure, so the true size of the effect is unclear.10 Paired with tranexamic acid it beat microneedling alone.7
Oral collagen Two analyses disagree
35 trials, 2,534 people: small to moderate gains in hydration and elasticity after 12+ weeks; wrinkle gains called statistical artifacts.8 23 trials, 1,474 people: in trials without company funding, no effect.9They ask different questions. One asks whether any effect survives careful pooling and finds hydration and elasticity do. The other asks who paid and finds the signal fades without company money. The 35-trial paper did not split by funding, so both can be true.
Lasers Not reviewed yet
Considered the strongest single-session resurfacing option, with downtime and a risk of dark marks in darker skin. Numbers not gathered for this version.

A 2026 review of aesthetic peptides found many trials limited by small samples, short follow-up, industry sponsorship or multi-ingredient creams, and results that do not carry from one peptide or route to another.31

4The peptides and other compounds

Ordered by how much human evidence sits behind them for skin. Melanotan 1, Melanotan 2 and PT-141 are in Tan; biotin, keratin and silicon in Nails.

Every compound at a glance

GHK-Cu

Cream: small human trialsInjected: no skin data

Skin loses firmness because the dermis makes less collagen each year and breaks more of it down. GHK-Cu is best understood as a reset message: a tiny copper-carrying piece that falls off collagen when tissue is damaged and tells nearby fibroblasts to rebuild like younger tissue.14 As a cream, 40 women aged 40 to 65 saw wrinkle volume fall 55.8% against the plain base in 8 weeks, and it beat a Matrixyl cream too.15 That is close to the whole human record: a 2026 review counted 2 trials among 20 studies, and the other one, after laser, found nothing measurable.17,16

What people report. 19 reviewers of a pure copper serum: 11 better (smoother texture, faded marks, finer eye lines), 3 no change, 2 mixed, 3 worse (breakouts, eye irritation). Firmness was named by only 1 or 2.77 No readable first-hand reports of injecting it; one clinician described site infections and raised liver enzymes and copper in heavy self-injectors.52

The deeper read
Best trial: 40 women, 8 weeks, GHK-Cu in a nano-lipid carrier twice daily. Against the base: volume down 55.8%, depth down 32.8%. Against a Matrixyl 3000 product: volume down 31.6%. Published in a low-profile journal not indexed in PubMed; funding not stated.15 The negative one: after CO2 laser around the mouth (13 finished), blinded graders and software saw no difference in redness, wrinkles or skin quality; only satisfaction differed.16 Famous figures: a 71-woman face cream and a 41-woman eye cream appear only inside a review by the molecule's developer; no primary paper was found, so treat them as unverified. The "70% vs vitamin C 50% vs retinoic acid 40%" thigh study does exist (Abdulghani 1998, traced in the Acne case): 20 healthy people, 1 month, counting people with any new collagen (7 of 10 copper, 4 of 10 tretinoin), with copper and tretinoin never on the same person.14 Injected: no human skin data in any review, and no adequately powered trial of microneedle delivery either.18,19,20 Copper: lysyl oxidase needs copper to weld collagen12; copper raised elastin about 100% in human skin kept in a dish, in a study run with a copper-textile company.13 Nobody has shown healthy skin is short of copper. Status: a cosmetic ingredient in creams; an FDA advisory panel was due to review injectable GHK-Cu in February.52

Full card: GHK-Cu in the Library

Matrixyl

One maker-run trialPalmitoyl pentapeptide-4 and its newer blends.

Fine lines deepen when the collagen holding the surface up thins out. Matrixyl is built as a fake collagen fragment, a note telling fibroblasts that collagen broke and needs replacing. In 93 women aged 35 to 55, half the face got moisturiser with 3 ppm of the peptide and half the same moisturiser without; after 12 weeks the peptide side had fewer fine lines by image analysis and expert graders.30 The trial was run by the company selling it, and in a later trial a Matrixyl 3000 cream lost to GHK-Cu.15

The deeper read
Matrixyl 3000 (palmitoyl tripeptide-1 with tetrapeptide-7) and tripeptide-38 appear only in multi-ingredient, company-funded products, so the peptide's own effect cannot be separated.31 Well tolerated in the 93-woman trial. No Library card yet.

Argireline and SNAP-8

Argireline: small trialsSNAP-8: no trial

Expression lines come from muscle pulling the same fold thousands of times. Argireline copies one end of SNAP-25, a protein nerves use to tell muscle to contract; picture a whisper where Botox is a wall. In 60 adults with eye wrinkles, 48.9% on Argireline reached the trial's anti-wrinkle score after 4 weeks, against 0% on placebo.27 Nobody has shown how much gets through skin to the muscle, and SNAP-8 has no peer-reviewed human trial at all.

What people report. 13 reviewers with an outcome (of 20) for a 10% Argireline solution: 7 saw mild softening of forehead, frown or eye lines, 4 saw nothing (one after 6 months), 2 mixed. Two said lines came back when they skipped days, and none called it Botox-like.79 SNAP-8: no first-hand reports found.

The deeper read
Other trials: 24 volunteers in 4 groups of 6, 60 days: smoother surface and less water loss.28 A microneedle patch with acetyl hexapeptide-8 beat the patch alone in 52 women, split-face, 4 weeks.29 Missing: any head-to-head with Botox.

Full card: SNAP-8 in the Library

Thymosin beta-4 and TB-500

Wound gel: phase 2≠ Hair: mice and human tissue disagree

Chronic leg ulcers stall because skin cells stop crawling in to close the gap. Thymosin beta-4 is a scaffolding crew holding actin, the cell's internal frame, so cells can move and new vessels can form.25 As a gel in 73 people with venous leg ulcers it was as safe as placebo, one dose may have sped healing, and about a quarter healed fully within 3 months.25 On hair it splits: it grew hair in mice but slowed human follicles in a dish.69,70 TB-500, the fragment people inject, was not the molecule in any of these trials.

The deeper read
Ulcers: a review reported healing almost a month faster, but only among people who healed, a subgroup rather than the whole trial.26 Rare blistering disease (EB): two trials stopped early, in 2010 (planned 30) and 2021 (4 enrolled), with no efficacy result.89 Hair in a dish: human scalp follicles made 10 to 20% less hair shaft and entered the shrinking phase early at 1,000 ng/ml.70 Cosmetic use: no human data. Banned in sport.20

Full cards: Thymosin Beta-4 · TB-500 frag 17 to 23

Collagen peptides

Human, contestedSwallowed, not applied.

Skin water and spring fade as dermal collagen thins. Swallowed collagen is digested into small pieces that may act as a signal to fibroblasts: more a slow moisturiser from the inside than a filler. Pooling 35 trials and 2,534 people found small to moderate gains in hydration and elasticity after 12 weeks or more.8 Pooling 23 trials, the effect vanished in studies without company funding.9 Expect a little more moisture, not fewer wrinkles.

What people report. Skin: 8 better, 12 no change, 2 unsure among 22 posters. Hair: 5 better, 8 no change. Nails: 7 better, 4 no change. Several in the better group changed other things at the same time.80

Full card: Collagen peptides in the Library

Exosomes and PDRN

Small, low-quality trialsClinic injections and post-procedure serums.

Both are sold on the promise of regeneration after a procedure. Exosomes are tiny packets cells send each other; PDRN is purified DNA fragments. Four exosome studies with 148 people reported wrinkles down 16.8 to 25.4%, at low to moderate certainty.33 Nine PDRN studies with 219 patients were low to moderate quality, with mild side effects.36 No exosome product is approved, and the FDA has treated cord-derived ones as unlicensed biologics after serious adverse events.35

The deeper read
A 2026 review of 21 exosome articles was consistently positive on elasticity, wrinkles and hydration and asked for larger standardised trials.32 A review of 19 regenerative-aesthetics studies (14 randomised) found no consistent high-quality evidence, unclear mechanisms and no long-term safety data.34

AHK-Cu

Hair: follicles in a dishSkin: no study

Most of what is said about AHK-Cu and skin is borrowed from GHK-Cu. Picture the same copper courier with a different first amino acid. The one place it has met human tissue is hair: it lengthened human hair follicles kept alive in a dish and helped dermal papilla cells survive.21 For skin there is no human, animal or tissue study at all, and no first-hand report could be read this pass.

Full card: AHK-Cu in the Library

KPV

Animal only for skinThe last three amino acids of α-MSH.

Eczema, rosacea and irritated skin are partly an alarm that keeps ringing. KPV is the off switch for that alarm: the tail of α-MSH, the hormone that both calms inflammation and tans, with almost all the calming kept and none of the tanning.22 Related α-MSH peptides calmed irritant and allergic skin reactions in animals.23 No human skin trial has been run, and no first-hand eczema or rosacea report was found.

Full card: KPV in the Library

BPC-157

Rat wounds only

Wounds and burns heal only as fast as new blood vessels reach them. BPC-157 is best pictured as a foreman calling those vessels in. In rats, from the group that discovered it, cuts, deep burns, diabetic ulcers and alkali burns healed faster.24 That one group writes most of the papers. There is no human skin, scar or cosmetic study, and even its human tendon data is a single uncontrolled case series.20

Full card: BPC-157 in the Library

GLOW and KLOW

Never studied as a blendGHK-Cu, BPC-157 and TB-500; KLOW adds KPV.

These blends promise glow by stacking four repair signals in one vial: the reset message, the foreman, the scaffolding crew and the alarm switch. No study of any blend exists, for benefit, stability or safety, and a 2026 review says most new peptides need more study before safe human use.85 Only GHK-Cu has human skin data, and only as a cream. One first-hand GLOW user described dewier, firmer skin peaking at week 8 of 12, alongside diet and sleep changes.83

What people report. GLOW 1 person, KLOW 0. Hair or nail gains for either: 0 first-hand reports, though product cards often claim them.

Full cards: GLOW · KLOW

GLP-1 drugs and skin

Surveys and small trialsSemaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide.

Losing a lot of weight fast takes fat from the face along with everywhere else, and that is most of what people call Ozempic face.Sagging rose step by step with the weight lost, the pattern you expect from fat loss. A direct drug effect on skin cells has only been shown in lab work so far.49 In a survey of 1,226 patients, sagging was reported by 15% who lost under 10% of body weight, 44% at 10 to 20% and 72% above 20%.48 Inflamed skin can go the other way: psoriasis scores fell from 21 to 10 in 31 people on semaglutide for 12 weeks in an open trial.50 About 22% of users show a nutrient shortfall within a year.49

What people report. In one forum thread, 4 of 7 said their face looked older or lost volume, 2 said better (with skincare), 1 no change.81

The deeper read
Psoriasis improved in 47% (22 of 47) and eczema in 27% (17 of 64) by self-report.48 A psoriasis primer counts 40 to 80% score drops, mostly in small, short studies without control groups.51 No energy device has proven benefit in GLP-1 users47; a split-face cream trial in 25 users reported laxity down 44% by week 12, run by the product maker.86

Full cards: Semaglutide · Tirzepatide · Retatrutide

5Tan: melanotans and the sun

A tan is pigment cells switching toward brown-black pigment. The switch is a receptor called MC1R. Switched on, it makes mostly eumelanin, which protects and tans; weak or off, it makes red-yellow pheomelanin, which does not protect. Common weak versions give red hair, freckles, poor tanning and a higher melanoma risk.40

Sun turns the switch indirectly, through damage to skin cells. Melanotans turn it directly, with no UV, so colour lands wherever pigment cells sit.Spots that already hold the most pigment cells, moles and freckles, darken most, and face, lips and gums can darken too.42,90 A sun tan follows where light hit. Whether a strong melanotan can push a weak redhead receptor into protective pigment has never been tested.

Melanotan 1 (afamelanotide)

Approved drug, for a disease

Some people cannot stand in sunlight without burning pain. Afamelanotide is a longer-lasting copy of α-MSH that builds protective pigment from the inside. In two trials of 74 and 94 people with that disease (EPP), an implant every 60 days raised pain-free time in sunlight; in the 94-person US trial, to a median 69.4 hours against 40.8 over 6 months. Funded by the maker.37 With narrowband UV it repigmented vitiligo better than UV alone, 48.6% vs 33.3% at day 168, mainly in darker skin.38 It is a medicine for a disease, not a cosmetic tan.

Full card: Melanotan 1 in the Library

Melanotan 2

Human, 3 menUnlicensed.

A tan without the sun is the whole appeal. MT-2 is a master key: it turns MC1R in pigment cells, and also MC3R, MC4R and MC5R, which drive appetite, erections and oil glands. The only human trial gave it to 3 men: 2 visibly darkened on face, upper body and buttocks after 5 low doses, with nausea, yawning and stretching, and erections lasting 1 to 5 hours.39 No safety trial exists, and moles are where the trouble shows.

What people report. 3 first-hand reviewers: all 3 tanned, all 3 had side effects, 1 got new dark moles and freckles; all 3 also used sunbeds.82 A study of 623 forum posts by 205 users found misinformation, sunbed use and shared or contaminated products.46

Full card: MT-2 in the Library

PT-141 (bremelanotide)

Phase 3 safety dataLicensed for low desire in premenopausal women.

PT-141 is taken for desire, not colour, but it turns the same receptors. Across about 3,500 trial subjects, nausea hit 40% against 1.3% on placebo and flushing 20.3%. Dark patches were rare at label dosing, but appeared in more than a third after up to 16 daily doses in a row.41

Full card: PT-141 in the Library

Moles and melanoma

  • Multiple new atypical moles within one week of two injections, confirmed by a dermatologist.45
  • Eruptive moles in a man with past melanoma who injected an α-MSH copy; they faded after he stopped.42
  • Melanoma case reports after MT-2, including melanoma in situ and a mouth melanoma in a 22-year-old after nasal spray.44,43,87 Dermatologists have called it a public health issue.88
  • Case reports cannot prove cause, and many users also sunbathe or use sunbeds. Darker moles are also harder to check for change.

6Nails

Fingernails grow about 0.1 mm a day, roughly 3.1 mm a month, the same for men and women and both hands, in a study of 500 fingernails.53 A whole fingernail takes 4 to 6 months to replace; a big toenail 12 to 18.The plate is dead keratin made under the skin fold. Any real change from a supplement has to grow out to the tip, which takes at least 3 to 6 months. A product that "works" in 1 or 2 weeks is very unlikely to be the product.

Brittle nails affect up to 20% of people, mostly women over 50. Two shapes: layers peeling at the tip, usually from water, soap and solvents; and lengthwise ridges and splits that start in the matrix. Treat the cause first: iron, thyroid, psoriasis and fungus all show up in nails.54 Placebo groups in nail trials improve too, so open "X% improved" numbers overstate the effect.61

Gloves, short nails, oil Consensus advice
Gloves for wet work, oil or hand cream rubbed into the nail and fold after washing, fewer acetone removals. Lowest risk, best logic, no trial.
Collagen peptides Open study
25 people, 2.5 g a day for 24 weeks: growth up 12%, broken nails down 42%, no placebo.60 The one placebo trial (85 women) measured only nail colour, and placebo caught up by day 84.61 7 of 11 posters said nails got stronger.80
Biotin Old open studies
41 of 45 had firmer nails after about 5.5 months, no placebo, 26 of 71 dropped from the count56; thickness up 25% in 8 people.57 All 18 published cases where it helped had a deficiency or disease underneath.58 It can throw off blood tests, including the troponin heart attack test.59
Solubilised keratin Industry trials
50 women, 90 days: better nail strength and appearance than placebo, though vitamins were added.62
Silicon (ch-OSA) Trial, nails vs baseline only
50 women, 20 weeks: skin smoother than placebo; nail brittleness fell only against the same group's start.63
Nail lacquer (HPCH) Small trials
Nail psoriasis score down 65% over 24 weeks while the untreated other hand stayed the same.65 In brittle nails, damage scores fell 57%, 62% with biotin added.64
GHK-Cu, AHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500 No nail data
PubMed searches returned 0 nail studies for any of them. Any nail claim is a guess from "it does collagen".
GLP-1 drugs Harm signal
575 users vs about 1,350 controls, self-reported: nail detachment 39% vs 9%, discolouration 46% vs 17%, still 2 to 3 times higher after adjusting for nutrition. Funded by a skincare company; about half had nail problems before starting.67,68

Supplements are not regulated, and a review of 176 "hair, skin and nails" products found most lacked independent testing.66 See a doctor for a brown or black stripe down one nail (a sign of nail melanoma)55, a thick yellow crumbly nail, a nail lifting off, or changes in all 20 at once.

7Hair and breakouts, in brief

Thinning hair
Each follicle grows for 2 to 6 years, shrinks for 2 to 3 weeks, then rests about 3 months.71 In sensitive follicles DHT shortens each growth phase until hairs turn fine.75 Finasteride held its benefit for 5 years while placebo kept thinning.74 The peptide data is thin and sometimes points both ways. Read the Hair case
Breakouts
Acne starts in the oil gland and follicle drawn in view A. Plain base creams alone cut acne spots 35 to 60% by 12 weeks in trials, and plain gel cut rosacea spots about 41%, so numbers here need a placebo to mean anything.91 Read the Acne case

9Pairings

Microneedling plus a cream Trials
Needles open a path: microneedling plus tranexamic acid beat microneedling alone7, and a microneedle patch with Argireline or EGF beat the patch alone.29 Microneedling plus GHK-Cu has no human trial.18
Afamelanotide plus UV light Trial
Beat UV alone for vitiligo, 48.6% vs 33.3% repigmentation.38
Retinoid plus sunscreen Standard
The usual pairing: the retinoid repairs, the sunscreen stops new damage that would undo it.1,11
Copper with acids or vitamin C Reports only
A common irritation complaint when layered in the same step; one reviewer broke out with copper and retinol at night and was fine once copper moved to the morning.78
MT-2 plus sunbeds Harm
All three first-hand MT-2 users also used sunbeds, which confounds both the tan and the moles.82,46
GLOW and KLOW stacks Never tested
Mixing has never been tested for benefit, stability or safety.85

11Usual questions

Does GHK-Cu actually work on wrinkles?

As a cream, probably a little. The best trial, 40 women over 8 weeks, cut wrinkle volume 55.8% against the base; a laser trial found no measurable benefit, and reviewers call the clinical data thin.

Is injecting GHK-Cu better than a cream?

Unknown. There is no human skin data for injection at all. One clinician reports site infections and raised liver enzymes and copper in heavy self-injectors.

Copper peptide or retinol?

Retinoids have decades of trials and biopsy proof. The famous "copper 70% vs retinoic acid 40%" figure comes from a 1-month study of 20 people's thigh skin that counted people with any new collagen (7 of 10 vs 4 of 10), in separate groups. Retinoid first.

Does oral collagen work?

It may improve hydration and elasticity a little after 12 weeks or more. Wrinkle claims do not survive careful analysis, and independent trials show no effect.

Is Argireline Botox in a jar?

No. Small 4-week trials show modest smoothing, users describe gentle softening that fades when they stop, and nobody has compared it with Botox.

Does Matrixyl work?

One maker-run trial of 93 women says yes for fine lines; a Matrixyl 3000 cream lost to GHK-Cu in another.

Do BPC-157 or TB-500 heal skin or scars?

BPC-157 in rats only. The thymosin beta-4 trials used the full protein as a gel on chronic ulcers, with modest phase 2 results; TB-500 itself has no skin data.

Is a melanotan tan safe?

No safety trial exists; the only human trial had 3 men. Case reports link it to new and changing moles and to melanoma.

Can redheads tan with melanotan?

Their weak MC1R makes red pigment that does not protect. Whether MT-2 overrides that has never been tested.

Do GLOW or KLOW give a glow?

Never studied as blends. One first-hand GLOW user reported dewier skin alongside other changes. No first-hand hair or nail reports exist for either.

Are exosome facials legit?

Early trials are positive but low quality, and no exosome product is approved; the FDA has warned about unlicensed ones.

What fixes melasma best?

Sunscreen plus tranexamic acid or hydroquinone. Oral tranexamic acid 250 mg three times a day for 12 weeks was the best dose studied.

Will a GLP-1 ruin my face?

Volume loss and sagging track fast, large weight loss: 72% reported sagging after losing more than 20% of body weight. Protein and nutrient gaps may add to it.

How long until nails change?

About 3 mm a month, so 3 to 6 months before new nail reaches the tip. Stop biotin a few days before blood tests and tell the lab.

Does biotin help hair or skin?

Only clearly when you are short of it: all 18 published cases where biotin helped hair or nails had a deficiency or disease underneath.58 It can also skew heart and thyroid blood tests.

White spots mean low calcium?

Almost always small knocks to the matrix, not a shortage.

12Warnings

Before you try anything here

  • Irritation: tretinoin's main side effect; stronger retinol gave more redness. Microneedling: redness in 6.8%.2,3,10
  • Copper serums: about 1 in 6 reviewers got worse, mainly breakouts and eye-area irritation.77 No published allergy case to GHK-Cu, but that is not proof of safety for injection.
  • Melanotans and PT-141: new and darker moles, melanoma case reports, dark patches with daily PT-141. Get moles checked before and during.41,45
  • Unregulated injectables: GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500, MT-2 and the blends have no approved product, no verified purity and unknown dosing. Thymosin beta-4 and TB-500 are banned in sport.20 Unlicensed exosome products have caused serious adverse events.35
  • Biotin: false heart and thyroid test results; tell your doctor and the lab.59
  • GLP-1 users: nail lifting or a sudden change in face volume is worth raising with your prescriber; check protein intake.48,67
  • Who paid: many trials here were run or funded by the maker (Matrixyl, afamelanotide, copper textiles, collagen, GLP-1 skincare, thymosin beta-4). Read the numbers with that in mind.
Before you trust a number
Ask what the comparison was (plain base, placebo, or the person's own start), who paid, and whether the product mixed several actives. Open "X% improved" figures with no control group, common for nails and peptides, overstate effects: plain gel alone cut rosacea spots about 41% in 12 weeks in one trial.91 A vial bought online has never been checked against what was studied.
To watch
An FDA advisory panel review of injectable GHK-Cu.52 A registered semaglutide psoriasis trial (NCT07401992).51 Full results of the peer-reviewed GLP-1 nail study.68

Study doses are given only to explain an experiment, never as dosing advice.

13Glossary

Dermis
The thick layer under the surface, made of collagen and elastin; where firmness lives.
Fibroblast
The cell that builds collagen and elastin.
TEWL
Transepidermal water loss: how much water escapes through the skin. Lower is a better barrier.
MMPs
Enzymes that break collagen down; UV switches them on.
MC1R
The switch on pigment cells that picks brown-black or red-yellow pigment.
Eumelanin, pheomelanin
Brown-black pigment that protects; red-yellow pigment that does not.
Vehicle or base
The same cream without the active ingredient, the fair comparison in skin trials.
Ex vivo, in a dish
Real human skin or hair follicles kept alive outside the body for a few days.
Anagen, catagen, telogen
The hair cycle: grow, shrink, rest.
Matrix
The living factory under the nail fold that makes the plate.
