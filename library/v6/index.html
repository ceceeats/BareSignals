How to read a sticker
  1. Big block: the main research area.
  2. Middle block and left side: other research areas.
  3. Bottom-left corner: what people report feeling.
  4. Dots: extra connections. filled is human research, a ring is animal or community only.
  5. Red dot: an FDA-approved medicine exists (not this vial). Red ring: approved outside the US only.
