F

What clashes

The six things that make the site feel inconsistent. Start here.

F1

Purple means four things

Appetite & satiety#DDD0F6role
H.trial badge#DDD0F6same lavender
Drive & mental stamina#B493E8role
Tip / How to mix box#B493E8same violet
Tip and H.trial text#6A4FB0not a palette colour
  • The purple you couldn't place is the Tip box (Library "How to mix", Supplements tips). It borrows the Drive role colour.
  • H.trial (human trial) borrows the Appetite colour, so on a GLP-1 tile the badge and the role tag look like the same thing.

SuggestionGive tips and H.trial their own non-role look (ink outline, no fill) so purple only ever means a role.

F2

Red means five things

Inflammatory signaling#FF5050role
Warning box edge#FF5050Supplements
FDA ✓ / Rx ✓#E00000badge fill
Warning box + red-flag test chips#C02626Library, Blood Work
Residue warning#A8261CCalculator
Heart & vessels#9E1B32role, also Skip box and warning titles
  • Red is your warning colour, but the brightest red on the site is a good-news badge (FDA approved).
  • Inflammation shares its exact red with the Supplements warning edge.
  • There are four different warning reds across pages.

SuggestionOne warning red (#C02626) everywhere. FDA ✓ moves to ink or green so approval never reads as danger.

F3

Orange is not only for tapping

Action orange#DB6830rule: interactive only
Action tint#FFEFE3buttons
Highlight peach#F9D9C2Home, deep read 'Mixed'
Fat metabolism#FFA51Frole
Glucose & insulin#FFCBAArole
  • Your rule: orange means you can tap it. Orange also colours the AA count on every tile, every dose number, the Community dose tag, the "Ever evolving" line on Home and the trial evidence word. None of those are buttons.
  • Fat and Glucose roles sit right next to action orange in hue.

SuggestionKeep orange for things you tap. AA count and dose numbers go to ink, Community dose gets a dashed ink outline.

F4

Green and the other advice colours are role colours

Cancer biology#79A54Drole, also Buy box
Digestive & gut#AACC96role, also Made 'Usually'
Tissue repair#E1DE62role, also Made 'Sometimes'
DNA & telomeres#74BCFFrole, also Made 'Liquid'
OK pill#2F6B46Calculator
  • Buy box = Cancer role green. Skip box = Heart role red. Tip box = Drive role violet. Warning = Inflammation role red.
  • Made page uses role colours for frequency (Usually, Sometimes, Rarely) and states (Liquid).

SuggestionAdvice and status colours get their own small set, tinted and outlined, never a filled role colour.

F5

One role tag, seven sizes, three spellings

APPETITEAPPETITEAPPETITE & SATIETYAPPETITE & SATIETYAppetite & satiety
  • Short name (APPETITE) on stickers and Home, long name (APPETITE & SATIETY) on cards and Blood Work, sentence case (Appetite & satiety) only on Supplements.
  • Home's mini tags are 5.6px, below what a phone can really read. Full list in section R.

SuggestionTwo sizes only: sticker tag (short name) and card tag (long name), both mono caps, same padding.

F6

Home still shows the personal line

  • "I bare my notes for those who can benefit from it." is still live on the Home page. You asked for it to come off to keep the site anonymous.

SuggestionRemove it on the next Home version.
