Every peptide on a Bare Signals card is a string of these 21 building blocks. Your genes spell them out three letters at a time, and the order decides what the chain becomes.

From gene to protein

01DNAA, T, C, G in the nucleus
02mRNACopied out, T swaps to U
03Codon3 letters, 64 options
04Amino acidtRNA brings the match
05ChainPeptide bonds, water leaves
06FoldHelix, sheet, 3D shape

Under about 50 amino acids the chain is called a peptide. Above that it is a protein.

Gene to peptide translator

Pick a peptide to see the gene that would spell it, or type your own DNA. Most research peptides are built chemically, one residue at a time, so this shows the biology, not how a vial is made.
