How to buy good vitamins v1

Supplements are not checked by anyone before they go on sale. Labels are often wrong, and the cheapest products are the ones most often under-dosed. This is how to tell a good product from a bad one, and what the evidence says about the brands people trust.

The short version

01A seal beats a promise"Third party tested" and "made in a GMP facility" are self-declared. Look for a seal you can look up: USP, NSF, Informed, BSCG, IFOS for fish oil. INFERRED
02Buy the form, not the nameMagnesium glycinate cut with oxide, "1,000 mg fish oil" with 300 mg omega-3, milk thistle at half its silymarin claim. Read the active amount per serving. VERIFIED1420
03Avoid marketplace sellersBrand site or an authorised shop. Unknown Amazon brands failed badly in astaxanthin and ubiquinol tests. VERIFIED15 REPORTED16
04Match the lot numberGood brands give a test certificate per lot. IFOS posts every fish oil lot online. No lot, no expiry date, no sale. VERIFIED5
05Big names fail tooAlmost every major brand below has a recall, warning letter or failed test on record. A seal on this product counts more than the brand's reputation. INFERRED
06In Indonesia, check BPOMSearch the number on cekbpom.pom.go.id. Supplements carry SD, SI or SL plus 9 digits. Make sure the maker shown matches the pack. REPORTED18

Where to buy

Marketplaces
  • Amazon long pooled identical stock from many sellers, so a fake could ship under a real listing. It said in Sept 2025 it would stop; not confirmed fully gone. REPORTED17
  • Recalled lots were still being delivered through Amazon months after a recall. VERIFIED21
  • Buy from the brand, its official store, or a shop the brand lists. Skip "sold by" third parties. INFERRED
Indonesia
  • Supplement numbers: SD (made locally), SI (imported), SL (licensed), then 9 digits. MD and ML are food, TR and TI traditional medicine. REPORTED18
  • Check on cekbpom.pom.go.id or scan the 2D code in the BPOM Mobile app. Codes get copied, so the maker name must match. Report fakes to HaloBPOM 1500533. REPORTED18
  • Unregistered imports are allowed for personal use in small amounts, not for resale. REPORTED22
  • iHerb ships here: max 5 supplement items per order, duty above USD 3, some couriers include tax. VERIFIED23

Ingredient makers worth seeing on a label

  • Kaneka QH: patented ubiquinol, with its own quality seal. REPORTED16
  • AstaReal (Sweden, USA) and BioAstin (Cyanotech, Hawaii): algae astaxanthin grown under control. REPORTED
  • Albion TRAACS: fully reacted magnesium bisglycinate chelate, no loose oxide. REPORTED
  • Quatrefolic or Magnafolate: patented methylfolate salts. Stability claims are the makers' own. REPORTED
  • Indena Siliphos: milk thistle silybin bound to phospholipid; absorption data comes from Indena. REPORTED
  • Epax, KD Pharma, AlaskOmega: concentrated fish oils, triglyceride or ethyl ester. REPORTED
  • AjiPure (Ajinomoto): fermented pharmaceutical grade glycine. REPORTED
