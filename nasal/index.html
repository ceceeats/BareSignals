How to set it up

One part BAC water to reconstitute, the rest saline to dilute, into a pump bottle. Default 1 mL BAC + 8 mL saline in a 10 mL bottle.

BAC water dissolves the peptide and keeps it stable; plain saline makes up the volume so the bottle fills and each spray is gentle. Prime the pump with a few sprays into a tissue before the first dose, and after it sits unused, so the first real spray is a full dose. Many protocols split a dose between nostrils: two sprays can mean one per side. Keep it refrigerated and use within the date on your draw log. Math only, not a dose recommendation.
